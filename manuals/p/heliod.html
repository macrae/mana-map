1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Heliod manufactures a resource nobody at the table prices -- cards in opponents' hands -- and five permanents convert it into Treasure, cards, a cost discount and life loss; but the only card in the 99 that converts any of that into a win is one seven-mana sorcery cast twice, which is why a goldfish with no blockers still reaches turn ten without a kill in 86.5% of games.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. A sweeper is on the stack, or an opponent holds four untapped mana in their own main phase, and Heliod is on the battlefield.

Indications.

  • Heliod is on the battlefield and Supreme Verdict or Day of Judgment is in your own hand.
  • An opponent has four or more untapped mana in their precombat main phase and has cast nothing.
  • Greater Auramancy is on the battlefield, so Heliod has shroud and neither Karametra's Blessing nor Guardian of Faith can be aimed at him.
  • The sweeper exiles rather than destroys. Ten of Heliod's fifty battlefield exits across 120 Forge games went to exile, which indestructible does not answer.
  • Your board is Heliod plus one blocker and the rest of your permanents are enchantments and artifacts.

Immediate action.

  1. Count what the wrath takes. If it takes Drift of Phantasms and nothing else, pass and let it resolve.
  2. Cast Teferi's Protection for {2}{W} when the sweeper exiles, or when it takes more than creatures. It does not target, so Greater Auramancy does not stop it, and every permanent you control phases out.
  3. Destroy your own Greater Auramancy when Teferi's Protection is not in hand. Disenchant costs {1}{W}; Cathar Commando flashes in for {1}{W} and sacrifices for {1}. Auramancy grants shroud to other enchantments and not to itself, so both can target it.
  4. Cast Karametra's Blessing for {W} on Heliod once Auramancy is off the battlefield. He is an enchantment creature on both faces, so he gains hexproof and indestructible until end of turn.
  5. Cast Guardian of Faith for {1}{W}{W} instead when the sweeper only destroys and you need two creatures back. It has flash and phases out other target creatures you control.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild the enchantments before the creatures. Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Gleaming Splendor, Howling Mine and Propaganda all survive a creature wrath.
  2. Cast your own Supreme Verdict before you recast Heliod, never after. Log 002 is the game where that order was reversed.
  3. Recast Greater Auramancy for {1}{W} ahead of Heliod when the table's removal is targeted, and hold it in hand while a second sweeper is unaccounted for and Karametra's Blessing is your answer.
  4. Recast Heliod for {2}{W}{W} plus the commander tax and return one of the ten non-God enchantments from your graveyard with the enters trigger.
  5. Rebuild to two permanents, not five, and keep casting at instant speed off Leyline of Anticipation while Heliod is away.
NOTEGreater Auramancy is the exception that bites on this page. It grants shroud to other enchantments you control, Heliod is an enchantment creature on both faces, and shroud stops your own targeting as well as theirs — so the deck's two answers to a wrath on the commander, Karametra's Blessing and Guardian of Faith, are both illegal targets while Auramancy is out. The way back is that Auramancy does not grant itself shroud: Disenchant, Cathar Commando and Loran of the Third Path can each destroy your own. There are no Auras in the 99, so Auramancy's second line does nothing here.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. Heliod is the target, or the spell you just cast is being answered.

Indications.

  • A removal spell is on the stack with Heliod as its target. Targeted removal is 22 of his 43 attributed exits across 120 Forge games, against 16 opposing wraths and 5 of your own.
  • Greater Auramancy is not on the battlefield and Heliod has been out a full turn.
  • The stack is two objects deep and both of them are yours to lose.
  • One opponent has held two or more untapped mana across a full turn cycle and cast nothing.
  • Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe or Gleaming Splendor is the target and Heliod is not.

Immediate action.

  1. Say the untapped mana at every seat out loud before you respond.
  2. Counter it with Fierce Guardianship for no mana while you control a commander. It counters target noncreature spell, which covers every instant and sorcery aimed at Heliod — including the Swords to Plowshares Alex redirected onto him in log 001.
  3. Cast Karametra's Blessing for {W} on Heliod when the counter is not in hand and Greater Auramancy is not on the battlefield.
  4. Cast Guardian of Faith for {1}{W}{W} instead when the removal exiles. Phasing answers an exile; the Blessing's indestructible does not.
  5. Let Heliod go when you hold none of those, and keep the counter for a spell that wins the game or kills you.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Greater Auramancy for {1}{W} at the first window. Ten of the eleven enchantments in the 99 gain shroud, Heliod included, and a spell cannot be redirected onto a shrouded permanent.
  2. Stop countering after the second exchange in a war you did not start.
  3. Recast Heliod for {2}{W}{W} plus the tax and take an enchantment out of the graveyard with the enters trigger.
  4. Cast the rest of the turn without him. Leyline of Anticipation grants the flash; nothing in the 99 replaces the discount.
NOTEV12 traded the deck's only Equipment for Greater Auramancy and the protection is differently shaped rather than simply larger. Auramancy covers ten enchantments and the commander at once and needs no equip step, but it has to be on the battlefield before the removal is cast, it does not protect itself, and it takes Karametra's Blessing and Guardian of Faith off Heliod while it is out. Forge has never exercised the response half: across 120 games on the v1.2.1 list, Teferi's Protection was cast 0 times, Guardian of Faith 0 times with 6 discards, and Karametra's Blessing 9 times with 2 resolving on Heliod. The 99 now holds no Equipment and no haste at all, so Kwain, Itinerant Meddler and Loran of the Third Path each wait a turn before they can tap.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:001.

3.3COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. An opponent is assembling a line that ends the game and you are not close to a second Approach.

Indications.

  • One seat tutors and the card it found does not go to the battlefield.
  • The table is attacking you and leaving the seat with the engine alone.
  • You hold a counter and cannot pay for it. Interaction sits in hand 0.929 of the time at turn six and is castable 0.627.
  • The line runs through a graveyard or a land.
  • A seat casts one permanent and the other two seats stop attacking it.

Immediate action.

  1. Stop saving mana for your own turn. Untap and hold {U}{U} through the combo seat's whole turn.
  2. Counter the first piece, not the payoff. Swan Song costs {U} and counters an enchantment, instant or sorcery; An Offer You Can't Refuse costs {U} and counters a noncreature spell.
  3. Use Fierce Guardianship for no mana when the piece is a noncreature spell and Heliod is on the battlefield.
  4. Overload Cyclonic Rift for {6}{U} when the pieces are already on the battlefield and return every nonland permanent you do not control.
  5. Name the seat and the card to both other players when you cannot stop it yourself.

Subsequent.

  1. Destroy the resolved artifact or enchantment. Disenchant, Cathar Commando and Loran of the Third Path destroy one each; Cyclonic Rift and Otawara, Soaring City bounce one instead.
  2. Concede the pieces you cannot reach and race. Artifacts, creatures and enchantments are the classes this deck answers.
  3. Go back to holding one counter a turn cycle once the piece is answered.
  4. Cast the first Approach of the Second Sun at the next window instead of adding a fourth gift piece.
NOTEA counter you win is not a game you win. In log 001 the pilot countered at the right moments, lost the one exchange that mattered and lost the game; in log 002 Oliver assembled a one-card Cranko line while the table beat on the pilot. Against a one- or two-card combo seat the plan is to be first, and the stage that ends the game here holds a single card.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:002.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. You have missed a land drop and the cast you need is {6}{W} on two separate turns.

Indications.

  • Turn six arrives and you are on five lands. The goldfish hits its land drop 0.875 at turn three, 0.727 at turn four and 0.536 at turn six.
  • benchmark.json puts the missed-land-drop rate at 0.254 with its worst turn at six.
  • Mean available mana crosses seven between turn seven (6.411) and turn eight (7.062), with the commander's discount switched off in the model.
  • A colour is missing rather than a land: 24 white and 27 blue sources against a Karsten target of 36 each, and 20 white to 23 blue among the lands.
  • Interaction is in hand and is not castable. Those two rates are 0.929 and 0.627 at turn six.

Immediate action.

  1. Play the untapped land. Irrigated Farmland and Temple of Enlightenment enter tapped; hold them for a turn you are not casting.
  2. Crack Flooded Strand for the colour you are short of, not for the land you would rather own.
  3. Cast a rock ahead of a gift piece. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Azorius Signet, Talisman of Progress, Thought Vessel, Mind Stone and Decanter of Endless Water are the seven.
  4. Resolve Smothering Tithe for {3}{W}. Every opponent draw is a Treasure unless that seat pays {2}, and Treasure is the only mana in this deck that banks across turns.
  5. Stop holding a counter you cannot cast and spend the mana on the gift.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Gleaming Splendor for {1}{W} behind a draw-step gift. One Treasure per opponent per turn cycle costs you nothing after the {1}{W}.
  2. Pay the Phyrexian half of the flip with two life when blue is short. The flip is {3}{U/P}.
  3. Sacrifice Mind Stone for {1} and a card once seven mana is on the table and the colourless is spare.
  4. Tap Mikokoro, Center of the Sea for {C} rather than paying its {2} to draw, until the land drops are landing again.
  5. Count to seven twice before you cast the first Approach. The bill is {6}{W} on two separate turns.
NOTENo logged game is filed under mana-drought; both losses are filed elsewhere, so this page is not grounded in a game. It rests on benchmark.json, goldfish_metrics.json and mana_analysis.json, all three of them measured on this list. Add the pilot's own correction from log 002 before you decide you cannot afford the cast: "with the treasure and the discount it is two or three, not seven." Count the Treasure on the table and the cards your opponents have drawn this turn, then read the price.

3.5STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. The table is drawing, you are alive, and Approach of the Second Sun is not in hand.

Indications.

  • Turn eight or later and Approach of the Second Sun has not been cast once.
  • Long-Term Plans or Mystical Tutor has been in your hand for two turns.
  • The gift is online and nothing converts it: no Rhystic Study, no Smothering Tithe, no Gleaming Splendor, no Consecrated Sphinx.
  • Win-condition access assembles 0.494 and 0.364 by turn six, while the table draw engine assembles 0.921 at a mean turn of 2.434.
  • You are holding cards you are not spending while two opponents build boards you cannot block.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Long-Term Plans for {2}{U} the turn you draw it and put Approach of the Second Sun third from the top.
  2. Cast Mystical Tutor for {U} at the end step before your turn when Long-Term Plans is not in hand, and put Approach on top.
  3. Transmute Drift of Phantasms for {1}{U}{U} and fetch Long-Term Plans. Transmute finds mana value three, and Enlightened Tutor finds artifacts and enchantments, so neither reaches a seven-mana sorcery.
  4. Cast the first Approach the turn you can pay {6}{W}. It gains 7 life and goes seventh from the top.
  5. Count your draws per turn cycle from that moment. Seven of them returns the card without a second tutor.

Subsequent.

  1. Convert the gift before you add to it. Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Smothering Tithe, Consecrated Sphinx, Faerie Mastermind, Gleaming Splendor and Teferi's Ageless Insight are the seven that make a symmetric draw one-sided.
  2. Stop deploying gift pieces once two are on the battlefield.
  3. Cast Prosperity or Skyscribing only with a converter already down.
  4. Flip Heliod for {3}{U/P} so the second cast can happen on an opponent's turn.
NOTEIn log 002 the pilot held Long-Term Plans because he read Approach as a seven-drop, waited, and the window closed. The Forge record on the v1.2.1 list says the same thing from the other side: 39 Approach casts across 120 games, 12 games reached a second cast, and the record attributes 12 of its 20 wins to the effect of the card. No opponent countered an Approach across all 39 casts. The deck's record at the table is 0 wins and 2 losses over two logged games, and the goldfish reaches turn ten without a kill in 0.865 of 10,000 games — which is why this page's procedure is a tutor and not a board.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.6POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. Two seats are attacking you and the third is being left alone.

Indications.

  • Smothering Tithe has resolved and you control no creatures.
  • Two opponents attack you on consecutive turns and neither attacks the other.
  • You have countered two spells from the same seat.
  • You have asked for Rhystic Study's {1} three times in one turn cycle.
  • You flipped Heliod and cast nothing afterwards.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the seat with the fastest board out loud, and name what it is doing.
  2. Take The Second Doctor's end-step trigger. Each opponent who draws cannot attack you or your permanents during their next turn.
  3. Activate Kwain, Itinerant Meddler or Temple Bell before you pass. A card and a life to each seat costs you nothing you were spending.
  4. Deploy Propaganda for {2}{U} or Ghostly Prison for {2}{W}. Each attacker costs that seat {2}.
  5. Stop countering spells that do not win the game or kill you.

Subsequent.

  1. Hold Smothering Tithe until you control a blocker, or until a wrath has already happened.
  2. Lead with Gleaming Splendor instead when both are castable. It costs {1}{W} against the Tithe's {3}{W}, it takes nothing from anybody, and it makes Treasure off cards the table was drawing already.
  3. Let the taxers deter. Propaganda, Ghostly Prison and Archangel of Tithes charge per attacker and show no card from your hand.
  4. Name the seat you want to stop attacking you when you tap Loran of the Third Path, and give them the card.
  5. Point the table at the combo seat every turn until somebody attacks it.
NOTEThe gift reads as generosity for exactly as long as nothing you control looks like a clock. Nine of the ten gift permanents hand the whole table cards, and across 120 Forge games this seat's combat damage to players had a mean of 3.28 and a median of 0.5, while the most commander damage it ever dealt in one game was 8 and it was never lethal. The table's read that you are harmless is correct until the second Approach. In log 002 the Tithe landed on an empty board and made the pilot the target on the spot.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. A flying board is attacking and you cannot block it.

Indications.

  • One opponent's board is fliers and your blockers are on the ground.
  • You are below 25 life on turn eight.
  • Kefnet the Mindful is on the battlefield and your hand is under seven cards, so he cannot block.
  • Across 120 Forge games this seat took a mean of 37.92 combat damage with a median of 30.5.
  • heliod was eliminated in 74 of those 120 games, 61 of them to damage: giada-angels 43, abaddon 11, baylen-tokens 7.

Immediate action.

  1. Count the damage on the board against your life total and decide whether you have one more turn or two.
  2. Cast the sweeper when the threat is creatures. Supreme Verdict costs {1}{W}{W}{U} and cannot be countered; Day of Judgment costs {2}{W}{W} and can.
  3. Deploy Propaganda for {2}{U} or Ghostly Prison for {2}{W} when the sweeper is not in hand. Each charges that seat {2} for each creature attacking you.
  4. Cast Secure the Wastes for {X}{W} at the end step before the attack. It is an instant and every token blocks once.
  5. Channel Eiganjo, Seat of the Empire for {2}{W} and deal 4 damage to an attacking creature. The cost drops {1} for each legendary creature you control.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild the fort rather than a board. Propaganda, Ghostly Prison and Archangel of Tithes assemble 0.509, and 0.370 by turn six, so deploy the first one you draw.
  2. Block with Drift of Phantasms and Archangel of Tithes. Both fly; Kefnet blocks only while you hold seven cards.
  3. Buy the turn with The Second Doctor's end-step trigger rather than with a card out of hand.
  4. Cast the first Approach for the 7 life when life is the resource you are short of.
NOTENo logged game is filed under raced, so this page is not grounded in a game. It rests on the 120-game Forge run against giada-angels, baylen-tokens and abaddon (record b0f44e4a), whose heliod seat is the v1.2.1 list — two versions back, and none of V12's three cards was in it. Read those figures as the shape of the loss and not as a rate for this list.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two to four lands with a white source and a blue source among them.
  • One gift piece castable by turn three: Howling Mine at {2}, Kami of the Crescent Moon at {U}{U}, Dictate of Kruphix at {1}{U}{U}, Temple Bell at {3}, Kwain, Itinerant Meddler at {W}{U} or Horn of Greed at {3}.
  • Something to cast before turn four that is not Approach of the Second Sun.
  • No more than two cards costing five or more.

Ship on sight.

  • One land, whatever else is in it.
  • Six or more lands with no gift piece and no converter.
  • A hand of counters with no gift piece.
  • Two lands that both enter tapped.
  • A hand whose cheapest spell is Heliod.
  • A hand whose white spells are Gleaming Splendor and Greater Auramancy. Both cost {1}{W} and both do nothing on an empty board.
NOTEKeep for the gift and the mana, not for Heliod. There is no pressure to rush the commander out in this build — the pilot's own line in log 002 — and the goldfish casts him at a median turn of 4 and 0.886 of the time by turn six without a hand being kept for him. Leyline of Anticipation in the opener starts on the battlefield and does not count against the second keep criterion: it grants the flash half of the back face and nothing else.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 74% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Play a land every turn. The deck hits its turn-three drop 0.875 of the time and its turn-six drop 0.536.
  2. Cast Mystic Remora on turn one or two when you have {U}. Pay the cumulative upkeep while it draws more than one card a cycle and let it go when it does not.
  3. Cast the gift piece ahead of the rock on turn two or three. Howling Mine at {2}, Kami of the Crescent Moon at {U}{U} and Dictate of Kruphix at {1}{U}{U} need nothing else on the battlefield.
  4. Hold Gleaming Splendor until a draw-step gift is down, then cast it for {1}{W}. Its trigger reads an opponent's SECOND card each turn and a natural draw step is one card, so on a bare board it makes nothing. Howling Mine, Font of Mythos, Kami of the Crescent Moon and Dictate of Kruphix each give every opponent a second card on that opponent's own turn; behind any one of them Splendor is one Treasure per opponent per turn cycle.
  5. Cast Enlightened Tutor for {W} on turn one or two and fetch the gift rather than the converter. It finds Howling Mine, Font of Mythos, Temple Bell, Horn of Greed or Dictate of Kruphix, and the reveal tells the table nothing this early.
  6. Hold Smothering Tithe until you control a blocker or until a wrath has already happened.
  7. Pass with mana open rather than casting Heliod on turn four to be casting something.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Gift first. One of the eleven pieces resolves at a mean turn of 2.434 and the group assembles 0.921. Nothing later in the deck fires until one of them is on the battlefield.
  2. Converter second. Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Smothering Tithe, Consecrated Sphinx, Faerie Mastermind, Gleaming Splendor and Teferi's Ageless Insight turn a symmetric draw one-sided. Gleaming Splendor and Faerie Mastermind read the opponent's second card, so they go behind a draw-step gift and not in front of one.
  3. Greater Auramancy third, for {1}{W}, and ahead of Heliod. It gives shroud to the other ten enchantments you control and Heliod is one of them, but it has to be on the battlefield before the removal is cast. The price is that Karametra's Blessing and Guardian of Faith can no longer target him; keep Disenchant or Cathar Commando as the way to undo it.
  4. Heliod fourth, and only behind a counter or behind Auramancy. He costs {2}{W}{W} and 22 of his 43 attributed exits across 120 games were targeted removal.
  5. Flip him fifth for {3}{U/P}, paying the Phyrexian half with two life when blue is short. The flip is sorcery speed and it is the last sorcery-speed decision you make.
  6. Fort sixth. Deploy the first of Propaganda, Ghostly Prison or Archangel of Tithes you draw; the group assembles 0.509 and 0.370 by turn six.
  7. Tutor last, and cast the tutor the turn you can see {6}{W} on the next one. Long-Term Plans puts Approach of the Second Sun third from the top; Mystical Tutor puts it on top.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Untap with mana open. With the back face on the battlefield you may cast every spell in the deck as though it had flash.
  2. Activate Temple Bell, Mikokoro, Center of the Sea, Kwain, Itinerant Meddler or Loran of the Third Path on your own turn before you cast anything. Those four and The Second Doctor's end step are the five pieces that feed the discount on your own turn.
  3. Count the cards each opponent draws in their draw step. That count is the discount on the next spell you cast during that turn.
  4. Spend Gleaming Splendor's {2}{W} on an opponent's turn and after their draw step, targeting yourself and that opponent. You draw, and their card is their second of the turn. On your own turn it is their first and the second-card triggers stay off. The activation is an ability, so the back face never discounts it.
  5. Cast the generic-cost artifacts at an opponent's end step. Isochron Scepter and Mind Stone cost {2} and are free behind two opponent draws; Horn of Greed costs {3} and is free behind three.
  6. Cast Isochron Scepter only with an instant of mana value two or less in hand — the imprint happens as it enters — then activate it for {2} each turn.
  7. Ask for Rhystic Study's {1} and Mystic Remora's {4} every single time.
  8. Take The Second Doctor's trigger at your end step and hand out the cards.
  9. Pass with {U}{U} rather than casting a gift piece you do not need.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Cast the first Approach of the Second Sun for {6}{W} on the turn you can pay it. It gains you 7 life and puts the card seventh from the top of your library.
  2. Count the draws you take per turn cycle from that moment. Seven returns the card to hand without a tutor.
  3. Cast Mystical Tutor for {U} or Long-Term Plans for {2}{U} when seven draws is more than two turns away.
  4. Hold the second cast for the turn your opponents have drawn the most, and take it at an end step with Heliod flipped. Each card they have drawn that turn takes {1} off the {6}.
  5. Count the untapped blue at every other seat before you cast it. That stack is the window they get, and a countered Approach goes to your graveyard instead of back into your library.
  6. Hold one answer for the answer. Dovin's Veto cannot be countered, Fierce Guardianship is free while Heliod is on the battlefield, and Swan Song counters an instant or sorcery for {U}.
  7. Cast it anyway when you will not be alive on your next turn.

What this model does not do (31 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Nine of the ten gift permanents hand the whole table cards and nothing you control looks like a clock: across 120 Forge games this seat's combat damage to players had a mean of 3.28 and a median of 0.5, and the most commander damage it dealt in any one game was 8.
  • Lead with Gleaming Splendor over Smothering Tithe when both are castable. Splendor costs {1}{W}, charges nobody anything and banks Treasure off cards the table was drawing already; the Tithe costs {3}{W} and takes {2} from every opponent for every card.
  • Deploy Smothering Tithe behind a blocker or after a wrath, never onto an empty board. It made the pilot the table's target the moment it landed in log 002.
  • Cast Greater Auramancy on a turn you also cast something. An enchantment that protects enchantments, played alone, tells the table you expect to be attacked.
  • Cast Enlightened Tutor on turn one or two. The reveal says nothing then and says the whole plan on turn seven.
  • Flip Heliod on a turn you also cast something. A flipped commander with no follow-up announces that the discount exists.

7.2When to reveal

  • Say the 7 life on the first Approach and move on. Do not name the card.
  • Ask for Rhystic Study's {1} every time. That question is how three seats learn to respect your open mana without you showing a card.
  • Cast Mystical Tutor at an end step rather than on your own main phase. The card lands on top either way.
  • Say "shroud" as soon as somebody targets one of your enchantments with Greater Auramancy out, and let them take the spell back before it resolves.
  • Do not show a held counter to deter an attack. Propaganda and Ghostly Prison do that job for one card and charge {2} per attacker.
  • Say the discount out loud when you take it, and say the number of cards it came from. The arithmetic is checkable and arguing it later costs more.

7.3Alliances

  • Point at the seat with the fliers and keep pointing. That seat caused 43 of the 61 damage eliminations this deck suffered across 120 Forge games.
  • Let another player's board be the reason a wrath gets cast. Your engine is enchantments and artifacts and a creature wrath leaves most of it standing.
  • Name the seat you want to stop attacking you when you tap Loran of the Third Path, and give them the card.
  • Do not counter a spell aimed at somebody else unless it wins the game or kills you.
  • Counter the combo seat's first piece rather than the aggro seat's third creature.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Kill the flier that connected, not the largest creature. Swords to Plowshares and Path to Exile cost {W} each.
  • Answer the permanent that stops your draw before the one that is biggest.
  • Destroy your own Greater Auramancy with Disenchant or Cathar Commando when you need Karametra's Blessing on Heliod. Auramancy grants shroud to other enchantments and not to itself, and while it is out Otawara, Soaring City cannot bounce your own enchantments either.
  • Destroy a resolved artifact or enchantment with Disenchant, Cathar Commando or Loran of the Third Path; those three destroy one each. Cyclonic Rift and Otawara, Soaring City bounce one instead.
  • Save the overloaded Cyclonic Rift at {6}{U} for a board you cannot block or a lock built out of permanents.
  • Aim Braingeyser and Stroke of Genius at yourself. Cast Prosperity or Skyscribing only with a converter already on the battlefield.
  • Leave the seat that has not attacked you alone.
