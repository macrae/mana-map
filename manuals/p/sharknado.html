1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Modal voltron: land either commander and keep it alive, let it tax every card that moves (Shabraz takes a counter and 1 life per draw, Brallin a counter and 1 damage to each opponent per discard), and win by 21 commander damage, with a wheel held until a commander is out as the finisher that turns one turn's hand into the kill.

3.1REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. a removal spell or ability is pointed at Shabraz or Brallin, and that commander's tax is about to go up by two

Indications.

  • A seat passes with {W}, {B} or {1}{U} open the turn after a commander lands.
  • A commander is on the battlefield with nothing on it: no Lightning Greaves, Brotherhood Regalia or Robe of Stars.
  • The table has already answered one commander. In game 001 Brallin was removed twice and the tax came on (log:001).
  • The commander has counters on it. The table reads counters as the kill and aims removal at them.

Immediate action.

  1. Read the removal. Note three things: does it target, is it a spell or an ability, and does it destroy, deal damage, exile or bounce.
  2. If it targets, answer the target first. Cast Deflecting Swat for free (you control a commander) and point it at one of their permanents, or cast Loran's Escape for {W}: hexproof makes the spell lose its target. Or channel Otawara, Soaring City for {2}{U} with one legend out, returning your own commander to your hand.
  3. If it is a noncreature spell and step 2 did not answer it, counter it. Stubborn Denial for {U} is a hard counter once a creature of yours has 4 power. Negate costs {1}{U}. Izzet Charm costs {U}{R} and they can pay {2}.
  4. If it is still coming: against destroy or damage, cast Flawless Maneuver for free. Against exile or bounce, phase the commander out: Robe of Stars ({1}{W}, if it is equipped) or March of Swirling Mist ({1}{U} for X=1).
  5. If nothing answers it, put the commander in the command zone and say the new tax aloud: Brallin 4, then 6, then 8; Shabraz 5, then 7, then 9.

Subsequent.

  1. A commander bounced to your hand stays in your hand. You recast it from hand, which adds no tax.
  2. Before recasting from the command zone, tap and sacrifice Command Beacon if you have it. It puts the commander in your hand, and that cast pays no tax.
  3. Recast with Lightning Greaves already on the battlefield. Equip it for {0} the turn he lands: haste and shroud, so the cast turn is an attack turn.
  4. If one commander has been removed twice, play the other one. The Equipment stays on the battlefield; Brotherhood Regalia moves to a legend for {1}.
  5. Keep Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat in hand from here on. Both are free only while you control a commander, so they do nothing on a turn both are in the command zone.
NOTELightning Greaves gives shroud, and shroud stops YOUR targeting too. With Greaves on a commander you cannot attach Embercleave or Brotherhood Regalia, cannot give him Shu Yun's double strike, cannot put Inti's counter on him, and the commanders cannot hand each other flying or trample. Equip everything else first and move Greaves off last. Game 001 was played on v1.2.0, before the voltron and answers merges (log:001).

3.2COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. another seat is assembling a loop or a one-turn kill, and your answers are a handful of instants

Indications.

  • A seat tutors twice in one turn, or plays a piece and holds its mana up.
  • A seat whose deck checks at bracket 4 sits at a table with your bracket 3 list. Game 002 ended on Ryan's infinite with his Teval deck (log:002).
  • A sacrifice outlet and a recursion piece sit on one board.
  • A seat's life total or token count jumps on a turn it attacks nobody.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the piece already on the battlefield that the loop needs. That piece is the target.
  2. Answer it at instant speed: Swords to Plowshares ({W}) exiles a creature, Get Lost ({1}{W}) destroys a creature or enchantment, Chaos Warp ({2}{R}) shuffles away any permanent, Prismari Command ({1}{U}{R}) destroys an artifact, Teferi, Master of Time's -3 phases a creature out on their turn.
  3. If the last piece is a noncreature spell from hand, hold {U} for Stubborn Denial or {1}{U} for Negate on that seat's turn and counter it.
  4. If nothing answers it, count your own kill for this turn: 21 commander damage at one seat, or Brallin's pings at every seat. If it is there, take it now.

Subsequent.

  1. From the turn a seat shows its first piece, keep {W} and {1}{U} open ahead of tapping out for Equipment or a conversion enchantment.
  2. Collective Defiance and Incendiary Command are sorceries. Cast them in your own main phase on the piece already down; they cannot be held up for the turn the loop goes off.
  3. If the combo seat's pieces are in its hand, aim Collective Defiance's first mode at that seat ({1}{R}{R}): it discards its hand and draws as many. The discards are theirs, so neither commander triggers.
  4. Cast Grand Abolisher before your kill turn. On your turn no opponent casts a spell or activates an artifact, creature or enchantment ability, so a combo piece cannot respond to your swing.
NOTEYour answers stop permanents, not a graveyard. Against a loop that returns pieces from the graveyard, hit the outlet or the engine on the battlefield and leave the yard. Game 002 was played on v1.3.0 at a table the pilot called bracket 4 against his bracket 3 list (log:002).

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.3MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the mana has stopped short of a commander, or the colour it needs is not there

Indications.

  • Three lands on turn four. The goldfish hits its land drop on turn 4 in 80.3% of games and on turn 5 in 70.9%.
  • No rock by turn three. In game 001 the only fast mana was Sol Ring in the opener and nothing followed it (log:001).
  • Four mana and no red: Brallin, Torbran ({1}{R}{R}{R}) and Gratuitous Violence ({2}{R}{R}{R}) sit in hand.
  • Game 003 on v2.0.0 was 'mana starved one round' and 'slow to get cards out' (log:003).

Immediate action.

  1. If you hold no land, loot before the land drop: Faithless Looting ({R}), Kitsa's tap, Izzet Charm's third mode ({U}{R}), or cycle Shark Typhoon for X=0 ({1}{U}) or Marauding Mako ({2}). Discard spells, keep lands.
  2. Play the land. Take the untapped one if it casts something this turn; Spectator Seating and Training Center enter untapped with two or more opponents, Sundown Pass with two or more other lands. Thundering Falls enters tapped: play it on a turn you have nothing to cast.
  3. Cast the cheapest rock before any other spell: Sol Ring, then Arcane Signet, Azorius Signet, Izzet Signet or Talisman of Creativity.
  4. With three lands, cast Frantic Search ({2}{U}): it draws two, discards two and untaps three lands, so it costs nothing net.
  5. With four mana and a red source, cast Brallin. He is one cheaper than Shabraz, and every loot after him grows him and pings the table.

Subsequent.

  1. With Stoneforge Mystic out, put Embercleave onto the battlefield with her ability for {1}{W} instead of casting it for six.
  2. Cast Prismari Command at the end of the turn before yours, choosing Treasure and the loot on yourself.
  3. Put Smothering Tithe down before a symmetrical wheel. Wheel of Fortune and Reforge the Soul make each opponent draw seven: every card they do not pay {2} for is a Treasure.
  4. Discard Islands and Pirates to Mary Read and Anne Bonny's loot. Each one makes a tapped Treasure.
  5. Sorcerer Class level 2 mana pays for instants, sorceries and Class levels only. It does not cast a commander or pay an equip.
NOTERed is the colour that runs short: mana-analysis counts 34 red sources against a target of 36, and the commanders' road through Brallin, Torbran, Razorkin Needlehead ({R}{R}) and Gratuitous Violence is red. When the hand has two colours of mana and red is not one of them, Shabraz ({3}{W}{U}) is the commander to cast. Game 003 was on v2.0.0; answers-v1, the powertune-v1 land pass and sharks-v1 came after it, and no game has been logged on v2.2.0 (log:003).

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:003.

3.4STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. it is turn five or later, no commander has stuck, and the hand is wheels and payoffs

Indications.

  • Turn five with no commander on the battlefield. The goldfish casts Brallin by turn six in 95.8% of games and Shabraz in 87.7%, with no removal in it, so this is already behind that ceiling.
  • A hand of two or more wheels and one creature. That was the keep in game 002 (log:002).
  • Both commanders have been answered and the taxes are 6 and 7.
  • Game 003 read 'slow to get cards out' with no removal drawn (log:003).

Immediate action.

  1. Cast a commander before any wheel, Brallin first. A wheel with no commander out pays no counter, no ping and no life. The one exception is step 4.
  2. Equip Lightning Greaves to it the turn it lands, after any other Equipment, and attack with the haste.
  3. Loot every turn after that: Kitsa, Mary Read and Anne Bonny, Faithless Looting, Teferi, Master of Time's +1 on another seat's turn, Shark Typhoon's cycling at the end of the turn before yours. Each loot pays Brallin a counter and a ping, or Shabraz a counter and a life.
  4. Hold the wheels until the commander has survived a full turn cycle (log:002). If Torbran is on the battlefield and Razorkin Needlehead is in hand, the wheel goes now with no commander: cast Needlehead ({R}{R}), then Wheel of Fortune or Reforge the Soul in the same main phase. Stack 011: 21 to each opponent.

Subsequent.

  1. If the table is ahead, refill only your own hand: Tolarian Winds and Fateful Showdown discard and redraw your hand alone. A symmetrical wheel refills the seats that are winning.
  2. Cast Fateful Showdown on the blocker or the engine in your way. It deals damage equal to your hand size, then wheels your hand.
  3. With both commanders taxed, recast one. Put the other's mana into a conversion piece for the one on the battlefield.
  4. If Idyllic Tutor is in hand, fetch the enchantment the commander on the battlefield needs: Torbran is a creature, so for Brallin take Gratuitous Violence or Artist's Talent; for Shabraz take Teferi's Ageless Insight.
NOTETwo exceptions to 'commander first'. Razorkin Needlehead with Torbran makes a wheel 21 per seat with both commanders in the command zone (stack 011); from 40 that leaves 19, so it is the wheel that sets up the kill, not the kill. And Tolarian Winds with no commander out when your hand cannot be cast: it digs for lands and protection, it is an instant, and it refills no opponent. Cast it at the end of the turn before yours.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:003.

3.5POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has turned on the wheels and you have become the problem

Indications.

  • A seat complains when you cast a wheel. The pilot saw the table split on them in game 001: 'people either loved it or hated it' (log:001).
  • You have wheeled twice before any commander is big. In game 002 the pilot named repeated discards as the thing the table did not enjoy (log:002).
  • Removal starts coming at you before your board is the largest one.
  • Two seats stop attacking each other.

Immediate action.

  1. Stop casting symmetrical wheels for card flow. Loot instead: Kitsa, Mary Read and Anne Bonny, Faithless Looting, Teferi, Master of Time's +1, Shark Typhoon's cycling.
  2. Save the next wheel for the turn it ends a game or puts a commander within one swing of 21 (log:002).
  3. When you need a refill, cast the one-sided ones: Tolarian Winds, Fateful Showdown, or Collective Defiance's first mode aimed at yourself.
  4. Spend your next removal spell on the seat that is ahead, and say so at the table.

Subsequent.

  1. Name Brallin's pings before you loot. They hit every opponent; do not promise a seat a pass you break with your next discard.
  2. Keep Razorkin Needlehead in hand. On the battlefield it tells the table your next wheel hits every seat for seven or more, and draws the removal before the wheel.
  3. Cast Consecrated Sphinx on the turn before the wheel, not earlier. Beside Brallin it is the board of stack 009, and the table will aim the next removal at it.
  4. Offer an answer for another seat's problem in exchange for a turn without removal aimed at you. Swords to Plowshares and Get Lost are the cards to offer.
NOTEA wheel that refills an opponent's empty hand is a gift to that seat. When one seat is at zero or one card, cast Tolarian Winds or Fateful Showdown, not Wheel of Fortune or Windfall.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:002.

3.6WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming or has landed, and a commander and its Equipment are on the board

Indications.

  • A white or black seat passes with four or more mana open and a board behind yours.
  • Both commanders are on the battlefield with counters, and you have cast your creatures into an open sweeper mana.
  • A seat with a sweeper in its colours has drawn a fresh hand from your wheel.
  • A seat that was losing on board stops casting creatures.

Immediate action.

  1. Read the sweeper. Note whether it destroys, deals damage, exiles, bounces or gives -X/-X.
  2. If it is a spell, counter it: Stubborn Denial ({U}, hard with a 4-power creature), Negate ({1}{U}) or Izzet Charm ({U}{R}).
  3. Against destroy or damage, cast Flawless Maneuver for free (you control a commander). Akroma's Will's second mode ({3}{W}) gives indestructible too.
  4. Against exile, bounce or -X/-X, phase the commander out: March of Swirling Mist with X equal to the creatures you save, exiling blue cards from hand to cut {2} each, or Robe of Stars ({1}{W}) on the equipped creature.
  5. Let it resolve. A commander bounced to hand stays in hand; one that dies or is exiled goes to the command zone, and you say the new tax aloud.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast one commander, not both. Keep the second in the command zone as the next rebuild.
  2. At the end of the turn before yours, cycle Shark Typhoon with every land left open: X is your open mana minus 2. It is an ability, so a counterspell does not stop it, and the flying X/X Shark is the next attacker. Brallin gives it trample for {R}.
  3. The Equipment survives a creature sweeper. Re-equip on the commander you recast: Brotherhood Regalia {1}, Lightning Greaves {0}, last.
  4. Hold the wheels until the commander you recast has survived a turn cycle. If Torbran survived or is recast and Needlehead is in hand, the wheel goes on the turn you cast Needlehead (stack 011).
  5. Before your next creature, count the sweeper seat's open mana again.
NOTERazorkin Needlehead in hand is a rebuild the sweeper never saw. Do not put it on the battlefield to fill a board; it is worth 21 per seat only on the turn a wheel follows it under Torbran (stack 011). Phasing out takes the Equipment with it and brings both back attached, and the counters stay on. Grand Abolisher stops spells only on your turn, so it never stops a sweeper cast on theirs. No logged game has ended on a wipe.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat's board will kill you before your commander reaches 21, and you cannot block it

Indications.

  • A go-wide board with more attackers than you have blockers. Stuart's zombie tokens in game 001 were a board the pilot could not prune (log:001).
  • A seat's attack takes a third or more of your life in one swing.
  • Your commander is two or more swings from 21 at the seat that is ahead.
  • You are tapping out on your turn and have no creature untapped on theirs.

Immediate action.

  1. Count both clocks aloud: how many of their swings kill you, and how many of yours kill them.
  2. If theirs is shorter, answer the largest attacker at instant speed on their turn: Swords to Plowshares ({W}), Get Lost ({1}{W}), Izzet Charm's 2 damage, Teferi, Master of Time's -3, or Fateful Showdown for damage equal to your hand size.
  3. In their beginning of combat, cycle Shark Typhoon: a flying X/X Shark that blocks this turn, X equal to your open mana minus 2. The discard and draw pay any commander you control.
  4. Against a board of small creatures, cast Incendiary Command ({3}{R}{R}) in your main phase choosing 2 to each creature and a second mode. Before you cast it, count your own creatures with 2 or less toughness.
  5. Leave Shabraz and Bard, King of Dale untapped as blockers. Bard has reach and vigilance and blocks a flyer after attacking.

Subsequent.

  1. Go over the board, not through it. Shabraz flies, Shabraz's {W/U} gives Brallin flying, and Brotherhood Regalia makes the carrier unblockable.
  2. Point commander damage at the racing seat until it is gone.
  3. Loot on their turn with Teferi, Master of Time's +1. With Shabraz out, each draw is 1 life.
  4. Cast Akroma's Will with both modes on your swing: lifelink and double strike on every creature you control.
NOTEBrallin's and Razorkin Needlehead's damage hits players, never creatures, and Needlehead has first strike only on your turn: it is not a blocker. A wheel does not remove a single token, so against a wide board the wheel is the finisher only after the board is answered. Game 001 has no logged result; it is cited for the board that could not be cut back, not as a loss to a race (log:001).

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two to five lands. With two lands, a rock as well: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Azorius Signet, Izzet Signet or Talisman of Creativity.
  • At least one creature or one rock.
  • At most one wheel. Two wheels and one creature is the hand the pilot doubted in game 002 (log:002).
  • A red source, so Brallin is castable on turn four.
  • Cheap card flow is a reason to keep: Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Kitsa, Faithless Looting, Stoneforge Mystic.

Ship on sight.

  • Zero or one land, or six or more.
  • Two or more wheels with no creature and no rock.
  • A hand with no red source and no rock that makes red.
  • A hand that needs a commander out before it does anything: Blade Historian, Embercleave, Gratuitous Violence, Fiery Emancipation and wheels with no early plays.
NOTEThe goldfish keeps its first seven in 79.2% of games; it mulligans on land count alone. In a multiplayer game the first mulligan does not cost a card. Gemstone Caverns in the opener goes onto the battlefield before turn one if you are not the starting player: exile a wheel or a payoff, never a rock or a land.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 79% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Turn one: land, then Sol Ring if you have it; otherwise Mystic Remora, Marauding Mako or Faithless Looting.
  2. Turn two: a rock, Kitsa, Stoneforge Mystic, Inti, Sorcerer Class, Faerie Mastermind or Ledger Shredder. Stoneforge fetches Lightning Greaves if the hand has no protection, Embercleave if it does.
  3. Turn three: Rhystic Study, Lae'zel, Shu Yun, Mary Read and Anne Bonny, Rielle, Glint-Horn Buccaneer or a second rock. Cast Jace's Archivist only to hold it; do not activate it before a commander is out.
  4. Keep Razorkin Needlehead in hand. It is not a turn-two 2/2; it comes down on the wheel turn.
  5. Cast no wheel in the first three turns.
  6. Spend Swords to Plowshares or Get Lost in the first three turns only on a creature that kills a commander on sight, or on a combo piece.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. First commander. Brallin on turn four with a red source; Shabraz first if Rhystic Study, Teferi's Ageless Insight or Bard is already out, or if red is missing.
  2. Protection on that commander the turn it lands: Brotherhood Regalia ({1} on a legend, ward {2}, unblockable) or Robe of Stars ({1}), then Lightning Greaves ({0}) last. Keep Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat in hand.
  3. The conversion piece for that commander. Brallin: Torbran, Gratuitous Violence, Artist's Talent, Glint-Horn Buccaneer or Magmakin Artillerist. Shabraz: Teferi's Ageless Insight, Bard, King of Dale or Knowledge Is Power. Either: Lae'zel.
  4. Loot every turn. Each card discarded is a counter on Brallin and a ping at every opponent; each card drawn is a counter on Shabraz and 1 life.
  5. The kit: one double-strike source (Shu Yun, Embercleave, Blade Historian, Akroma's Will) and one evasion source (Brotherhood Regalia, Shabraz's flying, Shabraz's {W/U} for Brallin, Brallin's {R} trample for Shabraz or a Shark token).
  6. Keep Shark Typhoon in hand and cycle it at the end of the turn before yours with the mana you held up and did not spend: X is that mana minus 2. Stack 012: X=4 puts a counter on Brallin, Marauding Mako and Shabraz, pings each opponent 1, gains 1 life and makes a 4/4 flying Shark.
  7. Second commander when the first is protected and the mana is spare.
  8. The wheel last, on the turn you swing (log:002). Stack 001: Windfall for seven with both out takes each opponent from 40 to 33 and leaves both commanders 10/10. With Torbran out and Razorkin Needlehead in hand, the wheel turn is also the Needlehead turn (closing, step 5).

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Untap. Count each opponent's open mana and colours before you cast anything.
  2. Loot in your main phase: Kitsa, Mary Read and Anne Bonny, Faithless Looting. Teferi, Master of Time's +1 waits for another seat's turn.
  3. Equip at sorcery speed, Lightning Greaves last.
  4. Attack with the commander that has evasion. With Inti out, discard a card as you attack: a counter and trample on an attacker, a Brallin trigger, and an exiled card you can play.
  5. Pass with Flawless Maneuver or Deflecting Swat in hand (both free with a commander out), and {W} for Swords to Plowshares or Loran's Escape, or {U} for Stubborn Denial.
  6. At the end of the turn before yours, spend the mana you held and did not use: cycle Shark Typhoon, or Marauding Mako for {2}.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count commander damage per commander per seat: 21 from Shabraz, or 21 from Brallin, on one player. The two do not add together.
  2. Count the size after the wheel. Each card drawn is +1 on Shabraz; each card discarded is +1 on Brallin; Lae'zel makes each placement one more. A 10/10 with double strike deals 20. Embercleave's +1/+1, Knowledge Is Power, Inti's counter or Jeskai Ascendancy's +1/+1 makes the 21st.
  3. Cast Grand Abolisher the turn before. On the kill turn no opponent casts a spell or activates an artifact, creature or enchantment ability.
  4. Sequence the turn: wheel first and collect the triggers, then double strike, then attack. The wheel is a noncreature spell, so it triggers Shu Yun: pay {R/W}{R/W} and target the commander. Embercleave has flash and costs {1} less per attacker: cast it after attackers are declared.
  5. With Torbran on the battlefield, cast Razorkin Needlehead ({R}{R}) in the main phase you wheel, then Wheel of Fortune or Reforge the Soul. Its trigger does not need it to attack. Stack 011: seven triggers per opponent at 3 each, 21 per seat. From 40 that leaves 19: cast it when every seat is at 21 or less, or when commander damage follows the same turn.
  6. With Consecrated Sphinx on the battlefield, both commanders out and 49 or more cards in your library, cast Wheel of Fortune. Stack 009: the cleanup discard fires Brallin past 40 at every seat.
  7. Wait a turn if a blue seat has mana open and you hold neither Grand Abolisher on the battlefield nor a counter, or if the count is short and nothing in hand adds the last point.

What this model does not do (52 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent, Tocasia's Welcome with its mana-value gate), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAW (Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Morbid Opportunist, Midnight Reaper) fires on every death this model produces — the sacrifice sweep under `model_sacrifice`, the one-turn copies leaving, and the measured rate under `model_deaths` — honouring 'nontoken' and 'once each turn' (2026-10-19); a deck with no death channel on has no deaths, and the payoff is absent. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent) and attack-triggered draw outside `model_combat` are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw', 'you may pay 2 life' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one, and 15 death triggers.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • DISCOVER, always on. An instant or sorcery with 'Discover N' is cast like any spell (storm, magecraft and per-cast damage see it), and each resolution — plus one per Zada copy — walks to the first nonland card with mana value N or less and CASTS IT FREE when it has a free door: a ritual, a draw spell, a discover spell, a storm spell, a pump or token copy (with a board, under model_combat), or a body. Any other hit goes to HAND and is cast later at full cost — a floor. The rest go to the bottom in a random order. A permanent's discover (the Hidden lands, an ETB, a death) and discover X are not read.
  • CASCADE, always on, the card's OWN keyword only (grants are not read). Every cast — paid, free or a cascade hit's own — walks to the first nonland card with mana value STRICTLY LESS and casts it free through the same doors as Discover; a hit with no free door goes to the bottom with the rest, in a random order (a floor). Copies never cascade. A cascade card no other loop casts is cast late in the main phase for the cascade alone, so it adds nothing to that turn's storm count.
  • A FREE-CAST CHAIN IS CAPPED at FREE_CAST_DEPTH_LIMIT nested free casts (8); a hit past it takes the not-cast path. The depth reached and the refusals are reported in metrics.free_cast.
  • MAGECRAFT FIRES ON EVERY COPY ('cast or copy'): one event per Zada copy of a spell, at every copy site (draw, pump, untap, storm, discover), counted once per spell however many arms the spell has.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • RITUALS ARE CAST WHEN THEY ARE THE DIFFERENCE (2026-10-05): a spell that adds mana (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Battle Hymn, Brightstone Ritual, Inner Fire) is cast only if its net mana makes a card in hand, or the commander, castable this turn; its mana lasts the turn. Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will / Rousing Refrain read the OPPONENTS' state this model does not have, on two AUTHORED rates in config.py: GEYSER_TAPPED_SHARE (0.5 of their lands still tapped on our turn) and OPPONENT_HAND (4 cards, 7 on a turn a wheel refilled them). Rituals with an additional cost or restricted mana are not read.
  • Extra card draw is modelled ONLY behind `model_draw` — the assumption above names its channels. With the flag OFF it is one card per turn, always, which understates every deck by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs: conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list that hides, and `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled` names the cards whose draw still has no channel, and is ABSENT rather than empty on a deck where every drawer is read.
  • CREATURES TAP WHEN THEY ATTACK (CR 508.1f) and untap at the start of your turn, so an ADDITIONAL COMBAT PHASE is only worth something if something untaps the team. Before this the swing was computed once and multiplied by the phase count, which made an extra combat free damage from a board that had already attacked: ur-dragon's damage at turn ten fell 68.798 -> 59.499 when it was corrected. LANDS still enter untapped, always — that assumption is unchanged and is about lands, not creatures.
  • A COMMANDER THAT COPIES A SINGLE-TARGET SPELL across the board is modelled behind `model_commander_copy`, declared per deck and checked against the commander's own text. Zada, Hedron Grinder is the only card in the corpus with the ability. It needs no authored rate: the copy count IS the number of other creatures you control, which this model already measures.
  • WHAT A COPIED SPELL DOES is read per effect, and each is one turn unless said otherwise: its draw, a pump (power only — toughness is tracked but no creature blocks here), a PERMANENT +1/+1 counter, a Treasure it creates, double strike, doubled power, damage every creature deals to each opponent, an additional combat phase, and an untap. A spell whose effect is none of these still counts toward the spell count and is otherwise inert.
  • THE SPELL COUNT IS TRACKED, so STORM is modelled, as are MAGECRAFT (which fires on a cast OR a copy) and per-cast damage (which fires on a cast only — that difference is a rules fact, not a simplification). Before this there was no spells-cast-this-turn variable at all and storm was unreadable.
  • REPLACEMENTS (2026-10-11): a counter replacement (Lae'zel +1, Doubling Season x2) applies per counter EVENT, and a damage replacement that adds (Torbran +2) per damage INSTANCE, each added before any multiplier: each ping, each storm copy, each attacker per strike. An attacker's colours ride on its battlefield entry (cast creatures, commanders, and draw/discard-made tokens such as The Locust God's blue-red Insects); a token from any other maker is colourless here, an understatement.
  • OPPONENTS' CASTS (2026-10-11): each opponent casts 1.264 spells a turn, MEASURED from 137,717 Forge turns (sim/pod_behaviour); Rhystic Study's tax is paid at the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE. Mind's Eye pays from mana left untapped at the end of our turn, a floor.
  • SPELLS AT A PLAYER (2026-10-11): damage a spell deals to a player counts against the one opponent ("any target" included). A spell whose only read effect is that damage is cast at the end of the main phase with what is left; a choose-ONE card whose other mode is read keeps that mode. Only an "each player" wheel refills the opponent; "discard your hand" and "target player" (us) do not. A discard additional cost is paid.
  • Treasures are a one-shot STOCKPILE, not a mana rock: spent only when lands and rocks fall short, and gone once broken. Reported separately from mean_available_mana_by_turn, which still means repeatable mana per turn.
  • Only Treasure triggers a goldfish can see are modeled — upkeep and landfall are RECURRING, Saga chapters likewise; a `cast` or enters-the-battlefield trigger pays out ONCE, when its own source resolves. Combat- and opponent-gated sources produce NOTHING here, because this model has no combat and no opponents; they are named in meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled so a low hoard figure is legible.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN FIRES ONLY ON THE DEATHS THIS MODEL PRODUCES. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance, Vraan and Vein Ripper key on a creature DYING, and a death happens here in three places and nowhere else: a free sacrifice outlet converting tokens (`model_sacrifice`), a one-turn copy leaving at the end step, and the per-turn rate read off a Forge run (`model_deaths`). No blockers and no removal, so a deck with neither channel on has no deaths and the contribution is ABSENT, not zero; a deck whose kill runs through them is understated by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.
  • BLOOD: a Blood token is created on an ETB, a per-opponent ETB (three, the pod) or a spell's resolution; Blood made by CONNECTING in combat is read and NOT credited, because a blocker can prevent it and this model has no blockers. AT MOST ONE IS CRACKED PER TURN, before the casting loops, and only with a card in hand, since discarding is part of the cost. One a turn is an authored floor: a pilot holding three with mana to spare cracks more, so this understates. Cracking one is a DISCARD and a DRAW, which the commander payoffs below charge for, and it sacrifices an ARTIFACT, which the artifact-sacrifice payoffs charge for.
  • ACTIVATED DRAW: a draw you BUY -- '{1}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: Draw a card' -- is paid for out of whatever mana the casting loops did not want, and a cost that eats its own source fires once. Energy and {X} activation costs are REFUSED and named, never priced at zero.
  • DISCARD: a wheel discards the WHOLE hand, lands included, and is cast only when the hand holds at most three nonland cards or a discard payoff is on the battlefield; a loot pitches lands beyond the next drop first, then the most expensive nonland. A discarded card stays 'seen' for target assembly (assembly is 'drawn by', and it was). Wheel of Misfortune is excluded (conditional). Activated loots and rummages, cycling and madness are NOT modelled and are named in meta.draw_not_modelled. Discard- and draw-triggered payoffs pay a flat amount per event, outside the combat gate; +1/+1 counters they put on their own body are added to the swing while the commander is out, an approximation. Payoffs that DRAW on a draw are not read: an uncapped loop would draw the library and call it steam.
  • DISCARD: a wheel a PERMANENT carries is activated from the battlefield under the same hand-size gate as a wheel that is cast, paying its mana cost every time. It fires at the earliest on the turn AFTER the permanent lands, which is the summoning sickness of the tap in its cost, and a cost that sacrifices its own source fires once and takes the body off the board. An ability activated from the graveyard (Runehorn Hellkite) is a zone this model does not have and is refused rather than fired.
  • WHEEL TIMING (2026-10-21): the turn of every wheel is observed and decided on by nothing. A FINISHER is a wheel resolved with a COMMANDER on the battlefield and a hand the thin-hand clause alone would not have thrown away. A deck's pilot_policy.json may HOLD a wheel (`hold_until` on the wheel channel: event_payoffs, commanders_out) — an AND-gate on the rule above, so a threshold of zero changes nothing; the policy is stamped on the artifact as meta.pilot_policy. A `keep` rule in the same file is read on the hand the model kept and reported as a pass rate, never enforced.
  • DISCARD: THE OPPONENT'S HALF OF A WHEEL IS NOW COUNTED, AND ONLY THE TAX ON IT. A symmetrical wheel refills the opponent too, so `whenever an opponent draws a card` payoffs (Razorkin Needlehead, Scrawling Crawler, Mind's Eye, Consecrated Sphinx) fire on their draw step and on every card a wheel deals them. DAMAGE IS ONE SEAT, the same convention the damage pillar uses -- Brallin's '1 damage to EACH opponent' is counted once against a single 40-life opponent, which is the right per-seat clock. WHAT WE GAIN off their draws is paid by every opponent and scales with GOLDFISH_OPPONENTS (3): Faerie Mastermind's card on each opponent's second draw, Consecrated Sphinx's two per opponent draw, a Blood per opponent (2026-10-04; at one seat Hallcreeper -> Mastermind read -0.17 cards by T8, at three +0.62). WHAT IS STILL NOT MODELLED IS THE COST: the opponent draws those cards and never gets to use them, because this model has no opponent turn, no opponent board and no interaction. So the tax is priced and the risk it offsets is not. A trigger on an opponent CASTING (Rhystic Study) is a different family and stays unread.
  • TREASURE OFF THEIR DRAWS RESTS ON AN AUTHORED RATE. Smothering Tithe makes a Treasure per opponent draw unless that opponent pays {2}; how often a table pays is not measurable here, so TITHE_PAY_RATE (0.5, config.py) decides each draw. Every Tithe figure is conditional on it, and `try` says so when a list relies on it.
  • DISCARD: A WHEEL THAT DRAWS 'CARDS EQUAL TO THE GREATEST NUMBER A PLAYER DISCARDED' IS A FLOOR HERE, AND THE FLOOR IS LOW. This model has no opponents holding cards, so Jace's Archivist and Windfall draw what OUR hand held -- and the gate only lets them fire when our hand is thin, which is exactly the board on which a real table pays them seven. The figure is not corrected, because the correction would be an authored opponent hand size driving a headline; read it as a lower bound and settle the question in Forge.

6.2Ignition

The COMMANDER half of the engine does not pay until Shabraz (5 mana, flying) or Brallin (4 mana) has resolved and survived: without one a loot or a wheel grows no commander and Lae'zel, Bard and Teferi's Ageless Insight have no commander trigger to multiply, which is why the pilot holds wheels until a commander is out. It is not the whole engine: the second collectors pay with both commanders in the command zone, Razorkin Needlehead by stack 011 (a wheel under Torbran, 21 per seat) and Glint-Horn and Magmakin by oracle text (output). Shabraz turns each card drawn into a +1/+1 counter and 1 life; Brallin turns each card discarded into a +1/+1 counter and 1 damage to each opponent. The two hand each other evasion by oracle text: Brallin's {R} gives a Shark trample and Shabraz is a Shark Bird; Shabraz's {W/U} gives a Human flying and Brallin is a Human Shaman. The deck is modal: either one alone can be the 21-commander-damage threat, so the ignition is redundant, not single, and each removal adds 2 to that commander's tax. Both start in the command zone, so drawing them is never the constraint; casting and keeping them is. goldfish_metrics.json (10,000 games, the current decklist sha): Shabraz is cast by turn six in 88.3% (mean turn 4.909, median 5), Brallin in 95.6% (mean 4.005), and both are on the battlefield by turn six in 87.8%. The goldfish casts each once and has no removal, so these are a ceiling on the ignition, not its survival; the protection group is measured by turn six in 80.1% (deck-audit). goldfish_targets still calls a WHEEL 'the ignition' and marks it `required`; that reading is the one this model replaces (proposed_goldfish_edits[0]).

6.5Output

What the engine produces besides a large commander: creatures that collect a second tax on the same cards. 7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Until a commander has counters, the table sees a loot deck with two 3/3s and ranks you low.
  • The first wheel changes that. The table split on it in game 001: 'people either loved it or hated it' (log:001).
  • A commander with Lightning Greaves reads as a creature; a commander with Embercleave or beside Shu Yun reads as the kill. Attach the double strike on the swing turn.
  • Torbran beside Razorkin Needlehead reads as the wheel kill. With both out, each opponent's draw step costs them 3 (oracle text), and the table aims the next removal at Torbran.
  • Consecrated Sphinx beside Brallin is the stack 009 board. Expect it to draw the next removal spell.
  • The pilot called game 002 a bracket 3 list at a bracket 4 table (log:002). At that table you are the slow deck, not the threat.

7.2When to reveal

  • Hold Embercleave in hand. It has flash and attaches when it enters; cast it after attackers are declared.
  • Hold Akroma's Will for the attack. Blade Historian goes down the turn before the swing, not earlier.
  • Hold Razorkin Needlehead until the wheel turn. On the battlefield early it is a 2/2 that tells every seat the wheel is aimed at them.
  • Hold Shark Typhoon as a cycle, not a cast. A six-mana enchantment on the battlefield is a removal target; a cycle at the end of the turn before yours is a Shark nobody saw coming.
  • Put Torbran, Gratuitous Violence and Fiery Emancipation down on the turn the wheel or Brallin's attack follows, or when Idyllic Tutor has a second target to find.
  • Cast Consecrated Sphinx when Wheel of Fortune follows on your next turn, or behind Grand Abolisher.
  • Pass with zero mana open when Flawless Maneuver or Deflecting Swat is the shield. Both are free while you control a commander.

7.3Alliances

  • Offer removal, not wheels. Swords to Plowshares or Get Lost on another seat's problem buys a rotation.
  • Brallin's pings hit every opponent on every discard. Do not promise a seat a pass you break with your next loot.
  • When a seat asks you to stop wheeling, agree and loot instead, and keep the one wheel you need for the kill (log:002).
  • Break a deal only on the turn the commander damage or the wheel kills.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Commander damage goes at one seat until it dies. A seat at 14 from Shabraz takes the next 7 from Shabraz, not from Brallin.
  • First, the seat with no flyer or reach to block Shabraz, or no untapped blocker for an unblockable or trampling Brallin.
  • Next, the combo seat. Game 002 ended on one (log:002).
  • The go-wide seat before the control seat when its board races you (log:001).
  • Never the seat holding open blue mana when you have neither Grand Abolisher out nor a counter in hand.
