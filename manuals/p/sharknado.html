1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Land Shabraz, keep him alive the turn he lands, and turn every card the deck draws into a +1/+1 counter on a flyer. Draw doublers and Lae'zel multiply the counters, and double strike cashes him in for 21 commander damage (stack 006); a damage doubler should do the same, but no stack has resolved it (open_questions[0]). Brallin is the second road, not the engine.

3.1REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. a removal spell or ability is pointed at Shabraz, and his tax is about to go up by two

Indications.

  • A seat passes with {W}, {B} or {1}{U} open the turn after Shabraz lands.
  • Shabraz is on the battlefield with no Swiftfoot Boots on him and no {U} or {W} open.
  • Shabraz carries counters. The table reads counters as the kill.
  • The table has already answered a commander once. In 001 Brallin was removed twice and the tax stacked (log:001).
  • In 007 Shabraz was taken out and the game stalled until he came back (log:007).

Immediate action.

  1. Read the removal: does it target, is it a spell or an ability, and does it destroy, deal damage, exile, bounce or make you sacrifice.
  2. If it targets Shabraz, answer it in this order: Deflecting Swat for free (you control a commander) onto one of their permanents; Slip Out the Back ({U}), which phases him out with a +1/+1 counter; Loran's Escape ({W}); Shelter ({1}{W}) naming the spell's colour, which also draws a card.
  3. If it is a noncreature spell and step 2 did not stop it, counter it: Stubborn Denial ({U}, a hard counter while Shabraz has 4 or more power) or Negate ({1}{U}).
  4. If it destroys or deals damage, cast Flawless Maneuver for free. If it exiles or tucks, channel Otawara, Soaring City on Shabraz ({3}{U}, {1} less per legendary creature you control): he returns to your hand and costs 5 from there.
  5. If nothing stops it, put Shabraz in the command zone and say the new tax aloud: 7, then 9. Brallin's is 6, then 8.

Subsequent.

  1. Hold every draw spell and draw-seven until Shabraz is back. A card drawn with him in the command zone puts no counter anywhere.
  2. Recast him on the first turn you can pay the tax plus one shield: 7 mana with Swiftfoot Boots already out to equip for {1}, or 7 plus {U} for Slip Out the Back.
  3. If the tax is 9 and you cannot pay it plus a shield within two turns, cast Brallin (4, or his own tax) as the threat. Loot with Kitsa and Teferi, Master of Time's +1 to grow him and ping the table, as you did to stay alive in 007 (log:007).
  4. Keep Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat in hand. Both are free while you control either commander; with both in the command zone they cost full price.
NOTEIf an opponent shuffles Shabraz into your library (Chaos Warp, a tuck) and Brallin is castable this turn or next, let Shabraz go to the library instead of the command zone: Brallin's partner trigger puts him into your hand, and from your hand he costs 5 with no tax. Swiftfoot Boots gives hexproof, not shroud, so your own Shelter, Slip Out the Back and equips still target him. Hexproof does nothing against an edict; Slip Out the Back does, because a phased-out Shabraz is not there to sacrifice. Game 001 was on v1.2.0 and 007 on v2.3.0 (log:001, log:007).

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:007.

3.2STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. Shabraz is gone or was never cast, the cards keep coming, and nothing is growing

Indications.

  • Shabraz is in the command zone at tax 7 or more and your hand holds draw spells. In 007 losing him 'killed my momentum' (log:007).
  • You have cast both commanders and neither carries Boots or a counter held up. The pilot's word after 007: 'spent too much time dicking around with both and wheels' (log:007).
  • You have cast a draw-seven with Shabraz off the battlefield.
  • Turn five with lands and rocks out and no commander cast. In 004 both commanders were held for a wheel that never came (log:004).
  • Treasure piles up and the hand is empty. 006 ended with 36 Treasure and no cards (log:006).

Immediate action.

  1. Stop casting draw spells until Shabraz is on the battlefield. Brainstorm, Shelter and the draw-sevens wait in hand.
  2. Cast Shabraz this turn if you can pay the tax. Put the shield down with him: equip Swiftfoot Boots for {1}, or hold {U} for Slip Out the Back.
  3. If you cannot reach the tax this turn, spend the turn on mana: a rock, Smothering Tithe, Prismari Command's Treasure mode at the end of the turn before yours.
  4. On the turn after Shabraz survives a rotation, cast the draw-seven. Seven cards is seven counters and seven life; in 007 two wheels after his return saved the life total and put him close to 21 on Stuart (log:007).

Subsequent.

  1. Leave Brallin in the command zone unless Torbran, Gratuitous Violence or Fiery Emancipation is on the battlefield or a Shark needs trample. The pilot's direction after 007: one commander, Brallin for red and late trample (log:007).
  2. Put one draw engine beside Shabraz before the second creature: Valeria Richards, Archmage Emeritus, Rhystic Study or Teferi, Master of Time.
  3. With spare mana and no play, cycle Shark Typhoon at the end of the turn before yours, crack Fiery Islet or cycle Raugrin Triome. Each draw is a counter on Shabraz.
  4. Equip Fireshrieker or Lizard Blades on Shabraz before you add a third creature to the board.
NOTEStalled here means the plan has stopped, not that the deck is short of cards. On this list a draw without Shabraz is the stall. Games 004 and 006 are logged as raced and combo and 007 as stalled; all three predate v3.0.0, and 004 and 006 describe the wheel-and-burn list the rebuild replaced (log:004, log:006, log:007).

Drawn from 3 logged game(s): log:007, log:004, log:006.

3.3WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming or has landed, and Shabraz is on the board

Indications.

  • A white or black seat passes with four or more mana open and a board smaller than yours.
  • Shabraz has counters and two or more of your Humans are beside him.
  • A seat with a sweeper in its colours has drawn a fresh seven from your draw-seven.
  • The pod has already wiped once this game. In 006 the deck survived several wraths and wipes (log:006).

Immediate action.

  1. Read the sweeper: destroy, damage, exile, bounce, -X/-X or sacrifice.
  2. If it is a spell, counter it: Stubborn Denial ({U}, hard while Shabraz has 4 or more power) or Negate ({1}{U}).
  3. Cast Slip Out the Back ({U}) on Shabraz. It answers all six kinds in step 1; his Equipment phases out with him and he returns at your untap with a +1/+1 counter.
  4. Without Slip Out the Back: against destroy, cast Flawless Maneuver for free. Against damage from a coloured source, cast Akroma's Will ({3}{W}) with both modes: indestructible and protection from each colour. Against exile or -X/-X, channel Otawara on Shabraz and recast him from hand for 5.
  5. Let the rest go. Say the new tax aloud for any commander that leaves.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Shabraz alone. Hold the Humans until the sweeper seat has passed a turn.
  2. Re-equip on Shabraz in this order: Swiftfoot Boots {1}, Brotherhood Regalia {1}, then Fireshrieker {2}.
  3. At the end of the turn before yours, cycle Shark Typhoon with every land left open for a flying X/X Shark and a counter on Shabraz.
  4. Before your next creature, count the sweeper seat's open mana again.
NOTEShelter's protection from a colour stops a coloured damage sweeper and nothing that destroys or exiles 'all'. Swiftfoot Boots' hexproof stops no sweeper. Grand Abolisher stops spells only on your turn, so it never stops a sweeper cast on theirs. Game 006 survived on v2.2.0 with phase-out cards that list carried; v3.0.0's phase-out is Slip Out the Back (log:006).

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:006.

3.4RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat will kill you before Shabraz reaches 21, and you cannot block it

Indications.

  • A seat warps or casts large hasty creatures every turn. Alex's warp deck outpaced the list in 004 (log:004).
  • A seat's permanent drains the whole table as its creatures enter. Goblin Storm did that in 005 before the commanders arrived (log:005).
  • Turn five with no commander on the battlefield. Game 003: 'slow to get cards out' (log:003).
  • You are casting small creatures while Shabraz sits in the command zone (log:005).
  • One attack takes a third or more of your life.

Immediate action.

  1. Count both clocks aloud: how many of their turns kill you, and how many of yours kill them.
  2. If theirs is shorter, answer the engine, not the bodies: Swords to Plowshares, Get Lost or Chaos Warp on Tannuk or the drain creature; Chaos Warp, Prismari Command or Otawara on Alex's turn-ending artifact in his main phase, after he spends the warp mana; Get Lost or Chaos Warp on a drain enchantment.
  3. Cast Shabraz this turn if he is castable, ahead of any other creature.
  4. If Willie Lumpkin connects with the racing seat, offer that player the card: if they draw it, they cannot attack you or your permanents on their next turn.
  5. In their beginning of combat, cycle Shark Typhoon with every land you held open: a flying X/X Shark that blocks this turn, and a counter on Shabraz.

Subsequent.

  1. Point every point of commander damage at the racing seat until it is gone.
  2. Gain life by drawing: each card is a life with Shabraz out. A draw-seven is seven; in 007 two wheels saved the life total (log:007).
  3. Cast Akroma's Will on your swing with both modes: flying, double strike, lifelink and indestructible on every creature you control.
  4. Leave Bard, King of Dale untapped on their turn: reach and vigilance make him a blocker for flyers after he attacks.
  5. Brotherhood Regalia on Shabraz makes him unblockable; go over the board, not through it.
NOTENothing in the 99 counters a creature spell; Negate and Stubborn Denial hit noncreature spells only. Grand Abolisher does nothing on Alex's turn, where his turn-ending artifact is activated. Hold Chaos Warp at Alex's table. Games 003, 004 and 005 predate v3.0.0 and the cards they name are gone (log:003, log:004, log:005, log:007).

Drawn from 4 logged game(s): log:003, log:004, log:005, log:007.

3.5COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. another seat is assembling a loop or a one-turn kill, and your answers are a handful of instants

Indications.

  • A seat tutors twice in one turn, or plays a piece and holds its mana up.
  • A seat whose deck checks at bracket 4 sits across from your bracket 3 list. Games 002 and 006 both ended on Ryan's Teval going infinite, the second around turn 8 or 9 (log:002, log:006).
  • A sacrifice outlet and a recursion piece sit on one board, or a seat's graveyard is its engine.
  • A seat's life total, token count or land count jumps on a turn it attacks nobody.
  • You are one card short of your own kill. In 006 every multiplier was on the board and the last piece never came (log:006).

Immediate action.

  1. Name the piece on the battlefield the loop needs. That piece is the target.
  2. Answer it at instant speed: Swords to Plowshares ({W}) or Get Lost ({1}{W}) on a creature, Get Lost on an enchantment, Prismari Command on an artifact, Chaos Warp ({2}{R}) on any permanent, Otawara's channel to bounce it, or Teferi, Master of Time's -3 to phase a creature out until its controller's next turn.
  3. If the last piece is a noncreature spell from hand, hold {U} for Stubborn Denial or {1}{U} for Negate through that seat's turn and counter it.
  4. If the loop runs from a graveyard, cast Echo of Eons or Time Reversal: each player shuffles hand and graveyard into the library.
  5. If nothing answers it, count your own kill this turn. If it is there, take it now; if it is short, swing at the combo seat.

Subsequent.

  1. From the turn a seat shows its first piece, keep {W} and {1}{U} open before you tap out for Equipment or a creature.
  2. Spend spare mana on that seat's turn digging for the answer: Shark Typhoon's cycling, Faerie Mastermind's {3}{U} (it hands every seat a card), Teferi's +1.
  3. Cast Grand Abolisher the turn before your kill turn. On your turn no opponent casts a spell or activates an artifact, creature or enchantment ability.
  4. Do not cast Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Reforge the Soul, Magus of the Wheel's ability, Jace's Archivist's ability or Collective Defiance's first mode against a graveyard deck. Each puts a full hand in its graveyard.
NOTEEcho of Eons and Time Reversal are the two cards in the 99 that empty an opponent's graveyard, and both refill the combo seat's hand to seven. Cast one only when the graveyard is the engine. Both grounding games predate v3.0.0: 002 was on v1.3.0 and 006 on v2.2.0 (log:002, log:006).

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:006.

3.6MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the mana has stopped short of five, or white or blue is missing for Shabraz

Indications.

  • Three lands on turn four with no rock.
  • Four mana and no white or no blue source: Shabraz ({3}{W}{U}) sits in the command zone.
  • Torbran ({1}{R}{R}{R}), Gratuitous Violence ({2}{R}{R}{R}) or Fiery Emancipation ({3}{R}{R}{R}) in hand with fewer than three red sources.
  • Game 003 was 'mana starved one round' (log:003). In 007 the pilot named the cost: 'two commanders, two taxes, 9 CMC total' (log:007).

Immediate action.

  1. If you hold no land, dig before the land drop: Brainstorm ({U}), Kitsa's loot, cycling Shark Typhoon for X=0 ({1}{U}), cycling Raugrin Triome ({3}), or sacrificing Mind Stone or Fiery Islet ({1}). Keep lands, put back spells.
  2. Play the land that casts something this turn. Training Center and Sea of Clouds enter untapped with two or more opponents. Crack a fetch for the colour you are missing.
  3. Cast the cheapest rock before any other spell: Sol Ring first, then the rock that makes the missing colour. White or blue: Azorius Signet or Talisman of Progress. White: Boros Signet or Talisman of Conviction. Blue: Izzet Signet or Talisman of Creativity. Either: Arcane Signet.
  4. Spend the mana on Shabraz alone. Brallin and the three-red cards wait until Shabraz is out.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Smothering Tithe before the next draw-seven: every card an opponent draws and does not pay {2} for is a Treasure.
  2. Cast Prismari Command at the end of the turn before yours choosing a Treasure for you and the draw-two on you.
  3. Put Valeria Richards down before the rocks still in hand: each noncreature spell costs {1} less.
  4. Spend Treasure on Shabraz and his shield before you spend it on cards.
NOTEGemstone Caverns makes coloured mana only if it starts on the battlefield, and it starts there only if you are not the starting player; in 004 the pilot went first and it made colourless all game (log:004). Game 003 was on v2.0.0 and 007 on v2.3.0; v3.0.0 added Boros Signet, Fellwar Stone and Talisman of Conviction (log:003, log:007).

3.7POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has turned on the draw-sevens or the taxes, and you have become the problem

Indications.

  • A seat complains when you cast a draw-seven. In 001 'people either loved it or hated it' (log:001).
  • You have cast two draw-sevens before Shabraz is big. In 002 the pilot named repeated discards as the thing the table did not enjoy (log:002).
  • Opponents pay {2} for every Smothering Tithe trigger and {1} for every Rhystic Study trigger.
  • Removal comes at Shabraz before your board is the largest one.

Immediate action.

  1. Stop casting symmetrical draw-sevens for card flow. Draw one-sided instead: Brainstorm, Shelter, Kitsa's loot, Teferi, Master of Time's +1, Shark Typhoon's cycling, Prismari Command's draw-two on you.
  2. Save the next draw-seven for the turn it puts Shabraz within one swing of 21 on a seat (log:002).
  3. Spend your next removal spell on the seat that is ahead, and say so at the table.
  4. Hold Willie Lumpkin's truce for the seat most likely to attack you: when he connects, offer that seat the card.

Subsequent.

  1. When one seat holds zero or one card, do not cast Wheel of Fortune or Windfall; it refills that seat to seven.
  2. Cast Consecrated Sphinx on the turn before the draw-seven, not earlier. It is stack 009's board, and the table aims the next removal at it.
  3. Offer an answer to another seat's problem in exchange for a turn without removal aimed at Shabraz. Swords to Plowshares and Get Lost are the cards to offer.
  4. Activate Faerie Mastermind's {3}{U} only when your draw is worth more to you than one card to every opponent: with Smothering Tithe or Consecrated Sphinx out.
NOTEEcho of Eons, Time Reversal and Winds of Change shuffle instead of discarding, which reads gentler at a table that hates losing its hand; Winds of Change at {R} is the cheapest of the three. Both grounding games were on the wheel-and-burn lists (log:001, log:002).

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Three to five lands, or two lands and Sol Ring or a two-mana rock: Arcane Signet, Azorius Signet, Boros Signet, Izzet Signet, Talisman of Conviction, Talisman of Creativity, Talisman of Progress, Mind Stone, Fellwar Stone.
  • A white source and a blue source, or a rock that makes the missing one, so Shabraz is castable on turn four or five. Red is not needed for him.
  • A play for turn two or three: a rock, Swiftfoot Boots, Kitsa, Willie Lumpkin, Faerie Mastermind, Sokka, Shang-Chi, Lae'zel or Rhystic Study.
  • A land-and-ramp hand with no payoff is a keep: Shabraz and the cantrips dig to the payoff (Q012, log:004).
  • A protection piece in hand (Swiftfoot Boots, Slip Out the Back, Shelter, Loran's Escape) is worth a land in a close call (log:001).

Ship on sight.

  • Zero or one land.
  • No white source and no blue source, and no rock that makes either: Shabraz is not castable.
  • Two or more draw-sevens with nothing to cast before turn four. That was the hand the pilot doubted in 002 (log:002).
  • A hand of cards that need Shabraz first (Fireshrieker, Lizard Blades, Blade Historian, Akroma's Will, Gratuitous Violence, Fiery Emancipation, draw-sevens) with no early plays.
NOTEIn a multiplayer game the first mulligan does not cost a card. Gemstone Caverns goes onto the battlefield before turn one only if you are not the starting player; in 004 the pilot went first and it made only colourless (log:004). When it does go down, exile a draw-seven or a payoff, never a rock or a land.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 82% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Turn one: land, then Sol Ring if you have it, otherwise Esper Sentinel.
  2. Turn two: a two-mana rock. With no rock, Swiftfoot Boots, Kitsa or Willie Lumpkin; hold Faerie Mastermind and flash it in at the end of an opponent's turn.
  3. Turn three: Rhystic Study, Shang-Chi, Sokka, Lae'zel or a second rock. With Sol Ring on turn one, Shabraz.
  4. Put Swiftfoot Boots on the battlefield before Shabraz, so the turn he lands costs 5 plus {1}.
  5. Cast no draw-seven before Shabraz is on the battlefield. Without him it grows nothing.
  6. Spend Swords to Plowshares or Get Lost in the first three turns only on a combo piece, Tannuk, or a creature that kills Shabraz on sight.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Shabraz on curve: turn four with a rock, turn five without. Never hold him for a draw-seven (log:004).
  2. Shield him the turn he lands: equip Swiftfoot Boots for {1}, or hold {U} for Slip Out the Back, {W} for Loran's Escape or {1}{W} for Shelter. Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat are free from here on. With neither Boots nor a spare mana, cast him anyway (Q013).
  3. A draw engine that fires every turn: Valeria Richards, Archmage Emeritus, Rhystic Study, Esper Sentinel or Teferi, Master of Time.
  4. A multiplier: Teferi's Ageless Insight or Bard, King of Dale doubles every draw after your first draw-step draw; Lae'zel adds one counter to every placement. Stack 008: Windfall for seven under Lae'zel leaves Shabraz 17/17.
  5. Double strike on Shabraz: Fireshrieker (equip {2}) or Lizard Blades (reconfigure {2}). Brotherhood Regalia ({1}) makes him unblockable.
  6. The draw-seven on the turn Shabraz is shielded and swings. Under Consecrated Sphinx it is 42 more draws (stack 009). With Kitsa at power 3 or more, copy it for {2} (stack 010).
  7. Brallin last: when Torbran, Gratuitous Violence or Fiery Emancipation is on the battlefield, when a Shark needs trample, or when Shabraz's tax is 9 (log:001, log:007).

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Untap. Count each opponent's open mana and colours before you cast anything.
  2. First main phase: cast the cheap draw. The first noncreature spell draws off Valeria; each instant or sorcery draws off Archmage Emeritus. Each card is a counter and a life.
  3. Equip at sorcery speed: Swiftfoot Boots first, then Fireshrieker or Lizard Blades, then Brotherhood Regalia.
  4. Pay {W/U} to give a Human flying. Attack with Sokka beside another creature to draw before damage.
  5. Pass with a shield: Flawless Maneuver or Deflecting Swat in hand, or {U} for Slip Out the Back or Stubborn Denial, or {W} for Loran's Escape or Swords to Plowshares.
  6. On opponents' turns spend what you held: Teferi, Master of Time's +1, Shark Typhoon's cycling at the end of the turn before yours, Fiery Islet's draw or Raugrin Triome's cycling.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count commander damage at one seat: 21 from Shabraz. Brallin's counts on its own; the two do not add.
  2. Count Shabraz's power after this turn's draws. With double strike, 11 power is 22. With Kitsa untapped to loot between the strikes, 10 is enough (stack 006).
  3. Add the damage multipliers: with Gratuitous Violence a double-striking Shabraz needs 6 power, with Fiery Emancipation 4. Torbran adds nothing to Shabraz; he is not red. No stack has yet resolved the multiplied damage as commander damage (Engineering, open question).
  4. Put Grand Abolisher on the battlefield the turn before. On the kill turn no opponent casts a spell or activates an artifact, creature or enchantment ability.
  5. Sequence the turn: draw in the first main phase, equip, Blade Historian before combat, then attack. Akroma's Will is an instant: cast it after blockers with both modes.
  6. With Consecrated Sphinx, Shabraz and Brallin out, cast Wheel of Fortune precombat. Stack 009: Shabraz ends 52/52 and the cleanup discard fires Brallin 42 times at every seat. Decline Sphinx triggers before your library runs out.
  7. Wait a turn if a blue seat has mana open and you have neither Grand Abolisher out nor a counter in hand, or if the count is short and nothing in hand adds the last point.

  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Under model_hand_limit a hand over seven discards down at end of turn (CR 514.1) unless a permanent or a rest-of-the-game sorcery says no maximum hand size; a commander's own text and a raised limit ('your maximum hand size is ten') are not read.

  • The turn deploys before it wheels: the commander, then rocks, payoffs and bodies, then a wheel on what is left, then whatever the wheel found. A wheel or a loot goes first only to dig: no land in hand and a commander still in the zone. A loot waits for a payoff like a wheel.
  • Repeatable activations run until the mana, a library floor of LIFE_CHAIN_LIBRARY_FLOOR (10, authored) or a hand of seven stops them, the hand only when no payoff is paid per draw.   • A rock or a land sacrifices itself for a card only with ROCK_SAC_MIN_MANA (7, authored) left after it and nothing in hand that mana wants. Cumulative upkeep is paid to age CUMULATIVE_UPKEEP_MAX_AGE (3, authored). Mystic Remora reads OPPONENT_CASTS_PER_TURN (measured) thinned by OPPONENT_NONCREATURE_SHARE (0.5, authored) at TITHE_PAY_RATE.
  • A doubler reaches what its text says: double strike and doubled power, combat only; 'a creature', combat and a creature's noncombat damage; 'a source', all damage; 'noncombat', that. Life loss is not damage and is never multiplied or bonused.

A one-a-turn discard payoff (Monument to Endurance) takes its modes in the printed order.   • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played.   • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent, Tocasia's Welcome with its mana-value gate), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAW (Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Morbid Opportunist, Midnight Reaper) fires on every death this model produces — the sacrifice sweep under `model_sacrifice`, the one-turn copies leaving, and the measured rate under `model_deaths` — honouring 'nontoken' and 'once each turn' (2026-10-19); a deck with no death channel on has no deaths, and the payoff is absent. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent) and attack-triggered draw outside `model_combat` are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw', 'you may pay 2 life' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one, and 15 death triggers.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • DISCOVER, always on. An instant or sorcery with 'Discover N' is cast like any spell (storm, magecraft and per-cast damage see it), and each resolution — plus one per Zada copy — walks to the first nonland card with mana value N or less and CASTS IT FREE when it has a free door: a ritual, a draw spell, a discover spell, a storm spell, a pump or token copy (with a board, under model_combat), or a body. Any other hit goes to HAND and is cast later at full cost — a floor. The rest go to the bottom in a random order. A permanent's discover (the Hidden lands, an ETB, a death) and discover X are not read.
  • CASCADE, always on, the card's OWN keyword only (grants are not read). Every cast — paid, free or a cascade hit's own — walks to the first nonland card with mana value STRICTLY LESS and casts it free through the same doors as Discover; a hit with no free door goes to the bottom with the rest, in a random order (a floor). Copies never cascade. A cascade card no other loop casts is cast late in the main phase for the cascade alone, so it adds nothing to that turn's storm count.
  • A FREE-CAST CHAIN IS CAPPED at FREE_CAST_DEPTH_LIMIT nested free casts (8); a hit past it takes the not-cast path. The depth reached and the refusals are reported in metrics.free_cast.
  • MAGECRAFT FIRES ON EVERY COPY ('cast or copy'): one event per Zada copy of a spell, at every copy site (draw, pump, untap, storm, discover), counted once per spell however many arms the spell has.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • RITUALS ARE CAST WHEN THEY ARE THE DIFFERENCE (2026-10-05): a spell that adds mana (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Battle Hymn, Brightstone Ritual, Inner Fire) is cast only if its net mana makes a card in hand, or the commander, castable this turn; its mana lasts the turn. Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will / Rousing Refrain read the OPPONENTS' state this model does not have, on two AUTHORED rates in config.py: GEYSER_TAPPED_SHARE (0.5 of their lands still tapped on our turn) and OPPONENT_HAND (4 cards, 7 on a turn a wheel refilled them). Rituals with an additional cost or restricted mana are not read.
  • Extra card draw is modelled ONLY behind `model_draw` — the assumption above names its channels. With the flag OFF it is one card per turn, always, which understates every deck by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs: conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list that hides, and `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled` names the cards whose draw still has no channel, and is ABSENT rather than empty on a deck where every drawer is read.
  • CREATURES TAP WHEN THEY ATTACK (CR 508.1f) and untap at the start of your turn, so an ADDITIONAL COMBAT PHASE is only worth something if something untaps the team. Before this the swing was computed once and multiplied by the phase count, which made an extra combat free damage from a board that had already attacked: ur-dragon's damage at turn ten fell 68.798 -> 59.499 when it was corrected. LANDS still enter untapped, always — that assumption is unchanged and is about lands, not creatures.
  • A COMMANDER THAT COPIES A SINGLE-TARGET SPELL across the board is modelled behind `model_commander_copy`, declared per deck and checked against the commander's own text. Zada, Hedron Grinder is the only card in the corpus with the ability. It needs no authored rate: the copy count IS the number of other creatures you control, which this model already measures.
  • WHAT A COPIED SPELL DOES is read per effect, and each is one turn unless said otherwise: its draw, a pump (power only — toughness is tracked but no creature blocks here), a PERMANENT +1/+1 counter, a Treasure it creates, double strike, doubled power, damage every creature deals to each opponent, an additional combat phase, and an untap. A spell whose effect is none of these still counts toward the spell count and is otherwise inert.
  • THE SPELL COUNT IS TRACKED, so STORM is modelled, as are MAGECRAFT (which fires on a cast OR a copy) and per-cast damage (which fires on a cast only — that difference is a rules fact, not a simplification). Before this there was no spells-cast-this-turn variable at all and storm was unreadable.
  • REPLACEMENTS (2026-10-11): a counter replacement (Lae'zel +1, Doubling Season x2) applies per counter EVENT, and a damage replacement that adds (Torbran +2) per damage INSTANCE, each added before any multiplier: each ping, each storm copy, each attacker per strike. An attacker's colours ride on its battlefield entry (cast creatures, commanders, and draw/discard-made tokens such as The Locust God's blue-red Insects); a token from any other maker is colourless here, an understatement.
  • OPPONENTS' CASTS (2026-10-11): each opponent casts 1.264 spells a turn, MEASURED from 137,717 Forge turns (sim/pod_behaviour); Rhystic Study's tax is paid at the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE. Mind's Eye pays from mana left untapped at the end of our turn, a floor.
  • SPELLS AT A PLAYER (2026-10-11): damage a spell deals to a player counts against the one opponent ("any target" included). A spell whose only read effect is that damage is cast at the end of the main phase with what is left; a choose-ONE card whose other mode is read keeps that mode. Only an "each player" wheel refills the opponent; "discard your hand" and "target player" (us) do not. A discard additional cost is paid.
  • Treasures are a one-shot STOCKPILE, not a mana rock: spent only when lands and rocks fall short, and gone once broken. Reported separately from mean_available_mana_by_turn, which still means repeatable mana per turn.
  • Only Treasure triggers a goldfish can see are modeled — upkeep and landfall are RECURRING, Saga chapters likewise; a `cast` or enters-the-battlefield trigger pays out ONCE, when its own source resolves. Combat- and opponent-gated sources produce NOTHING here, because this model has no combat and no opponents; they are named in meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled so a low hoard figure is legible.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN FIRES ONLY ON THE DEATHS THIS MODEL PRODUCES. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance, Vraan and Vein Ripper key on a creature DYING, and a death happens here in three places and nowhere else: a free sacrifice outlet converting tokens (`model_sacrifice`), a one-turn copy leaving at the end step, and the per-turn rate read off a Forge run (`model_deaths`). No blockers and no removal, so a deck with neither channel on has no deaths and the contribution is ABSENT, not zero; a deck whose kill runs through them is understated by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.
  • BLOOD: a Blood token is created on an ETB, a per-opponent ETB (three, the pod) or a spell's resolution; Blood made by CONNECTING in combat is read and NOT credited, because a blocker can prevent it and this model has no blockers. AT MOST ONE IS CRACKED PER TURN, before the casting loops, and only with a card in hand, since discarding is part of the cost. One a turn is an authored floor: a pilot holding three with mana to spare cracks more, so this understates. Cracking one is a DISCARD and a DRAW, which the commander payoffs below charge for, and it sacrifices an ARTIFACT, which the artifact-sacrifice payoffs charge for.
  • ACTIVATED DRAW: a draw you BUY -- '{1}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: Draw a card' -- is paid for out of whatever mana the casting loops did not want, and a cost that eats its own source fires once. Energy and {X} activation costs are REFUSED and named, never priced at zero.
  • DISCARD: a wheel discards the WHOLE hand, lands included, and is cast only when the hand holds at most three nonland cards or a discard payoff is on the battlefield, after the turn has deployed; a loot waits for the same payoff and pitches lands beyond the next drop first, then the most expensive nonland. A discarded card stays 'seen' for target assembly (assembly is 'drawn by', and it was). Wheel of Misfortune is excluded (conditional). Activated loots, cycling and flashback are read (2026-11-04); madness is NOT modelled and is named in meta.draw_not_modelled. Discard- and draw-triggered payoffs pay a flat amount per event, outside the combat gate; +1/+1 counters they put on their own body are added to the swing while the commander is out, an approximation. Payoffs that DRAW on a draw are not read: an uncapped loop would draw the library and call it steam.
  • DISCARD: a wheel a PERMANENT carries is activated from the battlefield under the same hand-size gate as a wheel that is cast, paying its mana cost every time. It fires at the earliest on the turn AFTER the permanent lands, which is the summoning sickness of the tap in its cost, and a cost that sacrifices its own source fires once and takes the body off the board. An ability activated from the graveyard (Runehorn Hellkite) is a zone this model does not have and is refused rather than fired.
  • WHEEL TIMING (2026-10-21): the turn of every wheel is observed and decided on by nothing. A FINISHER is a wheel resolved with a COMMANDER on the battlefield and a hand the thin-hand clause alone would not have thrown away. A deck's pilot_policy.json may HOLD a wheel (`hold_until` on the wheel channel: event_payoffs, commanders_out) — an AND-gate on the rule above, so a threshold of zero changes nothing; the policy is stamped on the artifact as meta.pilot_policy. A `keep` rule in the same file is read on the hand the model kept and reported as a pass rate, never enforced.
  • DISCARD: THE OPPONENT'S HALF OF A WHEEL IS NOW COUNTED, AND ONLY THE TAX ON IT. A symmetrical wheel refills the opponent too, so `whenever an opponent draws a card` payoffs (Razorkin Needlehead, Scrawling Crawler, Mind's Eye, Consecrated Sphinx) fire on their draw step and on every card a wheel deals them. DAMAGE IS ONE SEAT, the same convention the damage pillar uses -- Brallin's '1 damage to EACH opponent' is counted once against a single 40-life opponent, which is the right per-seat clock. WHAT WE GAIN off their draws is paid by every opponent and scales with GOLDFISH_OPPONENTS (3): Faerie Mastermind's card on each opponent's second draw, Consecrated Sphinx's two per opponent draw, a Blood per opponent (2026-10-04; at one seat Hallcreeper -> Mastermind read -0.17 cards by T8, at three +0.62). WHAT IS STILL NOT MODELLED IS THE COST: the opponent draws those cards and never gets to use them, because this model has no opponent turn, no opponent board and no interaction. So the tax is priced and the risk it offsets is not. A trigger on an opponent CASTING (Rhystic Study) is a different family and stays unread.
  • TREASURE OFF THEIR DRAWS RESTS ON AN AUTHORED RATE. Smothering Tithe makes a Treasure per opponent draw unless that opponent pays {2}; how often a table pays is not measurable here, so TITHE_PAY_RATE (0.5, config.py) decides each draw. Every Tithe figure is conditional on it, and `try` says so when a list relies on it.
  • DISCARD: A WHEEL THAT DRAWS 'CARDS EQUAL TO THE GREATEST NUMBER A PLAYER DISCARDED' IS A FLOOR HERE, AND THE FLOOR IS LOW. This model has no opponents holding cards, so Jace's Archivist and Windfall draw what OUR hand held -- and the gate only lets them fire when our hand is thin, which is exactly the board on which a real table pays them seven. The figure is not corrected, because the correction would be an authored opponent hand size driving a headline; read it as a lower bound and settle the question in Forge.

6.3Fuel

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Until Shabraz has counters, the table sees a draw deck with two 3/3s and ranks you low.
  • Shabraz with Swiftfoot Boots and a pile of counters reads as the threat. Expect the table's sweepers and edicts: hexproof does not stop them.
  • Smothering Tithe and Rhystic Study tax every seat. Once a seat pays for both, it is treating you as the threat.
  • Consecrated Sphinx beside Shabraz is the stack 009 board. Expect it to draw the next removal spell.
  • The first symmetrical draw-seven changes the table's read. It split the table in 001 (log:001).
  • At a table that steps up to bracket 4 you are the slow deck, not the threat (log:002, log:006).

7.2When to reveal

  • Hold Akroma's Will for the attack. Blade Historian goes down the turn of the swing, not earlier.
  • Hold Brallin in the command zone. He comes out when Torbran, Gratuitous Violence or Fiery Emancipation is down, or when a Shark needs trample.
  • Hold Shark Typhoon as a cycle, not a cast. A six-mana enchantment is a removal target; a cycle at the end of the turn before yours is a Shark nobody saw coming.
  • Put Gratuitous Violence and Fiery Emancipation down on the turn the attack follows.
  • Equip Fireshrieker or reconfigure Lizard Blades on the turn you swing, not the turn you cast them.
  • Pass with zero mana open when Flawless Maneuver or Deflecting Swat is the shield. Both are free while you control a commander.

7.3Alliances

  • Offer removal, not draw-sevens. Swords to Plowshares or Get Lost on another seat's problem buys a rotation.
  • Willie Lumpkin's card is a truce: the seat that takes it cannot attack you on its next turn. Offer it to the seat you most need to stop.
  • When a seat asks you to stop wheeling, agree and draw one-sided instead, and keep the one draw-seven you need for the kill (log:002).
  • Break a deal only on the turn commander damage kills.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Commander damage goes at one seat until it dies.
  • First, the seat with no flyer or reach to block Shabraz, or any seat once Brotherhood Regalia is on him.
  • Next, the combo seat. Games 002 and 006 ended on one (log:002, log:006).
  • The fast seat before the control seat when its board races you: Tannuk in 004, Goblin Storm in 005 (log:004, log:005).
  • In 007 Shabraz came close to 21 on Stuart's zombie board; a token deck has ground blockers and no answer to a flyer (log:007).
  • Never swing into the seat holding open blue mana when you have neither Grand Abolisher out nor a counter in hand.
