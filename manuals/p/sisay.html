The Plan

What this deck is trying to do, and what it is assuming away. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

Five colours, a tutor commander, forty lands: the table files you as a greedy pile. Let them. She is a {2}{W} creature with no tap symbol and no once-per-turn clause; this deck's currency is access.

Read her power, never her cost: a 2/2 plus one per colour among your OTHER legendary permanents, fetching any legend cheaper than that. Zero colours, three legal targets; one, ten; two, twenty-three; four, all 33 — nothing costs more than five. The rung that matters is one to two: +13 targets, Najeela legal. Colour-dense legend first, then activate. Stop at four; the fifth buys stats, not access.

Then the bill: four cards demand {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} and only Jegantha adds it. Sol Ring is colourless, Delighted Halfling legendary spells only, Unclaimed Territory creature spells only — deploy her early off those three, then pay with the six that can: Bloom Tender, Faeburrow Elder, Jegantha, Esika, Relic of Legends, Selvala.

Stop mulliganing for the captain: turn three in 8,028 of 10,000 seeded games, turn six in 99 of 100. The gate is Sisay plus a multi-colour mana body: a little over half by six. Keep the fuel.

Removal is a shopping list, not a card in hand: one unconditional creature answer, one sweeper. The rest is tax, legendary, mana value 2 to 4: Thalia (creatures and nonbasics enter tapped), Teferi and Kutzil (turns), Kambal and Sheoldred (spells and draws). Fetch the tax that hurts the seat beating you.

Close with Najeela. She and Derevi are the one line cleared against the Comprehensive Rules — an unbounded combat loop, no life cost. It re-buys five colours a combat and wants five creatures connecting; present it when the base pays that toll.

Know the hole. Twenty-six nonland cards are not legendary, so she never sees them: three of the six that pay her, every counterspell, both free protection spells, all four Game Changers. She finds payoffs; you draw fuel.

Keep or Ship

What a keepable hand looks like, and how often you get one. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

Everyone mulligans hunting for the commander. She is the one card you do not need — turn-three Sisay in 8,028 games of 10,000. Keep for what she cannot go and get.

Six cards in the 99 pay her five-pip activation; three are not legendary, so those three only arrive off the top. Fuel and colours in the opener, payoffs later from the battlefield.

A keep is three things. Two to four lands with one that makes any colour — Command Tower, City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Exotic Orchard, Forbidden Orchard, any fetch, Secluded Courtyard named on Human. An accelerant that pays a coloured pip: Birds of Paradise, Bloom Tender, Faeburrow Elder, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Relic of Legends. And a cheap multicolour legend, the piece that turns casting her into climbing.

Katilda, Kinnan, Lotho and Saffi buy two rungs for two mana, Derevi three for three. A seven with one of those and an any-colour source is a snap keep.

Ship the payoff hand on sight. Jodah, Kenrith, Surrak and Samut over two lands is four uncastable cards in front of a 2/2 whose menu is Mox Amber, Mikaeus and Yoshimaru — she could have fetched all three.

Ship the fast hand that pays nothing. Sol Ring plus Delighted Halfling deploys her a turn early and covers not one pip of what she does next — your best-looking opener until you tap out.

Ship counterspells on one land. Protection is there by turn six in 80 games of 100; you are not the hand holding the answer on turn one.

Two habits. An early accelerant arrives by turn six in only 64 games of 100, so a seven holding one is ahead of your curve. And count colours, not lands: you keep 84.2% of first sevens on the land rule alone, so lands were never the question.

The Lines

How it actually wins, resolved and checked against the rules. ✓ RULES-VERIFIED

Whether Najeela plus Derevi is genuinely a two-card infinite

The clause is CR 508.4: a creature put onto the battlefield attacking "never 'attacked'" for triggers. Najeela's tokens are invisible to the trigger that made them, so the loop grows between combats, never inside one.

CR 603.2c gives Derevi one untap trigger per creature that connects, not one per damage event; the activation stays five. Attackers follow 2A - 1: three, five, nine, seventeen, thirty-three — combat 1 two short and funded from outside, 2 exact, 3 four to spare.

On life the popular telling is wrong: mana abilities are not mandatory (CR 118.3c) and Command Tower with Plaza of Heroes cover all five colours, so nothing compels a tap of City of Brass or Mana Confluence. Zero life a combat.

CR 506.4 lists removal from combat exhaustively; a player leaving the game is not among the causes, so the seventeen attackers stay.

The holding: real, deterministic, unbounded — and not a two-card infinite, since neither makes mana. Five creatures connecting and a base paying {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} each combat are requirements. On the deck's nine other combo records, the record is silent.
