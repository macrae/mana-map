3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. Zur is on the battlefield and the search has fired once or not at all

Indications.

  • Zur has survived two of your turns and you have fetched once
  • No enabler on the battlefield: no Aqueous Form, Reconnaissance, Aether Tunnel, Spirit Mantle, Steel of the Godhead, Unquestioned Authority
  • You are declining the attack because a blocker trades with Zur
  • Your board is enchantments you hard-cast, and none of them arrived off an attack

Immediate action.

  1. Declare Zur as an attacker. The trigger fires on the attack, not on connecting, and it does not require Zur to survive.
  2. With no enabler on the battlefield, take the cheapest one: Aqueous Form at {U}, Reconnaissance at {W}, then Aether Tunnel or Spirit Mantle at two. An Aura put onto Zur during declare attackers makes that same attack unblockable.
  3. With Reconnaissance on the battlefield, let the search resolve, then pay {0} to remove Zur from combat and untap him before blockers are declared.
  4. With no enabler and a blocker that kills Zur, attack and fetch Reprobation onto that blocker. It loses all abilities and becomes a 0/1 Coward, and a creature without flying cannot block Zur (CR 509.1b).
  5. If Thassa, God of the Sea is on the battlefield and no Aura is, pay {1}{U} in your first main phase to make Zur unblockable, then attack.

Subsequent.

  1. Attack every turn from here. Across 60 Forge games the search resolved 1.22 times per game against the model's ceiling of 3.66 (goldfish_metrics.json, attack_tutor).
  2. Put one 'an enchantment you control enters' payoff on the battlefield before you spend a third fetch: Grim Guardian, Balemurk Leech, Underworld Coinsmith, Entity Tracker, Archon of Sun's Grace, Ajani's Chosen, Gremlin Tamer.
  3. Cast Enlightened Tutor for Reconnaissance or Aqueous Form when you hold neither. It puts the card on top of your library, so cast it in the end step before your attack turn.
  4. Take the most expensive legal card with every fetch after an enabler is on the battlefield.
NOTEThe fetch is card advantage out of your library, so an attack that loses Zur still costs you nothing in hand. The one attack to decline is the one where you have already fetched this turn and Zur is your only blocker against a seat that swings for lethal.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. Zur is targeted, or the Aura that lets him attack is

Indications.

  • An opponent leaves one or two mana untapped through your turn
  • Zur is your only creature on the battlefield
  • The enabler on Zur is an Aura, so removal on Zur takes the Aura with it
  • Zur has died twice and the next cast costs eight mana

Immediate action.

  1. Declare Zur as an attacker before you spend mana on anything else. The trigger does not require Zur to live, so removal cast in response still lets the search resolve.
  2. Fetch Sheltered by Ghosts and attach it to Zur. It gives him ward {2} and exiles a nonland permanent an opponent controls as it enters.
  3. After combat, play Talon Gates of Madara for your land drop and phase Zur out with its enters trigger. He phases in untapped on your next untap step.
  4. If the search has already resolved this turn, let Zur die rather than spending Counterspell, Muddle the Mixture or No More Lies on the removal.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Zur on a turn you can also attack, or on a turn an enabler is already on the battlefield.
  2. Run the drain route while Zur is off the board: Sanctum of Stone Fangs and Northern Air Temple in your first main phase, Bastion of Remembrance on every creature that dies, Underworld Coinsmith at {W}{B} and 1 life per activation. None of them needs an attack.
  3. Cast Enlightened Tutor for Reconnaissance so the recast turn is also an attack turn.
NOTESpirit Mantle and Unquestioned Authority grant protection from creatures, not protection from removal. They make Zur unblockable; they do not stop Swords to Plowshares. Ward {2} on Sheltered by Ghosts and phasing on Talon Gates of Madara are the two effects in the 99 that answer a targeted spell.

3.3WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming and your board is what the attacks built

Indications.

  • A white or black seat holds four or more untapped lands and casts nothing on its own turn
  • Your board is four or more permanents and most of them arrived off a Zur attack
  • You hold two or more enchantments you do not need to win this turn
  • The Meathook Massacre is in hand and not on the battlefield

Immediate action.

  1. Attack with Zur before you cast anything from hand. The fetched card comes out of your library, so a sweeper that answers it costs you no card in hand.
  2. Fetch a permanent the sweeper does not answer. Against a creature wipe take Bastion of Remembrance, Phyrexian Arena, Sanctum of Stone Fangs or Dawn of Hope.
  3. Stop at two creatures on the battlefield and hold the rest of the hand.
  4. If you hold Counterspell, Muddle the Mixture or No More Lies, leave its mana untapped through every opponent's turn until the sweeper is cast.

Subsequent.

  1. Read your board again after the wipe resolves. Athreos, God of Passage, Heliod, Sun-Crowned and Thassa, God of the Sea are indestructible and are not creatures at low devotion; Enduring Innocence and Enduring Tenacity return themselves as enchantments when they die as creatures.
  2. Return Fear of Infinity from your graveyard to your hand the next time an enchantment you control enters.
  3. Recast Zur and pay the tax. The next attack is the rebuild.
  4. Rebuild to two threats, not five.
NOTEThe Meathook Massacre is the exception to holding back. With it on the battlefield, every creature you lose to somebody else's sweeper takes 1 life from each opponent and gives you 1. Cast it for X=0, or fetch it, before the wipe rather than after.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the mana has stopped, and the colour that stops is black

Indications.

  • You missed a land drop and hold a double-black card: Master of the Feast, Nighthowler, Phyrexian Arena, Enduring Tenacity or The Meathook Massacre
  • Three lands on the battlefield and none of them makes {B}
  • Turn five and Zur is still in the command zone
  • Black is the colour that fails: 28 land sources against a 36-source target, and a 78% on-curve rate against 91% for white and 90% for blue (mana_analysis.json)

Immediate action.

  1. Play a land before you cast anything. Take the untapped mode on Godless Shrine and Watery Grave only on a turn you are casting a spell; otherwise pay nothing and let them enter tapped.
  2. Cast the cheapest rock in hand, in this order: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Dominance.
  3. If Zur can attack, fetch Land Tax while an opponent controls more lands than you. Fetch Urza's Saga instead if you have already used your land drop — it arrives on the battlefield as a land you did not have to play.
  4. Hold Master of the Feast, Nighthowler, Phyrexian Arena, Enduring Tenacity and The Meathook Massacre until a second black source is on the battlefield.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Black Market Connections and take Sell Contraband in every first main phase while you are a land behind.
  2. Tap Fetid Heath for {C} when you have nothing else; its filter mode needs {W} or {B} from another source first.
  3. Sacrifice a creature to Lazotep Quarry for one mana of any colour when the colour is what is missing.
  4. Urza's Saga chapter III searches for an artifact with mana cost {0} or {1}. Sol Ring is the one in this deck; if Sol Ring is already on the battlefield, chapter III finds nothing.
NOTESequence black first when the choice is free. Eleven of the 36 lands are basics — three Island, three Plains, five Swamp — and Ossification enchants a basic land you control, so a board with no basic land makes Ossification an illegal fetch.

3.5COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. a seat is assembling a line and you are not going to outrace it

Indications.

  • A seat tutors twice in one turn cycle
  • A seat holds seven or more cards and casts nothing
  • The piece already on the battlefield has hexproof or ward
  • A seat asks how much life you have before it taps out

Immediate action.

  1. Name the piece out loud and say what you will spend on it.
  2. If Zur can attack this turn, attack and fetch Imprisoned in the Moon or Reprobation onto the piece. Putting an Aura onto the battlefield does not target (CR 303.4f), so hexproof and ward do not stop it, and the Aura arrives in the declare attackers step (CR 508.2).
  3. If the piece is a creature you can target, cast Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile or Journey to Nowhere on it now rather than holding it.
  4. If the piece is still in hand and you hold Counterspell, Muddle the Mixture or No More Lies, keep its mana untapped through every opponent's turn.

Subsequent.

  1. Transmute Muddle the Mixture for a two-mana answer: Journey to Nowhere, Reprobation, Ossification or Sheltered by Ghosts.
  2. Spend Field of Ruin on the land the line needs.
  3. Ask the seat with counterspells to hold up mana and tell them which turn you expect the line.
NOTEThis deck carries no two-card infinite (bracket_report.json, combo_count 0) and its goldfish kill is median turn 9. You do not answer a combo by going faster; you answer it with the fetch, the counterspell or the table.

3.6RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat is faster and you cannot answer its board in combat

Indications.

  • You have taken combat damage from the same seat in two consecutive rounds
  • That seat's board is four or more attackers and yours is Zur
  • The Meathook Massacre is in hand and you have five or more lands
  • Your life total is below 25 before round six

Immediate action.

  1. Hard-cast The Meathook Massacre for the X that kills their board. Fetched with the attack trigger it enters with X=0 and sweeps nothing.
  2. Cast Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile on their attacker with the highest power. The life they gain is smaller than the damage it stops.
  3. Attack with Zur and fetch Reconnaissance, then pay {0} to pull him out of combat so he untaps and blocks on the way back.
  4. Fetch Steel of the Godhead or Sheltered by Ghosts onto Zur and start gaining life on every attack.

Subsequent.

  1. Convert the lifegain: with Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Enduring Tenacity on the battlefield, every point you gain takes that much off one opponent; with Marauding Blight-Priest each opponent loses 1 per gain.
  2. Put Archon of Sun's Grace on the battlefield. Every enchantment that enters makes a 2/2 flying Pegasus, and the Archon gives Pegasi lifelink.
  3. Use Ossification or Journey to Nowhere on the attacker you cannot block.
  4. Stop drawing off Phyrexian Arena, Black Market Connections and Bitterblossom while you are inside four turns of dead.
NOTEThe Meathook Massacre and Urza's Saga both have mana value counted with X=0 outside the stack, so Zur can fetch either. Fetch Meathook for its two death triggers, which work permanently; hard-cast it when you need the sweep.

3.7POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has decided you are the seat to answer

Indications.

  • Two seats have answered your permanents in one turn cycle
  • Your drains took every opponent down in the same turn and three players said so
  • An opponent names Zur before naming a board
  • You are the seat with the most enchantments and the fewest attackers

Immediate action.

  1. Point combat at one seat and keep it there. The constellation drains take from all three opponents whatever you do; combat is the part you choose.
  2. Stop activating Underworld Coinsmith until the activations take a seat out.
  3. Spend Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile or Journey to Nowhere on the permanent the rest of the table is afraid of, and say which one before you cast it.
  4. Hold Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose, Enduring Tenacity and Marauding Blight-Priest in hand until the turn you convert.

Subsequent.

  1. Fetch a permanent that reads as defence while the table is looking at you: Reconnaissance, Journey to Nowhere or Sheltered by Ghosts.
  2. Block with Zur. With Reconnaissance on the battlefield he attacks and blocks in the same round.
  3. Tap Forbidden Orchard only when you need the colour; every tap hands a named opponent a 1/1 Spirit.
  4. Cast Master of the Feast when you are behind. It draws each opponent a card every upkeep, which is three cards a turn cycle you are paying for.
NOTEThe drains are symmetric and that is what makes this seat everybody's problem at once: Grim Guardian, Balemurk Leech, Sanctum of Stone Fangs, Northern Air Temple, Bastion of Remembrance and Marauding Blight-Priest all take from three opponents on the same trigger. In the 60-game Forge run this seat received 0.33 interaction per game, so the model's table did not converge; a human table does.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Three lands, or two lands and a rock, with a source of {B} among them
  • A play on turn one or turn two: Aqueous Form, Reconnaissance, Land Tax, Mystic Remora, Northern Air Temple, Sol Ring, or a two-mana enchantment
  • Four mana on turn four with {W}, {U} and {B} available between the lands you hold
  • Two lands, Sol Ring or Arcane Signet, and an enabler
  • Any hand holding Reconnaissance or Aqueous Form plus three lands

Ship on sight.

  • One land, whatever else is in it
  • Seven cards with no black source and two or more black cards
  • A hand whose cheapest spell costs three, with no enabler and no tutor
  • Five lands and two spells you cannot cast before turn five
  • Six cards after one mulligan with no play before turn three
NOTEGemstone Caverns is a land you have already played if you are not the starting player: begin the game with it on the battlefield and exile a card from your hand. Count it as a land before you decide.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 80% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Turn one: play a land and cast Aqueous Form, Reconnaissance, Land Tax or Mystic Remora if you hold one.
  2. Play Choked Estuary and Isolated Chapel on a turn you are not casting a spell; pay the 2 life on Godless Shrine and Watery Grave only on a turn you are.
  3. Turn two: cast the enabler ahead of the payoff. Aqueous Form and Reconnaissance are what make Zur attack; a constellation body is worth what the attacks fetch.
  4. Turn three: cast a three-mana draw enchantment — Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena, Caretaker's Talent, Smuggler's Share or Black Market Connections — and play the land that makes {B}.
  5. Turn four: cast Zur. The measured median cast turn is 4 and 92.6% of games have him cast by turn six (goldfish_metrics.json).
  6. Cast Enlightened Tutor in the end step before your Zur turn when you hold no enabler; it puts Reconnaissance or Aqueous Form on top of your library.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Get an enabler onto the battlefield before Zur, or on the turn after him.
  2. Cast Zur.
  3. Attack on the first turn Zur can attack, whatever the board looks like.
  4. First fetch with no enabler on the battlefield: take the cheapest one — Aqueous Form {U}, Reconnaissance {W}, Aether Tunnel {1}{U}, Spirit Mantle {1}{W}, Steel of the Godhead {2}{W/U}, Unquestioned Authority {2}{W}.
  5. Every fetch after that: take the most expensive legal card.
  6. Put an 'an enchantment you control enters' payoff on the battlefield before the third fetch: Grim Guardian, Balemurk Leech, Underworld Coinsmith, Entity Tracker, Archon of Sun's Grace, Ajani's Chosen, Gremlin Tamer.
  7. Add the conversion last: Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose, Enduring Tenacity or Marauding Blight-Priest behind Underworld Coinsmith's lifegain.
NOTEAn Aura put onto the battlefield attaches to an object you choose and does not target (CR 303.4f), but the object still has to be legal. Ossification enchants a basic land you control and Sheltered by Ghosts enchants a creature you control — count what you control before you name either.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Upkeep: pay Mystic Remora's cumulative upkeep, or let it go.
  2. First main: choose Black Market Connections' modes, take Sanctum of Stone Fangs and Northern Air Temple triggers, and play a land.
  3. Declare Zur as an attacker.
  4. Let the search resolve: the cheapest enabler if none is on the battlefield, otherwise the most expensive legal card.
  5. With Reconnaissance out, pay {0} to remove Zur from combat and untap him before blockers are declared.
  6. Second main: cast enchantments one at a time and take each constellation and Eerie trigger before you cast the next.
  7. End step: leave {U}{U} or {W}{U} untapped when you hold Counterspell, Muddle the Mixture or No More Lies.
NOTEInteraction is in hand in 69% of games from turn one and castable in 9% of them at turn four, 27% at turn five (goldfish_metrics.json). The gap is mana, not cards: from turn five, leave the counterspell's mana up rather than casting a second enchantment.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count your reach before you attack: Sanctum of Stone Fangs and Northern Air Temple in your first main phase, Underworld Coinsmith at {W}{B} and 1 life per activation, Bastion of Remembrance and The Meathook Massacre on every creature that dies.
  2. Count the lifegain line: with Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Enduring Tenacity on the battlefield, each point of life you gain takes that much off one opponent.
  3. Attack the seat you can kill, not the seat that hurt you.
  4. Fetch Reprobation onto the creature that would block lethal. It arrives in the declare attackers step (CR 508.2), before blockers are declared (CR 509).
  5. Wait one turn when the drain takes a second seat out next turn and the attack takes nobody out this turn.
NOTEThe fetch is a pre-emption, not a reaction: the search resolves before blockers are declared, so you choose the answer without seeing the block. Fetch for the block you expect, and take Reconnaissance instead when you cannot name one.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (26 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • The table prices Zur before your board shows anything. A four-mana commander that searches the library reads as an engine on sight.
  • The drains are symmetric: three seats watch their life totals fall on the same trigger, so three seats respond at once.
  • Master of the Feast draws each opponent a card every upkeep. It reads as a gift because it is one.
  • Forbidden Orchard hands a named opponent a 1/1 Spirit on every tap. Tap it last.
  • In the 60-game Forge run against giada-angels, baylen-tokens and abaddon this seat received 0.33 interaction per game and dealt 11.82 combat damage against 44.68 taken. Forge's table left this seat alone; do not plan on a human one doing the same.

7.2When to reveal

  • Fetch the enabler early and the conversion late. Reconnaissance on turn five tells the table nothing; Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose tells them the whole plan.
  • Hold Vito, Enduring Tenacity and Marauding Blight-Priest in hand until the turn you convert lifegain into a kill.
  • Say what a fetched Aura does when it answers somebody's permanent. Journey to Nowhere and Sheltered by Ghosts buy goodwill only if the table sees who you pointed them at.
  • Show Reconnaissance once and let the table watch Zur untap. A seat that knows Zur blocks after attacking attacks somebody else.

7.3Alliances

  • Ask for the land answer you do not have, and offer Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Journey to Nowhere or Ossification for it.
  • Offer Sheltered by Ghosts as removal: it exiles a nonland permanent and stays on your creature, so you pay one card for two jobs.
  • Never trade away a turn of attacking. Zur's trigger fires on the attack, so a turn you do not attack is a fetch you do not get — promise a target instead of a pass.
