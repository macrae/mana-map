3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming, and under Zur or Starfield of Nyx your enchantments are creatures that die to it

Indications.

  • A seat is holding four or more mana in white and has cast nothing on their turn
  • Starfield of Nyx is on the battlefield and you control five or more enchantments, so every other non-Aura enchantment you own is already a creature
  • You animated three or more enchantments on earlier turns and they are still creatures — Zur's ability has no duration
  • A sweeper has already resolved this game from a seat that plays more than one
  • Your hand is empty and your enchantment count is the whole board

Immediate action.

  1. Stop animating. Zur's {1}{W} lasts forever, so an enchantment you animate this turn is still a creature when the sweeper resolves.
  2. Animate Heliod, Sun-Crowned, Thassa, God of the Sea and Athreos, God of Passage instead. All three are indestructible, and {1}{W} makes each a 3/3 whatever your devotion is.
  3. Keep one enchantment in hand. The board you rebuild from is the card you did not commit.
  4. If Academy Rector is on the battlefield, let the sweeper kill it and fetch Sphere of Safety onto the battlefield — it enters after the sweeper and taxes the crack-back.
  5. If you hold Counterspell or No More Lies, spend it on the sweeper rather than on the ramp spell in front of it.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Zur before you animate anything. Without him the bodies are vanilla and every removal spell in the pod works again.
  2. Rebuild to two threats, not five: the draw engine first, then one enchantment creature.
  3. Return an enchantment from your graveyard on each upkeep with Starfield of Nyx.
  4. Cast the drain that needs no combat step: Sanctum of Stone Fangs takes X in your first main phase, and Grim Guardian and Balemurk Leech each take 1 from every opponent whenever an enchantment enters.
  5. Spend {1}{W} on Enduring Tenacity, Enduring Innocence and Enduring Curiosity after they come back. Each returns itself to the battlefield as an enchantment when it dies as a creature, and animation makes it a body again.
NOTEZur's ability sets base power and toughness, so it shrinks as well as grows. Heliod and Thassa are 5/5 and Athreos is 5/4 once devotion turns them on; animate one then and you have a 3/3. Animate a God while your devotion is short, not after.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. a removal spell is on the stack, aimed at Zur or at an enchantment you have not animated

Indications.

  • The target is an enchantment you have not animated — Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena and Sphere of Safety carry no hexproof until they are creatures
  • The target is Zur himself: a 1/4 with flying and no hexproof of his own
  • A seat passes on your animated bodies and points removal at Zur
  • The spell is enchantment removal rather than creature removal
  • You have less than two mana untapped and one of it is not white

Immediate action.

  1. Animate the targeted enchantment with {1}{W} in response. It becomes an enchantment creature, Zur grants it hexproof, and the spell has no legal target left.
  2. If Zur is the target, counter the spell. The deck holds Counterspell {U}{U} and No More Lies {W}{U}; Muddle the Mixture counters an instant or a sorcery only.
  3. If the target is Academy Rector and the spell destroys rather than exiles, let it resolve. The death trigger fetches any enchantment onto the battlefield with no mana-value limit.
  4. If Zur dies, animate nothing else this turn. {1}{W} without him buys a vanilla body that the next removal spell kills.
  5. Recast Zur on the first turn you can also hold {1}{W} open.

Subsequent.

  1. Hold {1}{W} through every opponent's turn. Each two mana you keep is one removal spell you can blank.
  2. Put Sheltered by Ghosts on an enchantment creature rather than on Zur — hexproof stops the pod removing the body to break the Aura, and your own Aura can still target it.
  3. Animate the enchantments whose text the deck runs on — Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena, Black Market Connections — before you animate the ones you only want for power.
  4. Cast Enlightened Tutor for Sphere of Safety in an opponent's end step when the pod is spending removal one card at a time; it goes on top of your library and you draw it next turn.
NOTEHexproof stops opponents only. Zur's own {1}{W}, Sheltered by Ghosts, Heliod's counter and Eidolon of Astral Winds all still target your own enchantment creatures.

3.3COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. a seat is assembling a two-card line and you are the seat holding blue mana

Indications.

  • A seat has passed with two or more blue sources untapped for two turns
  • A tutor resolved and the card went to hand rather than to the battlefield
  • A commander that is a known combo piece has just been recast
  • Rhystic Study or Mystic Remora drew you a card off a spell that made no board
  • A seat is drawing three or more cards a turn and has stopped attacking

Immediate action.

  1. Counter the piece that comes out of the library, not the ritual in front of it. Counterspell {U}{U} and No More Lies {W}{U} stop any spell; Muddle the Mixture {U}{U} stops an instant or a sorcery.
  2. If the line runs through a creature, exile it: Swords to Plowshares {W}, Path to Exile {W}, Sheltered by Ghosts {1}{W} or Ossification {1}{W}.
  3. If the line runs through an opponent's commander, cast Imprisoned in the Moon on it. It becomes a colourless land with no abilities and stays on the battlefield, so it is not recast from the command zone.
  4. If the line runs through a graveyard, exile the card with Summoner's Sending in your end step.
  5. If you hold no answer, count your own lethal before you pass: animate everything, attack, and point Enduring Tenacity at the combo seat.

Subsequent.

  1. Transmute Muddle the Mixture for {1}{U}{U} on your own turn to fetch a mana-value-2 card to hand — The Meathook Massacre and Journey to Nowhere are both in range.
  2. Hold Counterspell over casting a fourth enchantment on the turn the combo seat untaps.
  3. Cast Rhystic Study before the combo seat's turn and make them pay {1} on every piece.
  4. Tell the table which card you are holding up. A pod that knows the counterspell exists spends a turn playing around it, and a turn is what this deck needs.
NOTEThis deck contains no two-card infinite line of its own — deck-facts reports zero contained combos and zero two-card infinites in the 99, and a test enforces it. Say so at the table; it is true, and it is why the pod should be pointing its answers elsewhere.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the land drops have stopped, or the colour you need is the one you do not have

Indications.

  • You have missed a land drop — the model misses one by turn five in 41% of games, and its land-drop hit rate falls to 0.64 by turn ten (diagnostic.json, 10,000 seeded iterations)
  • You hold a double-black card — Enduring Tenacity {2}{B}{B}, Nighthowler {1}{B}{B}, Phyrexian Arena {1}{B}{B} — and one black source
  • You hold Zur and are missing one of {W}, {U} or {B}
  • Darkwater Catacombs or Talon Gates of Madara is your only source of a colour
  • You have five or more cards in hand and cast one spell last turn

Immediate action.

  1. Play the painland untapped and pay the life. Caves of Koilos, City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Underground River and Spire of Industry each cost 1 life a tap, and lifelink pays it back.
  2. Crack Field of Ruin for {2}: destroy an opponent's nonbasic and search a basic onto the battlefield in the colour you are short.
  3. If Land Tax is on the battlefield and any opponent controls more lands than you, take three basics and choose for the colour you are short — six Swamps, three Plains and three Islands are in the deck.
  4. Cast the rock before the enchantment: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Dominance.
  5. Spend down to {1}{W} rather than to zero. One animation is the cheapest body this deck makes.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Enlightened Tutor {W} for Land Tax or Mystic Remora rather than for a payoff you cannot cast; it puts the card on top, so cast it in an opponent's end step.
  2. Hold the double-black cards until you control two black sources. Black is the tight colour: 27 land sources against the 36 a 90% on-curve rate would take, and 0.718 on curve for a double-black cost once the two gated lands are excluded (mana_analysis.json).
  3. Cast Black Market Connections and take Sell Contraband every turn until the land drops resume.
  4. Play Urza's Saga and take chapter III for Sol Ring when it is still in the library.
NOTEDarkwater Catacombs and Talon Gates of Madara are counted as sources of three colours each and cannot pay a coloured cost on curve: both charge extra mana on top of the tap (mana_analysis.json, sources.gated).

3.5STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. it is turn six or later, Zur is not on the battlefield, and the enchantments you control are doing nothing but sitting there

Indications.

  • Turn six or later and Zur has not resolved
  • You control four or fewer enchantments, so Starfield of Nyx would animate nothing worth animating
  • You are drawing one card a turn: none of Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Phyrexian Arena, Black Market Connections, Entity Tracker or Enduring Curiosity is on the battlefield
  • Your board is enchantments you have never animated
  • You have passed two turns without attacking

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Zur ahead of anything else that costs three. Zur and Starfield of Nyx are the two cards that turn an enchantment you control into a body, and Starfield needs five enchantments on the battlefield first.
  2. Animate the largest non-Aura enchantment you control. Power equals mana value, so Sphere of Safety and Cathars' Crusade are 5/5s and Rhystic Study is a 3/3.
  3. Cast the draw engine ahead of the payoff. The model reaches a draw engine that keeps drawing by turn three in 68% of games, and names it the bottleneck (diagnostic.json).
  4. Cast Academy Rector and put it in front of the largest attacker. You want it to die: it fetches any enchantment onto the battlefield with no mana-value limit.
  5. Take Starfield of Nyx with Demonic Tutor once you control four enchantments. From the fifth onward it animates every other non-Aura enchantment for no mana.

Subsequent.

  1. Count your enchantments at the start of every turn and say the number out loud. Five is the line Starfield of Nyx turns on at.
  2. Cast the expensive enchantment over the cheap one when both are castable and you intend to attack.
  3. Hard-cast Bitterblossom, Dawn of Hope and Felidar Retreat to put bodies on the board while the enchantment count climbs.
  4. Fetch to the battlefield with Academy Rector rather than to hand with Enlightened Tutor when you are behind on board.
NOTEZur the Enchanter is a card in the 99 now, not the command zone. His attack trigger still fetches an enchantment of mana value 3 or less when he is on the battlefield, but he costs four and he is not the plan — do not build a turn around attacking with him.

3.6POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the pod has decided you are the problem, and the removal is coming to your seat

Indications.

  • Two seats have named Rhystic Study or Sphere of Safety out loud
  • Removal is being pointed at Zur rather than at anything on your board
  • A seat has stopped attacking you and is holding cards
  • Your drain triggers have taken one opponent below 20 while the others are near 40
  • Nobody has attacked the seat with the largest board for two turns

Immediate action.

  1. Spread the drain. Enduring Tenacity and Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose each pick a target per trigger, so take the two lowest totals down together rather than one to zero.
  2. Spend your next removal spell on somebody else's threat and name the seat before you cast it — Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Journey to Nowhere, Ossification and Sheltered by Ghosts all read as help.
  3. Cast Sphere of Safety. Each attacker must pay {X} for your enchantment count, so a table that has decided to kill you pays for it three times over.
  4. Animate only what you need to block. A standing animated board reads as a threat every turn; {1}{W} on an opponent's turn makes the same deathtouch blocker and shows nothing until it is needed.
  5. Tap Forbidden Orchard last and name the seat with the fewest attackers — every tap hands a named opponent a 1/1 Spirit.

Subsequent.

  1. Hold Starfield of Nyx until the turn you attack with what it animates.
  2. Take Sell Contraband on Black Market Connections rather than Hire a Mercenary while you are the target; a Treasure is quiet and a 3/2 body that costs you 3 life is not.
  3. Recast the drain payoff after the pod has moved on to another seat.
  4. Stop reminding the table about Rhystic Study once they are paying {1} every time. A tax the pod has learned is still a tax.
NOTEDeathtouch changes the arithmetic of a block, not its optics. An animated 3/3 Rhystic Study kills whatever it blocks, and the pod prices it as a 3/3 until the first time it does.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat is faster than you and your board will not stop it on the ground

Indications.

  • A seat is attacking for eight or more a turn and you are not blocking profitably
  • Your life total is below 25 and you have paid for lands, Bitterblossom, Phyrexian Arena and Black Market Connections
  • You control no lifelink body: Zur has not resolved, or every enchantment creature you own is still in hand
  • Sphere of Safety is in the library and you control fewer than eight enchantments
  • The racing seat has more power on the battlefield than you and untaps first

Immediate action.

  1. Animate a blocker at instant speed. {1}{W} on any non-Aura enchantment during their turn makes a deathtouch body they did not declare around.
  2. Block with it. Deathtouch makes the trade theirs to lose, and lifelink turns the damage it deals into life.
  3. Cast Sphere of Safety. Animating an enchantment does not remove it from Sphere's count — it is a creature in addition to its other types — so the tax holds while you make blockers.
  4. Turn the block into a drain: with Enduring Tenacity or Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose on the battlefield, the life you gain comes off the racing seat's total.
  5. Cast The Meathook Massacre with X large enough to clear their board. Every creature you lose to it drains each opponent for 1.

Subsequent.

  1. Chump-block with Academy Rector and fetch Sphere of Safety onto the battlefield off the death trigger.
  2. Cast Bitterblossom and chump with a flying Faerie every upkeep.
  3. Gain life back with Honden of Cleansing Fire, Underworld Coinsmith and Daxos, Blessed by the Sun, and stop paying life for lands the turn you go below 15.
  4. Race back rather than stabilise once your board power passes theirs; the model puts board power at 22.09 by turn ten with combat modelled (goldfish_metrics.json, 10,000 seeded games).
NOTEThe Enduring cycle pays for a chump block. Enduring Tenacity, Enduring Innocence and Enduring Curiosity each return to the battlefield as an enchantment when they die as a creature, and {1}{W} makes one a creature again — the body is spent, the text is not.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Three lands with a white source among them: {1}{W} is the animation cost, and white carries 42 pips across 36 cards (mana_analysis.json)
  • Two lands plus Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or Talisman of Dominance
  • {W}, {U} and {B} all reachable by turn three, so Zur is castable on curve
  • A play on turn one or turn two: Land Tax, Mystic Remora, Hypnotic Siren, Sol Ring, Bitterblossom, Northern Air Temple, Sanctum of Stone Fangs, Daxos, Blessed by the Sun or Inquisitive Glimmer
  • Two black sources if the hand holds two or more black cards — black is the tight colour at 27 land sources

Ship on sight.

  • One land, whatever else is in it
  • No white source with three or more white cards
  • Nothing castable before turn four
  • Five lands and two spells that both cost four or more
  • Zur plus five cards that all cost four or more
NOTEGemstone Caverns is a land you have already played when you are not the starting player: begin the game with it on the battlefield with a luck counter and exile a card from your hand. Count it as a land, and as any colour, before you decide.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 80% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Play the land that makes black first when the hand holds a black two-drop. Black is the tight colour: 27 land sources against the 36 a 90% on-curve rate would take.
  2. Turn one, cast Land Tax, Mystic Remora, Hypnotic Siren or Sol Ring, in that order of preference.
  3. Turn two, cast the two-mana enchantment that draws or drains over the one that makes a body: Dawn of Hope, Sanctum of Stone Fangs, Bitterblossom, Journey to Nowhere.
  4. Turn three, cast Zur when you already control two or more enchantments. Otherwise cast the three-mana draw engine and cast Zur on turn four.
  5. Pay Mystic Remora's cumulative upkeep while it costs {1} or {2}. Let it go the turn it costs {3}.
NOTEThis deck pays life to run: five lands cost 1 life a tap and the three shocklands cost 2 on entry, and Bitterblossom, Phyrexian Arena and Black Market Connections each bill you every turn. Lifelink is what pays it back, so every point you spend before Zur lands is spent on credit.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Land a draw engine first: Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Phyrexian Arena, Black Market Connections, Entity Tracker, Tocasia's Welcome or Caretaker's Talent.
  2. Cast Zur second. Until he is on the battlefield the 16 enchantment creatures in the 99 are plain bodies with no deathtouch, no lifelink and no hexproof.
  3. Cast a drain payoff third — Enduring Tenacity, Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Marauding Blight-Priest — so that lifelinked combat damage takes life off a named opponent as well.
  4. Count to five enchantments, then cast Starfield of Nyx. From the fifth onward every other non-Aura enchantment you control is a creature with power equal to its mana value, for no mana.
  5. Cast Academy Rector last and put it where it dies. It fetches any enchantment onto the battlefield with no mana-value limit; Starfield of Nyx and Sphere of Safety are the two that change a board.
NOTEMana value is a virtue here, not a cost. Power equals mana value under both Zur and Starfield of Nyx, so the five-drop is the 5/5 and the one-drop is the 1/1. Cast the expensive enchantment over the cheap one whenever both fit and you intend to attack.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Upkeep: pay Mystic Remora or let it go, then return an enchantment from your graveyard with Starfield of Nyx.
  2. Make the land drop. Take the painland untapped over the tapland; lifelink gives the life back.
  3. Count your enchantments. Below five, cast the cheapest enchantment that raises the count. At five or more with Starfield of Nyx out, cast the most expensive one you can afford.
  4. Before declaring attackers, spend {1}{W} per animation, largest mana value first.
  5. Attack with every enchantment creature. Deathtouch makes each block a trade the opponent loses; lifelink turns the damage that connects into a drain through Enduring Tenacity or Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose.
  6. Pass with {1}{W} open. Animation has no timing restriction, so it makes a deathtouch blocker on an opponent's turn.
NOTENever animate Urza's Saga: its mana value is 0, so it becomes a 0/0 and dies on the spot. Starfield of Nyx does it for you — it animates each other non-Aura enchantment you control, the Saga included — so resolve Starfield only after the Saga has taken chapter III, or accept losing it.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Total the mana values of every non-Aura enchantment you can afford to animate, and add the power already on the battlefield.
  2. Add the drain. With Enduring Tenacity or Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose on the battlefield, every point of lifelinked damage that connects is a matching point off one opponent you name.
  3. Animate everything you intend to attack with before you declare attackers. A body animated after blockers cannot attack.
  4. Name a target for each trigger. Enduring Tenacity and Vito pick an opponent per lifegain event, so split the drain across two seats or focus one.
  5. Wait a turn when Sphere of Safety is not on the battlefield and the crack-back is lethal.
NOTEEvery speed figure in this book is the goldfish's: 10,000 seeded games against one opponent at 40 life who does not block and does not remove. It is the model's clock, not an expectation, and its combat figures have run 4-5x ahead of Forge on this deck. No Forge run exists for this commander at all — every simulated game on this list was played with Zur the Enchanter in the command zone.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (26 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Zur, Eternal Schemer costs three and reads as an unanswerable board the moment the table reads hexproof. A pod that cannot target you reaches for a sweeper instead — play as though a wipe is in every hand from the turn he lands.
  • Rhystic Study taxes every opponent on every spell. Expect it named and expect it answered.
  • Sphere of Safety is public arithmetic: each attacker pays {X} for your enchantment count, and the table can count your enchantments.
  • Forbidden Orchard hands a named opponent a 1/1 Spirit on every tap. Tap it last.
  • Enduring Tenacity and Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose take life off an opponent you name every time you gain any. The seat you name is the seat that comes for you.
  • An animated Rhystic Study is a 3/3 that also draws you cards. The pod prices the body and forgets the text.

7.2When to reveal

  • Cast the draw engine before the drain payoff. Rhystic Study and Phyrexian Arena read as value; Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose reads as a clock.
  • Hold Starfield of Nyx until the turn you can animate and attack. It costs nothing once you control five enchantments and it changes the whole board in one step.
  • Animate on an opponent's turn rather than in your own main phase while you are ahead. A body that appears during combat is a blocker; a body that stands there all round is a threat.
  • Cast Sphere of Safety the turn before you expect to be attacked, not the turn you can first afford it.
  • Keep the Enduring cycle's return trigger to yourself. A pod that has watched Enduring Tenacity come back once stops trading with it.

7.3Alliances

  • Say the deck runs no infinite combo. Deck-facts reports zero contained combos and zero two-card infinites in the 99, and a test enforces it.
  • Cast Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile on the table's fastest board, on their turn, so the seat you helped sees it happen.
  • Point the drain at the seat with the most life and tell the table you are doing it.
  • Spend a counterspell on the spell that beats three seats before the one that beats you.
  • Offer Imprisoned in the Moon on the commander the whole table is afraid of; it stays on the battlefield as a land and never returns to the command zone.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Attack the seat whose blockers are largest. Deathtouch means a bigger blocker trades for the same body a 1/1 would.
  • Leave the seat whose only interaction is targeted removal until last — hexproof blanks it, so that seat costs you the least while it is alive.
  • Kill the seat you have been draining. Enduring Tenacity and Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose have already done half the work on whoever you named.
  • Hit the seat holding the sweeper first when you control Starfield of Nyx; under it your enchantments die with your creatures.
  • Split attackers across two seats once your board power passes 15. Every lifelinked hit is a drain trigger, and each trigger picks its own opponent.
