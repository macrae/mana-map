The Plan

What this deck is trying to do, and what it is assuming away. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

The table counts the combo sheet in your colours and treats you like a fuse. Here is the truer read, and it is better news: Hapatra is a mint, not a source. Her combat trigger rides an unevasive 2/2 and is worth at most one extra counter per swing, so stop building turns around it. The half you actually play is the payout — every time you put -1/-1 counters on a creature she hands you a 1/1 deathtouch Snake.

So hunt sources, not payoffs. Eight sacrifice outlets in this deck cost no mana and exactly one of them, Yawgmoth, Thran Physician, also places a counter. You draw a tutor in 78.0% of games — point the first one at a repeatable source and never at a drain. Blowfly Infestation, Serrated Arrows, Contagion Clasp and Black Sun's Zenith are the same job on different bodies; cast whichever one this turn's mana can afford.

Most games you win by out-carding three people and then draining them, and that game assembles 67.2% of the time at mean turn 3.62. Pace for it. Clamp a Snake for two cards, cash a spare body to Deadly Dispute or Village Rites at instant speed, and let Grim Haruspex collect your creatures while Morbid Opportunist collects everyone else's. Every Snake you leave home blanks an attacker of any size, so the board that draws you cards is the board nobody wants to attack into.

The kill is one repeatable source plus one converter that hits all three seats, and you hold a converter in 51.9% of games. When the second half arrives, count the table's spent interaction and go — prefer Obelisk Spider, which drains on counter placement rather than on death and so pays four times per Druid cycle. Blood Artist names one player per death: take the value, do not call it a clock. One standing constraint on all of it — nothing in the 99 destroys an artifact, an enchantment or a land, so play around resolved permanents rather than planning to answer them.

What this does not model

  • Unsettled Is Hapatra + Blowfly Infestation actually a two-card kill? Blowfly triggers only when a creature dies that HAD a -1/-1 counter on it, and stack 001 step 11 establishes Hapatra's own placement is a combat-damage trigger — so the pair may need a connected attack or a third card to place the first counter, and it may need a 1-toughness body to keep dying.
  • Unsettled Does Host of the Hereafter loop with Hapatra the way Blowfly Infestation is claimed to? Its trigger moves the counters off a dying creature onto up to one target creature you control, which Hapatra would see as a placement; Commander Spellbook lists the pair as infinite death triggers.
  • Unsettled Is Scurry Oak + Ivy Lane Denizen a real infinite in this deck, and does it need a third card to be a win?
  • Simulation Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).

6 further questions about this engine are open and unsettled.

Keep or Ship

What a keepable hand looks like, and how often you get one. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

Everyone keeps the hand with Nest of Scarabs and Blood Artist in it. Do not be everyone. Those cards are exchange rates, and an exchange rate with nothing to exchange is a blank until the engine exists.

You can afford to be strict, because the deck is forgiving. You keep the first seven 79.9% of the time, average 0.243 mulligans, and put Hapatra on the table on turn two in 9,970 of 10,000 runs.

Keep on sight: two to four lands casting Hapatra on turn two, plus any of Yawgmoth, Devoted Druid, Blowfly Infestation or a tutor. Keep the two-lander with Birds of Paradise, Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, Sol Ring, Lotus Petal or Dark Ritual attached — that hand casts the commander a turn early with a spell left over. Keep Skullclamp beside any token maker. Keep Bitterblossom alone; it makes a body every upkeep and rebuilds through a wrath by itself.

Ship the five-lander with no tutor and no dork. Four zero-drops, sixteen ones and nineteen twos means that hand is holding two cards it will never spend. Ship converters with no source — Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat and Bastion of Remembrance are the classic trap keep. Ship hands built on Mikaeus alone; he is your only six-drop, he places no counters, and he needs a named partner.

Treat Scurry Oak, Ivy Lane Denizen and Host of the Hereafter as bodies rather than routes — the lines they are famous for are unsettled here. On the way to six, compress the test to two things: black mana on turn two, and one card that either places counters repeatedly or fetches one that does.

The Lines

How it actually wins, resolved and checked against the rules. ✓ RULES-VERIFIED

Does Hapatra's -1/-1 counter reset undying under Mikaeus?

The clause that decides this is undying's intervening “if”: return it “if it had no +1/+1 counters on it” (CR 702.93a). Undying hands it back wearing exactly one; the off-switch is inside the ability.

The claim was that Hapatra supplies the reset. Annihilation is automatic (CR 704.5q); her placement is a combat-damage trigger (CR 113.3c) that cannot be interleaved between sacrifices. Holding: the claim fails as written. A token yields zero iterations, a nontoken one — disposing of Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, Viscera Seer, Carrion Feeder, Blasting Station and Yahenni, Undying Partisan.

Mikaeus with Devoted Druid is unbounded in deaths, counters, Snakes and drain — not in mana (CR 302.6). It assembles in 2.5% unassisted, 31.7% with a tutor, mean turn 5.69. Walking Ballista with Mikaeus is unbounded as a three-card assembly (CR 613.4c). Yawgmoth loops, bounded by the library his own draw empties (CR 104.3c).

The combo sheet claims nineteen two-card infinites in these colours. This record verifies one, downgrades a second, refutes six, and on the remaining eleven it is silent.

Sub-question 2 — counter annihilation is a state-based action.
As soon as a player would receive priority, if a permanent has both a +1/+1 and a -1/-1 counter, N of each are removed. One of each leaves zero. There is no window in which the creature holds both while a player has priority.

Step 8 of 16
Sub-question 3 — annihilation does reset undying, with one sharp exception.
If the annihilation happened in an earlier SBA check and the creature survived, then at its later death it genuinely has zero +1/+1 counters and undying triggers. THE TRAP: if the -1/-1 counters both annihilate and take toughness to 0 in the SAME check, last known information is read from before any of those SBAs — the +1/+1 counter is still visible and undying does NOT trigger. The CR's own example is exactly this case. Strip the counter first, let SBAs run, kill it separately.

SUB-QUESTION 4, part C-bis — Yawgmoth, Thran Physician is one of the deck's EIGHT mana-free sacrifice outlets and the only one that is also a repeatable reset. It needs a SECOND body.

Mechanism — Devoted Druid's untap ability uses the stack, and its counter is a cost.
It cannot add mana, so it is an ordinary activated ability created on the stack. Its cost, the -1/-1 counter, is paid on activation, not resolution. On the activation that brings the Druid to 0 toughness the counter is already on it while the untap ability still sits on the stack, so the next SBA check puts the Druid in the graveyard before the untap resolves.
