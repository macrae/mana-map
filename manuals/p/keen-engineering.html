1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Sai in the command zone turns each of the deck's 43 artifact spells into a cast plus a 1/1 flying Thopter, so the board goes wide on its own; the game ends when an artifact-count scaler (Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry, Steel Overseer, Nettlecyst, Adaptive Omnitool — with Forsaken Monument's flat +2/+2 on the colourless ones) or a self-scaling body (Darksteel Juggernaut, Broodstar, Myr Battlesphere, Graaz, Kappa Cannoneer) turns that width into damage, while Sai's sacrifice-two-draw and the artifact draw suite keep the casts coming.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming, or has landed, and the board is Thopters and lords.

Indications.

  • An opponent in white or black with four or more mana untapped through your turn, having cast nothing for two turns.
  • Six or more artifacts on your board with Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry or Steel Overseer among them.
  • Your own Nevinyrral's Disk untapped with {1} open, and two opponents' boards wider than yours.
  • Hedron Archive, Mind Stone or Ichor Wellspring on the battlefield with mana to spare.

Immediate action.

  1. With the sweeper on the stack, activate Sai: {1}{U}, sacrifice two Thopters, draw a card. Repeat once for every {1}{U} you have open.
  2. Sacrifice Ichor Wellspring and Myr Retriever to Sai ahead of any Thopter. Wellspring draws a card as it leaves; Retriever's death returns an artifact card already in your graveyard to your hand.
  3. If the sweeper names artifacts, tap and sacrifice Mind Stone ({1}) and Hedron Archive ({2}) for cards before it resolves.
  4. Cast Counterspell ({U}{U}) or Negate ({1}{U}) at the sweeper only when Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry or Steel Overseer is on the board. A board of Thopters alone regrows from the command zone; let that one resolve and keep the counter.
  5. Put Sai in the command zone, not the graveyard, when he dies.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Sai before any other spell: {2}{U} plus {2} for each time he has already been cast from the command zone.
  2. Recast the cheap artifacts two to a turn. Lead with Myr Retriever and Ichor Wellspring; each cast is a Thopter again.
  3. Activate Buried Ruin ({2}, tap, sacrifice) to return the lord that died to your hand.
  4. Cast Nettlecyst, Adaptive Omnitool or Forsaken Monument before Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry or Steel Overseer. The equipment and the Monument are not creatures and the next creature sweeper leaves them.
  5. With Shimmer Myr on the battlefield, cast the artifacts on the end step of the player who sweeps, not in your own main phase.
NOTENevinyrral's Disk is your own sweeper and stack 003 (checker-passed) says what it leaves: Darksteel Juggernaut as a 2/2, Darksteel Citadel, and nothing else of yours — Sai goes back to the command zone, the Thopters cease to exist, and Padeem's hexproof stops none of it. All Is Dust is the other reset and it takes Sai, every blue creature, Propaganda, Fall from Favor, Thopter Spy Network and Nettlecyst's Germ with it, while every Thopter, Myr and colourless rock stays. Cast it on a turn your board is tokens and rocks and theirs is coloured. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. Sai, or the lord the board is counting on, is targeted.

Indications.

  • An opponent with two or more mana open during your turn in a colour that kills or bounces a creature.
  • Sai on the battlefield with four or more Thopters and no Padeem, Consul of Innovation.
  • Master of Etherium or Darksteel Juggernaut the largest creature on the table.
  • Aether Spellbomb or an untapped Master Transmuter on your battlefield with {U} spare.

Immediate action.

  1. If the target is an artifact and Master Transmuter is untapped: {U}, tap, return the targeted artifact to your hand, and put an artifact card from your hand onto the battlefield in its place.
  2. If the target is Sai and Aether Spellbomb is on the battlefield: {U}, sacrifice it, return Sai to your hand. A cast from hand pays no commander tax.
  3. Otherwise, with the spell on the stack, activate Sai: {1}{U}, sacrifice two artifacts, draw a card — once for every {1}{U} open.
  4. Cast Counterspell ({U}{U}) or Negate ({1}{U}) only when the target is Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry, Steel Overseer or Padeem, or Sai on his third cast from the command zone. Before that, let the spell at Sai resolve.
  5. Put Sai in the command zone, not the graveyard.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Sai before any other three-drop: {2}{U} plus {2} for each earlier cast from the command zone.
  2. Cast Padeem, Consul of Innovation ({3}{U}) before the next lord: every artifact you control has hexproof from then on.
  3. Cast Kappa Cannoneer ({5}{U}, improvise by tapping artifacts) as the next finisher; its ward {4} is paid on top of the next removal spell's cost.
  4. Return the dead lord with Buried Ruin ({2}, tap, sacrifice) or by sacrificing Myr Retriever to Sai on a turn the lord is in your graveyard.
  5. Hold Launch Mishap ({2}{U}) for the creature that would race the rebuilt board; it makes a Thopter as it counters.
NOTEPadeem gives hexproof to artifacts, and Sai is a Human Artificer: he is never covered. Nothing in the 99 returns him from the graveyard or lowers the tax, so each trip through the command zone costs {2} more than the last. Cast Padeem one turn before the lord, never the same turn after it. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

3.3COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. somebody at the table is going off, and the fliers cannot race it.

Indications.

  • An opponent casting a tutor and passing the turn with mana open.
  • Two named pieces of a known line on one opponent's battlefield, or one on the battlefield and the seat holding cards.
  • A graveyard across the table with ten or more cards and a reanimation deck behind it.
  • Counterspell, Negate or Launch Mishap in your hand with fewer than {U}{U} untapped.

Immediate action.

  1. From the turn a tutor resolves, pass with {U}{U} untapped on every opponent's turn. Stop casting artifacts into that mana.
  2. Counter the piece cast last, not the first: Counterspell ({U}{U}) stops any spell, Negate ({1}{U}) a noncreature spell, Launch Mishap ({2}{U}) a creature or planeswalker spell.
  3. If the line is already on the battlefield, sacrifice Aether Spellbomb ({U}) to return the creature half to its owner's hand.
  4. Activate Master Transmuter ({U}, tap, return a Thopter to hand) to put Duplicant or Meteor Golem onto the battlefield in response: Duplicant exiles a nontoken creature on entry, Meteor Golem destroys a nonland permanent an opponent controls.
  5. Activate Nevinyrral's Disk ({1}, tap) when the line is made of artifacts, creatures or enchantments and nothing above answers it.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Soul-Guide Lantern ({1}) and exile the combo player's best graveyard card on entry; against recursion, tap and sacrifice it to exile every opponent's graveyard before the line starts.
  2. Keep one counter in hand and {U}{U} open on every turn after; spend the rest of the mana on artifacts.
  3. Attack the combo player and nobody else with every flier that connects; Thopter Spy Network and Research Thief draw a card when one does.
  4. Say which seat you countered and which piece, so the table's remaining answers stay pointed there.
NOTENegate does not stop a creature and Launch Mishap does not stop a noncreature; of the three counters, Counterspell is the one that stops both, and there is one copy. Nevinyrral's Disk enters tapped and answers nothing the turn it is cast. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the land drops have stopped, or the hand is seven-drops and the board cannot pay for them.

Indications.

  • No land drop on turn three or turn four.
  • Three or fewer lands on the battlefield on turn five.
  • Broodstar, Graaz, Myr Battlesphere, Meteor Golem or Duplicant in hand and fewer than four artifacts on the board.
  • Lonely Sandbar or Remote Isle in hand.

Immediate action.

  1. Play Lonely Sandbar or Remote Isle as the land drop. Do not cycle a land while you are short of lands.
  2. Cast Sai ({2}{U}), then the cheapest artifact in hand each turn: Sol Ring, Aether Spellbomb and Soul-Guide Lantern at {1}; Mind Stone, Thought Vessel, Arcane Signet, Silver Myr, Ornithopter of Paradise, Myr Retriever, Ichor Wellspring, Mazemind Tome, Steel Overseer and Adaptive Omnitool at {2}. Each one raises the artifact count and makes a Thopter.
  3. Cast Etherium Sculptor ({1}{U}) or Foundry Inspector ({3}) before any artifact that costs three or more.
  4. Count the artifacts you control. At four, Thoughtcast costs {U} and Memory Guardian {U}; at six, Thought Monitor costs {U} and draws two. Cast the one the count has paid for.
  5. At the end of each turn, spend the spare mana on a card: Mazemind Tome ({2}, tap, page counter), Aether Spellbomb ({1}, sacrifice), Sai ({1}{U}, sacrifice two Thopters).

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Thirst for Knowledge ({2}{U}) on an opponent's end step and discard the artifact in hand with the highest mana value. Keep every land.
  2. Cast Master Transmuter ({3}{U}) once four lands are out. From then on Myr Battlesphere, Meteor Golem, Duplicant and Graaz arrive for {U} and a returned Thopter.
  3. Sacrifice Mind Stone ({1}, tap) or Hedron Archive ({2}, tap) for cards only after the fifth land.
  4. Hold Forsaken Monument, All Is Dust and the eight-drops. Do not discard a land to Thirst for Knowledge or Pull from Tomorrow.
NOTEForty lands and nine rocks, and the goldfish still misses a land drop by turn five in 31.5% of games, because the deck draws 0.49 extra cards by turn four. The one that bites: Sai's draw eats the artifacts the affinity cards count. Sacrifice Thopters to him, never a rock. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

3.5STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. turn six or later, Sai on the battlefield, the board is 1/1 fliers and no lord or finisher has arrived.

Indications.

  • Six or more Thopters and none of Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry, Steel Overseer, Nettlecyst, Adaptive Omnitool or Forsaken Monument on the battlefield.
  • The table's life totals unchanged for two turns.
  • Darksteel Juggernaut, Broodstar or Kappa Cannoneer in hand with eight or more artifacts on your board.
  • Whirler Rogue on the battlefield with two untapped Thopters beside it.

Immediate action.

  1. Attack with every Thopter at the seat with no fliers and no reach. A 1/1 flier that connects draws a card with Thopter Spy Network or Research Thief out.
  2. Sacrifice two Thopters to Sai ({1}{U}) at the end of every turn you have the mana spare. Draw into the lord.
  3. Equip Nettlecyst ({2}) or Adaptive Omnitool ({3}) to a Thopter: it gets +1/+1 for each artifact you control and keeps flying.
  4. Cast Darksteel Juggernaut ({5}) or Broodstar ({8}{U}{U}, {U}{U} at eight artifacts): each is the artifact count in one body.
  5. Activate Whirler Rogue: tap two Thopters, and the biggest body cannot be blocked this turn. Thopters made this turn pay the cost (stack 002).

Subsequent.

  1. Pay for cards each end step: War Room ({3}, tap, one life), Tamiyo's Logbook ({5}{U}, tap, {1} less for each other artifact — {U} at five), Mazemind Tome ({2}, tap).
  2. Cast Shimmer Dragon ({4}{U}{U}): tap two Thopters, draw a card, at instant speed, every turn.
  3. Cast Thopter Spy Network ({2}{U}{U}): a Thopter at every upkeep and a card for every connecting attack.
  4. Activate Foundry of the Consuls ({5}, tap, sacrifice) for two Thopters on a turn the mana has nothing else to do.
NOTEThe connector half of the kill — Chief of the Foundry, Master of Etherium, Steel Overseer, Nettlecyst, Adaptive Omnitool, Whirler Rogue — is in 36.4% of openers, the thinnest component the goldfish counts. Darksteel Juggernaut attacks each combat if able: cast it on a turn the attack is one you want. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

3.6POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the Thopter board is the widest at the table and three seats are looking at it.

Indications.

  • Two opponents attacking you in the same turn cycle.
  • Removal spent on a Thopter or on Sai while a bigger threat sits across the table.
  • You are the monarch through Fall from Favor with ten or more power pointed at you.
  • Propaganda in hand and not on the battlefield with eight or more Thopters out.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Propaganda ({2}{U}) before the next Thopter: each creature attacking you costs its controller {2}.
  2. Hold {3}{U} through the attack. After attackers are declared and before blockers, cast Aetherize: every attacking creature returns to its owner's hand.
  3. Flash in Misleading Signpost ({2}{U}) during the declare attackers step and point the largest attacker at another player.
  4. Chump-block with Thopters only the attacker that would kill you; let the rest through and keep the artifact count.
  5. Say the board out loud as it is — 1/1 fliers and a 1/4 — and name the seat with the fastest clock and its damage per turn.

Subsequent.

  1. Attack only the seat that attacked you last turn. The two that did not are the two you need next turn.
  2. Cast Fall from Favor ({2}{U}) on the largest creature of the seat that cannot pay Propaganda; it stays tapped while you are the monarch.
  3. Keep Nevinyrral's Disk untapped on the battlefield and name it: stack 003 says it leaves you Darksteel Juggernaut and Darksteel Citadel and leaves them nothing.
  4. Hold Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry and Graaz in hand until the turn the attack is lethal; cast them on that turn and no earlier.
NOTEFall from Favor makes you the monarch, and the monarch is attacked from all three seats; cast it only with Propaganda already on the battlefield. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat is dealing fifteen or more a turn before your artifact count is six.

Indications.

  • An opponent's board at fifteen or more power by turn four.
  • Your life under 25 before turn five.
  • A commander with equipment or auras attached, swinging alone.
  • Fewer than two untapped Thopters at the end of your turn.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Propaganda ({2}{U}) ahead of Sai when both are in hand and the racer has four or more attackers; with fewer, Sai first.
  2. Keep {3}{U} open on the racer's turn. After three or more attackers are declared at you, cast Aetherize: every attacker returns to hand, and every token attacker ceases to exist.
  3. Block each attacker that matters with a Thopter. After blockers are declared, sacrifice the blocking Thopters to Sai ({1}{U}, two at a time): the attacker stays blocked and you draw.
  4. Cast Fall from Favor ({2}{U}) on the racer's largest attacker: it taps and does not untap while you are the monarch.
  5. Cast Skysovereign, Consul Flagship ({5}): 3 damage to the racer's best creature on entry, and 3 more each time it attacks with crew 3.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Launch Mishap ({2}{U}) at the racer's next creature spell; the Thopter it makes is a blocker.
  2. Attack the racer and nobody else with every flier. The other two seats are not racing you.
  3. Once Steel Hellkite deals combat damage to the racer, pay {X} with X equal to the mana value of their equipment or token maker; pay {0} to destroy every token they control that is not a copy.
  4. Cast Duplicant ({6}) exiling the racer's commander. It comes back only through the command zone, at a tax.
NOTEAetherize costs {3}{U} held through the racer's whole turn, and the goldfish reads the deck's interaction castable on turn six in 18.8% of games because it spends the mana on the board. Hold the mana on purpose; the Thopters you did not cast that turn are the ones Aetherize saves. Untested at a table: no game has been logged with this deck.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two to four lands, one of them an Island that enters untapped.
  • One artifact at mana value two or less: Sol Ring, Aether Spellbomb, Soul-Guide Lantern, Arcane Signet, Mind Stone, Thought Vessel, Silver Myr, Ornithopter of Paradise, Steel Overseer, Myr Retriever, Ichor Wellspring, Mazemind Tome, Adaptive Omnitool or Etherium Sculptor.
  • A second artifact spell castable by turn four from the cards in hand.
  • Etherium Sculptor or Foundry Inspector: with one on the battlefield every two-mana artifact costs {1}.
  • Sol Ring with two lands: Sai on turn two.

Ship on sight.

  • One land, or six or more.
  • A hand whose artifacts all cost five or more: Forsaken Monument, Darksteel Juggernaut, Psychosis Crawler, Mind's Eye, Skysovereign, Duplicant, Steel Hellkite, Meteor Golem, Myr Battlesphere, Graaz, Kappa Cannoneer, Thought Monitor, Broodstar.
  • Three lands and four answers with no artifact: Counterspell, Negate, Launch Mishap, Aetherize, Propaganda, Fall from Favor are the cards that make that hand.
  • Lonely Sandbar and Remote Isle as two of three lands: both enter tapped and Sai slips to turn four.
NOTEMulligan to the fuel, not to a named card. The goldfish keeps 84.1% of first sevens, 88.7% of kept hands can act by turn three, and Sai is cast on turn three in 8,968 of 10,000 games off the land count alone. The affinity cards — Thoughtcast, Memory Guardian, Thought Monitor, Broodstar — read as uncastable in an opener and cost {U} or {U}{U} by turn five on a normal board; keep them behind two cheap artifacts, never instead of them. Spire Golem's affinity reads Islands, not artifacts.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 84% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Turn one: play an untapped Island and cast Sol Ring if you have it. Hold Aether Spellbomb and Soul-Guide Lantern for a turn Sai is on the battlefield.
  2. With Sol Ring out, cast Sai on turn two. Without it, cast a rock or Etherium Sculptor on turn two: Arcane Signet, Mind Stone, Thought Vessel, Silver Myr or Ornithopter of Paradise.
  3. Turn three: cast Sai ({2}{U}) before any other spell that turn. Everything cast after him is a Thopter.
  4. With the mana left after Sai, cast the cheapest artifact in hand.
  5. Play Lonely Sandbar or Remote Isle on a turn the tapped land costs nothing: turn one with no one-drop, or the turn after Sai.
NOTEThe goldfish casts Sai on turn two in 739 of 10,000 games and on turn three in 8,968, and it holds nothing back. A rock cast before Sai is mana; the same rock cast after him is mana and a body. Sol Ring is the one that does not wait.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Sai first. No stage below runs without him.
  2. A cost reducer second: Etherium Sculptor ({1}{U}) or Foundry Inspector ({3}). With one out, Ichor Wellspring, Soul-Guide Lantern, Aether Spellbomb and Mazemind Tome cost {0} or {1}.
  3. Vedalken Archmage ({2}{U}{U}) before the run of cheap artifacts, not after. Each artifact cast after him is a Thopter and a card.
  4. The cheap artifacts in a burst: two or three in one turn once a reducer is out, each one a Thopter.
  5. The lord on the turn you attack with the Thopters: Master of Etherium ({2}{U}), Chief of the Foundry ({3}) or Steel Overseer ({2}). Stack 001: the Thopter from the lord's own cast enters before the lord and counts for it.
  6. Padeem, Consul of Innovation ({3}{U}) once an artifact on the board is worth protecting. At your upkeep he draws a card if you control the artifact with the greatest mana value.
NOTEMaster Transmuter puts an artifact onto the battlefield. It is not cast, so Sai makes no Thopter for it and Vedalken Archmage draws nothing. Cheat in the seven-drops and up — Myr Battlesphere, Meteor Golem, Duplicant, Graaz — and cast everything cheaper.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Upkeep: take Thopter Spy Network's Thopter if you control an artifact, Padeem's card if you hold the largest artifact, and Scrawling Crawler's draw for every player.
  2. Play the land. An untapped Island first; hold Lonely Sandbar and Remote Isle to cycle once five lands are out.
  3. Count the artifacts you control. That number is Master of Etherium, Darksteel Juggernaut and Broodstar's size, Nettlecyst's and Adaptive Omnitool's bonus, and the discount on Thoughtcast, Memory Guardian, Thought Monitor and Broodstar.
  4. Cast the artifacts, cheapest first. Each is a Thopter.
  5. Attack with the fliers at the seat with no fliers and no reach. A connecting artifact creature draws with Thopter Spy Network or Research Thief out.
  6. End of turn, sink the spare mana in this order: Mazemind Tome ({2}, tap), Tamiyo's Logbook ({5}{U}, tap, {1} less per other artifact), War Room ({3}, tap, one life), and Sai ({1}{U}, two artifacts) only when the hand is empty.
  7. Pass with {U}{U} open on any turn a counter is in hand.
NOTEActivate Steel Overseer on the end step before your turn, not in your main phase. The counters stay, and the Overseer untaps for a second activation on your own turn.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count the artifacts you control and the untapped fliers and reach on the seat you are attacking.
  2. Total the power that connects: each Thopter is 1, plus 1 for Master of Etherium and 1 for Chief of the Foundry on the battlefield, plus 1 per Steel Overseer activation, plus 2 under Forsaken Monument; add Darksteel Juggernaut or Broodstar at the artifact count.
  3. Pick the connector: Whirler Rogue taps two Thopters and the largest body cannot be blocked (stack 002); Myr Battlesphere taps X untapped Myr on attack for +X/+0 and X damage to the player it attacks.
  4. If the total is short by three or fewer, cast one more artifact first: a Thopter and one more on the count.
  5. If the total is a seat's life or more, attack that seat with everything that connects and nothing else.
  6. If it is short by more, pass with {U}{U} open and the lord in hand, and cast the lord on the turn you attack.
NOTEDarksteel Juggernaut attacks each combat if able, and Graaz makes every other creature you control a 5/3 Juggernaut that must attack — Sai and the Thopters included. Cast Graaz on the turn the attack is lethal and never the turn before. The goldfish's median kill is turn 10 with none of the Thopters counted; the number that decides is your count, not its figure.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (50 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent, Tocasia's Welcome with its mana-value gate), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAW (Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Morbid Opportunist, Midnight Reaper) fires on every death this model produces — the sacrifice sweep under `model_sacrifice`, the one-turn copies leaving, and the measured rate under `model_deaths` — honouring 'nontoken' and 'once each turn' (2026-10-19); a deck with no death channel on has no deaths, and the payoff is absent. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent) and attack-triggered draw outside `model_combat` are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw', 'you may pay 2 life' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one, and 15 death triggers.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • DISCOVER, always on. An instant or sorcery with 'Discover N' is cast like any spell (storm, magecraft and per-cast damage see it), and each resolution — plus one per Zada copy — walks to the first nonland card with mana value N or less and CASTS IT FREE when it has a free door: a ritual, a draw spell, a discover spell, a storm spell, a pump or token copy (with a board, under model_combat), or a body. Any other hit goes to HAND and is cast later at full cost — a floor. The rest go to the bottom in a random order. A permanent's discover (the Hidden lands, an ETB, a death) and discover X are not read.
  • CASCADE, always on, the card's OWN keyword only (grants are not read). Every cast — paid, free or a cascade hit's own — walks to the first nonland card with mana value STRICTLY LESS and casts it free through the same doors as Discover; a hit with no free door goes to the bottom with the rest, in a random order (a floor). Copies never cascade. A cascade card no other loop casts is cast late in the main phase for the cascade alone, so it adds nothing to that turn's storm count.
  • A FREE-CAST CHAIN IS CAPPED at FREE_CAST_DEPTH_LIMIT nested free casts (8); a hit past it takes the not-cast path. The depth reached and the refusals are reported in metrics.free_cast.
  • MAGECRAFT FIRES ON EVERY COPY ('cast or copy'): one event per Zada copy of a spell, at every copy site (draw, pump, untap, storm, discover), counted once per spell however many arms the spell has.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • RITUALS ARE CAST WHEN THEY ARE THE DIFFERENCE (2026-10-05): a spell that adds mana (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Battle Hymn, Brightstone Ritual, Inner Fire) is cast only if its net mana makes a card in hand, or the commander, castable this turn; its mana lasts the turn. Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will / Rousing Refrain read the OPPONENTS' state this model does not have, on two AUTHORED rates in config.py: GEYSER_TAPPED_SHARE (0.5 of their lands still tapped on our turn) and OPPONENT_HAND (4 cards, 7 on a turn a wheel refilled them). Rituals with an additional cost or restricted mana are not read.
  • Extra card draw is modelled ONLY behind `model_draw` — the assumption above names its channels. With the flag OFF it is one card per turn, always, which understates every deck by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs: conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list that hides, and `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled` names the cards whose draw still has no channel, and is ABSENT rather than empty on a deck where every drawer is read.
  • CREATURES TAP WHEN THEY ATTACK (CR 508.1f) and untap at the start of your turn, so an ADDITIONAL COMBAT PHASE is only worth something if something untaps the team. Before this the swing was computed once and multiplied by the phase count, which made an extra combat free damage from a board that had already attacked: ur-dragon's damage at turn ten fell 68.798 -> 59.499 when it was corrected. LANDS still enter untapped, always — that assumption is unchanged and is about lands, not creatures.
  • A COMMANDER THAT COPIES A SINGLE-TARGET SPELL across the board is modelled behind `model_commander_copy`, declared per deck and checked against the commander's own text. Zada, Hedron Grinder is the only card in the corpus with the ability. It needs no authored rate: the copy count IS the number of other creatures you control, which this model already measures.
  • WHAT A COPIED SPELL DOES is read per effect, and each is one turn unless said otherwise: its draw, a pump (power only — toughness is tracked but no creature blocks here), a PERMANENT +1/+1 counter, a Treasure it creates, double strike, doubled power, damage every creature deals to each opponent, an additional combat phase, and an untap. A spell whose effect is none of these still counts toward the spell count and is otherwise inert.
  • THE SPELL COUNT IS TRACKED, so STORM is modelled, as are MAGECRAFT (which fires on a cast OR a copy) and per-cast damage (which fires on a cast only — that difference is a rules fact, not a simplification). Before this there was no spells-cast-this-turn variable at all and storm was unreadable.
  • REPLACEMENTS (2026-10-11): a counter replacement (Lae'zel +1, Doubling Season x2) applies per counter EVENT, and a damage replacement that adds (Torbran +2) per damage INSTANCE, each added before any multiplier: each ping, each storm copy, each attacker per strike. An attacker's colours ride on its battlefield entry (cast creatures, commanders, and draw/discard-made tokens such as The Locust God's blue-red Insects); a token from any other maker is colourless here, an understatement.
  • OPPONENTS' CASTS (2026-10-11): each opponent casts 1.264 spells a turn, MEASURED from 137,717 Forge turns (sim/pod_behaviour); Rhystic Study's tax is paid at the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE. Mind's Eye pays from mana left untapped at the end of our turn, a floor.
  • SPELLS AT A PLAYER (2026-10-11): damage a spell deals to a player counts against the one opponent ("any target" included). A spell whose only read effect is that damage is cast at the end of the main phase with what is left; a choose-ONE card whose other mode is read keeps that mode. Only an "each player" wheel refills the opponent; "discard your hand" and "target player" (us) do not. A discard additional cost is paid.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN FIRES ONLY ON THE DEATHS THIS MODEL PRODUCES. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance, Vraan and Vein Ripper key on a creature DYING, and a death happens here in three places and nowhere else: a free sacrifice outlet converting tokens (`model_sacrifice`), a one-turn copy leaving at the end step, and the per-turn rate read off a Forge run (`model_deaths`). No blockers and no removal, so a deck with neither channel on has no deaths and the contribution is ABSENT, not zero; a deck whose kill runs through them is understated by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.
  • BLOOD: a Blood token is created on an ETB, a per-opponent ETB (three, the pod) or a spell's resolution; Blood made by CONNECTING in combat is read and NOT credited, because a blocker can prevent it and this model has no blockers. AT MOST ONE IS CRACKED PER TURN, before the casting loops, and only with a card in hand, since discarding is part of the cost. One a turn is an authored floor: a pilot holding three with mana to spare cracks more, so this understates. Cracking one is a DISCARD and a DRAW, which the commander payoffs below charge for, and it sacrifices an ARTIFACT, which the artifact-sacrifice payoffs charge for.
  • ACTIVATED DRAW: a draw you BUY -- '{1}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: Draw a card' -- is paid for out of whatever mana the casting loops did not want, and a cost that eats its own source fires once. Energy and {X} activation costs are REFUSED and named, never priced at zero.
  • DISCARD: a wheel discards the WHOLE hand, lands included, and is cast only when the hand holds at most three nonland cards or a discard payoff is on the battlefield; a loot pitches lands beyond the next drop first, then the most expensive nonland. A discarded card stays 'seen' for target assembly (assembly is 'drawn by', and it was). Wheel of Misfortune is excluded (conditional). Activated loots and rummages, cycling and madness are NOT modelled and are named in meta.draw_not_modelled. Discard- and draw-triggered payoffs pay a flat amount per event, outside the combat gate; +1/+1 counters they put on their own body are added to the swing while the commander is out, an approximation. Payoffs that DRAW on a draw are not read: an uncapped loop would draw the library and call it steam.
  • DISCARD: a wheel a PERMANENT carries is activated from the battlefield under the same hand-size gate as a wheel that is cast, paying its mana cost every time. It fires at the earliest on the turn AFTER the permanent lands, which is the summoning sickness of the tap in its cost, and a cost that sacrifices its own source fires once and takes the body off the board. An ability activated from the graveyard (Runehorn Hellkite) is a zone this model does not have and is refused rather than fired.
  • WHEEL TIMING (2026-10-21): the turn of every wheel is observed and decided on by nothing. A FINISHER is a wheel resolved with a COMMANDER on the battlefield and a hand the thin-hand clause alone would not have thrown away. A deck's pilot_policy.json may HOLD a wheel (`hold_until` on the wheel channel: event_payoffs, commanders_out) — an AND-gate on the rule above, so a threshold of zero changes nothing; the policy is stamped on the artifact as meta.pilot_policy. A `keep` rule in the same file is read on the hand the model kept and reported as a pass rate, never enforced.
  • DISCARD: THE OPPONENT'S HALF OF A WHEEL IS NOW COUNTED, AND ONLY THE TAX ON IT. A symmetrical wheel refills the opponent too, so `whenever an opponent draws a card` payoffs (Razorkin Needlehead, Scrawling Crawler, Mind's Eye, Consecrated Sphinx) fire on their draw step and on every card a wheel deals them. DAMAGE IS ONE SEAT, the same convention the damage pillar uses -- Brallin's '1 damage to EACH opponent' is counted once against a single 40-life opponent, which is the right per-seat clock. WHAT WE GAIN off their draws is paid by every opponent and scales with GOLDFISH_OPPONENTS (3): Faerie Mastermind's card on each opponent's second draw, Consecrated Sphinx's two per opponent draw, a Blood per opponent (2026-10-04; at one seat Hallcreeper -> Mastermind read -0.17 cards by T8, at three +0.62). WHAT IS STILL NOT MODELLED IS THE COST: the opponent draws those cards and never gets to use them, because this model has no opponent turn, no opponent board and no interaction. So the tax is priced and the risk it offsets is not. A trigger on an opponent CASTING (Rhystic Study) is a different family and stays unread.
  • TREASURE OFF THEIR DRAWS RESTS ON AN AUTHORED RATE. Smothering Tithe makes a Treasure per opponent draw unless that opponent pays {2}; how often a table pays is not measurable here, so TITHE_PAY_RATE (0.5, config.py) decides each draw. Every Tithe figure is conditional on it, and `try` says so when a list relies on it.
  • DISCARD: A WHEEL THAT DRAWS 'CARDS EQUAL TO THE GREATEST NUMBER A PLAYER DISCARDED' IS A FLOOR HERE, AND THE FLOOR IS LOW. This model has no opponents holding cards, so Jace's Archivist and Windfall draw what OUR hand held -- and the gate only lets them fire when our hand is thin, which is exactly the board on which a real table pays them seven. The figure is not corrected, because the correction would be an authored opponent hand size driving a headline; read it as a lower bound and settle the question in Forge.

6.1Mana

6.2Ignition

Sai, Master Thopterist is the first thing that has to happen: 'Whenever you cast an artifact spell, create a 1/1 colorless Thopter artifact creature token with flying.' Only two other cards in the 99 trigger on a cast at all — Vedalken Archmage draws on an artifact cast and Forsaken Monument gains 2 life on a colourless one — and neither makes a body, so until Sai is on the battlefield the deck is 43 artifact spells doing their own printed jobs. The goldfish casts him on mean turn 2.979 (median 3; 8,968 of 10,000 games on turn 3) and by turn 6 in 99.7% of games. From there every artifact cast is two artifacts on the board (the spell and the token), which is what the conversion stage counts and what the fodder stage feeds: his second ability, {1}{U} and sacrifice two artifacts, draws a card, so two Thopters are a card whenever the hand runs dry. The goldfish does NOT model either ability (goldfish_targets._model_note; model-coverage reads nothing dark because no corpus card shares the text), so every bodies and damage figure in this model is a floor that leaves out the Thopters. Sai is a creature, not an artifact: he dies to Nevinyrral's Disk and to All Is Dust, has no hexproof from Padeem, and the deck has no recursion that reaches the command zone, so each re-cast costs two more. The engine degrades rather than stops without him: 24 of the deck's creatures are artifact creatures, no scaler needs Thopters specifically (they count artifacts, artifact creatures or colourless creatures), and Thopter Spy Network and Thopter Fabricator make tokens on their own, so a Sai-less board still grows, just one body per cast instead of two.

6.4Fodder

The deck converts artifacts on the board into cards and into evasion: Sai's {1}{U} sacrifice two artifacts draws a card, Shimmer Dragon taps two untapped artifacts to draw, Whirler Rogue taps two to make a creature unblockable, Kappa Cannoneer taps them to improvise its cost, and the Vehicles crew with them. The resource eaten is mostly Thopter tokens, which are not cards and which the goldfish never creates. The four cards here are the ones whose job is to supply or to be that resource: Thopter Spy Network makes a Thopter at every upkeep with no cast at all, and draws when artifact creatures connect; Thopter Fabricator makes one whenever a second card is drawn in a turn (Archmage, Monitor, Thirst and Sai's own draw all reach that); Ichor Wellspring draws when it enters and again when it is sacrificed, so feeding it to Sai is two cards for {1}{U}; Myr Retriever returns another artifact card from the graveyard when it dies, so sacrificing it to Sai gets a spent Wellspring, Spellbomb or Mind Stone back to be cast again — and cast again means another Thopter. The goldfish reads mean bodies 2.567 by turn 6 and 6.729 by turn 10 with none of this modelled, which is why it is a floor and why this is the stage the map cannot see at all: nothing in these four texts says 'sacrifice'.

6.5Conversion

The engine proper: what turns a wide artifact board into power or into cards. Master of Etherium has power equal to artifacts you control and gives other artifact creatures +1/+1; Chief of the Foundry gives them +1/+1; Steel Overseer taps to put a +1/+1 counter on every artifact creature, Thopters included; Nettlecyst and Adaptive Omnitool make one creature +1/+1 per artifact (Omnitool also digs an artifact when it attacks); Forsaken Monument gives colourless creatures +2/+2 — every Thopter and Myr token and 17 of the deck's 24 artifact creatures, but not the seven blue ones (Sculptor, Master of Etherium, Kappa, Transmuter, Memory Guardian, Research Thief, Thought Monitor) — and adds one more {C} each time a permanent is tapped for {C}. Whirler Rogue brings two Thopters and taps two artifacts to make a creature unblockable, so the board becomes one connecting body; Shimmer Dragon taps two artifacts to draw and is hexproof at four artifacts; Master Transmuter bounces an artifact (a token will do) to put an artifact from hand onto the battlefield, which is how a seven- or eight-mana body arrives for {U} — but putting onto the battlefield is not casting, so Sai makes no Thopter for it. The goldfish reads the declared PAYOFF group (Chief, Master, Nettlecyst, Overseer, Omnitool, Monument, Padeem, Spy Network, Archmage) in 49.8% of openers and by turn 6 in 77.0% of games, mean turn 2.79; mean board power 4.939 by turn 6 and 13.704 by turn 10, with zero Thopters counted. Every card here except Rogue and the Dragon is itself an artifact spell. The three scalers phrase the same job three ways, and the map splits them across three cities on that phrasing.

6.7Protection

What keeps the board alive. Counterspell, Negate and Launch Mishap (which also makes a Thopter) are the three counters; Aetherize bounces every attacker; Propaganda taxes each attacker {2}; Misleading Signpost flashes in during declare attackers to point an attacker elsewhere and then taps for {U}. Padeem gives every artifact hexproof and draws each upkeep if you hold the biggest artifact — Sai himself is not an artifact and gets none of it. Shimmer Myr lets artifact spells be cast as though they had flash, which is the deck's answer to a sorcery-speed wrath: hold the artifacts, cast them on the end step, and Sai's Thopters arrive with counter mana still up. deck-audit reads protection 4 copies UNDER the 5-7 base target and notes Darksteel Citadel's indestructible as unclassified; the goldfish reads interaction in hand 79.5% on turn 6 but castable only 19.3% of the time that turn, the gap being mana spent on the board. The deck has no way to regrow Sai, so this stage protects the artifacts and leaves the commander to the tax.

6.8Win condition

How the game ends: a body whose size is the artifact count, or one that brings its own army. Darksteel Juggernaut (indestructible, power equal to artifacts, must attack) and Broodstar (affinity, flying, power equal to artifacts) are the board in one creature; Myr Battlesphere brings four Myr and taps X Myr on attack for +X/+0 and X damage to the defending player — Silver Myr, Palladium Myr, Myr Retriever and Shimmer Myr are Myr too; Graaz makes every other creature a 5/3 Juggernaut, so a Thopter swarm becomes 5-power flyers; Kappa Cannoneer grows and goes unblockable whenever it or another artifact you control enters, improvises off the board, and has ward {4}; Steel Hellkite flies, pumps for {2} and wipes a mana value off any player it hits. The goldfish reads the declared KILL group (eight bodies AND one of Chief, Master, Overseer, Nettlecyst, Omnitool, Rogue) assembled by turn 6 in 43.4% of games, 67.4% ever, mean turn 4.862; its six-card connector half is the thinnest component in the deck at 36.4% of openers. Combat without the Thopters: median kill turn 10, mean 9.508, 48.9% of games with no kill by turn 10, kill by turn 8 in 4.0% and by turn 9 in 21.0%; mean damage 11.498 by turn 10. These are floors — Sai's tokens are not in them — and the model has no blockers. Whether the kill is the wide board or the one body is the open question CONTEXT.md names; this model says the two are usually the same card: Juggernaut and Broodstar are sized by the artifact count, Battlesphere and Kappa by the bodies that enter, Graaz sizes the swarm instead of itself, and Steel Hellkite is the one fixed body.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Lead with rocks, Thopters and Propaganda. Cast Master of Etherium, Chief of the Foundry and Steel Overseer on the turn you attack with them, not before.
  • Say 'the precon, as printed' when asked. It is true, and it is the deck's cover.
  • Keep Nevinyrral's Disk untapped on the battlefield and name it once. A reset nobody wants triggered buys turns.
  • Hold Kappa Cannoneer, Broodstar and Graaz in hand until the artifact count makes them lethal. A 1/4 making 1/1s reads as the slow seat.

7.2When to reveal

  • Hold Aetherize until three or more creatures attack you.
  • Spend Launch Mishap first of the three counters; it makes a Thopter. Hold Counterspell for a sweeper or a combo piece, Negate for the same.
  • Cast Whirler Rogue on the turn you activate it, not the turn before.
  • Reveal All Is Dust only by casting it. With it in hand, keep your coloured permanents to Sai and one lord.

7.3Alliances

  • Offer the two resets as the table's: Nevinyrral's Disk and All Is Dust each clear a board nobody else can, and say which of yours each one leaves.
  • Say plainly that nothing in the deck answers a land, and that the graveyard is answered only by Soul-Guide Lantern; ask the seat that carries those to spend theirs.
  • Trade Fall from Favor on the archenemy's largest creature for a turn of not being attacked.
  • Take the deal that leaves Sai on the battlefield. Break it on the turn the fliers are lethal, and say so before you declare attackers.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Attack the seat with no fliers and no reach. 1/1 fliers connect where nothing blocks them.
  • Point Skysovereign's 3 damage at the mana creature or the lord, not at the largest body.
  • Cast Meteor Golem at the permanent that beats you — a lock piece, a combo piece, an enchantment nobody else can touch — not the biggest one.
  • Exile a commander with Duplicant: it returns only through the command zone, at a tax.
  • After Steel Hellkite connects, pay {X} at the mana value of the one permanent you need gone, and {0} for every token that is not a copy.
  • Cast Fall from Favor on a commander: it does not untap while its controller is not the monarch.
