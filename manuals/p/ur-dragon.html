The Plan

What this deck is trying to do, and what it is assuming away. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

They count to nine in your command zone, see five colours, and file you under durdle. Let them. It is the only free thing you get all game. Eminence works from the zone, so every other Dragon costs {1} less from your first upkeep.

So stop building turns around the commander: mean cast turn 8.967, 0.0% by turn six, and none at all inside ten turns in 8,634 of 10,000 games. Cast the discounted Dragon. Every time.

Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas and Terror of the Peaks pay for every Dragon entering after them, nothing for those already down. You hold one by turn six in 62.2% of games; play the early one into an empty board and be patient. Land Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm before your top end: with Panharmonicon, Terror of the Peaks and Dragon Tempest down, one Lathliss cast is 64 damage in twelve separately aimed instances (stack 002).

Aggravated Assault plus a mana dragon assembles in 3.6% of games by turn six — your ceiling, not your schedule. Never wait on it; hold the enchantment until the turn it kills someone. All three infinites run through it, and Scourge of the Throne, your only other extra-combat effect, works once a turn and only on the highest-life player.

Count real mana before you swing. Haven of the Spirit Dragon, Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon, Unclaimed Territory, Rivaz of the Claw and an attacking Klauth make spells-only mana, and an ability is not a spell — none of it pays Assault's {3}{R}{R}. Treasures do: bank Old Gnawbone, Goldspan Dragon, Cavern-Hoard Dragon, a dead Atsushi.

Early, be the value seat: ramp, fix, let a louder player eat the removal, hold protection. You have one by turn six in 66.0% of games — your signal to commit the wave. Then take a window. Hellkite Courser rents the commander out for a hasty attack, Sneak Attack drops Drakuseth for {R} (stack 005), Wrathful Red Dragon turns the next sweeper into a 39-point execution (stack 004).

Keep or Ship

What a keepable hand looks like, and how often you get one. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

Keep a seven with two to four lands and one piece of acceleration. Two lands and a seven-drop is not a keep, however good it looks.

The acceleration that decides games is a cost reducer. Ramp or fixing arrives by turn six in 71.6% of games; a reducer in 33.2%. When Dragonspeaker Shaman, Urza's Incubator or Herald's Horn is in your opener, stop reading the hand and keep it. Reducer plus ramp — the hand that casts a turn-five Dragon nobody expected — arrives by turn six 23.1% of the time. Never ship one hunting for prettier Dragons.

Want a payoff before your first big body: Dragon Tempest, Garruk's Uprising, Roiling Dragonstorm, Korlessa. One is drawn by turn six 62.2% of the time; on turn two, you beat that number instead of matching it.

Never mulligan toward the commander. Eminence works from the command zone, so a hand that can eventually cast him is worth no more than one that cannot.

Ship two shapes on sight. Lands plus top end with nothing to do on turn three — land drops fall to 60.3% on turn five and 49.7% on turn six, so the bridge is not coming to you. And hands whose colours cannot cast their own cards: black fails first, 11 land sources and a 26.1% on-curve rate, and Haven, Maelstrom, Unclaimed Territory, Rivaz and Klauth only ever pay for spells.

Aggravated Assault in an opener is a hold, not a keep: it needs a body, funding and a turn where it kills somebody, and on turn three it only announces you. Protection is a genuine keep-bonus: Teferi's Protection, Heroic Intervention, Flawless Maneuver let you commit the board later.

Trust your sevens: the simulator keeps 75.1% of first hands at 0.313 mean mulligans. Mulligan for function, never for glamour.

Dragon Tempest + Ancient Gold Dragon

the d20 damage burst

The clause that decides this is one word inside Miirym's trigger: nontoken. Fifteen Faerie Dragons arrive on a single instruction and not one of them qualifies, so she copies nothing at all. Dragon Tempest carries no such reservation, and CR 603.2c splits one event with fifteen occurrences into fifteen separate triggers — each choosing its target as it goes on the stack (CR 115.1d), each recounting your Dragons at its own resolution (CR 608.2h). The holding: seventeen damage, fifteen times, with priority passing between every instalment. 255 available; the 30-life opponent is dead on the second trigger aimed at them, and anything still pointed at that player afterwards is countered on resolution (CR 608.2b).

Miirym under Panharmonicon

how many copies?

Panharmonicon does not read the trigger. Its clause asks only what caused the trigger — an artifact or creature entering — so Miirym's 'nontoken Dragon' and Terror of the Peaks's 'another creature' are treated exactly alike, and each triggers an additional time (CR 603.2d). What keeps the cascade finite is the same word that bounded the last case: the copies are tokens, and every nontoken clause in the chain steps straight over them. The holding: one six-mana cast yields two nonlegendary Lathliss copies and 64 damage in twelve separately aimed instances — 36 from Terror, 28 from Tempest at optimal trigger ordering. Enough for the 32-life opponent. Not enough for both.
