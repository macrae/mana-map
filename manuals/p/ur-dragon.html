1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

An eminence-discounted Dragon deck whose engine is the ARRIVAL, not the attack: cost reduction lands two Dragons in one turn, Miirym and Lathliss turn each arrival into several, and Terror of the Peaks, Dragon Tempest and Scourge of Valkas price every arrival in damage — and V6 bought a response suite four of whose six additions are castable at instant speed as printed, so the mana held for them is not dead; that the CASCADE itself can be taken on somebody else's turn needs Whirlwing Stormbrood on the battlefield and nothing verifies it.

1.1Stages this deck does not have

Fodder. RE-VERIFIED against the current 97 entries rather than carried forward from the v1.0.2 model, because the landbase changed twice since. A regex scan for 'sacrific' returns eleven cards and not one is a sacrifice OUTLET. Four fetchlands (Arid Mesa, Bloodstained Mire, Scalding Tarn, Wooded Foothills), Gemstone Mine and Sunbaked Canyon sacrifice THEMSELVES as their own cost; Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon sacrifices itself to tutor; Gadrak, Ganax and Goldspan Dragon only print the Treasure token's own self-sacrifice reminder; and Sneak Attack's end-step sacrifice is the price of its effect, not a conversion the deck chooses to make. Nothing converts a permanent you already control into anything else. The standing oddity is still Gadrak, the Crown-Scourge — a Treasure per nontoken creature that died this turn, a fodder PAYOFF in a deck with no fodder and no way to kill its own creatures on purpose. The claim is about permanents as an engine cost, not about resources in general; Treasure is of course spent, and that is why there is a fuel stage. Manufacturing a fodder stage to fill the table would misdescribe the deck.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. a spell or ability on the stack is pointed at one of your permanents

Indications.

  • An opponent holds two or more untapped sources through your whole turn and casts nothing
  • The Ur-Dragon has just resolved and a seat with blue or white mana is still untapped — log 006, where Stephen answered the commander on the turn it landed
  • Your only cost reducer is on the battlefield and the second is in hand — log 002, where the reducer was answered before the discount engine did anything
  • You have just cast Twinflame Tyrant, Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm or Panharmonicon into an open table — log 005, where the Tyrant went down before the Dragons and was removed immediately
  • Lightning Greaves is on the battlefield attached to nothing

Immediate action.

  1. Stop before the spell resolves. Count your untapped lands, your untapped mana creatures and the instants in hand, then decide — log 006 skipped this step with a response available and let the removal resolve.
  2. Tap Bloom Tender or Faeburrow Elder for one mana per colour among your permanents before you decide you cannot pay. With all five colours on your side that is five mana from one creature.
  3. Cast Stubborn Denial for {U} if the spell is noncreature. With a creature of power 4 or greater on your board it counters outright; 21 of the 32 creatures in the 99 have power 4 or greater.
  4. Cast Mithril Coat for {3}. It has flash and attaches on entry to a legendary creature you control; you have twelve of them, the commander included.
  5. Cast Deflecting Swat and send the spell at a token or a mana creature. Check that a commander is on the battlefield first — without one it costs {2}{R}.

Subsequent.

  1. Equip Lightning Greaves in your precombat main phase, before you cast the Dragon you need to keep. Equip is sorcery speed and cannot be done in response to the answer.
  2. Cast Thunderbreak Regent before the Dragon that matters. Every opponent spell or ability that targets a Dragon you control then deals its controller 3.
  3. Cast Asceticism for {3}{G}{G} once two or more Dragons are down. Hexproof stops every targeted answer at the table and still lets your own Mithril Coat and your own {1}{G} regenerate target them.
  4. Hold Sarkhan's Triumph for the rebuild. It is an instant that puts a Dragon in hand; cast it on the turn after the answer lands, not before it.
NOTELightning Greaves grants shroud as well as haste. A Dragon wearing the Greaves cannot be targeted by your own Mithril Coat trigger, by Boros Charm's double-strike mode or by Asceticism's regenerate. Move the Greaves off before the turn you need to target that body. And Mithril Coat answers destruction: exile, bounce and an edict go straight through it.

Drawn from 3 logged game(s): log:002, log:005, log:006.

3.2WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming and the board is committed

Indications.

  • The pod has wiped on each of the last two nights — log 002 records the cycle as board established, board erased, repeat, and log 006 names Stuart's board wipes
  • Three or more untapped sources in white or black held through your turn
  • An opponent who has cast nothing for two turns and is holding four or more cards
  • Four or more Dragons on your board and no attack made yet
  • Miirym's token copies on the board — they die with everything else and do not come back

Immediate action.

  1. Count the damage the board already presents. If it kills a seat this turn, attack that seat now instead of adding a Dragon.
  2. Stop deploying at three Dragons and a payoff. Keep the fourth Dragon and the second reducer in hand.
  3. Leave {1}{G}, {2}{W} or {3} untapped across the table's turns, for Heroic Intervention, Teferi's Protection or Mithril Coat.
  4. Cast Flawless Maneuver while a commander is on the battlefield; it costs nothing then.
  5. Cast Heroic Intervention rather than Flawless Maneuver when the board you are saving includes Panharmonicon, Dragon Tempest or Garruk's Uprising. Flawless Maneuver covers creatures.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild to two Dragons and a payoff, not to five bodies.
  2. Cast Dragon Tempest, Terror of the Peaks or Scourge of Valkas before the next Dragon, so the rebuild deals damage as it happens rather than after it finishes.
  3. Cast Atsushi, the Blazing Sky into the next wipe on purpose. Its death trigger is three Treasures or two cards off the top.
  4. Recast The Ur-Dragon only behind a printed reducer.
NOTETeferi's Protection phases your whole board out until your next turn, so casting it in your own main phase costs you that turn's attack — cast it on an opponent's turn. Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat are free only while a commander is on the battlefield, and the goldfish has The Ur-Dragon on the battlefield by turn six in 0.333 of 10,000 runs; before that they cost {2}{W} and {2}{R}. Indestructible and hexproof both do nothing against exile, bounce or an edict.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:006.

3.3POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has read you as the problem and is converging

Indications.

  • Your board reads lethal on a seat and you cannot attack until next turn
  • Somebody has said your damage total out loud — log 003, where the table converged inside one cycle of reading "Sean is swinging for 28 in the air"
  • You have killed two commanders with entry damage — log 004, where that made you the obvious problem and three players picked you apart inside one rotation
  • Two opponents have passed a turn cycle without attacking each other
  • A seat is assembling quietly while the table's removal is pointed at you — log 004: Steven won with a tap/untap engine nobody attacked

Immediate action.

  1. Count what you can kill this turn. If it is not a whole seat, hold the next Dragon and pass with mana up.
  2. Cast Thrakkus the Butcher or Atarka, World Render in the precombat main phase of the turn you attack, never the turn before.
  3. Name a second threat before you pass. Point at one permanent somebody else controls and say what you would spend removal on.
  4. Attack the seat with the most life. Scourge of the Throne's dethrone counter and its extra combat both require it, and it puts your damage where the table already wants it.
  5. Hold Teferi's Protection until a second player commits to you. One removal spell is not the convergence.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Whirlwing Stormbrood and deploy at the end of the turn before yours from then on. It gives your sorceries and your Dragon spells flash, so the board arrives after the table has spent its turn.
  2. Rebuild to one Dragon and one payoff, and let the next two turns be somebody else's.
  3. Spend Swords to Plowshares or Generous Gift on the seat that is not attacking you, once, in front of the table.
  4. Attack the quiet assembler once every turn cycle from the moment you name him.
NOTELog 003's mistake was the order, not the cards: Gadrak, the Crown-Scourge landed a turn before the deck could present lethal, and the table had a full cycle to strip the three artifacts Gadrak needs to attack, fight the Dragons and ping the rest. Deploy the multiplier and the attack on the same turn.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:003, log:004.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the colours have stopped arriving, or a land-hate effect has taken them

Indications.

  • Four or five lands on turn six while the table is on eight or more — log 002
  • No green source on the battlefield with Farseek, Nature's Lore or a green Dragon in hand — log 005, lost with exactly that hand
  • Black stranding first: on-curve from lands alone reads 0.524 for black against 0.718 or better for every other colour
  • Blood Moon or an equivalent resolved — log 001. Seven basics survive it: four Mountain, one Forest, one Island, one Plains, and there is no Swamp in the 99
  • Your untapped sources are Cavern of Souls, Delighted Halfling, Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon or Unclaimed Territory and the spell in hand is not a creature, a Dragon or a legend

Immediate action.

  1. Play the untapped land that makes the colour you are missing and pay the life. Battlefield Forge, City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Spire of Industry and Sunbaked Canyon all enter untapped and all charge life.
  2. Crack the fetch for a dual rather than a basic and pay the two life. Arid Mesa, Bloodstained Mire, Scalding Tarn and Wooded Foothills each reach several of the ten lands in the 99 that carry a basic type.
  3. Cast Bloom Tender or Faeburrow Elder ahead of any other play at that cost, and play the coloured land before them — each adds one mana per colour already among your permanents.
  4. Point Farseek at Blood Crypt, Overgrown Tomb or Smoldering Marsh. Those are the black sources it reaches, and black is the short colour.
  5. Cast Chromatic Lantern on the turn a colour is actually missing rather than on curve. It turns every land you control into a source of that colour.

Subsequent.

  1. Under a land-hate lock, play the colourless and red half and pick one seat. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Dominance, Chromatic Lantern, Dragon's Hoard, Herald's Horn, Urza's Incubator, Lightning Greaves, Mithril Coat and Steel Hellkite need no coloured mana at all, and the red Dragons are castable off Mountains.
  2. Cast Skyshroud Claim off five mana rather than a five-drop Dragon. It puts two Forest-typed lands onto the battlefield untapped, and Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista, Cinder Glade, Overgrown Tomb and Stomping Ground all carry the type.
  3. Sacrifice Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon for {4} once you have a spare land drop; it puts a Dragon in hand.
  4. Recast The Ur-Dragon only once a printed reducer is down. Dragonspeaker Shaman and Urza's Incubator each take {2} off it; eminence does not, because eminence discounts other Dragon spells.
NOTEFive sources pay for SPELLS only: Cavern of Souls, Delighted Halfling, Desolation of Smaug, Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon and Unclaimed Territory. Four of them also carry a plain "{T}: Add {C}", which pays a generic cost such as Mithril Coat's {3} equip or Steel Hellkite's {X}; none of them pays a coloured activation such as Asceticism's {1}{G} regenerate or Lathliss's {1}{R}. Count that mana twice before you call a hand castable.

Drawn from 3 logged game(s): log:001, log:002, log:005.

3.5STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. the reducer and the ramp never came together and the table is developing past you

Indications.

  • Turn six with no cost reducer on the battlefield — the goldfish draws one by turn six in 0.421 of 10,000 runs, and reducer plus ramp together in 0.337
  • Two Dragons at mana value five or more in hand on four lands — log 006, where Smaug the Magnificent and Thrakkus the Butcher were kept and neither was cast on time
  • Your cost reducer was answered and the second is still in hand — log 002, where the discount engine never did its job
  • The Ur-Dragon still uncast on turn seven: the median cast turn is 7 and it never arrives at all in 2,277 of 10,000 runs
  • No Treasure source and no per-Dragon payoff on the battlefield

Immediate action.

  1. Cast the cheapest thing that carries a per-Dragon trigger rather than holding for the big one: Dragon Tempest at {1}{R}, then Firespitter Whelp at {2}{R}.
  2. Cast Breaching Dragonstorm or Roiling Dragonstorm for the card rather than for the body. Each returns to your hand when a Dragon you control enters, so it is recastable rather than spent.
  3. Cast Sarkhan's Triumph for the Dragon your mana pays for this turn, not for the biggest one.
  4. Cast Desolation of Smaug for its mana half when you hold a Dragon you cannot otherwise afford: 3 damage to each non-Dragon creature, and four mana in any combination of colours to spend on a Dragon spell.
  5. Cast the cost reducer before the ramp spell when you can only have one.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Hellkite Courser for {4}{R}{R} and put The Ur-Dragon onto the battlefield instead of paying nine.
  2. Cast Sneak Attack and put Drakuseth, Maw of Flames in for {R}. Its trigger is on the attack, so all 17 damage lands before the end-step sacrifice.
  3. Cast Garruk's Uprising or Temur Ascendancy before the next Dragon of power 4 or greater, so the rebuild draws as it lands.
  4. Cast Steel Hellkite off six mana of any colours when the coloured Dragons are stranded.
NOTEHellkite Courser puts The Ur-Dragon onto the battlefield and returns it to the command zone at the beginning of that turn's end step. Neither event is casting, so the commander tax never moves and the next cast is still {4}{W}{U}{B}{R}{G} — stack 008 step 11, on the Courser's official 2020-11-10 ruling. The Courser grants haste to the COMMANDER, not to itself: with no Dragon Tempest, Temur Ascendancy or Lightning Greaves already on the battlefield, the Courser sits out the turn it lands and only the commander attacks. Stack 008 is checker-failed on one citation in step 4 and its four answers do not rest on it; read the page before you run the line.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:006.

3.6COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. a seat is assembling a line and you cannot interact with every piece

Indications.

  • A seat tutoring twice inside one turn cycle — log 006: Stephen's Sisay is a tutor on a stick for any legendary piece
  • A seat with an engine on the battlefield that nobody has attacked — log 004: Steven won with a tap/untap engine while the table spent its removal on you
  • A seat holding mana open every turn and casting nothing
  • A graveyard filling on the other side of the table

Immediate action.

  1. Say what you have seen. Name the piece to the table so a seat holding an answer has a reason to spend it.
  2. Cast Stubborn Denial for {U} on the noncreature half of the line. With a creature of power 4 or greater on your board it counters outright rather than taxing.
  3. Cast Transcendent Dragon at flash speed for {4}{U}{U}: it counters the spell, exiles it, and lets you cast that spell yourself for nothing.
  4. Spend Chaos Warp or Generous Gift on the piece already on the battlefield. Chaos Warp is an instant and both reach any permanent.
  5. Change target. Attack the assembling seat every turn from here, whatever the arithmetic says about the other two.

Subsequent.

  1. Keep attacking the same seat after the line is broken once; they reassemble faster than you rebuild.
  2. Cast Sarkhan's Triumph for Drakuseth, Maw of Flames rather than for a value Dragon — the answer to a line you cannot interact with is ending the game first.
  3. Activate Steel Hellkite for {X} equal to the combo piece's mana value on a turn it has dealt that seat combat damage; it destroys each nonland permanent at that mana value they control.
  4. Hold Teferi's Protection for the turn the line goes off, and cast it against damage or drain.
NOTETeferi's Protection buys one turn cycle against damage and drain and buys nothing against mill, an alternate-win clause, or a lockout that does not target you. Reading every oracle text in the 99, one card mentions a graveyard at all — Transcendent Dragon, exiling the spell it counters. There is nothing to point at a graveyard, so do not spend removal on a creature that comes back; spend it on whatever puts it there.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:004, log:006.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat is on a faster clock than yours and the table is not answering it

Indications.

  • An opponent attacking by their fourth turn while your first attack is two turns out
  • A wide board of small bodies opposite you and no blocker of yours on the ground
  • Your hand is six-drops and the table is already trading life
  • Your board power under ten on turn five — the goldfish's mean is 9.755 at turn five and 14.877 at turn six, unopposed and against nothing that blocks

Immediate action.

  1. Cast the cheapest body that blocks and still pays you: Firespitter Whelp at {2}{R}, then Thunderbreak Regent at {2}{R}{R}.
  2. Block with the tokens. Miirym's copies and Lathliss's 5/5s are blockers before they are damage and cost nothing to lose.
  3. Cast Crux of Fate on the non-Dragon mode and accept the price: it also kills Birds of Paradise, Bloom Tender, Delighted Halfling, Faeburrow Elder, Ignoble Hierarch, Dragonlord's Servant, Dragonspeaker Shaman and Solphim, Mayhem Dominus, which are the eight non-Dragon creatures in the 99.
  4. Spend Generous Gift or Chaos Warp on the anchor of the wide board rather than on the biggest single body. Swords to Plowshares gives the racing seat life — save it for the one attacker you cannot block.
  5. Attack the racing seat, not the open one.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Smaug the Magnificent or Glorybringer on the turn you need damage now; both have haste.
  2. Cast Twinflame Tyrant before the next Dragon. Every Dragon Tempest and Scourge of Valkas trigger after it deals double.
  3. Equip Lightning Greaves to the first Dragon that lands so it attacks the turn it arrives.
  4. Activate Steel Hellkite for {X} of 0 on a turn it has connected with the racing seat: that destroys every token and every Treasure they control.
NOTENo logged game has ended this way, so this page is reasoned from the list and the model rather than from a night at the table — read it as weaker than the six pages above it. Twinflame Tyrant doubles combat and noncombat damage; Solphim, Mayhem Dominus doubles noncombat damage, so Solphim adds nothing to a swing.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Three lands, one of Birds of Paradise / Bloom Tender / Delighted Halfling / Faeburrow Elder / Ignoble Hierarch, and a cost reducer
  • Two lands with Sol Ring or Arcane Signet — log 003's turn-one four mana, off the one keep the pilot writes up as the mana working
  • Three lands, one of Farseek / Nature's Lore / Skyshroud Claim, and a Dragon at mana value five or less
  • Any hand with Bloom Tender and two green sources
  • Three lands and two of Dragon Tempest, Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas, Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm

Ship on sight.

  • A hand with no green source — the pilot's own rule after log 005, where Farseek and the green Dragons sat in hand all game
  • Three lands, one rock and two Dragons at mana value five or more with no cost reducer — log 006's keep, which the pilot does not stand by
  • A hand whose cheapest spell costs five — log 002's keep, and the mana never arrived
  • Two lands with no Sol Ring, no Arcane Signet, no Talisman of Dominance and no one-mana mana creature
  • Six or seven lands
NOTEMulligan on colours and on the cheapest spell, not on land count. The early land drops hold — 0.891 on turn three — and then fall to 0.756 on four and 0.615 on five, while the reducer-plus-ramp hand assembles in 0.462 of 10,000 runs. The hand you are looking for makes three colours and casts something on turn two.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 77% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Play the untapped land on turn one and pay the life if it asks for it.
  2. Cast Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or Talisman of Dominance on turn one ahead of any other play.
  3. Cast the mana creature on turn two, and play a coloured land before Bloom Tender or Faeburrow Elder — both count the colours already among your permanents.
  4. Cast the cost reducer on turn three. Dragonspeaker Shaman and Urza's Incubator each take {2} off a Dragon spell; Dragonlord's Servant and Herald's Horn take {1}.
  5. Point Farseek at the black or blue dual and Nature's Lore or Skyshroud Claim at a Forest-typed dual, and take the colour you are shortest on rather than the land that enters untapped.
NOTEEminence discounts other Dragon spells and never The Ur-Dragon itself, so the commander costs nine until a printed reducer is on the battlefield. Dragonspeaker Shaman and Urza's Incubator together take it to five.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Cast the payoff before the Dragon. Dragon Tempest, Terror of the Peaks and Scourge of Valkas all read "whenever a Dragon enters"; a Dragon that lands first is damage you did not deal.
  2. Cast Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm ahead of the expensive Dragon rather than after it. Every nontoken Dragon behind her arrives twice.
  3. Cast Whirlwing Stormbrood once you hold two or more Dragons, and from then on cast your Dragons and your sorceries at the end of the turn before yours.
  4. Cast The Ur-Dragon with a printed reducer already down, or put it onto the battlefield with Hellkite Courser for {4}{R}{R}.
  5. Equip Lightning Greaves to the piece you cannot rebuild — Miirym, or the last reducer — and move it off before the turn you need to target that body.
NOTEHellkite Courser grants haste to the commander it deploys and not to itself, so without Dragon Tempest, Temur Ascendancy or Lightning Greaves already out the Courser sits out the turn it lands. The commander returns to the command zone at that turn's end step whether it attacked or not, and the next cast still costs {4}{W}{U}{B}{R}{G} — nothing here is a cast, so no tax is added.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Count every seat's life at the start of your turn, before you untap.
  2. Leave {U} up for Stubborn Denial from the moment a creature with power 4 or greater is on your board; 21 of the 32 creatures in the 99 qualify.
  3. Stop before any removal spell or ability resolves. Count your untapped lands, your untapped mana creatures and the instants in hand, then let it resolve — log 006 is the game that skipped this with five mana available and a response in hand.
  4. Tap Bloom Tender or Faeburrow Elder for one mana per colour among your permanents. With all five colours out, that is five mana from one creature.
  5. Attack the player with the most life, so Scourge of the Throne's extra combat turns on.
NOTEYou hold an answer in 0.890 of goldfish runs at turn six and can cast one in 0.510 — the mana to hold a response up arrives later than the response does. Cast the ramp before the protection every time the choice appears.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count the target seat's life, then count the Dragons you control. Dragon Tempest and Scourge of Valkas each deal that number for every Dragon that enters.
  2. Cast Twinflame Tyrant before the Dragon that carries the trigger. It doubles every point; Solphim, Mayhem Dominus doubles noncombat damage.
  3. Cast Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm, then the Dragon. The copy is a second "enters" for every payoff on the battlefield.
  4. Aim every instance at one seat. Stacks 002 and 007 put that board at 42 damage on a single player by the eighth of twelve instances; split across two seats the same board kills neither.
  5. Attack with Thrakkus the Butcher or Atarka, World Render on the board and Garruk's Uprising down for trample.
NOTEToken copies do not re-trigger Miirym or Lathliss, so the cascade terminates — stacks 001 and 002 both settle this. Count the nontoken arrivals, not the bodies.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (33 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • Treasures are a one-shot STOCKPILE, not a mana rock: spent only when lands and rocks fall short, and gone once broken. Reported separately from mean_available_mana_by_turn, which still means repeatable mana per turn.
  • Only Treasure triggers a goldfish can see are modeled — upkeep and landfall are RECURRING, Saga chapters likewise; a `cast` or enters-the-battlefield trigger pays out ONCE, when its own source resolves. Combat- and opponent-gated sources produce NOTHING here, because this model has no combat and no opponents; they are named in meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled so a low hoard figure is legible.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

The first thing that has to happen is a Dragon costing less than it says. 7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Cast your Dragons and sorceries at the end of the turn before yours once Whirlwing Stormbrood is on the battlefield. A board that appears after the table has spent its turn gives nobody a window to price it.
  • Cast the reducer before the Dragon. A Dragonlord's Servant reads as a Goblin; a Terror of the Peaks reads as a countdown.
  • Keep the board at three Dragons until the turn you attack.
  • Leave one Dragon back untapped. A seat that cannot be attacked profitably is not the seat the table attacks.
  • Do the arithmetic in your head and say the number out loud on the turn it is lethal — in log 003 the table converged inside one cycle of hearing it.

7.2When to reveal

  • Hold Mithril Coat and Stubborn Denial until an answer is on the stack. Both cost less than the turn they save.
  • Show a protection spell once. A table that has watched Heroic Intervention resolve prices the next wipe differently.
  • Spend removal on somebody else's threat early, in front of the table, so your own enchantments have a reason to be left alone.
  • Hold Sarkhan's Triumph until after another seat has tutored.
  • Keep Teferi's Protection unannounced. It decides whether the convergence works, and it works once.

7.3Alliances

  • Name the other threat before you pass, every turn, and be specific: one permanent, and what you would spend on it.
  • Take the deal that has you attacking the leader. Scourge of the Throne needs the most-life seat anyway, so the political ask and the mechanical one are the same ask.
  • Trade one removal spell for a problem that is not yours, early, and say so while you do it.
  • Hold one Dragon back when two seats ask you to attack the third. Swinging with everything on somebody else's turn plan ends your turn with no blockers.
  • Attack the seat the table has already named, once per turn cycle, and say that is what you are doing.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Attack the player with the most life first. Dethrone and Scourge of the Throne's extra combat both require it.
  • Attack the quiet assembler second — log 004: the seat nobody attacked won the game.
  • Spend Chaos Warp and Generous Gift on an artifact, an enchantment or a land. Swords to Plowshares, Crux of Fate, Glorybringer and Desolation of Smaug reach creatures, and Steel Hellkite reaches a nonland permanent only on a turn it has dealt that player combat damage.
  • Point Swords to Plowshares at the blocker with flying or reach rather than at the largest attacker. This deck's damage is in the air and its problem is the one body that stops it.
  • Hold Crux of Fate for a board you are behind on, and read the non-Dragon mode as also killing your five mana creatures, both creature reducers and Solphim, Mayhem Dominus.
