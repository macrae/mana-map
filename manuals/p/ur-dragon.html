1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

A five-colour Dragon deck that buys its own curve down with five cost reducers, then multiplies each arrival twice over — once into more bodies and triggers, once into doubled damage — and whose kill is proven on a board and still unproven as a board it can reach.

1.1Stages this deck does not have

Fodder. RE-VERIFIED against the current 96 entries rather than carried forward. A regex scan for 'sacrific' returns exactly eight cards and not one is a sacrifice OUTLET: Fabled Passage, Prismatic Vista and Gemstone Mine sacrifice themselves as their own cost; Gadrak, Ganax and Goldspan Dragon only print the Treasure token's self-sacrifice reminder; Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon sacrifices itself to tutor; and Sneak Attack's end-step sacrifice is the price of its own effect, not a conversion the deck chooses to make. Nothing converts a permanent you already control into anything. The new finding is Gadrak, the Crown-Scourge, added in v1.0.2: it makes a Treasure per nontoken creature that died this turn, which is a fodder PAYOFF in a deck with no fodder and no way to kill its own creatures on purpose. Treasures are of course spent — the claim is about creatures and permanents as an engine cost, not about resources in general. Manufacturing a fodder stage to fill the table would misdescribe the deck.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (28 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • Treasures are a one-shot STOCKPILE, not a mana rock: spent only when lands and rocks fall short, and gone once broken. Reported separately from mean_available_mana_by_turn, which still means repeatable mana per turn.
  • Only Treasure triggers a goldfish can see are modeled — upkeep and landfall are RECURRING, Saga chapters likewise; a `cast` or enters-the-battlefield trigger pays out ONCE, when its own source resolves. Combat- and opponent-gated sources produce NOTHING here, because this model has no combat and no opponents; they are named in meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled so a low hoard figure is legible.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

6.1Mana

Pays for a nine-mana commander and a Dragon top end out of 36 land copies (32 entries), four rocks, three land-fetching sorceries and — the v1.0.2 change — five mana CREATURES where artifacts used to be: Birds of Paradise, Bloom Tender, Delighted Halfling, Faeburrow Elder, Ignoble Hierarch. 44 cards, the largest stage by far. Mean available mana at turn five reads 5.934 against v1.0.0's 4.219, and 6.734 at six. 'Ramp or fixing drawn' is the deck's best-covered component at 0.904 assembled / 0.824 by turn six, mean turn 2.213. deck-audit prices the cost: 22 ramp copies against a 10-12 ratio, verdict 'over'.\n\nTwelve of the 36 land copies enter tapped and only one always — exactly a third, where v1.0.0 ran 15 of 36 and mana_analysis's notes flagged it as over the one-in-three budget; that flag is gone. Colour is still uneven: on-curve from lands alone G 0.849, U 0.810, W 0.787, R 0.718, B 0.441 on 13 black land sources against a 30-source yardstick, lifted to 0.636 by rocks and dorks.\n\nThe cost of the fixing is LIFE and fragility. mana_analysis counts 8 life per tap-cycle recurring and 13 one-time on entry. The recurring 8 is six cards enumerated: Battlefield Forge, City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Shivan Reef and Spire of Industry at 1 each, Tarnished Citadel at 3 — and four of the six carry a free '{T}: Add {C}' mode, so the toll is paid only for colour. Gemstone Mine is three activations and then it is gone. Forbidden Orchard hands an opponent a 1/1 every time you tap it.\n\nTwo lands are not only mana: Cavern of Souls makes a Dragon spell paid with its mana uncounterable, and Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon sacrifices for a Dragon. Six sources are DRAGON- or LEGEND-restricted and deck-facts warns they pay for spells only, never a coloured activation.

6.2Ignition

What has to happen before the deck is doing its thing is that a Dragon becomes affordable ahead of curve, and half of it is true on turn one. Eminence works from the command zone: other Dragon spells cost {1} less with nothing to cast, nothing to resolve and nothing an opponent can remove. The other half is four printed reducers — Urza's Incubator {2}, Dragonspeaker Shaman {2}, Herald's Horn {1}, Dragonlord's Servant {1}. An oracle scan of all 96 entries for 'less to cast' returns exactly those four plus eminence.\n\nPriced from the brief: a reducer drawn is 0.521 assembled, 0.422 by turn six, mean turn 3.264. At four cards that is 25.8% of opening sevens and 32.1% by turn three, against the cited yardstick that '5 copies is 31% of openers, 7 is 41%'. deck-audit does NOT flag it thin — it flags at 3 or fewer, and three components carry the flag: Dragon-restricted mana at 2, THE ENTRY KILL's damage leg at 3, THE COMBAT KILL's multiplier leg at 2. Reducer AND ramp together, the turn-five Ur-Dragon hand, is 0.463 / 0.336 at mean turn 4.195.\n\nSarkhan, Fireblood and Desolation of Smaug are a separate two-card component (0.302 / 0.234, thin) because their mana is spendable only on Dragon spells: manabase.land_colors scores restricted mana as zero sources, correct for colour maths and exactly wrong for 'can I cast a Dragon'. Desolation is also a one-sided sweeper.\n\nThe commander's BODY is not part of ignition in practice: mean cast turn 6.916, median 7, cast by turn six 0.329, and never cast by turn ten in 2,222 of 10,000 iterations — much improved on v1.0.0's 5,997 and still a fifth of games.

6.3Fuel

What keeps the engine running once a Dragon has landed: Treasure, a specific Dragon on demand, and cards. Five of the declared six Treasure sources are here — Atsushi, Goldspan Dragon, Old Gnawbone, Gadrak and Ganax; the sixth, Smaug the Magnificent, is under wincon because the Treasures it counts are the damage its own attack trigger deals. The group reads 0.676 assembled / 0.560 by turn six, mean turn 3.068. That drawn-rate is complete; every Treasure-derived MANA figure is a FLOOR, because meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled names THREE of the six (Atsushi, Gadrak, Ganax) — which is why the component's own label, 'the model can SPEND only two', no longer matches the file it is measured against. What the model does see is small: mean hoard 2.077 by turn ten, treasure engine online 0.155 by turn ten. Two of the five are CONDITIONAL in a way the count hides. Atsushi's three Treasures are a DEATH trigger, and Gadrak makes one per nontoken creature that died this turn — in a deck with no sacrifice outlet at all (see absent_stages). Ganax is the unconditional one and is new in v1.0.2: a Treasure whenever Ganax or another Dragon you control enters, tokens included, which makes it the fuel side of the entry cascade. Breaching and Roiling Dragonstorm each return to their owner's hand when a Dragon you control enters, which makes them recurring rather than one-shot — Roiling draws two and discards one, Breaching free-casts a nonland card of mana value 8 or less. That conversion-to-fuel loop is printed on the fuel, not on the payoff. Finding a Dragon is two cards wide (Sarkhan's Triumph, Worldly Tutor) plus Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon, filed in mana; Enlightened Tutor finds Panharmonicon or a Dragonstorm.

6.4Conversion

The engine proper, and after v1.0.2 it has THREE families under one roof rather than two. ENTRY. Miirym copies every other nontoken Dragon; Panharmonicon doubles every trigger an entering creature or artifact causes; Lathliss makes a 5/5 per nontoken Dragon; Ancient Gold Dragon rolls a d20 on combat damage; Utvara Hellkite makes a 6/6 per attacking Dragon; Sneak Attack puts one in from hand for {R}; Hellkite Courser is the only card in the 99 that puts a commander onto the battlefield from the command zone, and 005 refutes Sneak Attack doing it. COMBAT. Atarka grants double strike to every attacking Dragon, Thrakkus doubles each Dragon's power, Radiant Destiny is a flat +1/+1, and Scourge of the Throne is the deck's only additional combat phase — it multiplies the combats rather than the damage inside one. DAMAGE DOUBLING, NEW IN v1.0.2 AND THE REAL CHANGE TO THIS STAGE. Twinflame Tyrant doubles any damage a source you control would deal to an opponent or their permanents; Solphim doubles NONCOMBAT damage to the same targets. Both are replacement effects sitting behind the whole output stage, and neither is measured: the goldfish files Twinflame Tyrant and Solphim in THE COMBAT KILL's castable-DRAGON leg, as bodies, while its multiplier leg stays two cards and thin. SPOF STAYS NULL, and 007 is why that needs an argument. Without Panharmonicon, 002's board falls from 64 damage to 20 and kills nobody. But Panharmonicon converts NOTHING alone — it doubles triggers other cards cause — while Miirym, Lathliss, Ancient Gold Dragon, Utvara Hellkite, Sneak Attack and Hellkite Courser each widen a board on their own printed text. 007 bounds one board's magnitude, not the stage's availability.

6.5Output

What the multiplied board produces, priced per entry rather than per turn. DAMAGE. Dragon Tempest deals X to any target for each Dragon entering, where X is the Dragons you control; Terror of the Peaks deals that creature's power; Scourge of Valkas does Tempest's job on a 4/4 flying body and counts itself. Those three are THE ENTRY KILL's entire damage leg, thin at 3, and the target reads 0.235 assembled / 0.150 by turn six at mean turn 4.977. Firespitter Whelp deals damage too and is NOT in that leg, correctly: its trigger is on CASTING a noncreature or Dragon spell, not on a Dragon entering, and it hits each opponent for 1 rather than aiming X. Stack 002 prices one Lathliss cast at 64 damage in twelve separately-targeted instances — 36 from Terror, 28 from Tempest — and 007 settles how that total may be spent: every instance reads 'any target', nothing forces them apart, and aimed at one seat the running total reaches 42 by the eighth, so a fresh 40-life seat dies. What neither board can do is kill two seats, 64 against 72. CARDS. Temur Ascendancy draws on each power-4-or-greater arrival, Garruk's Uprising on the first and grants trample, Return of the Wildspeaker and Rishkar's Expertise cash the biggest body. TARGETED ACTIONS. Swords to Plowshares, Generous Gift, Chaos Warp, Glorybringer's exert, and Crux of Fate — asymmetric by choice, though less so than it was. From the type lines: 22 of the 30 creatures in the 99 are Dragons, and the eight that are not are five mana creatures, two cost reducers and Solphim — so Crux of Fate's non-Dragon mode now costs you the whole acceleration package. WHAT LEFT: Wrathful Red Dragon is out of the 99, and stack 004, which verified it, names a board this list cannot make. Nothing here cites it.

6.6Protection

Six answers held out of the same open mana for the same turn — Teferi's Protection, Heroic Intervention, Flawless Maneuver, Boros Charm, Deflecting Swat and Lightning Greaves — measured 0.673 assembled / 0.565 by turn six at mean turn 2.959. That is the declared component exactly. WHAT v1.0.2 TRADED, and it is not a like-for-like swap: three counterspells (Counterspell, Swan Song, Dovin's Veto) came out for Lightning Greaves. Greaves answers targeted removal at equip {0} and cannot answer a board wipe, which all three counterspells could; its shroud also stops YOUR own targeting. The group still reads 'protection held for the big turn' and that is the turn Greaves is for, but its coverage is narrower than the count suggests. TWO OF THE SIX ARE PROBABLY NOT FREE HERE. Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat both read 'If you control a commander' — and The Ur-Dragon is a permanent only while it is on the battlefield. The goldfish casts the commander by turn six in 0.329 of runs at median turn 7, so on the turns this deck pivots these are often a {2}{W} and a {2}{R} spell. Two limits on that inference: the goldfish counts CASTS, not control, and Hellkite Courser is a second route onto the battlefield it does not model. The measurable shape of the problem is interaction_castable_by_turn: 0.279 / 0.371 / 0.455 on turns four to six, and the model's own note calls that a FLOOR because it spends rather than holds. THUNDERBREAK REGENT IS THE SEVENTH CARD AND SITS IN NO DECLARED PROTECTION COMPONENT — the goldfish files it in THE COMBAT KILL's castable-Dragon leg, as a body. It prices removal (3 damage to the player) rather than preventing it, so it belongs to the function of this stage and not to its measured rate.

6.7Win condition

TWO OF THE FOUR PRESENTABLE PASSING STACKS ELIMINATE A PLAYER. 001 kills every opponent on its board; 002 kills the 32-life seat (704.5a); 005 deals 17. 007 settles 002 in both directions: without Panharmonicon the same board deals 20 and kills nobody, and on 002's own board the twelve instances may all be aimed at one seat, reaching 42 by the eighth — so a fresh 40-life seat dies. Neither board kills two seats, 64 against 72. What is unproven is REACHABILITY against THREE seats at 40. 001 fixes its d20 at 15 for determinism and the kill does not need 15: the threshold is 12 or better, nine faces of twenty, and its precondition is that Ancient Gold Dragon has already connected in combat. THE KILL MOSTLY DOES NOT LIVE IN THIS STAGE. It lives in OUTPUT — Dragon Tempest, Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas — declared as THE ENTRY KILL at 0.235 assembled / 0.150 by turn six, damage leg thin at 3. THE COMBAT KILL reads 0.243 / 0.157 at mean turn 4.866 with a multiplier leg thin at 2. Do not add the rates; the schema cannot express their union. THIS STAGE IS NOW TWO CARDS. v1.0.2 removed both alternate finishers: Hellkite Tyrant, whose twenty-artifact upkeep clause was the deck's only alternate win, and Moltensteel Dragon. An oracle scan of all 96 entries for 'wins the game' / 'loses the game' now returns nothing. What is left is Drakuseth, which deals 10 on the attack before blockers and 7 in the air, and Smaug the Magnificent, which deals damage equal to your Treasures to any target and mints one per upkeep. Both are damage; the deck now has exactly one way to end a game.
