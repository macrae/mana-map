The Plan

Count real mana before you swing. Keep or Ship

What a keepable hand looks like, and how often you get one. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

Keep a seven with two to four lands and one piece of acceleration. Two lands and a seven-drop is not a keep, however good it looks.

The acceleration that decides games is a cost reducer. Ramp or fixing arrives by turn six in 71.6% of games; a reducer in 33.2%. When Dragonspeaker Shaman, Urza's Incubator or Herald's Horn is in your opener, stop reading the hand and keep it. Reducer plus ramp — the hand that casts a turn-five Dragon nobody expected — arrives by turn six 23.1% of the time. Never ship one hunting for prettier Dragons.

Want a payoff before your first big body: Dragon Tempest, Garruk's Uprising, Roiling Dragonstorm, Korlessa. One is drawn by turn six 62.2% of the time; on turn two, you beat that number instead of matching it.

Never mulligan toward the commander. Eminence works from the command zone, so a hand that can eventually cast him is worth no more than one that cannot.

Ship two shapes on sight. Lands plus top end with nothing to do on turn three — land drops fall to 60.3% on turn five and 49.7% on turn six, so the bridge is not coming to you. And hands whose colours cannot cast their own cards: black fails first, 11 land sources and a 26.1% on-curve rate, and Haven, Maelstrom, Unclaimed Territory, Rivaz and Klauth only ever pay for spells.

Aggravated Assault in an opener is a hold, not a keep: it needs a body, funding and a turn where it kills somebody, and on turn three it only announces you. Protection is a genuine keep-bonus: Teferi's Protection, Heroic Intervention, Flawless Maneuver let you commit the board later.

Trust your sevens: the simulator keeps 75.1% of first hands at 0.313 mean mulligans. Mulligan for function, never for glamour.

the d20 damage burst

The holding: seventeen damage, fifteen times, with priority passing between every instalment. 255 available; the 30-life opponent is dead on the second trigger aimed at them, and anything still pointed at that player afterwards is countered on resolution (CR 608.2b).

Miirym under Panharmonicon

how many copies?

Panharmonicon does not read the trigger. Its clause asks only what caused the trigger — an artifact or creature entering — so Miirym's 'nontoken Dragon' and Terror of the Peaks's 'another creature' are treated exactly alike, and each triggers an additional time (CR 603.2d). What keeps the cascade finite is the same word that bounded the last case: the copies are tokens, and every nontoken clause in the chain steps straight over them. The holding: one six-mana cast yields two nonlegendary Lathliss copies and 64 damage in twelve separately aimed instances — 36 from Terror, 28 from Tempest at optimal trigger ordering. Enough for the 32-life opponent. Not enough for both.
