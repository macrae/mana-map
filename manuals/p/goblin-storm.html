The Plan

What this deck is trying to do, and what it is assuming away. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

The table counts your 1/1s, files you under aggro-that-runs-out-of-gas, and points its removal somewhere else. Take the gift. Every body you land is a card Zada, Hedron Grinder cashes later: an instant or sorcery targeting only her is copied once for each other creature you control, so one red mana across four Goblins is five cards and a hasted team (stack 001). Build the board first. Hold the cantrip second.

She is not the deck, and most pilots get that backwards. Of the five verified lines here only two put her on the battlefield: 002 runs off Impact Tremors, 005 off Storm-Kiln Artist, and 004 states outright that she is in the command zone while the turn happens. One of the three engine targets wants her; the other two do not. So when she eats a removal spell, do not rebuy her and do not pass the turn sulking. Keep casting.

Your ramp is the swarm, not your lands. Battle Hymn adds {R} for each creature you control, Brightstone Ritual adds {R} for each Goblin on the battlefield, and Skirk Prospector turns any spare token into red at instant speed. The goldfish has you at 5.182 mana on turn six — an ordinary curve. On the big turn, count Goblins, not Mountains.

Pick your exit before you cast the first spell; you have three and they are not interchangeable. Impact Tremors turns eight storm tokens into 8 damage to each opponent with no attack step (stack 002). Fists of Flame turns the same board into trample and takes a seat off 30 in one swing (stack 003). Haze of Rage with Storm-Kiln Artist simply does not stop (stack 005) — and it hits nobody by itself, so it is fuel, not a kill. A storm payoff and a ritual arrive together in 26.5% of games at a mean turn of 4.91, so when that hand shows up, know which of the first two ends the game before you tap anything.

Keep or Ship

What a keepable hand looks like, and how often you get one. ◆ DATA-DERIVED★ COACHING

Most players mulligan hunting for the payoff. You do not have to. Zada waits in the command zone at four mana and lands on a mean turn of 4.35, with 90% of games having her cast by turn six. Your opening seven does not need a win condition in it.

Keep three lands — or two plus Sol Ring, Skirk Prospector or Ruby Medallion — alongside a cheap token maker like Dragon Fodder, Krenko's Command, Mogg War Marshal or Searslicer Goblin, and bank a one-mana cantrip for the turn Zada resolves. That is the ordinary hand here, not the dream: 79.1% of first sevens clear the keep rule and the average game costs you a quarter of a mulligan. Keep the ordinary hand.

Ship the hands that are all payoff and no setup. Mana Geyser, Empty the Warrens and Blasphemous Act on two lands is a mulligan every time — rituals need a board and storm needs a cast count. A storm payoff and a ritual arrive together in only 26.5% of games, so stop waiting for that seven and take the one that makes bodies on turn two. An early token maker is the thing that actually shows up: 60.0% of games, mean turn 3.18.

Two cards rescue a marginal keep. Faithless Looting and Witch's Mark both trade dead cards for live ones for two mana or less, so lands plus one body plus a looter is a keep — snap it off and dig.

One habit for the big turns: say your cast count out loud, and say it on the cast, not on the resolution. Copies go on the stack without being cast and they never raise the count (stack 002). Ten-spell turns are precisely where good pilots miscount and fire Grapeshot two short.

Fists of Flame on Zada

the sequential-draw lethal

Fists of Flame draws first and buffs second, and CR 608.2h decides what that ordering is worth: if an effect requires information from the game, the answer is determined only once, when the effect is applied. Each copy therefore prices its own +1/+0-per-card at its own resolution and never revises it. The buffs land ascending and they are final; nothing grows retroactively. One caution the record insists on, because this math comes out exactly lethal — a player at 0 or less life loses as a state-based action (CR 704.5a), checked after combat damage is dealt, not during.
