1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

A Goblin swarm that is really a spell-count machine: bodies make the mana and set the copy count, cheap spells pointed at Zada turn each body into a card, and one payoff turn cashes the whole board into damage.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. turn six or later, fewer than three creatures on the battlefield, and the cheap spells in hand have nothing to copy through.

Indications.

  • Fewer than three creatures on the battlefield after turn five.
  • Crimson Wisps, Expedite or Renegade Tactics in hand with one creature to copy to.
  • Zada or Storm-Kiln Artist on the battlefield under an otherwise empty board.
  • Dragon Fodder, Krenko's Command, Mogg War Marshal and Searslicer Goblin all still unseen.
  • Land drops landing on time and the hand the same size it was three turns ago.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast the cheapest body in hand before any cantrip: Dragon Fodder, Krenko's Command, Mogg War Marshal or Searslicer Goblin.
  2. Cast Goblin Matron and fetch Mogg War Marshal or Skirk Prospector.
  3. Cast Faithless Looting and discard the rituals — Seething Song, Battle Hymn, Brightstone Ritual — keeping every creature.
  4. Hold Crimson Wisps, Expedite and Renegade Tactics until three creatures are on the battlefield.
  5. Attack with one creature so Searslicer Goblin makes a Goblin at your end step.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild to three bodies before you spend a cantrip on Zada.
  2. Cast Empty the Warrens off Brightstone Ritual or Seething Song and skip the Zada turn: stacks 002 and 005 are checker-passed and name no commander.
  3. Play Idol of Oblivion once a token maker has resolved, and take the draw on every turn a token entered.
  4. Recast Zada once, at {5}{R}. After that, spend the mana on bodies.
NOTEGoblin Matron finds any Goblin card, Krenko, Mob Boss included. On a stalled board Krenko taps for one token, and only if something already gave him haste; Mogg War Marshal is two bodies for {1}{R}. Fetch the Marshal.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:001.

3.2RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. an opponent kills the table before you have a board, and you cannot answer it on board.

Indications.

  • An opponent with a token maker and a damage payoff both on the battlefield by turn three.
  • Two or more mono-red decks at the table.
  • Your life below 30 before your fourth land drop.
  • Every creature in your hand costing four or more.
  • No seat has cast a removal spell yet.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast every creature you can afford each turn and keep them home as blockers.
  2. Cycle Gempalm Incinerator for {1}{R} at the racer's largest creature; X is every Goblin on the battlefield, theirs counted.
  3. Cast Chaos Warp on the racer's engine permanent, not on their largest body.
  4. Cast Blasphemous Act once six or more creatures are on the battlefield; it costs {1} less for each of them.
  5. Cast Empty the Warrens at whatever storm count you have — two tokens per copy is blockers.

Subsequent.

  1. Attack the racer and only the racer until they are dead.
  2. Spend Grapeshot and Siege-Gang Commander's activation on their creatures rather than on their life total.
  3. Cast Goblin Negotiation for X at their largest creature; the excess damage comes back as your own Goblins.
  4. Cast Past in Flames once the graveyard holds two rituals and a payoff.
NOTEBlasphemous Act deals 13 to each creature and kills your board with theirs. With Boggart Shenanigans or Pashalik Mons on the battlefield first, every one of your own Goblin deaths is a point aimed at a player, and the card stops being a reset.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.3WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming and the board is the whole plan.

Indications.

  • An opponent in white or black holding four or more mana open through your turn.
  • A seat that has cast nothing for two turns with cards in hand.
  • Your board at six or more creatures with neither Boggart Shenanigans nor Pashalik Mons on the battlefield.
  • Blasphemous Act in your own hand costing four or less — the board that cheapens it for you cheapens theirs.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Battle Hymn or Brightstone Ritual in response; both are instants and both read the board that is about to die.
  2. Spend that mana on Crimson Wisps or Expedite targeting only Zada — every copy draws a card before the sweeper resolves.
  3. Sacrifice the remaining Goblins to Goblin Bombardment, one damage each at the opponent closest to dead.
  4. Return Zada to the command zone rather than the graveyard.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild to three bodies, not eight. A second sweeper answers eight as easily as three.
  2. Play Boggart Shenanigans or Pashalik Mons before the next token maker, so the following sweeper pays you a point per Goblin.
  3. Cast Empty the Warrens off Brightstone Ritual and Seething Song instead of recasting Zada at {5}{R}.
  4. Cast Past in Flames to flash back the cantrips and rituals the last turn put in the graveyard.
NOTESkullclamp equips only as a sorcery. Attach it in your main phase on a turn you expect the sweeper; with the sweeper on the stack the Equip ability is not available and the two cards do not arrive.

3.4REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. Zada, or the one permanent this turn is built on, is targeted.

Indications.

  • An opponent with two mana open in a colour that answers a creature, during your turn.
  • Zada on the battlefield with three or more other creatures and a cantrip in your hand.
  • Storm-Kiln Artist on the battlefield with Haze of Rage in hand.
  • Swiftfoot Boots on the battlefield attached to nothing.

Immediate action.

  1. With the removal spell on the stack, cast Crimson Wisps or Expedite targeting only Zada — your spell resolves first and every copy draws.
  2. Spend the rest of the turn's mana on a second instant aimed at Zada before the removal resolves.
  3. Return Zada to the command zone rather than the graveyard.
  4. Spend the cards you just drew on bodies, not on the payoff.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Zada at {5}{R} only with three or more creatures already on the battlefield.
  2. Equip Swiftfoot Boots to Zada in your main phase, before the next cantrip rather than after it.
  3. Run the storm half without her: two rituals into Empty the Warrens with Impact Tremors on the battlefield is checker-passed and names no commander (stack 002).
  4. Cast Frontline Heroism to copy a single-target spell once, at a Soldier it makes, on the turns Zada is in the command zone.
NOTERenegade Tactics and Wild Ride are sorceries and cannot be cast with removal on the stack. Crimson Wisps, Expedite, Fists of Flame and Ancestors' Aid are the instants that target one creature, and Fists of Flame draws before it pumps.

3.5COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. an opponent is assembling a loop, and one card in the 99 answers a noncreature permanent at instant speed.

Indications.

  • An opponent searching their library twice in one turn.
  • An opponent untapping with six or more mana and cards in hand.
  • A permanent entering that has ended a game at this table before.
  • An opponent holding priority after their own spell resolves.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Chaos Warp on the piece already on the battlefield. Grepping all 79 entries, it is the one card that answers an enchantment or a land.
  2. Sacrifice a Goblin to Goblin Trashmaster to destroy an artifact piece; the ability costs no mana and works while you are tapped out.
  3. Cycle Gempalm Incinerator for {1}{R} at a creature piece; X is every Goblin on the battlefield.
  4. Attack the combo seat with the whole board and keep nothing back for a second turn.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Vandalblast on your next turn for {R} at their artifact, or overloaded for {4}{R} at every artifact you do not control.
  2. Point Goblin Bombardment, Pashalik Mons and Siege-Gang Commander at the combo seat until it is dead.
  3. Cast Empty the Warrens and Grapeshot on the turn you hold them rather than waiting for a larger storm count.
  4. Tell the seat holding counterspells which permanent you cannot answer, and ask them to hold up mana for it.
NOTENothing in the 99 touches a graveyard. A loop that rebuilds out of a graveyard is answered by killing the player, and the clock for that is the board you already have.

3.6MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the land drops have stopped, or the lands in play make no red.

Indications.

  • Three lands on turn five.
  • Two or more of Forgotten Cave, Smoldering Crater, Hidden Volcano and Spinerock Knoll in hand; all four enter tapped.
  • Fountainport, Goblin Burrows, Kher Keep, Reliquary Tower or War Room as your only untapped land.
  • A hand of four- and five-drops with Sol Ring undrawn.

Immediate action.

  1. Cycle Forgotten Cave for {R} or Smoldering Crater for {2} to dig for a land.
  2. Cast Sol Ring or Ruby Medallion ahead of any body.
  3. Cast Skirk Prospector and Impulsive Pilferer: one turns a Goblin into {R}, the other becomes a Treasure when it dies.
  4. Cast Faithless Looting and discard the five-drops.
  5. Activate War Room for {3}, a tap and one life at the end of a turn you cast nothing.

Subsequent.

  1. Play the tapped lands on turns you cast nothing and the untapped ones on turns you cast something.
  2. Cast Brightstone Ritual with two or more Goblins on the battlefield and Battle Hymn with three or more creatures; below that they cost more than they add.
  3. Cast Glimpse the Impossible only with four mana left afterwards — the three exiled cards are playable this turn only.
  4. Activate Fountainport for {2} and a spare token to draw a card once the board is wide enough to lose one.
NOTEFive of the fifteen land entries — Fountainport, Goblin Burrows, Kher Keep, Reliquary Tower and War Room — tap for {C} and no red. Count red sources rather than lands when you decide whether a hand casts {R}{R} on turn two.

3.7POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has decided you are the deck to kill, and the board is 1/1s.

Indications.

  • Two seats attacking you while a third builds.
  • An opponent naming Zada before you have cast her.
  • Removal aimed at Impact Tremors or Goblin Bombardment rather than at a creature.
  • The table stops trading with each other the turn after you cast Empty the Warrens.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast the payoff this turn rather than the turn after: Empty the Warrens, Grapeshot or Fists of Flame, at whatever count you have.
  2. Sacrifice Goblins to Goblin Bombardment and Siege-Gang Commander at the seat with the lowest life, before the board is answered.
  3. Leave Zada in the command zone and run the lines that name no commander (stacks 002 and 005).
  4. Block with the 1/1s. A body absorbing an attack is worth more than a copy count you never spend.

Subsequent.

  1. Attack the seat that attacked you, not the seat that is winning, until they stop.
  2. Rebuild only to the number of bodies you will spend next turn.
  3. Offer Goblin Bombardment and Gempalm Incinerator as the table's answer to the board that is winning, and use them there.
  4. Cast Chaos Warp on a threat aimed at another seat once, out loud.
NOTEIn the 2026-09-02 Forge run against giada-angels, baylen-tokens and abaddon the pod aimed a mean 0.31 interaction spells per game at this seat, against 1.21 received by giada-angels, and this seat was still eliminated in 90 of 100 games. Forge's AI does not read a Goblin board; a table does.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two to five lands, at least two of them adding red.
  • A creature costing two or less, or a token maker: Skirk Prospector, Goblin Lackey, Goblin Bushwhacker, Impulsive Pilferer, Mogg War Marshal, Searslicer Goblin, Rundvelt Hordemaster, Grenzo, Havoc Raiser, Conspicuous Snoop, Dragon Fodder, Krenko's Command.
  • A second body reachable by turn three from the cards in hand.
  • A red source you can tap on turn one.
  • Kher Keep counts as a body source: {1}{R} and a tap makes a 0/1 Kobold, every turn.

Ship on sight.

  • Cantrips and a copy engine with no creature under three mana. That is the hand that lost game 001: clean lands, one-mana instants and sorceries, Storm-Kiln Artist, zero Goblins.
  • A hand whose cheapest creature costs four or more. Three basics, Dwarven Mine and Krenko, Mob Boss is the hand that lost game 002.
  • Rituals with no board: Battle Hymn adds {R} for each creature you control, Brightstone Ritual for each Goblin on the battlefield.
  • Six or seven lands.
  • One land with no Sol Ring.
NOTEDwarven Mine is a body as well as a land, and it arrives late: it enters tapped unless you already control three other Mountains, and only an untapped Dwarven Mine makes the 1/1 Dwarf. That Dwarf counts for Zada's copy count and for Battle Hymn, and it is not a Goblin, so Brightstone Ritual does not count it. Game 002's opener was three basics, Dwarven Mine and Krenko, Mob Boss — the Dwarf was a turn-four body at the earliest, and the hand was shipped too late.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 79% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Play an untapped red source on turn one. Hold Forgotten Cave, Smoldering Crater, Hidden Volcano and Spinerock Knoll for a turn you cast nothing.
  2. Cast a one-mana Goblin on turn one: Skirk Prospector, Goblin Lackey, Impulsive Pilferer, or Goblin Bushwhacker unkicked.
  3. Cast Dragon Fodder or Krenko's Command on turn two, ahead of Sol Ring and ahead of any cantrip.
  4. Cast a third body on turn three: Mogg War Marshal, Searslicer Goblin, Rundvelt Hordemaster, or Goblin Matron fetching one of them.
  5. Count the creatures at the end of turn three. Cast Zada on turn four only if that count is three or more.
NOTEMogg War Marshal's echo is {1}{R} at your next upkeep. Declining it sacrifices the Marshal and makes another Goblin, so the body count is two either way — pay the echo only on a turn a third body changes a copy count.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Play Impact Tremors or General Kreat, the Boltbringer before the next token maker resolves; each creature entering afterwards is a damage trigger at every opponent.
  2. Play Goblin Warchief or Goblin Chieftain before Krenko, Mob Boss. The Warchief locks Krenko at {1}{R}{R} and the haste is what lets him tap the turn he lands (stack 004).
  3. Cast Zada with three or more other creatures already on the battlefield.
  4. Equip Swiftfoot Boots to Zada in your main phase, before the first cantrip.
  5. Cast Crimson Wisps, Expedite or Renegade Tactics targeting only Zada. Four other creatures is four copies plus the original, five cards (stack 001).
NOTEFrontline Heroism makes one 1/1 Soldier and copies the spell once, at that Soldier, however wide you are. It is redundancy for the effect and not for the multiplier.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Play the land. Play the untapped one and hold the cycling lands.
  2. Cast a body before you cast a spell.
  3. Count the creatures on the battlefield. That number is Zada's copy count, Battle Hymn's mana and Impact Tremors' damage per creature entering.
  4. Attack with at least one creature so Searslicer Goblin makes a Goblin at your end step, and so Grenzo, Havoc Raiser turns combat damage into a card off that player's library.
  5. Activate Idol of Oblivion at your end step on any turn a token entered.
  6. Spend leftover mana on War Room: {3}, a tap and one life for a card.
NOTEStorm counts spells CAST. Copies are put on the stack without being cast, so Empty the Warrens at storm count 3 makes eight tokens and a Grapeshot after it still sees a count of 4 (stack 002).

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count the creatures on the battlefield and add Zada. That number is the copy count.
  2. Total the damage before you cast anything: Impact Tremors and General Kreat pay one per creature entering, Goblin Bombardment one per body, Boggart Shenanigans and Pashalik Mons one per Goblin death, Siege-Gang Commander two per Goblin eaten.
  3. If the total is short of a seat's life, pass the turn with the payoff in hand and add a body.
  4. If it is lethal, grant haste — Goblin Bushwhacker kicked, Goblin Chieftain, Howlsquad Heavy or Goblin Warchief — then declare the attack.
  5. After blockers are declared, cast Fists of Flame targeting only Zada, then sacrifice the blocked Goblins to Goblin Bombardment at the player you are killing.
NOTEFists of Flame's copies do not grow retroactively. Each copy draws before it pumps, so the order they resolve in sets the size: stack 003 locks the tokens at +3/+0, +4/+0, +5/+0, +6/+0 and Zada at +7/+0 with trample, 32 power in total.

What this model does not do (19 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.

6.1Mana

Fifteen land entries and thirty-six copies, of which mana_analysis counts 33 as red sources: Fountainport, Reliquary Tower and War Room tap for {C} only. (It also credits Kher Keep red on a land whose printed mana ability reads '{T}: Add {C}', so 33 is the artifact's figure and an upper bound on it.) Add Sol Ring, Ruby Medallion and Throne of Eldraine. That part only has to hit drops. The part that decides games is the declared ritual group of four, which scales off the board rather than off lands: Battle Hymn adds {R} per creature, Brightstone Ritual {R} per Goblin, Seething Song is flat, Mana Geyser reads the opponents' tapped lands. Once bodies exist, mana is downstream of the body count. (The taxonomy reads ramp:ritual at 6, adding Ancestors' Aid and Glimpse the Impossible; this model files the second of those in fuel.) The brief's goldfish figure for turn five is 4.589 mean available mana — nowhere near a storm turn, which is the point: the big turn is bought with a ritual cast into an existing board, not with land drops. Goblin Warchief sits here as a cost reducer rather than with the Goblins: stack 004 locked Krenko at {1}{R}{R} because of it, and the haste it grants is what let Krenko tap the turn he landed. Ancestors' Aid is filed here because through the hub its Treasure clause is a Treasure per creature — a ritual with a pump stapled on. The utility lands are mostly NOT here for their mana: Reliquary Tower's 'no maximum hand size' is why a deck that draws five cards off one {R} (stack 001) plays a colourless land, War Room's '{3}, {T}, pay life: draw a card' is repeatable advantage that deck-audit lists under card-advantage's uncounted, and Fountainport turns a spent token into a card.

6.2Ignition

Every multiplier in this deck multiplies by the number of creatures already on the battlefield. Zada with an empty board copies nothing. Brightstone Ritual adds one {R}. Impact Tremors fires once. So the first thing that has to happen is not the hub and not a ritual — it is bodies, cheaply, early. Dragon Fodder and Krenko's Command are two 1/1s for two mana; Mogg War Marshal is two over its life and replaces itself when it dies; Searslicer Goblin is one more at every end step you attacked. The brief prices the declared group at 5 cards, 31.2% in an opener and 38.6% by turn three, assembling 60% of games at mean turn 3.184 — the earliest of the three declared targets by 1.730 turns against the next-earliest, the storm payoff plus ritual at mean turn 4.914, and the reason this deck can afford a five-mana payoff. The declared group's fifth card is Skirk Prospector, which creates no tokens; it is held in conversion here, where it eats them, and that difference is filed as a proposed goldfish edit so the price and the stage can agree.

6.3Fuel

Storm counts CASTS, and stack 002 established the hard half of that: copies are put on the stack without being cast, so a storm count of 3 becomes 8 tokens but the next spell still only sees 4. Nothing here can be replaced by a copy — every point of count has to be paid for one card at a time, which is why fuel is one of the two largest non-mana stages, 13 cards, tied with output. Crimson Wisps, Expedite and Renegade Tactics cost {R} and cantrip on their own; pointed at the hub they draw one card per body (stack 001: five cards off a single {R} with four Goblins out). Witch's Mark, Faithless Looting, Glimpse the Impossible and Daring Discovery are the deck's paper card advantage, and not one of them refills a hand: Faithless Looting is draw two then discard two, net zero and a filter; Witch's Mark is net +1 and gated on already holding a card to pitch, which is the resource a storm turn has just spent; Glimpse the Impossible exiles three cards playable only this turn; Daring Discovery is Discover 4, which is one card. deck-audit reads card-advantage at 8 copies against a floor of 10, verdict under. The rule above therefore has no exception. Goblin Matron and Moggcatcher find the missing piece; Conspicuous Snoop casts Goblins off the top of the library. Past in Flames gives flashback to every instant and sorcery in the graveyard, which after a storm turn is where the whole hand went: that is the rebuild plan, and it is one card. Goblin Dark-Dwellers recasts exactly one spell, and only at mana value 3 or less — a far smaller second option, not redundancy for it. Grenzo turns combat damage into impulse cards.

6.4Fodder

Goblins are the resource this deck converts, and they are spent four different ways: counted (Zada's copy count, Brightstone Ritual, Battle Hymn, Impact Tremors), sacrificed for mana or damage, clamped for cards, and pumped for lethal. So the pile is a stage in its own right rather than a threat count. Krenko, Mob Boss is the largest single producer — stack 004 measured one activation at five tokens, because Krenko counts himself — four Goblins were out when he was cast and five when the ability resolved — leaving ten Goblins on the table. Howlsquad Heavy and Goblin Chieftain hand the pile haste, which matters because most of these bodies are made on the turn they are meant to be spent. Goblin Chieftain, Rundvelt Hordemaster and Goblin Trashmaster are lords: they raise what every 1/1 is worth in combat and, in Hordemaster's case, turn Goblin deaths back into cards. Nothing here is a win condition on its own, and that is the correct reading of it.

6.5Conversion

Seven cards that take one resource and hand back another, cheaply and repeatably. Zada is the multiplier: an instant or sorcery targeting only her is copied once per other creature, so the board size is the exchange rate (stack 001: four copies plus the original off one {R}). Frontline Heroism is redundancy for the EFFECT and not for the multiplier — read the text, it makes one Soldier and copies the spell once, at that token, however wide you are. Storm-Kiln Artist turns every cast and every copy into a Treasure, which is what makes the buyback loop mana-positive in stack 005. Skirk Prospector eats a Goblin for {R} — stack 004 measured at most ten, one shot, and it empties the board doing it. Skullclamp turns a 1/1 into two cards, which is why a sweeper is a draw step here. Goblin Bombardment is the free outlet, converting any body into a point of damage at instant speed. Idol of Oblivion draws one card per TURN, gated on having created a token — the one rate in this paragraph that is not per unit. Zada is this stage's single point of failure and the brief agrees from the other side: she is the only load-bearing card in the deck, the one card named by two passing stacks. Being the commander makes an answer a tax rather than a loss, but nothing else in the 99 multiplies, so the tax is charged every time the engine restarts. This is the stage the map cannot see: its seven cards have four different city addresses, because a card's text names what it pays and never what it eats.

Siege-Gang Commander, Siege-Gang Lieutenant and Broadside Bombardiers buy damage with a body at a cost, and all three can point it at a player. 7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Deploy bodies first and the damage payoff last. Four 1/1s draw no removal; four 1/1s beside Impact Tremors draw all of it.
  • Keep Krenko, Mob Boss in hand until the turn you can tap him — a Krenko that survives to untap is the table's first target.
  • Say what the board does when asked. A count of 1/1s is public information and the number the table is bad at is the copy count, not the board.
  • Cast the rituals in the same turn as the payoff. A Seething Song on an empty turn tells the table there is a storm turn coming.

7.2When to reveal

  • Hold Empty the Warrens, Haze of Rage and Fists of Flame until the turn they kill a seat.
  • Cast Grapeshot early only to kill a creature that is racing you; otherwise it is the last five points.
  • Play Impact Tremors on the turn the token maker follows it, not two turns before.
  • Cast Zada on a board of three or more. She reads as a 3/3 to a table that has not seen the cantrips.

7.3Alliances

  • Offer Goblin Bombardment and Gempalm Incinerator as the table's removal and spend them on the seat that is winning.
  • Say plainly that Chaos Warp is the only answer to an enchantment or a land in the deck and nothing here touches a graveyard, and ask the seat that carries those to spend theirs.
  • Trade a turn of not attacking a seat for a turn of them not attacking you, and take the deal that buys two turns rather than one.
  • Break the deal on the turn the board is lethal, and say so before you declare attackers.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Point every optional damage — Goblin Bombardment, Pashalik Mons, Boggart Shenanigans, Siege-Gang Commander, Siege-Gang Lieutenant, Broadside Bombardiers — at one seat until it is dead. Spread across three seats it kills nobody.
  • Attack the seat with the fewest untapped blockers; 1/1s connect where nothing blocks and nowhere else.
  • Cast Chaos Warp on the permanent that beats you, not the permanent that is biggest.
  • Cycle Gempalm Incinerator at the creature attacking you; X counts every Goblin on the battlefield, your opponents' included.
  • Kill the seat holding a sweeper before the seat holding a bigger board.
