1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Edgar mints a free Vampire on every Vampire cast and three replacement doublers turn one mint into eight; v1.1.0 kept the combat plan (a lord or anthem is drawn in 80.0% of games) and added a conversion layer beside it - seven sacrifice outlets feeding ten death payoffs (Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Cordial Vampire, Elenda, Blade of the Bloodchief, Crossway Troublemakers, Liliana, Skullclamp, High-Society Hunter for nontoken deaths, and Reactor Raid) that turn a dying body into cards, counters or life loss - and the two checker-passed kill routes, a doubled attack drain and a two-card lifegain loop, need no combat damage to land.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming, or is on the stack, and your board is committed

Indications.

  • A seat has four or more untapped lands, has cast nothing this turn cycle, and passed with the mana up.
  • Somebody has named you the problem out loud. Log 001, turn three: five Vampires for three mana spent, 'this is ridiculous', and every answer at the table pointed at Edgar from then on.
  • You have more bodies on the battlefield than the other seats combined.
  • No death payoff is on the battlefield: no Blood Artist, no Cruel Celebrant, no Cordial Vampire, no Elenda, the Dusk Rose, no Crossway Troublemakers, no Skullclamp, no Liliana, Dreadhorde General.
  • Anointed Procession, Caretaker's Talent or Sanguine Bond is on the battlefield. Those are enchantments, and Flawless Maneuver and Akroma's Will name creatures.
  • Two of the five logged games are filed under a sweeper, and the deck has no sweeper of its own by role.

Immediate action.

  1. Count the untapped lands at every seat before you cast your next Vampire, and name the seat that can sweep.
  2. Put a death payoff on the battlefield ahead of any further body. Cast Cruel Celebrant before Blood Artist: Celebrant bills each opponent, Blood Artist bills one target player.
  3. Stop deploying at the body that beats the table's board. Keep the next Vampire and the next doubler in hand.
  4. With a death payoff on the battlefield and the sweeper on the stack, sacrifice Elenda, the Dusk Rose to a free outlet, then let the sweeper resolve. She leaves 1/1 lifelink Vampires equal to her power, and each of them dies to the sweeper and bills the table again.
  5. Spend Boros Charm or Teferi's Protection only on the enchantments. Those two are the cards in the 99 that save Anointed Procession, Caretaker's Talent and Sanguine Bond; Flawless Maneuver and Akroma's Will leave them to die.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast a Vampire at mana value two or less to restart eminence. Thirteen are declared in the 99 and one is in hand in 95.0% of goldfish games, mean turn 2.22.
  2. Take Crossway Troublemakers' draw one trigger at a time and decline the 2 life on every trigger past the cards you need.
  3. Redeploy the worst threat first and keep the best one in hand for the second sweeper.
  4. Rebuild to the payoff, not to the bodies: a free outlet and a drain beat five 1/1 tokens.
  5. Cast Edgar Markov the first turn you can pay six. In log 001 he arrived at eight mana, and the turn he landed took a seat to 1 life.
NOTETeferi's Protection phases your board out, so nothing dies and no death payoff fires. On a board holding Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Elenda, the Dusk Rose, Crossway Troublemakers or Skullclamp it turns the refuel off. Log 002 prices the other choice: a wipe with the drains online killed eleven creatures, took 11 life off every opponent, gained 11, and left the pilot at 50 against a table at 20. Cast Teferi's Protection to save an engine you cannot rebuild, not creatures you can.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:002.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. one seat is answering your permanents one at a time

Indications.

  • The same seat has answered a permanent of yours on two consecutive turns. Log 002: Anointed Procession resolved and was removed the following turn, and the card-flow permanents went the same way.
  • An opponent is pinging your 1/1 tokens instead of attacking your large bodies. Log 004: Tom's Goblin Sharpshooter did this all game and fed the draw.
  • Your own answers have been in hand for two turns. Goldfish holds interaction in 88.3% of games by turn six and can pay for it in 26.9%.
  • Your mana is fully spent on bodies every turn. Log 002 names that as the structural half: a deck that cannot afford to hold up interaction does not run interaction.
  • The permanent they answered was a doubler or a draw engine, not a body.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the seat answering you and count its untapped mana.
  2. Leave one answer's worth of mana unspent from this turn on. Despark costs two, Anguished Unmaking and Stroke of Midnight three, Path to Exile one; Deflecting Swat and Flawless Maneuver cost nothing while Edgar Markov is on the battlefield.
  3. Spend that answer on the permanent that is answering you, not on the largest board.
  4. Equip Skullclamp to a 1/1 eminence token for {1} and draw two. Their next removal spell then costs them a card and costs you nothing.
  5. Redeploy in this order: free outlet, death payoff, doubler. The doubler draws the answer, so it goes last.

Subsequent.

  1. Re-equip Blade of the Bloodchief for {1} on your turn after they kill the body carrying it. The Equipment survives creature removal; the counters on the body do not.
  2. Cast the doubler on a turn you also cast a Vampire, so the extra bodies arrive before the answer does.
  3. Deploy the bodies that pay when they die first: Elenda, the Dusk Rose, Bloodtracker, Crossway Troublemakers, Liliana, Dreadhorde General.
  4. Recount the answering seat's mana each turn and cast into the window where it is tapped out.
NOTERead High-Society Hunter's clause before you count on it as a death payoff: it sees another NONTOKEN creature dying, so it is blind to every eminence token. Two further limits belong on the same page: Deflecting Swat only redirects a spell or ability that targets, and nothing in the 99 answers a land. No logged game is filed under this cause; logs 002 and 004 describe it from inside two games filed elsewhere.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:004.

3.3COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. an opponent is assembling a two-card kill and you hold no counterspell

Indications.

  • A seat has tutored. Log 004: Tom tutored a two-card infinite and closed the game before the pilot's lethal attack resolved.
  • A seat has stopped developing and holds no creatures. That is the seat that killed the table in log 004.
  • A seat is holding up mana and passing every turn.
  • There are no counterspells in the 99. Once a piece resolves, the answers are Despark, Anguished Unmaking and Stroke of Midnight, or nothing.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the seat that has stopped developing.
  2. Attack that seat first, ahead of the seat with the biggest board.
  3. Cast Despark or Anguished Unmaking on a combo piece the turn it resolves. Both are instants, and the piece's resolution is the last profitable moment.
  4. If the kill spell or ability targets, cast Deflecting Swat and change the target.
  5. Cast Vampiric Tutor in the end step before your turn for the missing half of your own route: Bloodletter of Aclazotz if Sanctum Seeker is out (stack 003), or Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Sanguine Bond if Bloodthirsty Conqueror is out (stacks 004 and 006). It puts the card on top, so your draw step is what finds it.

Subsequent.

  1. Take the drain window the turn it opens. Both verified loops end a pod turn on their own and need no combat damage; the attack does not.
  2. Count every opponent's untapped mana before you declare lethal.
  3. Recast Edgar Markov and rebuild the outlet-and-payoff pair. Eminence mints from a zone no answer reaches.
NOTEThe infinite is the ceiling, not the plan, and the pilot's stated direction keeps it subordinate rather than cutting it. Bloodthirsty Conqueror with Vito or Sanguine Bond assembles in 7.8% of goldfish games (15.1% with Vampiric Tutor) and 3.4% by turn six; the Bloodletter route assembles in 4.1% (9.4% assisted) and 1.6% by turn six. Both rest on one body each. Play the drain engine underneath them. Log 004's board was winning when the combo landed.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:004.

3.4STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. the board is even, you have cast one Vampire this game, and your hand does nothing

Indications.

  • You have cast one Vampire this game and Edgar Markov is already on the battlefield. Log 003 is that game exactly, from a turn-one four-mana opener.
  • Every opponent has a blocker larger than your largest Vampire.
  • No draw engine is on the battlefield. Goldfish draws one in 83.5% of games at mean turn 2.62, so this is the other 16.5%.
  • Your hand is lands.
  • 'Ran out of steam' or 'no card draw' is the cause the pilot names in logs 001, 002 and 003.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Reactor Raid for {2}{B}: sacrifice a token or an artifact, draw two, and play Midgar, City of Mako from exile on a later turn.
  2. Equip Skullclamp to a 1/1 eminence token for {1} and draw two.
  3. Cast the draw body before the lord: Bloodtracker, Champion of Dusk, Welcoming Vampire, Preacher of the Schism, Crossway Troublemakers.
  4. Pay {B} and 2 life to Bloodtracker on every turn it is on the battlefield. It draws one card per +1/+1 counter when it leaves. Log 004 names skipping this as the missed line of the night.
  5. Attack with Crossway Troublemakers on the battlefield. Attacking Vampires have deathtouch and lifelink, so a stalled board trades up.

Subsequent.

  1. Equip Blade of the Bloodchief to a lifelink Vampire for {1} and sacrifice tokens to a free outlet. Each death puts two counters on the equipped body, which is how a stalled board produces one Vampire worth attacking with.
  2. Activate Castle Locthwain for {1}{B}{B} while your hand is empty. It bills life equal to your hand size, which is why it is cheapest here.
  3. Channel Takenuma, Abandoned Mire for {3}{B}, one less per legendary creature you control, to mill three and return a creature.
  4. Cast Night's Whisper for two cards and 2 life, and stop paying life once a drain seat is live.
NOTEThe 28 August pivot asked for a self-mill package as a second way to dig and it was never built. Altar of Dementia and Takenuma, Abandoned Mire are the only cards in the 99 that mill, and Takenuma is the only one that returns anything from your graveyard. Until that changes the dig is card draw and the eminence tokens that feed it, so the outlet goes down before the lord, every time.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:003.

3.5POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has agreed you are the archenemy and is pointing its answers at you

Indications.

  • Somebody said it out loud on turn three. Log 001: five Vampires for three mana spent, 'this is ridiculous', and every answer at the table pointed at Edgar from then on.
  • You are the highest-life seat. Lifelink bodies, Elenda, the Dusk Rose and Preacher of the Schism push you there fast; log 002 had you at 50 against a table at 20 with an empty hand.
  • Two seats have passed with mana up while a third develops.
  • Your board shows a doubler. Anointed Procession, Mondrak, Glory Dominus and Elspeth, Storm Slayer are what a table reads as the threat.

Immediate action.

  1. Deploy the free outlet and the death drain before the doubler. Tokens and lifelink bodies read as fair.
  2. Keep Bloodthirsty Conqueror, Bloodletter of Aclazotz and Sanctum Seeker in hand until the turn you use them. In log 001 both cards that would have refuelled the deck were countered on the way down.
  3. Point Blood Artist's drain at the seat the table has already agreed to kill.
  4. Attack the seat that is winning and give the reason as board position. Log 004 removed Alex first on exactly that ground.
  5. Spend your removal on the permanent that is answering you and leave the biggest board for somebody else to handle.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild after each wipe. The low curve put a real board back in one or two turns, three times, in log 001.
  2. Recount who has the most life each turn. Preacher of the Schism draws while you are highest and mints a lifelink token while you attack the highest.
  3. Take the drain window the turn it opens, and expect every held answer to appear the moment you present lethal.
NOTEThe only measurement the bench holds on who gets attacked is Forge's AI, and its own limits file says it is not human politics: no deals, no grudges, no table talk, no memory of last turn. It attacks the seat that has dealt the most combat damage 69.2% of the time, 95% interval [0.682, 0.702], across 7,949 decisions in 840 games. Read it as a claim about how a board LOOKS, not about your pod. No logged game is filed under this cause; log 001 describes the dynamic and is filed under a sweeper.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:001.

3.6RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat is killing you with damage you cannot block, and your drain has not closed the table

Indications.

  • A seat is dealing damage without attacking. Log 005: Tom's Purphoros pings finished the game while your board was the largest at the table and Oliver was a turn behind all night.
  • A planeswalker is on the battlefield and no untapped Vampire is at home. Log 005: Elspeth was exposed while the attack went all-in on Tom, and she was taken down before the pings finished you.
  • You are the lowest-life seat and still paying for lands and cards.
  • The seat racing you has flyers and your Vampires are on the ground.
  • Against a pod holding a Purphoros pinger model, Forge eliminated this seat in 35 of 40 games, 12 of them by that seat, all 21 recorded eliminations by damage (n40, 2026-09-03, on v1.1.0).

Immediate action.

  1. Name the seat that is faster and count the damage it deals per permanent it plays, not the power on its board.
  2. Keep one deathtouch body home before you attack: Gifted Aetherborn, Vampire of the Dire Moon, Nighthawk Scavenger, Vampire Cutthroat. If Edgar Markov is on the battlefield, hold Deflecting Swat instead and attack with everything.
  3. Spend Anguished Unmaking or Despark on the damage source. Those two are the interaction copies that exile a noncreature permanent; Stroke of Midnight destroys, and Path to Exile, Winds of Abandon, Elspeth's -3 and Vish Kal want a creature.
  4. Stop paying life. Skip Champion of Dusk, Night's Whisper, Castle Locthwain, Bloodtracker's activation, Crossway Troublemakers' 2 life and Vampiric Tutor until the race is decided. A full tap cycle of this manabase costs 8 life.
  5. Cast Teferi's Protection on the turn the table can kill you. Until your next turn your life total cannot change, and it is the one card in the 99 whose text stops life loss.

Subsequent.

  1. Declare lethal the turn it is on the board. Logs 004 and 005 both reached a dominant board and lost after it.
  2. Resolve Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Sanguine Bond before the lifelink attack, so every point you gain is a point an opponent loses.
  3. Attack with every Vampire while Sanctum Seeker is on the battlefield. Each attacking Vampire bills each opponent for 1 whether or not it connects.
  4. Cast Akroma's Will with Edgar Markov on the battlefield and choose both modes: flying, vigilance and double strike, plus lifelink, indestructible and protection from each colour.
  5. Recount your life against the table's before you pay for another card.
NOTEPath to Exile and an overloaded Winds of Abandon hand the seat you slowed a basic land, and nothing in the 99 answers a land, so both leave a racing seat with more mana than it started with. Two limits on the figures: Forge's runs read the combat half only, because life loss that is not damage, which is every drain in this deck, never appears in a damage figure; and the Purphoros run above was measured on v1.1.0, one version behind the list you are holding.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:005.

3.7MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. you are short of black or short of a land drop, and the board has stalled at two bodies

Indications.

  • Black is the short colour: 32 sources against a 36 target, 78.5% on curve on lands alone and 85.5% with the rocks, across 38 black cards and 54 black pips.
  • You are holding Bloodletter of Aclazotz. Its {1}{B}{B}{B} is the only cost in the 99 with three identical coloured pips.
  • You missed a land drop. Goldfish hits turn three in 91.9% of games and turn six in 59.8%.
  • Your untapped lands are the ones that bill you: City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Caves of Koilos, Sulfurous Springs, Battlefield Forge, Silent Clearing and Ancient Tomb.
  • Three lands enter tapped unconditionally (Midgar, City of Mako, Nomad Outpost, Savai Triome) and twelve more enter tapped off curve.

Immediate action.

  1. Play the land that makes black.
  2. Cast the rock before the second body: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Indulgence, Herald's Horn or Patchwork Banner. One is in hand in 67.5% of games at mean turn 3.58.
  3. Tap an untapped creature to Survivors' Encampment for the colour you are missing.
  4. Sacrifice a creature to Master of Dark Rites for {B}{B}{B} and spend it on a Vampire, Cleric or Demon spell. That mana cannot pay an activated ability.
  5. Stop tapping the painlands once you are the lowest-life seat. A full tap cycle of this manabase costs 8 life.

Subsequent.

  1. Name Vampire on Herald's Horn as it enters and take the free look at the top card each upkeep.
  2. Cast Night's Whisper or Champion of Dusk to find land, and stop paying life once a drain seat is live.
  3. Crack Bloodstained Mire or Marsh Flats for the land that makes the colour you are missing, and take an untapped one where there is a choice.
  4. Hold the doubler until you can cast it and a Vampire in the same turn.
NOTENothing in the 99 answers a land: Despark, Anguished Unmaking and Stroke of Midnight are nonland-only, and Path to Exile and Winds of Abandon hit creatures. A land-based lock is raced, not answered. No logged game is filed under this cause, so this page is built from mana_analysis and the goldfish rather than from the table, and the pilot logs a missed land drop rather than a missed pip.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • A black source among the lands. Black carries 54 of the pips on 32 sources against a 36 target, and the model's keep rule counts lands and never their colours.
  • Two or three lands, or two lands plus Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or Talisman of Indulgence.
  • One play at mana value two or less: a Vampire, a free sacrifice outlet (Viscera Seer, Ashnod's Altar, Altar of Dementia), or a rock. Log 005 opened on a swamp into a turn-one Viscera Seer and held momentum all game.
  • A second spell you can cast by turn four: a draw engine (Bloodtracker, Welcoming Vampire, Preacher of the Schism, Skullclamp, Night's Whisper) or a second Vampire.

Ship on sight.

  • Two lands with no black source and nothing at mana value one or two.
  • A hand whose only action is Edgar Markov.
  • Five or more lands with no Vampire at mana value two or less.
  • Answers and no bodies. There are no counterspells here and every drain needs creatures.
NOTERead the colours by hand. The model's keep rule counts lands, so a two-land hand with no black source passes it and loses the game; log 002 kept that hand knowing better and names the keep as the mistake. On the model's own rule the deck can act by turn three in 96.4% of games at a mean 0.23 mulligans.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 81% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Play the land that makes black.
  2. Cast the rock before the second body: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Indulgence.
  3. Cast a Vampire at mana value one or two every turn you can. Each mints a 1/1 from the command zone whether or not Edgar has been cast (stacks 002 and 010).
  4. Name Vampire on Herald's Horn or Patchwork Banner as it enters.
  5. Count the table's untapped mana at the end of turn three before you add a fourth body.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Put a free sacrifice outlet on the battlefield: Viscera Seer, Ashnod's Altar or Altar of Dementia. One is in hand in 72.3% of games at mean turn 3.16.
  2. Put a death payoff beside it. Cruel Celebrant bills each opponent, Blood Artist bills one target player, Cordial Vampire puts a +1/+1 counter on every Vampire you control each time a creature dies.
  3. Cast Edgar Markov the first turn you can pay six, and cast him before you commit a planeswalker. Deflecting Swat and Flawless Maneuver cost nothing only while he is on the battlefield.
  4. Cast the doubler after the outlet and the payoff, not before.
  5. Equip Skullclamp to a 1/1 eminence token and draw two, or equip Blade of the Bloodchief to a lifelink Vampire and feed the outlet.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Sacrifice a token to the free outlet every turn a death payoff is on the battlefield.
  2. Draw before you deploy: Reactor Raid, Skullclamp, Bloodtracker, Castle Locthwain, Minas Tirith.
  3. Pay {B} and 2 life to Bloodtracker on every turn it is on the battlefield.
  4. Attack the seat with the most life with Preacher of the Schism for a 1/1 lifelink Vampire, and draw a card off it on every turn you are the seat with the most life.
  5. Leave one answer's worth of mana unspent. Goldfish holds interaction in 88.3% of games by turn six and can pay for it in 26.9%.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count the damage on the board before anything else: board power, plus 1 per attacking Vampire per opponent from Sanctum Seeker, plus 1 per opponent per death from Cruel Celebrant.
  2. Name the route if the board is short: Sanctum Seeker attacking with Bloodletter of Aclazotz out (stack 003), or Bloodthirsty Conqueror with Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Sanguine Bond (stacks 004 and 006).
  3. Count every opponent's untapped mana and name the answer each seat can be holding.
  4. Hold Deflecting Swat or Flawless Maneuver up for the turn you commit, not the turn after. Both are free while Edgar Markov is on the battlefield.
  5. Declare the attack the turn lethal is on the board. Logs 004 and 005 both reached a dominant board and lost after it.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (31 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • A board of 1/1 tokens and lifelink bodies reads as fair. Anointed Procession, Mondrak, Glory Dominus and Elspeth, Storm Slayer are what a table reads as the threat, so they land after the outlet and the payoff.
  • Five bodies on turn three off three mana spent is what got this deck named archenemy in log 001. Price the attention in rather than trying to avoid it.
  • Lifelink, Elenda, the Dusk Rose and Preacher of the Schism make you the visible leader while your hand empties. Log 002: 50 life against a table at 20, with nothing left to cast.
  • Forge's AI attacks the seat that has dealt the most combat damage 69.2% of the time, [0.682, 0.702] over 7,949 decisions. Its own limits file records no deals, no grudges and no memory, so take it as a reading on how a board looks, not on your pod.

7.2When to reveal

  • Show the tokens. Keep Bloodthirsty Conqueror, Bloodletter of Aclazotz and Sanguine Bond in hand until the turn they close: log 001 lost Bloodthirsty Conqueror to a counterspell on the way down.
  • Cast the doubler on a turn you also cast a Vampire, so the extra bodies arrive before the answer does.
  • Cast Edgar Markov the first turn you can pay six and take the heat that comes with him.
  • Say the eminence trigger out loud on every Vampire spell, including with Edgar in the command zone and never cast. Stack 010 proves the token is made and that Elspeth, Storm Slayer doubles it.
  • Commit a planeswalker only on a turn you can leave a blocker or a free answer up. Log 005: Elspeth came down, the attack went all-in on Tom, and she was taken down before the pings finished you.

7.3Alliances

  • Point Blood Artist's drain at the seat the table has already agreed to kill; Cruel Celebrant hits everyone and needs no such choice.
  • Ask the seat holding counterspells to cover the combo seat. You hold no stack interaction and five spot answers, so trade the jobs rather than duplicating them. Log 005 seated an Azorius counters deck at the same table as the deck that killed you.
  • Attack the seat that is winning and give the reason as board position. Log 004 removed Alex first on that ground and records it as position, not grudge.
  • Name the seat that has answered you twice and ask the table to split its answers. Log 002 lost to one seat picking off the card-flow permanents unopposed.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Kill the seat that deals damage without attacking before the seat with the biggest board. In log 005 Tom's board was not the problem and Tom's pings closed the game.
  • Kill the seat that has stopped developing before the seat with the biggest board. In log 004 the seat holding no creatures is the seat that comboed out.
  • Aim removal at the permanent that answers you, not at the largest creature. Despark exiles a permanent at mana value four or greater for two mana, Anguished Unmaking exiles any nonland permanent for three mana and 3 life, Stroke of Midnight destroys a nonland permanent and hands back a 1/1.
  • Spend no answer on a land. Nothing in the 99 answers one, so a land-based lock is raced.
  • Deploy Cruel Celebrant ahead of Blood Artist when both are castable: each opponent against one target player.
