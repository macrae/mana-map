1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Edgar turns every Vampire cast into a 1/1 from the command zone, free outlets turn those bodies into deaths, death drains bill the table on each one and four gain-to-loss converters bill it again on every gain (three by the amount, Marauding Blight-Priest by the event) - and with the two-card infinite cut for bracket 3, the game ends by accumulation, its one verified finisher a Bloodletter attack (stack 003: seven Sanctum Seeker triggers, each doubled, fourteen a seat).

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming, or is on the stack, and the board is committed

Indications.

  • A seat has four or more lands untapped, has cast nothing this turn cycle, and passed with the mana up.
  • Somebody has named you the problem. Log 001, turn three: five Vampires off three mana spent, 'this is ridiculous', and every answer at the table pointed at Edgar from then on.
  • You have more creatures than the other seats combined and no free sacrifice outlet on the battlefield.
  • The outlet, the death drain and the converter are in hand rather than on the battlefield. Log 006: Vish Kal went down before the wipes and Blood Artist arrived after them, so neither did its job.
  • Three of the six logged games are filed under a sweeper (001, 002, 006), and the pilot's read of this pod is 'heavy creature removal and heavy board wipes' (002).

Immediate action.

  1. Count the untapped lands at every seat before you cast the next Vampire, and name the seat that can sweep.
  2. Put a free sacrifice outlet and a death drain on the battlefield ahead of any further body. Outlets: Viscera Seer, Phyrexian Tower, Ashnod's Altar, Yahenni, Undying Partisan, Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter. Drains: Cruel Celebrant and Vraan, Executioner Thane bill each opponent; Blood Artist and Vein Ripper bill one target.
  3. Put Sanguine Bond or Enduring Tenacity down before Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Marauding Blight-Priest. The enchantment survives a creature sweeper and turns every drain's gain into a second loss after it.
  4. With the sweeper on the stack and Edgar Markov on the battlefield, cast Flawless Maneuver for nothing if the sweeper destroys. If it exiles, bounces or gives -X/-X, cast Teferi's Protection instead.
  5. If you cast neither, sacrifice every creature the sweeper would take to the outlet while it is on the stack, Elenda, the Dusk Rose last. When Enduring Tenacity dies, put its return trigger on the stack above your death drains so it is back as an enchantment when they resolve.

Subsequent.

  1. Draw off the dead first: Grim Haruspex and Midnight Reaper draw for each nontoken creature of yours that died with them, Morbid Opportunist once, Liliana, Dreadhorde General for every creature. Pay Crossway Troublemakers' and Erebos, Bleak-Hearted's 2 life a card while you are not the lowest-life seat.
  2. Play a land. Bloodghast returns on the land drop; it is a nontoken body, so the outlet that eats it draws off Grim Haruspex and Midnight Reaper.
  3. Cast a Vampire at mana value two or less to restart eminence. Eleven are in the 99; a castable Vampire is drawn in 71.8% of goldfish games by turn six.
  4. Rebuild to outlet, drain, converter, in that order, and stop there. Five 1/1 tokens are what the next sweeper is for; log 006's rebuilds were 'small blockers, nothing threatening'.
  5. Cast Edgar Markov the first turn you can pay for him. On the battlefield he makes Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat free for the next sweeper.
NOTEKalitas, Traitor of Ghet changes what a sweeper does. While he is on the battlefield, an opponent's nontoken creature is exiled instead of dying, and that holds if he dies in the same sweeper (official ruling, an input): you get a 2/2 Zombie for each, after the sweeper, so they survive it. The cost is that Blood Artist, Vein Ripper, Morbid Opportunist, Cordial Vampire, Elenda and Yahenni see none of those deaths. Teferi's Protection stops your life total changing, so no converter fires while it holds; cast it to keep Sanguine Bond, Anointed Procession or Elspeth, Storm Slayer, never to save tokens. Enduring Tenacity's return is established for destroy (stack 012, cited for that clause only), not for exile or bounce. All six logged games predate v3.0.0 (001-003 on v1.0.0, 004-005 on v1.1.0, 006 on v1.1.1); the v3.0.0 draw bodies (Grim Haruspex, Midnight Reaper, Kalitas) have met no sweeper at the table.

Drawn from 3 logged game(s): log:001, log:002, log:006.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. one seat is answering your permanents one at a time, or Edgar, a converter or the doubler is targeted

Indications.

  • The same seat has answered a permanent of yours on two consecutive turns. Log 002: Anointed Procession resolved and was gone the following turn, and the draw permanents went the same way.
  • A spell or ability is pointed at Edgar Markov, Bloodletter of Aclazotz, Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Elspeth, Storm Slayer. Log 001: Elspeth was targeted the turn she landed; log 005: she was taken down with no blocker home.
  • A seat is pinging your 1/1 tokens instead of attacking. Log 004: Tom's Goblin Sharpshooter did, and the deaths drew cards.
  • Your answers have sat in hand for two turns with the mana spent on Vampires. Goldfish: interaction is in hand in 89.4% of games by turn six and castable in 27.3%.
  • The permanent they answered was a doubler, a draw engine or a converter, not a body.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the seat answering you and count its untapped mana at the end of each of your turns.
  2. If the spell targets and Edgar Markov is on the battlefield, cast Deflecting Swat for nothing and move it to a permanent of theirs or a token of yours.
  3. If the creature cannot be saved, sacrifice it to a free outlet in response. The death pays through Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Vraan, Executioner Thane, Vein Ripper and Morbid Opportunist, and through Grim Haruspex and Midnight Reaper if it is a nontoken creature; their spell does nothing.
  4. If the target is Enduring Tenacity as a creature and the spell destroys it, let it resolve: it returns as an enchantment with its drain intact.
  5. Leave one answer's mana unspent from this turn on: {1}{W}{B} for Anguished Unmaking, {W} for Path to Exile; Deflecting Swat and Flawless Maneuver cost nothing while Edgar Markov is on the battlefield.

Subsequent.

  1. Redeploy in this order: free outlet, death drain, then the converter or the doubler. The piece that draws the answer goes last, on a turn you hold Deflecting Swat or Flawless Maneuver.
  2. Put Sanguine Bond down before Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose and Marauding Blight-Priest. Creature removal does not reach it.
  3. Cast Vein Ripper into the removal-heavy seat. Ward makes their spell cost a creature, and that creature's death is a Vein Ripper trigger unless Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet exiles it.
  4. Play a land every turn. Bloodghast returns on the land drop and is the body you hand to edicts and pings.
  5. Cast Drana and Linvala when a creature's activated ability is killing your engine rather than feeding it. In log 004 the pings fed the draw, so read which it is before you cast her.
NOTEDeflecting Swat changes targets; it does nothing against a sweeper or an edict that names no target. Anowon, the Ruin Sage's upkeep edict is yours too and takes a non-Vampire of yours: Grim Haruspex, Midnight Reaper, Morbid Opportunist, Ingris Stingerquill, Enduring Tenacity, an animated Erebos, or a Zombie or Soldier token. Hold a Kalitas Zombie or a Liliana Zombie for it, and do not cast Anowon on a turn your only non-Vampire is a draw body. No logged game is filed under this cause; 001, 002, 004 and 005 describe it from inside games filed elsewhere. All six logged games predate v3.0.0 (001-003 on v1.0.0, 004-005 on v1.1.0, 006 on v1.1.1).

Drawn from 4 logged game(s): log:001, log:002, log:004, log:005.

3.3COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. a seat is assembling a kill you cannot block, and you hold no counterspell and no loop of your own

Indications.

  • A seat has tutored. Log 004: Tom tutored a two-card infinite and closed the game before your lethal attack resolved.
  • A seat has stopped developing and holds no creatures. That is the seat that killed the table in log 004.
  • A seat passes every turn with mana up and a full hand.
  • No card in the 99 counters a spell. The instants are Anguished Unmaking, Path to Exile, Deflecting Swat, Flawless Maneuver, Akroma's Will, Teferi's Protection and Vampiric Tutor.
  • Your own clock is accumulation: v3.0.0 holds no two-card infinite (deck-facts: 0 contained lines), and the goldfish's median kill turn is 8.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the seat that has stopped developing and count its untapped mana.
  2. Cast Anguished Unmaking on their first piece the turn it resolves. It exiles any nonland permanent at instant speed; Path to Exile answers a creature piece for {W}.
  3. If the combo runs through a creature's activated ability, cast Drana and Linvala: those abilities cannot be activated while she is on the battlefield.
  4. If you hold no answer, cast Vampiric Tutor in the end step before your turn for Anguished Unmaking, or Demonic Tutor on your turn with the mana to cast what it finds.
  5. If the kill targets, cast Deflecting Swat with Edgar Markov on the battlefield and change the target. If it is life loss or damage, cast Teferi's Protection: your life total cannot change until your next turn.

Subsequent.

  1. Attack the combo seat ahead of the seat with the biggest board. Sanctum Seeker bills it on the attack declaration whether or not anything connects.
  2. Cast Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet against a combo that loops an opponent's nontoken creature through death or the graveyard: it is exiled instead of dying.
  3. Cast Anowon, the Ruin Sage against a non-Vampire creature combo: each upkeep that seat sacrifices a non-Vampire creature.
  4. Count every opponent's untapped mana before you declare lethal, and take the Bloodletter of Aclazotz turn the turn it opens.
NOTEThe tutors fetch answers now. With the two-card infinite cut in v3.0.0, nothing in the 99 ends the game on assembly, so a tutor spent on a payoff while a combo seat is live is a tutor not spent on Anguished Unmaking. Kalitas exiles nontoken creatures only; a token loop goes through him. Log 004 is on v1.1.0, when the deck carried its own infinite; no game on v3.0.0 has met a combo seat.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:004.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. you are short of a land drop, or of the black for a triple-pip spell

Indications.

  • You missed a land drop. Goldfish hits the turn-three drop in 88.8% of games, turn four in 76.0%, turn six in 60.3%.
  • You are holding Bloodletter of Aclazotz or Vein Ripper. {1}{B}{B}{B} and {3}{B}{B}{B} are the two triple-black costs in the 99.
  • Your untapped lands are the ones that bill you: City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Caves of Koilos, Sulfurous Springs, Battlefield Forge, Silent Clearing. A full tap cycle of the recurring ones costs 6 life (mana_analysis).
  • Twelve of 35 lands enter tapped on some condition; Midgar, City of Mako and Savai Triome always do.
  • You kept two lands. Log 002: a two-land keep after one mulligan, which the pilot names as the mistake.

Immediate action.

  1. Play the land that makes black and enters untapped. Play a Swamp before Castle Locthwain so the Castle enters untapped.
  2. Cast the rock before the second body: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Hierarchy, Talisman of Indulgence, Talisman of Conviction, Orzhov Signet, Rakdos Signet.
  3. Tap an untapped creature to Survivors' Encampment for the colour you are missing, or tap Exotic Orchard for a colour an opponent's land makes.
  4. Sacrifice a token to Phyrexian Tower for {B}{B}, which pays any cost, or to Master of Dark Rites for {B}{B}{B}, which pays a Vampire spell and nothing else; Bloodletter of Aclazotz, Vein Ripper and Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet are Vampires.
  5. Cast Reactor Raid for {2}{B}: sacrifice a token, draw two, and play Midgar, City of Mako from exile as a later land drop.

Subsequent.

  1. Crack a fetchland for the dual or Swamp that makes the colour you are short.
  2. Cast Urza's Incubator naming Vampire: Vampire creature spells cost up to {2} less, and Edgar Markov costs {1}{R}{W}{B}.
  3. Stop tapping the painlands once you are the lowest-life seat.
  4. Hold the doubler until the turn you can cast it and a Vampire together.
NOTENo logged game is filed under this cause; log 002's two-land keep is the nearest and is filed under wipe. On v3.0.0 black reads 32 land sources against a 36 target, 38 with rocks and Master of Dark Rites; red 22 of 22 and white 24 of 22 with rocks (mana_analysis, this sha). v3.0.0 traded a colourless land for a fourth Swamp. Draw on this list is mostly death- and entry-triggered, so a dry turn eight is a cards problem as often as a mana problem; the stalled page is the other one to open. All six logged games predate v3.0.0 (001-003 on v1.0.0, 004-005 on v1.1.0, 006 on v1.1.1).

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.5STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. the board is even, the hand is empty, and the drain has not started

Indications.

  • You have cast one Vampire this game and Edgar Markov is already on the battlefield. Log 003 is that game, from a four-mana turn one.
  • Your hand is lands by turn seven. 'Ran out of steam' is the pilot's own phrase in 001, 002, 003 and 006.
  • No draw engine is on the battlefield. One is drawn in 63.6% of goldfish games by turn six.
  • Every opponent has a blocker larger than your largest Vampire, and the free outlet has nothing left to eat.
  • Bodies die and nothing draws: your deaths are tokens, and your death-draw is Grim Haruspex or Midnight Reaper, which read nontoken creatures only.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast Reactor Raid for {2}{B}: sacrifice a token, draw two.
  2. Put a draw body down before a lord: Welcoming Vampire, Morbid Opportunist, Grim Haruspex, Midnight Reaper, Preacher of the Schism, Clavileño, First of the Blessed, Crossway Troublemakers, Twilight Prophet; or Phyrexian Arena, Caretaker's Talent or Liliana, Dreadhorde General.
  3. Feed the draw what it reads. Tokens to Morbid Opportunist, Liliana, Crossway Troublemakers and Erebos, Bleak-Hearted; Bloodghast and other nontoken bodies to Grim Haruspex and Midnight Reaper. Pay the 2 life where it is asked.
  4. Attack the seat with the most life with Preacher of the Schism for a lifelink token, and a card if you are the seat with the most life. Attack with two creatures and pay {1}{W} to Minas Tirith for a card.
  5. Activate Castle Locthwain for {1}{B}{B} while your hand is empty: it costs 1 life then, and more for every card you are holding.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast Diabolic Intent sacrificing a token for the draw engine or the converter you are missing; the sacrifice is a death trigger.
  2. Channel Takenuma, Abandoned Mire for {3}{B}, one less per legendary creature you control, to return a creature from the graveyard to hand.
  3. Attack with Clavileño, First of the Blessed out and name the Vampire you send into the blocker: when it dies it draws a card and leaves a tapped 4/3 flier.
  4. Sacrifice tokens to Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter when the stall is size: one flying lifelinker attacks past the ground, its gain feeds any converter, and the counters come off as -1/-1 on the blocker.
  5. Cast Edgar Markov. The attack trigger puts a counter on each Vampire, and the haste swing is the turn log 001 took a seat to 1 and log 003 to twelve commander damage.
NOTEv3.0.0 added Grim Haruspex and Midnight Reaper as the answer to the empty hand, and the exception that bites is that neither draws off an eminence token. Bloodghast is the body that feeds them on repeat. Midnight Reaper deals 1 damage to you per card; it is not life gain and no converter reads it. The goldfish reads 2.86 extra cards by the end of turn eight on v3.0.0 with 10 of 18 draw cards modelled, a floor. All six logged games predate v3.0.0 (001-003 on v1.0.0, 004-005 on v1.1.0, 006 on v1.1.1); whether v3.0.0 stops running dry is the open question CONTEXT.md names, and no game has answered it.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:003, log:006.

3.6POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. the table has agreed you are the problem and its answers are pointed at you

Indications.

  • Somebody said it on turn three. Log 001: five Vampires off three mana, 'this is ridiculous', and every answer at the table pointed at Edgar after that.
  • You are the highest-life seat. Lifelink and the drains put you there; log 002 had you at 50 against a table at 20 with an empty hand.
  • Two seats have passed with mana up while a third develops.
  • A converter, a doubler or the drain doubler is on your battlefield: Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose, Sanguine Bond, Enduring Tenacity, Marauding Blight-Priest, Anointed Procession, Elspeth, Storm Slayer or Bloodletter of Aclazotz.

Immediate action.

  1. Deploy the free outlet and the death drain before the doubler and before the converters. Tokens and 1/1 lifelinkers read as fair.
  2. Keep Bloodletter of Aclazotz in hand until the turn you attack with it out, and cast it with Deflecting Swat or Flawless Maneuver held and Edgar Markov on the battlefield.
  3. Point Blood Artist's, Vein Ripper's, Vito's, Sanguine Bond's and Enduring Tenacity's triggers at the seat the table has already agreed to kill. Cruel Celebrant, Vraan, Executioner Thane and Marauding Blight-Priest need no choice.
  4. Attack the seat that is winning, and give the reason as board position. Log 004 removed Alex on that ground.
  5. Spend Anguished Unmaking on the permanent that is answering you, and leave the biggest board to the other two seats.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild after each wipe to outlet, drain, converter, not to width. The low curve put a board back in a turn or two, three times, in log 001.
  2. Recount who has the most life each turn. Preacher of the Schism draws while you lead and mints a lifelink token when you attack the leader.
  3. Cast Charismatic Conqueror while the table has a bigger problem than you; its tax reads every seat at once.
  4. Count the table's open mana before you commit, and expect every held answer the turn you present lethal.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat is killing you with damage you cannot block, and the drain has not closed the table

Indications.

  • A seat is dealing damage without attacking. Log 005: Tom's Purphoros pings finished the game while your board was the largest at the table.
  • A planeswalker is on the battlefield and no untapped Vampire is home. Log 005: Elspeth exposed, taken down, then the pings.
  • You are the lowest-life seat and still paying life for painlands, Phyrexian Arena, Crossway Troublemakers, Erebos, Bleak-Hearted and Midnight Reaper.
  • The seat racing you has fliers and your blockers are 1/1 tokens.
  • Lethal is on your board and you have not declared it. Logs 004 and 005 both reached it and lost after.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the faster seat and count the damage it deals per permanent it plays, not the power on its board.
  2. Cast Anguished Unmaking on the damage source. It exiles, so an indestructible enchantment goes with it. Path to Exile answers a creature source; Deflecting Swat with Edgar Markov out answers a ping that targets.
  3. Stop paying life for cards: Crossway Troublemakers, Erebos, Bleak-Hearted, Castle Locthwain, Vampiric Tutor. Every Midnight Reaper trigger is 1 damage to you.
  4. Turn the gain into their loss: with Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose, Sanguine Bond or Enduring Tenacity out, point every trigger at the racing seat.
  5. Cast Teferi's Protection the turn the table can kill you: your life total cannot change until your next turn.

Subsequent.

  1. Declare lethal the turn it is on the board.
  2. Keep Preacher of the Schism home as a deathtouch blocker before you attack. If Edgar Markov is on the battlefield, hold Flawless Maneuver instead and attack with everything.
  3. Attack with every Vampire while Sanctum Seeker is on the battlefield: each attacker bills each opponent 1 on declaration, 2 with Bloodletter of Aclazotz out on your turn. Stack 003: seven attackers, 14 a seat before blockers.
  4. Activate Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose for {3}{B}{B} before combat, or cast Akroma's Will with Edgar Markov out and take both modes: each lifelinker that connects is a separate gain, and Marauding Blight-Priest bills each opponent 1 for each.
  5. Cast Anowon, the Ruin Sage against a creature-ETB racer: each upkeep that seat sacrifices a non-Vampire creature.
NOTENothing on v3.0.0 gains you life when an opponent loses it, so a ping aimed at you refunds nothing; the gain against a racer is lifelink, Cruel Celebrant, Vraan, Vein Ripper, Sanctum Seeker, Wispdrinker Vampire and Kalitas. Marauding Blight-Priest triggering once per lifelinker is the card's official ruling, an input with no stack on this list. Drana and Linvala stops activated abilities and does not stop a triggered ping. Log 005 is on v1.1.0 and no game on v3.0.0 has met a racer.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two or three lands with one that makes black, or two lands plus Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or a Talisman. Black carries 63 of the 92 coloured pips in the 99 (deck-facts).
  • One Vampire at mana value two or less: Viscera Seer, Master of Dark Rites, Tithe Drinker, Cruel Celebrant, Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord, Blood Artist, Vraan, Executioner Thane, Charismatic Conqueror, Bloodghast, Cordial Vampire or Bloodtithe Harvester. Log 005 opened on a one-drop off a Swamp and held momentum all game.
  • A second spell you can cast by turn four: a free outlet, a draw engine or a second Vampire.

Ship on sight.

  • Two lands with no black source and nothing at mana value two or less.
  • A hand whose only action is Edgar Markov or Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter.
  • Five or more lands with no Vampire at mana value two or less.
  • Answers and no bodies. Every death drain needs a death and every converter needs a gain.
NOTERead the colours by hand. The model's keep rule counts lands and not their colours, so a two-land hand with no black passes it; log 002 kept a two-land hand after one mulligan knowing it should go back. On the model's own rule v3.0.0 keeps 77.4% of first sevens, 93.2% of kept hands act by turn three, and the mean is 0.28 mulligans. A tutor in the opening hand is not half a combo any more; it is a card that finds the draw engine or the answer.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 77% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Play the land that makes black. Play a Swamp before Castle Locthwain.
  2. Cast the rock on turn one or two: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, a Talisman, a Signet.
  3. Cast a Vampire at mana value two or less every turn you can. Each is a 1/1 from the command zone with Edgar Markov uncast; his text reads 'in the command zone or on the battlefield'.
  4. Cast Urza's Incubator naming Vampire on turn three ahead of a four-mana Vampire.
  5. Count the table's untapped mana at the end of turn three before you add a fourth body.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Put a free sacrifice outlet on the battlefield: Viscera Seer, Phyrexian Tower, Ashnod's Altar, Yahenni, Undying Partisan or Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter. One is drawn in 71.6% of goldfish games, mean turn 3.66.
  2. Put a death drain beside it. Cruel Celebrant and Vraan, Executioner Thane bill each opponent; Blood Artist and Vein Ripper bill one target. One is drawn in 62.9% of games, mean turn 3.97.
  3. Put the draw engine down before the lord: Morbid Opportunist, Welcoming Vampire, Grim Haruspex, Midnight Reaper, Caretaker's Talent, Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Crossway Troublemakers.
  4. Cast Edgar Markov the first turn you can pay for him, and before you commit a planeswalker. Flawless Maneuver and Deflecting Swat cost nothing, and Akroma's Will takes both modes, only while he is on the battlefield.
  5. Land a converter: Sanguine Bond or Enduring Tenacity first, then Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Marauding Blight-Priest on a turn you hold Flawless Maneuver or Deflecting Swat.
  6. Cast the token doubler after the outlet and the drain, not before.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Play a land. Bloodghast returns from the graveyard and is the body the outlet eats first.
  2. Sacrifice a token to the free outlet every turn a death drain or a death-draw is on the battlefield. Morbid Opportunist and Vraan, Executioner Thane fire once a turn; Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Cruel Celebrant and Blood Artist on every death.
  3. Sacrifice Bloodghast, not a token, when Grim Haruspex or Midnight Reaper is out: they draw off nontoken deaths only.
  4. Pay the 2 life per death to Crossway Troublemakers and Erebos, Bleak-Hearted while you are not the lowest-life seat.
  5. Attack the seat with the most life with Preacher of the Schism. Attack with two creatures and pay {1}{W} to Minas Tirith for a card.
  6. Leave one answer's mana unspent at the end of each turn: {1}{W}{B} for Anguished Unmaking, {W} for Path to Exile, nothing for Deflecting Swat and Flawless Maneuver with Edgar Markov out.
  7. Keep Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter in hand until the wipe has passed, then cast him and feed him the tokens. Log 006: 'played it too early'.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count the drain before the damage. Per death: Cruel Celebrant 1 each opponent, Blood Artist 1 to a target, Vein Ripper 2 to a target, Vraan 2 each opponent once a turn. Per gain event: Marauding Blight-Priest 1 each opponent; Vito, Sanguine Bond and Enduring Tenacity that much to a target.
  2. Count the attack: Sanctum Seeker 1 per attacking Vampire per opponent, Ingris Stingerquill 1 per attacker per opponent. Each Sanctum Seeker trigger also gains you 1, which every converter reads.
  3. If Bloodletter of Aclazotz is in hand, cast it in your first main phase and attack the same turn. On your turn every opponent's loss is doubled; stack 003 is seven attackers through Sanctum Seeker, 14 a seat before blockers.
  4. Count every opponent's untapped mana and name the answer each seat can hold.
  5. Hold Deflecting Swat or Flawless Maneuver for the turn you commit, not the turn after. Both are free while Edgar Markov is on the battlefield.
  6. Declare the attack the turn lethal is on the board. Logs 004 and 005 both reached a winning board and lost after it.

What this model does not do (48 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Under model_hand_limit a hand over seven discards down at end of turn (CR 514.1) unless a permanent or a rest-of-the-game sorcery says no maximum hand size; a commander's own text and a raised limit ('your maximum hand size is ten') are not read.
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 redraws, keep the last. The first is free (CR 103.5c); the second bottoms a card, a land past the fourth or the dearest card.
  • The turn deploys before it wheels: the commander, then rocks, payoffs and bodies, then a wheel on what is left, then whatever the wheel found. A wheel or a loot goes first only to dig: no land in hand and a commander still in the zone. A loot waits for a payoff like a wheel.
  • Repeatable activations run until the mana, a library floor of LIFE_CHAIN_LIBRARY_FLOOR (10, authored) or a hand of seven stops them, the hand only when no payoff is paid per draw. Treasure pays a repeat only under Smothering Tithe, whose return is the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE (0.5). An activation's draws are not castable that turn: a floor.
  • A rock or a land sacrifices itself for a card only with ROCK_SAC_MIN_MANA (7, authored) left after it and nothing in hand that mana wants. Cumulative upkeep is paid to age CUMULATIVE_UPKEEP_MAX_AGE (3, authored). Mystic Remora reads OPPONENT_CASTS_PER_TURN (measured) thinned by OPPONENT_NONCREATURE_SHARE (0.5, authored) at TITHE_PAY_RATE.
  • A doubler reaches what its text says: double strike and doubled power, combat only; 'a creature', combat and a creature's noncombat damage; 'a source', all damage; 'noncombat', that. Life loss is not damage and is never multiplied or bonused.
  • A symmetric X draw (Prosperity, Fascination) is cast only with a payoff on the table's draws out, and the table draws X. A one-a-turn discard payoff (Monument to Endurance) takes its modes in the printed order. Narset's end step is taken when the turn cast at least as many spells as the hand holds; those discards and draws pay payoffs next main phase, a turn late for pings and on time for counters.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent, Tocasia's Welcome with its mana-value gate), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAW (Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Morbid Opportunist, Midnight Reaper) fires on every death this model produces — the sacrifice sweep under `model_sacrifice`, the one-turn copies leaving, and the measured rate under `model_deaths` — honouring 'nontoken' and 'once each turn' (2026-10-19); a deck with no death channel on has no deaths, and the payoff is absent. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent) and attack-triggered draw outside `model_combat` are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw', 'you may pay 2 life' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one, and 15 death triggers.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • DISCOVER, always on. An instant or sorcery with 'Discover N' is cast like any spell (storm, magecraft and per-cast damage see it), and each resolution — plus one per Zada copy — walks to the first nonland card with mana value N or less and CASTS IT FREE when it has a free door: a ritual, a draw spell, a discover spell, a storm spell, a pump or token copy (with a board, under model_combat), or a body. Any other hit goes to HAND and is cast later at full cost — a floor. The rest go to the bottom in a random order. A permanent's discover (the Hidden lands, an ETB, a death) and discover X are not read.
  • CASCADE, always on, the card's OWN keyword only (grants are not read). Every cast — paid, free or a cascade hit's own — walks to the first nonland card with mana value STRICTLY LESS and casts it free through the same doors as Discover; a hit with no free door goes to the bottom with the rest, in a random order (a floor). Copies never cascade. A cascade card no other loop casts is cast late in the main phase for the cascade alone, so it adds nothing to that turn's storm count.
  • A FREE-CAST CHAIN IS CAPPED at FREE_CAST_DEPTH_LIMIT nested free casts (8); a hit past it takes the not-cast path. The depth reached and the refusals are reported in metrics.free_cast.
  • MAGECRAFT FIRES ON EVERY COPY ('cast or copy'): one event per Zada copy of a spell, at every copy site (draw, pump, untap, storm, discover), counted once per spell however many arms the spell has.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • RITUALS ARE CAST WHEN THEY ARE THE DIFFERENCE (2026-10-05): a spell that adds mana (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Battle Hymn, Brightstone Ritual, Inner Fire) is cast only if its net mana makes a card in hand, or the commander, castable this turn; its mana lasts the turn. Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will / Rousing Refrain read the OPPONENTS' state this model does not have, on two AUTHORED rates in config.py: GEYSER_TAPPED_SHARE (0.5 of their lands still tapped on our turn) and OPPONENT_HAND (4 cards, 7 on a turn a wheel refilled them). Rituals with an additional cost or restricted mana are not read.
  • Extra card draw is modelled ONLY behind `model_draw` — the assumption above names its channels. With the flag OFF it is one card per turn, always, which understates every deck by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs: conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list that hides, and `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled` names the cards whose draw still has no channel, and is ABSENT rather than empty on a deck where every drawer is read.
  • CREATURES TAP WHEN THEY ATTACK (CR 508.1f) and untap at the start of your turn, so an ADDITIONAL COMBAT PHASE is only worth something if something untaps the team. Before this the swing was computed once and multiplied by the phase count, which made an extra combat free damage from a board that had already attacked: ur-dragon's damage at turn ten fell 68.798 -> 59.499 when it was corrected. LANDS still enter untapped, always — that assumption is unchanged and is about lands, not creatures.
  • A COMMANDER THAT COPIES A SINGLE-TARGET SPELL across the board is modelled behind `model_commander_copy`, declared per deck and checked against the commander's own text. Zada, Hedron Grinder is the only card in the corpus with the ability. It needs no authored rate: the copy count IS the number of other creatures you control, which this model already measures.
  • WHAT A COPIED SPELL DOES is read per effect, and each is one turn unless said otherwise: its draw, a pump (power only — toughness is tracked but no creature blocks here), a PERMANENT +1/+1 counter, a Treasure it creates, double strike, doubled power, damage every creature deals to each opponent, an additional combat phase, and an untap. A spell whose effect is none of these still counts toward the spell count and is otherwise inert.
  • THE SPELL COUNT IS TRACKED, so STORM is modelled, as are MAGECRAFT (which fires on a cast OR a copy) and per-cast damage (which fires on a cast only — that difference is a rules fact, not a simplification). Before this there was no spells-cast-this-turn variable at all and storm was unreadable.
  • REPLACEMENTS (2026-10-11): a counter replacement (Lae'zel +1, Doubling Season x2) applies per counter EVENT, and a damage replacement that adds (Torbran +2) per damage INSTANCE, each added before any multiplier: each ping, each storm copy, each attacker per strike. An attacker's colours ride on its battlefield entry (cast creatures, commanders, and draw/discard-made tokens such as The Locust God's blue-red Insects); a token from any other maker is colourless here, an understatement.
  • OPPONENTS' CASTS (2026-10-11): each opponent casts 1.264 spells a turn, MEASURED from 137,717 Forge turns (sim/pod_behaviour); Rhystic Study's tax is paid at the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE. Mind's Eye pays from mana left untapped at the end of our turn, a floor.
  • SPELLS AT A PLAYER (2026-10-11): damage a spell deals to a player counts against the one opponent ("any target" included). A spell whose only read effect is that damage is cast at the end of the main phase with what is left; a choose-ONE card whose other mode is read keeps that mode. Only an "each player" wheel refills the opponent; "discard your hand" and "target player" (us) do not. A discard additional cost is paid.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN FIRES ONLY ON THE DEATHS THIS MODEL PRODUCES. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance, Vraan and Vein Ripper key on a creature DYING, and a death happens here in three places and nowhere else: a free sacrifice outlet converting tokens (`model_sacrifice`), a one-turn copy leaving at the end step, and the per-turn rate read off a Forge run (`model_deaths`). No blockers and no removal, so a deck with neither channel on has no deaths and the contribution is ABSENT, not zero; a deck whose kill runs through them is understated by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • A board of 1/1 tokens and lifelink bodies reads as fair. The converters, Anointed Procession, Elspeth, Storm Slayer and Bloodletter of Aclazotz are what a table reads as the threat, so they land after the outlet and the drain.
  • Five bodies on turn three off three mana is what made this deck the problem in log 001. Price the attention in rather than trying to avoid it.
  • Lifelink and the drains make you the visible leader while the hand empties. Log 002: 50 life against a table at 20 with nothing left to cast. Preacher of the Schism draws while you lead.
  • With no infinite, the kill takes turns and the table watches it happen. Each converter on the battlefield is a seat losing life every time you gain; expect it to draw the answer that used to go to the loop.

7.2When to reveal

  • Show the tokens. Keep Bloodletter of Aclazotz in hand until the turn it closes; log 001 lost a key spell to a counterspell on the way down.
  • Cast a converter with Deflecting Swat or Flawless Maneuver held and Edgar Markov on the battlefield, Sanguine Bond and Enduring Tenacity before the creature converters.
  • Cast the token doubler on a turn you also cast a Vampire, so the extra bodies arrive before the answer does.
  • Say the eminence trigger out loud on every Vampire spell, Edgar Markov in the command zone included.
  • Commit Elspeth, Storm Slayer or Liliana, Dreadhorde General only on a turn you leave a blocker or a free answer up. Log 005: Elspeth came down, the attack went all-in, and she was taken down before the pings finished you.

7.3Alliances

  • Point Blood Artist, Vein Ripper and the target converters at the seat the table has already agreed to kill; Cruel Celebrant, Vraan, Executioner Thane and Marauding Blight-Priest hit everyone and need no such choice.
  • Ask the seat holding counterspells to cover the combo seat. You hold none; log 005 seated an Azorius counters deck at the same table as the deck that killed you.
  • Attack the seat that is winning and give the reason as board position. Log 004 removed Alex first on that ground and records it as position, not grudge.
  • Name the seat that has answered you twice and ask the table to split its answers. Log 002 lost to one seat picking off the draw permanents unopposed.
  • Cast Charismatic Conqueror while the table has a bigger problem than you; its tax reads every seat at once.

7.4Who to hit first

  • Kill the seat that deals damage without attacking before the seat with the biggest board. In log 005 Tom's board was not the problem and Tom's pings closed the game.
  • Kill the seat that has stopped developing before the seat with the biggest board. In log 004 the seat holding no creatures is the seat that comboed out.
  • Aim Anguished Unmaking at the permanent that answers you or at the combo piece, not at the largest creature. Path to Exile is for a creature piece and hands its owner a basic land.
  • Do not swing the big lifelinker into the indestructible board. Take Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter's counters off as -1/-1 on the blocker first, or attack the seat without one. Log 006.
  • Remove an opponent's creature with Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet out when you want the Zombie; remove it before Kalitas lands when you want the death to trigger Blood Artist and Vein Ripper.
  • Spend no answer on a land; nothing in the 99 reaches one.
