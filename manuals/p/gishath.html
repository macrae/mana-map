1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

Cast an eight-mana Gishath off a dork-and-land ramp base, make one hit land, and let the reveal put Dinosaurs onto the battlefield in batches whose entry triggers pay in cards and bodies, until a committed trampling alpha ends it by commander damage.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. you have become the table's problem, and the board that ends a seat is already on the table

Indications.

  • The table names you aloud. On 29 October Mirari's Wake drew "that's going to be a problem" the turn it landed, and after the first reveals every seat was dealing with Sean (log:003).
  • Your total power is greater than one seat's life plus its untapped blockers. On 15 September it was 8 to 9 Dinosaurs and 30-plus power against Alex at 18 and Oliver at 15 (log:002).
  • You have swung two or three creatures out of a full board, or none, for a turn. Both logged losses went this way (log:002, log:003).
  • A seat you have named as the danger has not been attacked. On 29 October that was Alex's artifact board and its growing creature, and Alex won (log:003).
  • A no-attack deal is standing while you hold the biggest board (log:002).

Immediate action.

  1. Count every seat before you declare an attacker: your power, after Overwhelming Stampede, Berserkers' Onslaught or Kessig Wolf Run, against that seat's life plus its untapped blockers. Say each total aloud. Count Gishath's commander damage per seat toward 21.
  2. If two or more seats die this turn, attack every one of them. A seat left alive saves the seat you attacked, and on 15 September that seat won (log:002).
  3. If no seat dies, send everything at the seat whose board beats yours, not at the seat that is easy to hit. On 29 October that was Alex (log:003).
  4. Declare every creature that can attack. Losing Dinosaurs in blocks is the price of the swing; a Dinosaur held home for fear of a block is the loss on 29 October (log:003).
  5. Keep one shield castable after attacks. Flawless Maneuver costs nothing while Gishath is on the battlefield; otherwise leave {1}{G} for Heroic Intervention or {R}{W} for Boros Charm untapped.

Subsequent.

  1. Main two: cast the Dinosaurs in hand. With Hunting Velociraptor out and a Dinosaur having dealt combat damage this turn, each costs prowl {2}{R}.
  2. Next turn, attack again with everything, including the Dinosaurs the reveal put onto the battlefield. They entered after attackers were declared and could not swing until now.
  3. Do not stop to rebuild between swings while a seat is in range. Keep attacking until the seat whose board beats yours is dead.
NOTERevealed Dinosaurs enter after attackers are declared, so they attack the turn after the reveal. That following turn is the pivot: the board is at its largest and the table has not yet answered it. Both losses in the log were boards that were ahead on that turn and did not commit (log:002, log:003).

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:003.

3.2WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming, or has landed, and it takes the mana creatures with the Dinosaurs

Indications.

  • A seat passes its own turn with four to six mana open and casts nothing in its main phase.
  • Your board is the largest at the table by three or more creatures.
  • Your mana stands on creatures: Ixalli's Lorekeeper, Bloom Tender, Drover of the Mighty, Intrepid Paleontologist, Atzocan Seer, Faeburrow Elder, Selvala, Hulking Raptor, Regal Behemoth. A creature sweeper takes all of them.
  • The pod has already wiped once. On 15 September it was "wipe after wipe" for two and a half hours (log:002).
  • On 28 August the table spent its wipes on the mid-tier Dinosaurs and had none left when Gishath landed (log:001).

Immediate action.

  1. Stop casting creatures. Every body still in hand waits until the sweeper resolves or that seat taps out.
  2. Read the sweeper's verb. Destroy or damage: Flawless Maneuver (free with Gishath out), Heroic Intervention {1}{G}, Boros Charm {R}{W}, Akroma's Will {3}{W}. Cast the cheapest one you hold.
  3. Exile, -X/-X, bounce or sacrifice: cast Eerie Interlude {2}{W} with the sweeper on the stack and target every nontoken creature you want back. They return at the next end step.
  4. With no shield in hand, sacrifice Atzocan Seer in response to return a Dinosaur card already in your graveyard to your hand.
  5. If Vaultborn Tyrant is on the battlefield and nothing saves the board, let it die: it leaves a token copy behind.

Subsequent.

  1. Count what survived: lands, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Urza's Incubator, Mirari's Wake, Garruk's Uprising, Berserkers' Onslaught, Kessig Wolf Run. That is your mana and your next swing.
  2. Recast Gishath before any second body. His reveal is the rebuild: about two Dinosaurs per seven damage off 28 Dinosaurs in the 99.
  3. Rydia's Return {3}{G}{G}, mode two: return two permanent cards from your graveyard to your hand. Take Mirari's Wake if it died, otherwise the two largest Dinosaurs.
  4. Redeploy one threat behind Gishath, not three. Hold the best Dinosaur in hand for the next sweeper.
  5. If your board is empty and theirs is not, cast Blasphemous Act. It costs {1} less per creature on the battlefield and clears only their side (log:001).
NOTETemple Altisaur prevents all but 1 of the damage to your OTHER Dinosaurs. It takes the full damage itself, and every non-Dinosaur mana creature dies, so a damage sweeper with Altisaur out still takes the climb. Eerie Interlude returns cards, not tokens: Palani's Hatcher Eggs, the Regisaur Alpha token and Quartzwood Crasher tokens are gone.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:002.

3.3REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. a spot removal spell is pointed at Gishath, or at the permanent that makes his cast or his hit

Indications.

  • A seat holds {W} or {1}{W} open after you cast Gishath: the Path to Exile and Swords to Plowshares shape.
  • Blue mana is open while Gishath is on the stack.
  • An artifact and enchantment sweep is in play at the table. On 29 October Steven's removed Mirari's Wake along with other players' artifacts and enchantments (log:003).
  • Removal goes at Mirari's Wake, Urza's Incubator, Berserkers' Onslaught or a reducer rather than at a body. That seat has read the deck.

Immediate action.

  1. Read the spell. Destroy a creature: Flawless Maneuver, Heroic Intervention, Boros Charm or Akroma's Will stops it. Destroy an artifact or enchantment: only Heroic Intervention {1}{G} or Boros Charm {R}{W}, which cover permanents. Exile or bounce: from hand, only Heroic Intervention's hexproof, Akroma's Will's protection from each colour against a coloured source, or Eerie Interlude. Devil Dinosaur on the battlefield already gives Gishath hexproof.
  2. If the shield in hand does not match and {2}{W} is open, cast Eerie Interlude on Gishath. The removal loses its target. When he is exiled, leave him in exile; he returns at the next end step.
  3. If the target is a reducer or Mirari's Wake and the shield costs the mana you need for Gishath, let it resolve. Shields are for Gishath and the board.
  4. If nothing answers it, let it resolve, put Gishath in the command zone, and write down the recast: eight, plus {2} per earlier command-zone cast, less {1} for Otepec Huntmaster, {2} for Knight of the Stampede and {2} for Urza's Incubator.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast Gishath with Cavern of Souls mana named Dinosaur when a blue seat is open. That spell cannot be countered.
  2. Put Devil Dinosaur on the battlefield before the next Gishath cast. It gives your other Dinosaurs, Gishath included, hexproof against opponents.
  3. If Mirari's Wake is gone, the replacement doubler is Regal Behemoth: one extra mana per land tapped while you are the monarch. On 29 October it carried the recast after Wake was removed (log:003).
  4. Rydia's Return mode two returns Mirari's Wake and one more permanent card to hand.
NOTEIndestructible stops neither Path to Exile nor Swords to Plowshares. Against exile, Flawless Maneuver and Boros Charm do nothing. Devil Dinosaur's hexproof covers targeting only; it does nothing against a sweeper.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:003.

3.4STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. the mana is down but nothing is on the board, and Gishath is not yet castable

Indications.

  • Turn four with four mana and no play that uses it. On 29 October the board was empty until Mirari's Wake (log:003).
  • Turn six and Gishath is not cast. The goldfish casts him by turn six in 0.405 of games, median turn 7.
  • Your hand is five- and six-drops with no reducer.
  • Your board is mana creatures and no Dinosaur.

Immediate action.

  1. Count the cast: mana available against eight, plus {2} per earlier command-zone cast, less the reducers on the battlefield.
  2. If you are short by one or two, cast a reducer before any body: Otepec Huntmaster {1}{R}, Knight of the Stampede {3}{G}, Urza's Incubator {3} named Dinosaur.
  3. If you have five mana and three or more lands, cast Mirari's Wake. Each land tapped makes one more; on 29 October it took five mana to ten (log:003).
  4. If you hold Natural Order and a green mana creature, sacrifice the creature for Ghalta, Primal Hunger, Vaultborn Tyrant or Carnage Tyrant. These are threats that need no command zone.
  5. If a Dinosaur dealt combat damage to a player this turn and Hunting Velociraptor is on the battlefield, cast Dinosaurs in main two for prowl {2}{R}.

Subsequent.

  1. Enlightened Tutor {W} puts Mirari's Wake, Urza's Incubator or Berserkers' Onslaught on top of your library. Cast it at the end of the opponent's turn before yours.
  2. Keep the cheap Dinosaurs home as blockers until Gishath lands. Pugnacious Hammerskull gets a stun counter if it attacks alone.
  3. Cast Ghalta, Primal Hunger when your total power reaches eight; it then costs {G}{G} plus two.
NOTENatural Order puts the creature onto the battlefield. Zacama's land untap reads "if you cast it", so a Natural Order Zacama does not untap your lands. Ixalli's Lorekeeper's mana pays for Dinosaur spells only: it pays for Gishath, not for Mirari's Wake or Natural Order.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:003.

3.5MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the colours have stopped: lands in play, but not the colour the hand needs

Indications.

  • Turn four with fewer than four lands.
  • Your hand holds double-red cards (Berserkers' Onslaught, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Quartzwood Crasher, Etali, Trumpeting Carnosaur) and you have one red source or none. Red is on curve from lands alone in 0.548 of goldfish games.
  • Double-green cards stranded in hand. On 15 September Selvala, Rishkar's Expertise and the GG Dinosaurs sat in hand through a long green screw (log:002).
  • Bloom Tender or Faeburrow Elder makes one colour only.

Immediate action.

  1. Cast land search before any body. Nature's Lore or Three Visits ({1}{G}) for Stomping Ground, Cinder Glade or Jetmir's Garden when red is short; Skyshroud Claim {3}{G} for two Forest cards.
  2. Crack fetches for the missing colour. Wooded Foothills and Arid Mesa find a Mountain; Windswept Heath and Verdant Catacombs find Stomping Ground, Cinder Glade or Jetmir's Garden; Prismatic Vista finds a basic. Pay the 2 life to have Stomping Ground or Temple Garden enter untapped.
  3. Cast a mana creature that makes any colour: Ixalli's Lorekeeper {G}, Drover of the Mighty or Intrepid Paleontologist {1}{G}, Atzocan Seer {1}{G}{W}.
  4. If you are short of generic mana, not colour, cast a reducer instead of a body.
  5. If a creature with power 4 or greater is on the battlefield, cast Selvala and tap her with {G} for X mana in any colours, X the greatest power you control.

Subsequent.

  1. Hold creatures through a wipe-heavy table while the mana catches up. On 15 September the screw kept them out of the early wipes by accident; do it on purpose (log:002).
  2. Cast Hulking Raptor for {G}{G} each first main phase, and Regal Behemoth to become the monarch for one extra mana per land.
  3. Cast Itzquinth or Otepec Huntmaster before Bloom Tender or Faeburrow Elder when red is short. A red permanent on the battlefield adds red to what each of them makes.
NOTEBloom Tender and Faeburrow Elder count colours among your permanents, and lands are colourless. With only green permanents out, each makes {G} and nothing else. Ancient Ziggurat pays for creature spells only; Cavern of Souls, Unclaimed Territory and Secluded Courtyard pay colours only for Dinosaur creature spells.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.6RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. a seat will kill you before your board kills anyone, and its clock is not one your blockers stop

Indications.

  • A seat's damage comes from spells or drains, not attackers. On 15 September Oliver's vampire drain killed Sean from behind a 30-power board (log:002).
  • A seat's board grows every turn and you have not attacked it. On 29 October Alex's artifacts, Treasures and growing creature won the game (log:003).
  • Your life is under 15 and a seat is gaining what it drains.

Immediate action.

  1. Count the turns: their kill against yours. If theirs comes first, you attack this turn.
  2. Remove their best attacker or engine: Path to Exile or Swords to Plowshares {W}; Itzquinth's entry for {2}, one of your Dinosaurs deals its power to it; Zacama {2}{R} for 3; Trumpeting Carnosaur's discard {2}{R} for 3; Thrashing Brontodon {1} for an artifact or enchantment.
  3. Swing every creature at that seat. Overwhelming Stampede or Berserkers' Onslaught before combat if you hold one.
  4. Gain life where it costs no attack: Zacama {2}{W} for 3, Tranquil Frillback's {G} for 4, Vaultborn Tyrant's 3 per power-4 entry, Akroma's Will's lifelink.

Subsequent.

  1. Keep Gishath home only when he blocks the attacker that kills you. He has vigilance, so an attack costs no block.
  2. Kinjalli's Sunwing on the battlefield makes the racing seat's new creatures enter tapped. Cast it before your next turn's swing.
NOTEBlockers do nothing against a drain. Against a seat whose clock is a spell, the only defence is killing that seat first (log:002).

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:002, log:003.

3.7COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. somebody at the table is assembling a win that does not attack

Indications.

  • A seat stops attacking and spends its turns searching its library.
  • A known combo piece resolves and its controller passes with mana open.
  • An artifact or enchantment enters that does nothing on its own.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the piece you can reach. A creature: Path to Exile or Swords to Plowshares. An artifact or enchantment: Thrashing Brontodon {1} and sacrifice, or Zacama {2}{G}. A planeswalker: Boros Charm's 4 or Trumpeting Carnosaur's discard.
  2. Spend the answer now, at instant speed, on the first piece. The deck has no counterspell, and a held answer is useless once the combo resolves.
  3. With no answer in hand, count your swing against that seat's life. If it dies, attack it with everything this turn.
  4. Keep Thrashing Brontodon on the battlefield with {1} untapped whenever an artifact or enchantment seat is at the table.

Subsequent.

  1. Bronzebeak Foragers' entry exiles one nonland permanent from each opponent. Point it at the combo piece.
  2. Against a graveyard combo, Tranquil Frillback exiles a graveyard on entry, and Intrepid Paleontologist exiles one card for {2}.
NOTEThe deck's defence against combo is a fast kill, not answers. Instant-speed interaction is the two white one-drops, Boros Charm, Carnosaur's discard and the on-board activations of Brontodon and Zacama.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two to four lands with a green source.
  • One mana piece castable by turn two: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Ixalli's Lorekeeper, Bloom Tender, Drover of the Mighty, Intrepid Paleontologist, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, or a reducer.
  • A red source, or a fetch or Forest search that finds one.
  • A play on turn two and a play on turn three.

Ship on sight.

  • Seven cards with no green source.
  • Five or more lands with no mana piece and no reducer.
  • A hand whose cheapest action is turn five.
  • A hand of mana creatures with no land after the first.
NOTEThe goldfish keeps the first seven in 0.787 of games and draws ramp or fixing in 0.979. Ship to find mana, never to find a named Dinosaur: Gishath is in the command zone.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 79% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Turn one: play the land that enters tapped if you hold one, then Sol Ring or Ixalli's Lorekeeper.
  2. Name Dinosaur on Cavern of Souls, Unclaimed Territory, Secluded Courtyard, Urza's Incubator and Roaming Throne as each enters. Every time.
  3. Turn two: a mana creature, Arcane Signet, Otepec Huntmaster, or Nature's Lore or Three Visits for a Forest card that makes red.
  4. Turn three: Faeburrow Elder, Atzocan Seer, Urza's Incubator or Garruk's Uprising. Mana before bodies.
  5. Attack with nothing before Gishath. Spend Path to Exile or Swords to Plowshares only on a creature that kills Gishath on sight or an engine that wins alone.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Reducers first: Otepec Huntmaster, Knight of the Stampede and Urza's Incubator lower Gishath and every Dinosaur after him. All three put Gishath at {R}{G}{W} on his first cast.
  2. Mirari's Wake on the turn it makes Gishath castable next turn, not earlier. The table reads it as the threat (log:003); keep {1}{G} for Heroic Intervention behind it if you hold it.
  3. Garruk's Uprising before the first reveal. It draws for each power-4 Dinosaur the reveal puts onto the battlefield and gives every creature trample.
  4. Berserkers' Onslaught or Roaming Throne named Dinosaur on the turn before Gishath attacks. Both multiply his hit; neither is verified by a stack.
  5. Cast Gishath on a turn he can hit a seat whose untapped blockers do not stop him, with Flawless Maneuver in hand or shield mana open. He has haste: the cast turn is the attack turn.
  6. Set the top on the swing turn. Cast Congregation at Dawn or Worldly Tutor after your draw step and after Bonehoard Dracosaur's upkeep exile, before combat damage.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Upkeep: Bonehoard Dracosaur exiles two; play the land among them. Count Huatli's saga chapter.
  2. Main one: land, then a reducer, then one body. Leave shield mana open.
  3. Beginning of combat: Palani's Hatcher turns an Egg into a 3/3.
  4. Declare attackers: Gishath, and Sunfrill Imitator as a copy of Gishath. After the first reveal, every creature that can attack.
  5. On the connect, put Pantlaza's discover on the entering Dinosaur with the greatest toughness; it fires once a turn. Garruk's Uprising and Vaultborn Tyrant draw once per power-4 entry.
  6. Main two: cast Dinosaurs from hand, for prowl {2}{R} if Hunting Velociraptor is out and a Dinosaur dealt combat damage.
  7. End step: hold Flawless Maneuver, Heroic Intervention or Boros Charm through every opponent's turn.

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count every seat aloud: power against life plus untapped blockers, and Gishath's commander damage toward 21.
  2. Add the multipliers you hold: Overwhelming Stampede {3}{G}{G}, +X/+X and trample with X your greatest power; Rydia's Return +3/+3; Berserkers' Onslaught's double strike; Kessig Wolf Run {X}{R}{G} on one attacker; Huatli's chapter IV double strike and trample.
  3. Akroma's Will with Gishath out, cast before blockers: both modes, flying, double strike, lifelink, indestructible and protection from each colour.
  4. Attack every seat that dies this turn (log:002).
  5. If no seat dies, attack the seat whose board beats yours with everything (log:003).
  6. Wait a turn only when you hold no shield and a seat has four or more mana open with a sweeper colour among it. Otherwise swing.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (50 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Under model_hand_limit a hand over seven discards down at end of turn (CR 514.1) unless a permanent or a rest-of-the-game sorcery says no maximum hand size; a commander's own text and a raised limit ('your maximum hand size is ten') are not read.
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 redraws, keep the last. The first is free (CR 103.5c); the second bottoms a card, a land past the fourth or the dearest card.
  • The turn deploys before it wheels: the commander, then rocks, payoffs and bodies, then a wheel on what is left, then whatever the wheel found. A wheel or a loot goes first only to dig: no land in hand and a commander still in the zone. A loot waits for a payoff like a wheel.
  • Repeatable activations run until the mana, a library floor of LIFE_CHAIN_LIBRARY_FLOOR (10, authored) or a hand of seven stops them, the hand only when no payoff is paid per draw. Treasure pays a repeat only under Smothering Tithe, whose return is the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE (0.5). An activation's draws are not castable that turn: a floor.
  • A rock or a land sacrifices itself for a card only with ROCK_SAC_MIN_MANA (7, authored) left after it and nothing in hand that mana wants. Cumulative upkeep is paid to age CUMULATIVE_UPKEEP_MAX_AGE (3, authored). Mystic Remora reads OPPONENT_CASTS_PER_TURN (measured) thinned by OPPONENT_NONCREATURE_SHARE (0.5, authored) at TITHE_PAY_RATE.
  • A doubler reaches what its text says: double strike and doubled power, combat only; 'a creature', combat and a creature's noncombat damage; 'a source', all damage; 'noncombat', that. Life loss is not damage and is never multiplied or bonused.
  • A symmetric X draw (Prosperity, Fascination) is cast only with a payoff on the table's draws out, and the table draws X. A one-a-turn discard payoff (Monument to Endurance) takes its modes in the printed order. Narset's end step is taken when the turn cast at least as many spells as the hand holds; those discards and draws pay payoffs next main phase, a turn late for pings and on time for counters.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent, Tocasia's Welcome with its mana-value gate), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAW (Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Morbid Opportunist, Midnight Reaper) fires on every death this model produces — the sacrifice sweep under `model_sacrifice`, the one-turn copies leaving, and the measured rate under `model_deaths` — honouring 'nontoken' and 'once each turn' (2026-10-19); a deck with no death channel on has no deaths, and the payoff is absent. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent) and attack-triggered draw outside `model_combat` are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw', 'you may pay 2 life' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one, and 15 death triggers.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • DISCOVER, always on. An instant or sorcery with 'Discover N' is cast like any spell (storm, magecraft and per-cast damage see it), and each resolution — plus one per Zada copy — walks to the first nonland card with mana value N or less and CASTS IT FREE when it has a free door: a ritual, a draw spell, a discover spell, a storm spell, a pump or token copy (with a board, under model_combat), or a body. Any other hit goes to HAND and is cast later at full cost — a floor. The rest go to the bottom in a random order. A permanent's discover (the Hidden lands, an ETB, a death) and discover X are not read.
  • CASCADE, always on, the card's OWN keyword only (grants are not read). Every cast — paid, free or a cascade hit's own — walks to the first nonland card with mana value STRICTLY LESS and casts it free through the same doors as Discover; a hit with no free door goes to the bottom with the rest, in a random order (a floor). Copies never cascade. A cascade card no other loop casts is cast late in the main phase for the cascade alone, so it adds nothing to that turn's storm count.
  • A FREE-CAST CHAIN IS CAPPED at FREE_CAST_DEPTH_LIMIT nested free casts (8); a hit past it takes the not-cast path. The depth reached and the refusals are reported in metrics.free_cast.
  • MAGECRAFT FIRES ON EVERY COPY ('cast or copy'): one event per Zada copy of a spell, at every copy site (draw, pump, untap, storm, discover), counted once per spell however many arms the spell has.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • RITUALS ARE CAST WHEN THEY ARE THE DIFFERENCE (2026-10-05): a spell that adds mana (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Battle Hymn, Brightstone Ritual, Inner Fire) is cast only if its net mana makes a card in hand, or the commander, castable this turn; its mana lasts the turn. Mana Geyser and Jeska's Will / Rousing Refrain read the OPPONENTS' state this model does not have, on two AUTHORED rates in config.py: GEYSER_TAPPED_SHARE (0.5 of their lands still tapped on our turn) and OPPONENT_HAND (4 cards, 7 on a turn a wheel refilled them). Rituals with an additional cost or restricted mana are not read.
  • Extra card draw is modelled ONLY behind `model_draw` — the assumption above names its channels. With the flag OFF it is one card per turn, always, which understates every deck by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs: conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list that hides, and `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled` names the cards whose draw still has no channel, and is ABSENT rather than empty on a deck where every drawer is read.
  • CREATURES TAP WHEN THEY ATTACK (CR 508.1f) and untap at the start of your turn, so an ADDITIONAL COMBAT PHASE is only worth something if something untaps the team. Before this the swing was computed once and multiplied by the phase count, which made an extra combat free damage from a board that had already attacked: ur-dragon's damage at turn ten fell 68.798 -> 59.499 when it was corrected. LANDS still enter untapped, always — that assumption is unchanged and is about lands, not creatures.
  • A COMMANDER THAT COPIES A SINGLE-TARGET SPELL across the board is modelled behind `model_commander_copy`, declared per deck and checked against the commander's own text. Zada, Hedron Grinder is the only card in the corpus with the ability. It needs no authored rate: the copy count IS the number of other creatures you control, which this model already measures.
  • WHAT A COPIED SPELL DOES is read per effect, and each is one turn unless said otherwise: its draw, a pump (power only — toughness is tracked but no creature blocks here), a PERMANENT +1/+1 counter, a Treasure it creates, double strike, doubled power, damage every creature deals to each opponent, an additional combat phase, and an untap. A spell whose effect is none of these still counts toward the spell count and is otherwise inert.
  • THE SPELL COUNT IS TRACKED, so STORM is modelled, as are MAGECRAFT (which fires on a cast OR a copy) and per-cast damage (which fires on a cast only — that difference is a rules fact, not a simplification). Before this there was no spells-cast-this-turn variable at all and storm was unreadable.
  • REPLACEMENTS (2026-10-11): a counter replacement (Lae'zel +1, Doubling Season x2) applies per counter EVENT, and a damage replacement that adds (Torbran +2) per damage INSTANCE, each added before any multiplier: each ping, each storm copy, each attacker per strike. An attacker's colours ride on its battlefield entry (cast creatures, commanders, and draw/discard-made tokens such as The Locust God's blue-red Insects); a token from any other maker is colourless here, an understatement.
  • OPPONENTS' CASTS (2026-10-11): each opponent casts 1.264 spells a turn, MEASURED from 137,717 Forge turns (sim/pod_behaviour); Rhystic Study's tax is paid at the authored TITHE_PAY_RATE. Mind's Eye pays from mana left untapped at the end of our turn, a floor.
  • SPELLS AT A PLAYER (2026-10-11): damage a spell deals to a player counts against the one opponent ("any target" included). A spell whose only read effect is that damage is cast at the end of the main phase with what is left; a choose-ONE card whose other mode is read keeps that mode. Only an "each player" wheel refills the opponent; "discard your hand" and "target player" (us) do not. A discard additional cost is paid.
  • Treasures are a one-shot STOCKPILE, not a mana rock: spent only when lands and rocks fall short, and gone once broken. Reported separately from mean_available_mana_by_turn, which still means repeatable mana per turn.
  • Only Treasure triggers a goldfish can see are modeled — upkeep and landfall are RECURRING, Saga chapters likewise; a `cast` or enters-the-battlefield trigger pays out ONCE, when its own source resolves. Combat- and opponent-gated sources produce NOTHING here, because this model has no combat and no opponents; they are named in meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled so a low hoard figure is legible.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN FIRES ONLY ON THE DEATHS THIS MODEL PRODUCES. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance, Vraan and Vein Ripper key on a creature DYING, and a death happens here in three places and nowhere else: a free sacrifice outlet converting tokens (`model_sacrifice`), a one-turn copy leaving at the end step, and the per-turn rate read off a Forge run (`model_deaths`). No blockers and no removal, so a deck with neither channel on has no deaths and the contribution is ABSENT, not zero; a deck whose kill runs through them is understated by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

6.1Mana

Pays for an eight-mana commander, then for the Dinosaurs he did not reveal. Three layers: every land but Kessig Wolf Run, plus Sol Ring and Arcane Signet; land search (Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Skyshroud Claim, Huatli's basic); and since v1.2.0 a crop of mana creatures (Bloom Tender, Faeburrow Elder, Drover of the Mighty, Ixalli's Lorekeeper, Intrepid Paleontologist, Atzocan Seer) above the big producers: Selvala (X equal to the greatest power you control), Mirari's Wake (one extra mana per land tapped), Regal Behemoth (one extra per land tapped, but only while you are the monarch, which combat damage to you takes away) and Hulking Raptor (a fixed {G}{G} each first main phase, which does not scale). 6.2Ignition

Nothing in the deck is the engine until Gishath deals combat damage to a player: the trigger reveals that many cards and puts every Dinosaur among them onto the battlefield. Two things must resolve: the cast (eight mana, the stage above) and the hit. He has haste, vigilance and trample on his own card, so the connect needs size and a second count, not haste. Berserkers' Onslaught gives attackers double strike: two combat damage steps, two triggers (open_questions[2]); Akroma's Will's first mode and Boros Charm's third grant double strike for one turn, and they sit in protection because that is the mode they are usually held for. Sunfrill Imitator, attacking, can become a copy of Gishath and carry the same trigger, which the pilot's game 003 records happening (a log, not a stack). Kessig Wolf Run is a repeatable land pump: the reveal counts damage dealt, so every point that gets through is one more card. Gishath lives in the command zone, so he can never be revealed or tutored, and he is the stage's single point of failure; each removal costs commander tax rather than the card. The reveal is a PUT, not a cast: Zacama's 'if you cast it' untap does nothing through this door. No passing stack on this 99 resolves a connect (open_questions[0]).

6.3Fuel

The reveal is only as good as the top of the library, and the 99 holds 28 Dinosaurs for it to find (Roaming Throne is a Golem in the library and does not count). Congregation at Dawn and Worldly Tutor put creatures on TOP at instant speed, and the stack has to be built ON the swing turn: cast earlier, your draw step takes the top card and Bonehoard Dracosaur's upkeep exiles two more, and Etali's attack trigger exiles one more, so the window is after those, in the declare-attackers or declare-blockers step before damage. Enlightened Tutor is a draw tutor, not a reveal tutor: its targets (Roaming Throne, Berserkers' Onslaught, Mirari's Wake, Garruk's Uprising) are not Dinosaur creature cards, so one left on top only spends a reveal slot and goes to the bottom; it is cast before your draw step. Natural Order eats a green mana creature for a green fatty straight onto the battlefield. Rishkar's Expertise draws off the greatest power on the board. Bonehoard Dracosaur exiles two off the top in each of your upkeeps for you to play; Etali exiles the top card of each library only when it attacks. Pugnacious Hammerskull is a cheap 6/6, power the payoffs count. There is no fodder stage: the creature sacrifices in the 99 are Natural Order's additional cost, Palani's Hatcher eating its own Eggs, and Atzocan Seer and Thrashing Brontodon sacrificing themselves. None is a theme.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Until Gishath, the table sees mana creatures and a few big Dinosaurs. The commander is visible in the command zone the whole game.
  • Mirari's Wake is a tell. On 29 October the table called it a problem the turn it landed, and an artifact and enchantment sweep took it later (log:003).
  • After the first reveal you are the archenemy. On 29 October every seat was dealing with Sean (log:003).
  • On 28 August the table spent its wipes on the mid-tier Dinosaurs and held nothing for Gishath (log:001). That is one table on one night.

7.2When to reveal

  • Cast reducers the turn they are castable. Each turn one sits in hand is a turn of discount lost.
  • Hold Congregation at Dawn and Worldly Tutor for the swing turn.
  • Pass with no mana open when Flawless Maneuver is the shield; it costs nothing with Gishath out.
  • Hold Blasphemous Act until your board is smaller than theirs or empty (log:001).

7.3Alliances

  • Offer removal, not attacks. A Path to Exile or Swords to Plowshares on the table's shared problem buys a rotation.
  • No deal survives the first reveal. Your board is then the table's problem whatever you promised.
  • Break a deal only to kill a seat this turn, and then kill every seat the swing reaches (log:002).
  • Make no deal with the seat whose board you named as the danger (log:003).

7.4Who to hit first

  • First connect: the seat with no untapped blocker that stops Gishath. The reveal pays the same at any life total.
  • After that: the seat whose board beats yours, before the seat with the lowest life (log:003).
  • A seat whose clock is a drain or burn spell is next, because blockers do nothing against it (log:002).
  • Put commander damage on one player. The seat that has taken 7 takes the next hit toward 21 (log:001, log:002).
