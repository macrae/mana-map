1General

What the machine is and what it is for.

The currency of this deck is a Dinosaur ENTERING the battlefield, not a creature attacking: Gishath's combat-damage reveal manufactures entries in batches (goldfish, 10,000 games: it fired at all in 7,300 of them, and AVERAGED OVER ALL 10,000 it fired 2.016 times for 4.08 free bodies — `commander_reveal.mean_fired` and `mean_bodies` divide by iterations, not by the games that fired, so both means are diluted by the 2,700 games with no reveal), and V5 rebuilt the layer that cashes them — Purphoros turns every entry into 2 damage at each seat and Molten Echoes turns every nontoken Dinosaur entry into a second one, so a deck that used to need its 7/6 to keep connecting now has a payoff layer that pays without a combat step.

3Emergency procedures

One condition per page. What is happening, how you know, what to do.

3.1WIPE

a board wipe is coming, or has landed

Condition. a sweeper is coming, or has landed, and the board it takes is the mana as much as the bodies

Indications.

  • A seat passes on its own turn with four to six mana open, white or black among it, and casts nothing in its main phase.
  • Your board is three or more Dinosaurs and nobody else's is. At Alex's the table aimed every wrath at exactly that board and held nothing for the commander (log:001).
  • Your mana is standing on creatures: Bloom Tender, Hulking Raptor, Selvala, Regal Behemoth, Huatli. The land drop hits on turn 6 in 57.5% of goldfish games, so the deck rides those bodies after it.
  • Wipe after wipe was the whole shape of the four-player game on 15 September (log:002).

Immediate action.

  1. Stop casting Dinosaurs. Everything with a body stays in hand until the wipe resolves or that seat taps out.
  2. Count the mana that survives a creature wipe: lands, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Mirari's Wake, Urza's Incubator, Belbe's Portal. Bloom Tender, Hulking Raptor, Selvala, Regal Behemoth, Huatli, Otepec Huntmaster and Knight of the Stampede die with the board. If the survivors do not reach eight, spend this turn on a rock or a land-ramp spell and nothing else.
  3. Cast Purphoros and Molten Echoes into the wipe. Purphoros is indestructible and is not a creature at all while your devotion to red is under five; Molten Echoes is an enchantment. Neither sits on the board a sweeper takes.
  4. If Gishath is on the battlefield and Flawless Maneuver is in hand, pass with zero mana open — it costs nothing while you control a commander. Heroic Intervention needs {1}{G}, Boros Charm {R}{W}, Akroma's Will {3}{W}. Hold the mana for the one you hold.
  5. If Eerie Interlude is in hand with {2}{W} open, cast it with the wipe on the stack and target every creature you want back. They return at the next end step as one wave of entries: 2 damage to each opponent per creature with Purphoros out, and a Molten Echoes copy of each nontoken Dinosaur for 2 more.

Subsequent.

  1. Rebuild with the commander, not the hand. There are 29 Dinosaur cards counting him, so seven cards off one connection average about two free bodies.
  2. Put Purphoros down before a second body. Two damage at every seat per entry pays whether or not the body survives the next sweeper.
  3. Redeploy one Dinosaur behind him, not three. A second wipe takes what you put in front of him (log:001).
  4. If your board is empty and theirs is not, cast Blasphemous Act: it costs {1} less for each creature on the battlefield, at nine creatures it is {R}, and it does not touch Purphoros (log:001, stack 004).
NOTEWakening Sun's Avatar destroys all non-Dinosaurs only if you CAST it from your hand. Off Gishath's reveal, off Savage Order, off Belbe's Portal or off a Molten Echoes copy it is a 7/7 and nothing else — the same clause that stops a put-onto-the-battlefield Zacama untapping your lands. Temple Altisaur prevents all but 1 of the damage dealt to ANOTHER Dinosaur you control: not to itself, and not against exile or bounce.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:002.

3.2REMOVAL

the commander or a key permanent is targeted

Condition. a spot removal spell is pointed at Gishath, or at the permanent that cashes his reveal

Indications.

  • A seat passed with {W} or {1}{W} open after you cast Gishath: the Path to Exile and Swords to Plowshares shape.
  • Blue mana open while Gishath is on the stack. He can be countered; Carnage Tyrant cannot.
  • The removal went at Molten Echoes, Urza's Incubator or Otepec Huntmaster rather than at a body. That seat has read the deck.
  • A seat holds two mana and plays nothing on a turn you cast no creature. Your damage is now non-combat, and so is the answer to it.

Immediate action.

  1. Read the spell before you answer it. Against destroy: Flawless Maneuver, Heroic Intervention, Boros Charm and Akroma's Will each stop it. Against targeted exile or bounce: only Heroic Intervention's hexproof and Akroma's Will's protection from each colour stop it, and indestructible stops neither Path to Exile nor Swords to Plowshares.
  2. If the shield in hand does not match the spell and {2}{W} is open, cast Eerie Interlude on Gishath in response. When he is exiled, decline the command-zone replacement and leave him in exile: he returns to the battlefield at the next end step.
  3. If the target is a reducer and the shield is not free, let the reducer go. The shields are for the commander and the board.
  4. If nothing in hand answers it, let it resolve and write the recast down: eight, plus {2} for each earlier command-zone cast, less {1} for Otepec Huntmaster, {2} for Knight of the Stampede and {2} for Urza's Incubator on the battlefield. All three reduce generic mana only, so the floor is {R}{G}{W} plus the tax.
  5. Spend the next turn on that mana, not on a body: Nature's Lore, Skyshroud Claim, a fetch, or Selvala with a power-4 Dinosaur on the table.

Subsequent.

  1. Recast him on a turn he can also attack. He has haste; the cast turn is the attack turn.
  2. While Purphoros is on the battlefield, stop waiting on the commander. Cast Dinosaurs from hand instead: each entry is 2 at every seat and needs no combat step.
  3. Worldly Tutor finds Purphoros or Knight of the Stampede and puts it on top; Enlightened Tutor finds Molten Echoes, Purphoros, Urza's Incubator or Garruk's Uprising. Each spends your next draw.
  4. Recast into open blue mana only with Flawless Maneuver or Heroic Intervention in hand; otherwise land a reducer first and recast a turn later for less.
NOTEPurphoros is the hardest permanent in this deck to remove — indestructible, and not a creature at all while devotion to red is under five, which is most boards: only 14 permanents in the 99 carry a red pip. He folds to exile, to bounce and to enchantment removal. Hunting Velociraptor gives Dinosaur spells prowl {2}{R} once a Dinosaur has dealt combat damage to a player this turn; whether that alternative cost is available on a command-zone cast, with the commander tax on top, has not been resolved by a stack.

3.3COMBO

somebody at the table is going off

Condition. somebody at the table is going off, and the deck holds no counterspell

Indications.

  • A seat tutored twice in one turn, or drew five cards off one spell.
  • A sacrifice outlet and a recursion piece on one board.
  • A player counting aloud: storm, life, or cards in a graveyard.
  • A seat's life total climbing while it attacks nobody.

Immediate action.

  1. Name the permanent already on the battlefield that the kill needs. That is the target.
  2. Answer it at instant speed where you can: Path to Exile ({W}) and Swords to Plowshares ({W}) take a creature, Trumpeting Carnosaur is {2}{R} and a discard for 3 damage to a creature, Zacama's {2}{R} deals 3 to a creature and his {2}{G} destroys an artifact or an enchantment.
  3. If the piece is an artifact or an enchantment and Zacama is not on the battlefield, cast Savage Order ({2}{G}{G}, sacrificing a creature with power 4 or greater) in your main phase and search out Bronzebeak Foragers: it enters, exiles one nonland permanent from each opponent until it leaves, and Savage Order gives it indestructible until your next turn. Take the combo piece, not the biggest body.
  4. If the kill is a life total rather than a loop, count your own damage before you spend an answer. Each creature entering is 2 at that seat with Purphoros out, 4 on a nontoken Dinosaur with Molten Echoes out, and Boros Charm is 4 more at instant speed.
  5. With nothing left to answer it, attack that seat with everything every turn and point the next connection at them.

Subsequent.

  1. Savage Stomp and Savage Order are sorceries. Cast them in your own main phase on the piece already down; they cannot be held up for the turn the combo goes off.
  2. From the turn that seat's second piece lands, keep {W} open on its turn while Path to Exile or Swords to Plowshares is in hand.
  3. Next game against that seat, keep the hand with a reducer and ramp and ship the hand of payoffs.
  4. Bronzebeak Foragers' exile lasts only while it is on the battlefield. Do not chump-block with it, and do not sacrifice it to Savage Order.
NOTEThere is no counterspell in the 99, and after V5 the only instant-speed answer to a noncreature permanent is Zacama's {2}{G} — a nine-drop's activated ability. Everything else that touches an artifact or an enchantment is a sorcery-speed creature. A storm turn or an instant-speed kill from hand has no answer in this deck at all; the plan against it is to be the faster clock.

3.4MANA DROUGHT

the mana has stopped

Condition. the mana has stopped short of the commander, in count or in colour — and after V5 the colour that stops is red

Indications.

  • Three lands on the battlefield on turn five. The land drop hits on turn 5 in 64.8% of goldfish games and turn 6 in 57.5%; mean available mana is 5.13 on turn 5 and 6.04 on turn 6.
  • Eight mana and no {R} among it. From lands alone the deck is on curve in red 55% of the time, against 83% white and 76% green.
  • Purphoros ({3}{R}), Molten Echoes ({2}{R}{R}) and Hunting Velociraptor ({2}{R}) sitting in hand behind green mana.
  • A hand of double-green Dinosaurs with no green source — the first hour of the game on 15 September (log:002).

Immediate action.

  1. Count lands in hand and on the battlefield. Under four lands on turn four, cast the land ramp before anything else: Nature's Lore ({1}{G}) and Skyshroud Claim ({3}{G}) both put Forest CARDS onto the battlefield, and Stomping Ground and Cinder Glade are Forest cards that make {R}.
  2. Crack a fetch for red. Arid Mesa, Bloodstained Mire and Wooded Foothills search Mountain; Windswept Heath and Verdant Catacombs search Forest; Stomping Ground, Cinder Glade and Jetmir's Garden answer to both. Pay the 2 life to have Stomping Ground enter untapped.
  3. Do not count Bloom Tender for red. Vivid pays one mana for each colour among the permanents you control and lands are colourless, so with only lands and itself out it makes {G} and nothing more. Play Otepec Huntmaster or Itzquinth first and it makes {R} as well.
  4. If the shortfall is one or two generic mana, cast a reducer instead of a body: Otepec Huntmaster {1}{R} for {1} off, Knight of the Stampede {3}{G} for {2} off, Urza's Incubator {3} for {2} off. All three on the table put Gishath at {R}{G}{W}.
  5. If a Dinosaur with power 4 or greater is on the battlefield, cast Selvala: {G} and a tap makes X mana in any combination of colours, X the greatest power you control. With Gishath out that is seven, and it is where the red comes from.

Subsequent.

  1. Name Dinosaur on Unclaimed Territory and Secluded Courtyard as they enter. Their coloured mana casts creature spells of that type and nothing else, so keep it off Rogue's Passage and Kessig Wolf Run.
  2. Krosan Verge fetches a Forest card and a Plains card: take Cinder Glade and Temple Garden and one land drop gives you all three colours, both tapped.
  3. Cast Up the Beanstalk ({1}{G}) early. It draws as it enters and again for every spell of mana value 5 or greater, which is Gishath and 23 other cards in the deck.
  4. Wayward Swordtooth's extra land drop pays only with a land in hand. Cast it after Huatli or a fetch, not before.
NOTETen of the thirty-five lands enter tapped. Five mana on turn six is this deck's normal, not a drought; the drought is three lands and no rock, or eight mana with no red in it. The green audit and the land pass in V5 moved green and white up and left red the colour that strands the new cards.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.5STALLED

the engine has not assembled and the window is closing

Condition. Gishath has not resolved, the window is closing, and another seat's engine is running

Indications.

  • Turn seven, Gishath in the command zone, no reducer on the battlefield. The goldfish casts him by turn 6 in 26.7% of games, the median is turn 7, and 23% of games never cast him by turn 10.
  • Your board is three mid-tier Dinosaurs and the table has stopped answering them.
  • The commander has been answered twice and the tax is at {4}.
  • Another seat's engine is visibly running: a sacrifice loop, four enchantments, a graveyard past ten cards.

Immediate action.

  1. Stop waiting on the commander and put the clock on the board instead. Purphoros costs {3}{R} and needs no combat step: every creature that enters after him is 2 at each seat, 6 across a three-opponent table.
  2. Count the cast: mana available, less {1} for Otepec Huntmaster, {2} for Knight of the Stampede, {2} for Urza's Incubator on the battlefield. At or under what you have, cast him and attack the seat with no untapped blocker.
  3. One or two short, spend the turn on a reducer, not on a body. Each one lowers every Dinosaur cast after it.
  4. With Dinosaurs in hand and no reducer, put them down through Belbe's Portal ({3}, tap, Dinosaur named) or Monster Manual ({1}{G}, tap). Neither is a cast: take the body and know that Zacama will not untap your lands and Wakening Sun's Avatar will not wipe.
  5. Keep the mid-tier Dinosaurs home as blockers until he lands. Attack only for lethal or into an empty board.

Subsequent.

  1. Cast World War Hulk ({3}{G}{G}) as the stall breaker. Chapter I casts your next red or green creature spell for free, Zacama is a red creature spell, and a cast Zacama untaps every land you control.
  2. Enlightened Tutor finds Purphoros, Molten Echoes or Urza's Incubator; Worldly Tutor finds Purphoros or Knight of the Stampede.
  3. The turn he lands, attack. Do not hold him back for a wider board.
  4. Rogue's Passage costs {4} on top of his cast. Hold it for the second attack, not the first.
NOTEWorld War Hulk's chapters II and III land on your next two turns and both want a creature already on the battlefield. If the game will not last three more turns, spend the five mana on Purphoros and a body instead.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

3.6POLITICS

you have become the table's problem

Condition. you have become the table's problem — which is now the turn Purphoros lands, not the turn the reveal puts four bodies down

Indications.

  • Purphoros resolved and every seat took damage off your next creature. Damage at every seat at once reads as a clock nobody can block.
  • Two seats attacked you in one rotation.
  • The removal is going at Gishath, Molten Echoes and Purphoros, not at the bodies.
  • A seat offers you a no-attack deal while a third seat holds the larger board. That deal was made on 15 September, broken to kill Tom, and the seat it was made with won the game (log:002).

Immediate action.

  1. Count lethal across the whole table before you declare a single attacker: your total power against each seat's life, blockers subtracted. On 15 September it was 8 to 9 Dinosaurs and 30-plus power against Alex at 18 and Oliver at 15 (log:002).
  2. If two seats die in one alpha strike, swing at both. A seat left alive saves the seat you attacked, and on 15 September that is exactly what happened (log:002).
  3. If only one seat dies, attack the seat that can answer you rather than the lowest life total.
  4. Count the shields in hand and leave that mana open on every opponent's turn: Flawless Maneuver is free with a commander out, Heroic Intervention {1}{G}, Boros Charm {R}{W}, Akroma's Will {3}{W}, Eerie Interlude {2}{W}.
  5. Keep two blockers home. Gishath has vigilance and blocks after attacking; Curious Altisaur and Runic Armasaur are 2/5s and Temple Altisaur a 3/4.

Subsequent.

  1. Break a deal only to end a seat this turn, and say which board made you do it (log:002).
  2. When another seat's board is bigger than yours, say so with numbers: total power on each side, cards drawn this rotation.
  3. Once the table's wipe has landed on you, cast Blasphemous Act into their rebuilt boards (log:001).
  4. Do not promise a seat you will not damage them while Purphoros is on the battlefield. He hits every opponent for the same 2 and you cannot aim him.
NOTEThe one logged loss was not lost to a wipe or to removal. Lethal was on the board against two seats, the swing went at one of them, the other saved him, and the drain came back the same rotation (log:002). Purphoros makes this page more urgent, not less: the table's clock on you starts before your combat step does.

Drawn from 2 logged game(s): log:001, log:002.

3.7RACED

somebody is faster and you cannot answer on board

Condition. somebody's clock is faster than the commander, and blockers do not touch it

Indications.

  • Under 20 life on turn six with Gishath still in the command zone.
  • The clock is a drain, not an attack. Oliver's vampire spell gained, lost and dealt damage on one card and killed from 13 with the game otherwise won (log:002).
  • Their board doubled in one turn and yours did not.
  • A seat's first attack landed on you by round four.

Immediate action.

  1. Count your turns to Gishath. Two or more away, block with everything that is not a mana creature: chump with the bodies before Bloom Tender, Selvala or Hulking Raptor.
  2. Against a creature clock, cast Kinjalli's Sunwing ({2}{W}) first. Their creatures enter tapped, which strips the flashed-in blocker and every haste attacker.
  3. Against a drain or a burn clock, stop blocking and race. Cast Purphoros and then every creature you can: 2 at each seat per entry, 4 on a nontoken Dinosaur with Molten Echoes out, and Vaultborn Tyrant gains you 3 life on each power-4 entry.
  4. Count the creatures on the whole table for Blasphemous Act: at nine it is {R}. Cast it while your board is the smaller one or empty.
  5. If Gishath is on the battlefield and Akroma's Will is in hand, cast it after attackers and before blockers and take both modes: flying, double strike, lifelink, indestructible and protection from each colour. Fourteen from him, and the life back.

Subsequent.

  1. Zacama's {2}{W} is 3 life an activation and costs no other card.
  2. Savage Order ({2}{G}{G}, sacrificing a creature with power 4 or greater) puts Temple Altisaur or Trapjaw Tyrant onto the battlefield with indestructible until your next turn: a blocker their clock cannot kill.
  3. Point the connection at the seat racing you, not at the lowest life total.
  4. Purphoros's {2}{R} gives your creatures +1/+0 until end of turn. Activate it before combat damage, not after: Gishath's reveal is as wide as his power.
NOTEThe goldfish's kill by turn 8 is 0.609 against one opponent with no blockers, and its table counts Purphoros's 'each opponent' once. Read both as tempo, not as a race you are already winning.

Drawn from 1 logged game(s): log:002.

4Normal procedures

Pre-flight, startup, assembly, cruise, closing.

4.1Pre-flight

the mulligan: what a keepable hand looks like

Keep if all of.

  • Two to four lands, one of them making green: 39 cards in the deck carry a green pip.
  • A red source, or a fetch that finds one. Red is the deck's worst colour on curve — 0.61 with rocks and dorks against 0.87 white and 0.82 green — and Purphoros, Molten Echoes and Hunting Velociraptor all need it.
  • One mana piece castable by turn three (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Bloom Tender, Nature's Lore) or one reducer (Otepec Huntmaster, Knight of the Stampede, Urza's Incubator).
  • A play on turn two and a play on turn three.
  • A hand with a reducer and ramp over a hand with a payoff and ramp.

Ship on sight.

  • Seven cards with no green source.
  • Five or more lands with neither a mana piece nor a reducer.
  • A hand whose cheapest action is turn five: Pantlaza, Wrathful Raptors, Trapjaw Tyrant or Vaultborn Tyrant with nothing in front of them.
  • A hand whose only red card is Molten Echoes and whose only lands are Forests.
NOTEPurphoros and Molten Echoes are the payoff layer now and both are red. A hand that ramps in green and never finds a Mountain-typed land casts neither of them, and the reveal has nothing to cash into.

The model's own keep rule — two to five lands, up to two redraws — keeps 78% of first sevens. That is a floor: it does not read the cards.

4.2Startup

turns one to three, as a decision

  1. Turn one: play the land that enters tapped if you hold one, then Sol Ring.
  2. Name Dinosaur on Unclaimed Territory, Secluded Courtyard, Urza's Incubator, Belbe's Portal and Molten Echoes as each enters. Every time.
  3. Turn two: Otepec Huntmaster if he is in hand; otherwise Arcane Signet, Bloom Tender or Up the Beanstalk.
  4. Turn three: Nature's Lore for a Forest card that makes red — Stomping Ground or Cinder Glade — or Urza's Incubator. A body waits.
  5. Attack with nothing before turn four. In the first three turns spend Path to Exile or Swords to Plowshares on one thing only: a creature that kills Gishath on sight, or an engine that wins alone.

4.3Engine assembly

the order components come online, and why the order

  1. Mana to five by turn four or five: lands plus one rock or dork.
  2. Reducers before payoffs. Otepec Huntmaster, Knight of the Stampede and Urza's Incubator lower every Dinosaur cast after them, including the commander's.
  3. Purphoros before the fourth body. {3}{R} turns every later entry into 2 damage at each seat, and it survives the wipe that fourth body invites.
  4. Molten Echoes next, naming Dinosaur. From then, every nontoken Dinosaur that enters is two entries and two Purphoros triggers.
  5. Garruk's Uprising before another creature: it is an enchantment, it survives a creature wipe, and it draws off every power-4 arrival the reveal makes.
  6. Set the top on the turn he attacks, not before. Congregation at Dawn ({G}{G}{W}, instant) after your draw and before combat puts three Dinosaurs on top in your order, and all three fit inside a seven-card reveal.
  7. Cast Gishath on a turn he can attack a seat whose blockers do not kill him. He has haste and vigilance, so the cast turn is the attack turn; Flawless Maneuver in hand makes the tap-out safe.

4.4Cruise

the per-turn cycle

  1. Upkeep: Descendants' Path reveals the top card and CASTS a Dinosaur that shares a type — so a Zacama off it untaps your lands and a Wakening Sun's Avatar off it wipes. Bonehoard Dracosaur exiles two; play the land from among them.
  2. Main one: land, then a reducer, then Purphoros or Molten Echoes, then one body. Leave the shield mana open — {1}{G} for Heroic Intervention, {R}{W} for Boros Charm. Flawless Maneuver needs none.
  3. Combat: Gishath attacks the seat with no blocker that survives him. Rogue's Passage is {4} on top of everything else; Kessig Wolf Run is {X}{R}{G} for the extra trample damage.
  4. On the connection, order the triggers: the Pantlaza discover with the largest toughness on top, the Regisaur Alpha token last so it cannot re-trigger the once-a-turn discover, the Garruk's Uprising and Vaultborn Tyrant draws between (stack 001, decision 002).
  5. Count the Purphoros damage aloud as the bodies enter: 2 to each opponent per creature, and a second trigger per nontoken Dinosaur while Molten Echoes is out.
  6. When Molten Echoes copies a legendary Dinosaur — Itzquinth, Pantlaza, Zacama, Etali, either Ghalta — keep the original and put the copy in the graveyard. Its entry triggers are already on the stack and still resolve.
  7. After the connection, in main two, cast Dinosaurs from hand for prowl {2}{R} while Hunting Velociraptor is on the battlefield. A prowl cast is a cast, so Zacama untaps your lands and Wakening Sun's Avatar destroys all non-Dinosaurs.
  8. Still in main two: if Savage Order is in hand, sacrifice a Molten Echoes copy with power 4 or greater to it. The copy is exiled at the beginning of the next end step whatever you do.
  9. The rest of the board attacks only into an empty board or for lethal. The mid-tier Dinosaurs are blockers and wipe-bait (log:001).

4.5Closing

recognising lethal, and when to wait a turn

  1. Count commander damage per seat aloud. Twenty-one from Gishath ends a player: on 28 August it was 7 and then 14 (log:001), on 15 September 16 on top of 7 (log:002).
  2. Count the Purphoros route before the combat route: every creature you can land this turn is 2 at each seat, and each nontoken Dinosaur is 4 with Molten Echoes out.
  3. Eerie Interlude ({2}{W}) with Purphoros out is a burn spell. Exile your own board, and every creature re-entering at the next end step is 2 at each seat, doubled on the nontoken Dinosaurs.
  4. Overwhelming Stampede ({3}{G}{G}): +X/+X and trample to every creature, X your greatest power. Add X per attacker and compare the total with the life of the seat holding the fewest blockers.
  5. World War Hulk's chapter III doubles Gishath's power and gives trample: a 14/12 reveals fourteen cards.
  6. Akroma's Will with Gishath out, cast after attackers and before blockers, both modes: flying, double strike, lifelink, indestructible and protection from each colour on the whole board.
  7. Swing at every seat that dies this turn, not at the one that dies most comfortably (log:002).
  8. Wait a turn only when no shield is in hand and a seat holds four mana open with blue or white among it.

5.5Time to kill

What this model does not do (33 stated assumptions)
  • Simulates resource development, not full games (no interaction, no removal).
  • Draw every turn including turn 1 (multiplayer Commander).
  • Mulligan: keep 7-card hands with 2-5 lands; up to 2 fresh redraws, keep the last.
  • One land drop per turn when available.
  • LANDS ENTER UNTAPPED, ALWAYS, and the one in hand longest is the one played. A tapland costs nothing here and no land is ever chosen over another, so this model CANNOT rank two lands that make the same colours — `mana-analysis` and `mana-fit` are what answer a land question, and they are deterministic for exactly this reason.
  • Mana rocks ('{T}: Add') contribute from the turn after they are cast.
  • Commander cast on first affordable turn (highest spending priority).
  • Bodies count = creatures cast + tokens parsed from 'create ... token' text.
  • Target assembly counts cards DRAWN by a turn (cast cards still count).
  • X-SPELL DRAW IS PAID FOR OUT OF WHAT IS LEFT. An {X} instant or sorcery that draws X is cast AFTER every other loop has spent what it wanted, with the whole remaining pool, and only when X would be at least 2 — a pilot does not cast Stroke of Genius for one card, and every cheapest-first loop in this module would have, because Scryfall counts {X} as zero and the card's `cmc` is only its fixed part. That floor is the one authored number in this channel. Casting last makes the figure a FLOOR in the other direction too: the spell only ever gets mana nothing else asked for. Corpus sweep: 33 X-cost instants and sorceries that draw X, 28 credited and 5 skipped — a split card whose cmc is both halves, an alternative cost under which X is 0, and three whose X is bounded by a discard or by a graveyard this model does not have.
  • CARD DRAW IS ONE A TURN UNLESS `model_draw` IS SET. With the flag on, four channels are modelled — a card's own ETB draw, an instant or sorcery that draws, an upkeep trigger, and an ARRIVAL trigger that draws when other creatures or tokens enter (Welcoming Vampire, Caretaker's Talent), which rides the same single door the ETB damage payoffs use. Activated draw (needs a spending policy this model has no opinion on), X-based draw (board-dependent), and death- or attack-triggered draw (no deaths, and attacks only under `model_combat`) are NOT modelled and the cards are named in `meta.card_advantage.draw_not_modelled`. A conditional trigger — 'if you control an artifact', 'you may draw' — is treated as unmodelled rather than assumed to be on: 39 of the 348 ETB-draw cards in the corpus carry one.
  • AN ARRIVAL-DRAW ENGINE NEVER SEES ITS OWN ARRIVAL. Welcoming Vampire is a 2/3 that draws 'whenever one or more OTHER creatures you control with power 2 or less enter'; registered before its own entry it passed its own gate and drew a card it does not draw, overstating edgar-vampires by 22% at turn eight. The deferral applies to every card and not only the ones worded 'other', which understates Tocasia's Welcome and two relatives by one draw on the turn they land.
  • ARRIVAL DRAW IS SMALLER WITHOUT `model_combat`, and legitimately so. The channel rides the one door onto the battlefield, which `model_combat` also uses to spawn token copies and ETB-payoff tokens — those are real additional arrivals that do not exist in the resource-only model. Measured on edgar-vampires: 1.264 extra cards by turn ten with both flags against 1.106 with draw alone. Before 2026-08-28 the gap was 1.264 against 0.323, because every call to that door sat inside `if model_combat:` and a draw-only deck silently lost three quarters of its arrival draws.
  • HELD-UP INTERACTION is reported as two series and both are FLOORS. This model casts everything it can afford every turn, so `interaction_castable` is what a pilot developing at full speed has left over, not what a pilot choosing to hold up two mana would have. A low figure is therefore a real finding and a high one is unambiguous good news.
  • Tutors are modeled as wildcards: a CAST tutor that fetches to hand or the top — including a TYPED one (Worldly Tutor, Sarkhan's Triumph), which the old literal 'a card' match missed entirely — fills ONE missing any_of group. Consumed once, mana paid, and a tutor that puts the card on top of the library costs a turn. An ACTIVATED, ETB or death-triggered tutor does not count, because this model only knows 'drawn and affordable'; nor does a land fetch, which is ramp and has no channel here yet. Reported as the *_assisted figures; the unassisted figures beside them exclude tutors entirely.
  • ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD PAYOFFS are modelled: damage equal to the entering creature's power (Terror of the Peaks), X damage where X counts your board (Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest), a token (Lathliss) and a token COPY of what entered (Miirym). Every arrival fires them — cast, token or copy — and a copy is itself an arrival, so they compound, which is the deck working rather than a bug. A payoff worded 'another NONTOKEN' does not re-trigger on its own tokens, which is the brake the rules already had. A chain is capped at 12 deep. X counts the whole board rather than one subtype: exact where the creatures are Dragons, generous otherwise.
  • The COMMANDER joins the battlefield when cast, attacks, and fires its own triggers. It used to be a mana sink and a flag — cast, then dropped — so a 10/10 flier contributed no power and never swung. Commander tax, death and recasting are still not modelled: it is cast once and it stays.
  • STATIC cost reduction IS modeled: a commander's eminence from the command zone (live from turn one, unremovable) and typed reducers once they are on the battlefield. It pays GENERIC only and is floored at the coloured pip count, because a discount can never pay a pip. A reduction that SCALES with a board state (Animar, Rakdos, Hamza) is refused rather than counted flat, and cost reduction on artifacts, noncreature spells or a colour is not modeled at all.
  • Rituals are not modeled (conservative).
  • Extra card draw is NOT modeled: one card per turn, always. That understates every deck, but by an amount proportional to how much draw it runs — so it is conservative WITHIN a deck and not neutral BETWEEN them. `meta.card_advantage` reports how much of each list this hides. Card advantage is measured nowhere in this suite.
  • Treasures are a one-shot STOCKPILE, not a mana rock: spent only when lands and rocks fall short, and gone once broken. Reported separately from mean_available_mana_by_turn, which still means repeatable mana per turn.
  • Only Treasure triggers a goldfish can see are modeled — upkeep and landfall are RECURRING, Saga chapters likewise; a `cast` or enters-the-battlefield trigger pays out ONCE, when its own source resolves. Combat- and opponent-gated sources produce NOTHING here, because this model has no combat and no opponents; they are named in meta.treasure_sources_not_modelled so a low hoard figure is legible.
  • COMBAT: one opponent at 40 life who does nothing — no blockers, no removal, no interaction. This is a goldfish in the literal sense, so `kill_turn` is the turn an UNOPPOSED board would finish ONE seat, not a win rate.
  • Attacks with every creature that is not summoning-sick; haste is read from the ORACLE TEXT (this line said 'type line' and was simply wrong). There is nothing to block, so nothing is ever held back — in a real four-player game you would keep blockers, which makes this an optimistic clock and a pessimistic board.
  • A COMMANDER'S COMBAT-DAMAGE REVEAL IS DECLARED PER DECK (`model_commander_combat_reveal`, 2026-09-11, Gishath): when the commander is on the battlefield and the deck attacks, with the declared connect rate it reveals its damage's worth of cards, every creature of the named type among them enters through the one door (ETB payoffs, engines, reductions all register; nothing that needs a CAST fires), the rest go to the bottom. The rate is Forge's, named in the record; this model has no blockers.
  • AN ENTRY TRIGGER THAT NAMES A TYPE FIRES ON THAT TYPE (2026-09-11): Dragon Tempest no longer fires on a mana dork; Molten Echoes copies the chosen type. Tokens carry no type line here and pass the gate. A CAST fires what listens for a cast, at every door the commander included: draw on a spell of mana value N or more (Up the Beanstalk), draw for a paid mana on a creature cast (Lifecrafter's Bestiary, paid when the pool has it), damage on casting a creature of power N or more (Sarkhan's Unsealing, counted as noncombat damage to the one opponent). A spell that has a creature deal its power to each opponent (Chandra's Ignition) deals the biggest body's power once; the wipe half has nothing to hit. A tutor that puts a creature ONTO THE BATTLEFIELD (Savage Order) sacrifices the smallest 4-power nontoken body and fetches the highest-power creature of the named type the library holds -- a policy, stated. Evasion for the commander (Majestic Heliopterus) is Forge's to read: this model has no blockers and the connect rate is declared.
  • A LAND-MANA BONUS (Mirari's Wake and three others) adds one mana per land from the turn after it lands; DRAW EQUAL TO THE GREATEST POWER resolves against the board at cast and is held while the board is empty; DRAW A CARD FOR EACH <type> ON ENTRY counts the board it joins; an attack token AS BIG AS THE BEST OTHER ATTACKER (Ghalta and Mavren) takes the second-largest swing. NOT modelled and named: Etali, Primal Storm (casting other players' cards is outside this model) and draw equal to combat damage dealt by one creature (Hunter's Insight).
  • TEAM HASTE IS MODELLED (2026-09-11). A permanent that says 'creatures you control have haste' lets every creature attack the turn it lands, from the turn the grant itself lands; a typed grant (Karrthus: Dragons) reads the creature's type line; Dragon Tempest's grant reads keyword flying; riot is read as always choosing haste. TOKENS carry no type line and no flying here, so a typed or flying grant does not reach them. NOT read: a grant from the graveyard (Anger), an activated one (Crashing Drawbridge), one conditional on the board ('as long as'), and the lands whose mana carries haste (Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory) -- this model taps no particular land. Before this a granted creature waited a turn and its haste enabler was read as nothing.
  • Attack triggers, combat-damage triggers and additional combat phases are modelled, which is what makes Treasure sources gated on combat produce here when they produce nothing without this flag. Effects the parser cannot read are named in meta.combat_effects_not_modelled.
  • DAMAGE MULTIPLICATION is modelled and stacks MULTIPLICATIVELY as the rules do: doubling the power, swinging twice and doubling the damage dealt is eight times, not four. Three wordings are read — a replacement effect on damage you deal (Twinflame Tyrant), a granted double strike (Atarka) and a power doubling (Thrakkus). A grant worded 'each Dragon you control' is treated as applying to the WHOLE team: exact in a deck whose attackers are Dragons, generous in one where they are not. A creature's own double strike multiplies only itself. Board power is unaffected — a double-striker is not a bigger creature.
  • Bodies count CREATURES only under this flag. Without it a Treasure token scores as a creature, which inflates `mean_bodies_by_turn` for any deck that makes non-creature tokens.
  • LIFE LOSS ON ARRIVAL COUNTS AS DAMAGE. 'Each opponent loses 1 life' (Corpse Knight) and 'deals 1 damage to each opponent' (Impact Tremors) are the same event, the same cadence and the same number against one opponent at 40 life, and only the second was priced until 2026-08-28. They are NOT the same in the rules — lifelink, damage prevention and 'whenever an opponent loses life' all separate them — and this model has none of those. Nine corpus cards read through this channel, plus Mirkwood Bats through a token-creation channel of its own.
  • DEATH-TRIGGERED DRAIN IS NOT MODELLED AND IS THE LARGEST KNOWN GAP HERE. Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Elas il-Kor all key on a creature DYING, and nothing dies in this model: no blockers, no removal, no sacrifice outlets. Their contribution is ABSENT, not zero, and a deck whose kill runs through them is understated here by however much that line is worth. `simulate` against a real pod is where that number lives.

7Handling and rules of engagement

Threat optics, alliances, and who to hit first.

7.1Threat optics — what the table sees

  • Through turn six the table sees a ramp deck with a 4/4. The commander is visible in the command zone the whole time, and an experienced seat prices its wrath for the turn he becomes castable (decision 001).
  • Purphoros changes what the table sees. The first time every seat takes 2 off a creature entering, you are the clock — and you are it before you have attacked anybody.
  • On 28 August the table read the mid-tier Dinosaurs as the threat, spent its wraths on them and held nothing for the commander (log:001). That is one table on one night.
  • On 15 September the pilot's board stayed small through the early wipe waves because he was short on green, and it saved the creatures (log:002). Holding bodies back through a wipe war is a posture, not an accident.

7.2When to reveal

  • Cast the reducers the turn they are castable. They are the tell and they are the plan; a turn of hiding Otepec Huntmaster is a turn of discount lost.
  • Cast Purphoros on a turn no creature enters. A god that has damaged nobody yet draws less removal than one that has.
  • Hold Congregation at Dawn and Worldly Tutor for the turn of the swing.
  • Pass with zero mana open when Flawless Maneuver is the shield, and let the table read no protection (decision 001).
  • Blasphemous Act stays in hand until your board is smaller than theirs or empty (log:001, stack 004).

7.3Alliances

  • Offer removal, not attacks. A Path to Exile or a Swords to Plowshares on the table's shared problem buys a rotation of not being attacked.
  • Do not offer a no-attack deal while Purphoros is on the battlefield. He damages that seat every time a creature enters and you cannot turn him off.
  • No alliance survives the first connection. Four bodies off one trigger make you the board.
  • Break a deal only to end a seat this turn, and end every seat the swing reaches (log:002).
  • A tax seat is the one to break early. Three reducers hold Gishath at {R}{G}{W}; nobody else's eight-drop is castable under a tax.

7.4Who to hit first

  • The first connection goes at the seat with no untapped blocker. The reveal pays the same at any life total, and seven damage is not a kill.
  • After that, the seat whose clock is not a creature. A drain or a burn seat is the one blockers do nothing about (log:002).
  • Commander damage stacks on one player. The seat that has taken 7 takes the next 14: count toward 21 (log:001).
  • Never the lowest life total for its own sake. On 15 September the low seat was saved by the seat left untouched, and that seat won the game (log:002).
  • Purphoros picks no target. When two seats are within 6 of dead, land creatures instead of choosing.
