Mulligan Guide ★ COACHING

Keep hands that can do two things: make Goblins early and cast spells cheaply. The gold standard is three lands (or two plus Sol Ring, Skirk Prospector, or Ruby Medallion), a one-or-two-mana token maker — Dragon Fodder, Krenko's Command, Mogg War Marshal, Searslicer Goblin — and a cantrip like Crimson Wisps or Expedite banked for the turn Zada comes down. Zada is always waiting in the command zone at four mana, so your opening hand doesn't need a payoff; it needs bodies and mana. Ship hands that are all crescendo and no verse — Blasphemous Act, Mana Geyser, and Empty the Warrens with two lands is a mulligan, because rituals and storm cards do nothing without a board and a spell count. Faithless Looting and Witch's Mark upgrade marginal keeps by trading dead cards for live ones. One table tip: the sideboard's Red Mana and Storm Counter cards are Secret Lair table aids with no rules text — put them in front of you on big turns to track floating ritual mana and your storm count, because a ten-spell turn is exactly when bookkeeping errors happen. The goldfish numbers back the plan: under the 2-to-5-land keep rule, 79% of first sevens are keepable, and the deck averages its first token maker by turn 3.2 — so a keep with bodies and cheap spells is the statistical default, not a gamble.
