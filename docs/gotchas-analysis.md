# The analysis layer

Synergy, power creep and the obsolescence index, card roles, region clustering —
everything under `src/manamap/analysis/`. Read before touching any of it.

`CLAUDE.md` loads into every session; this does not, so the rules that bite
regardless of what you are touching stayed there and the full record lives here.

## The obsolescence index

- **IT PUBLISHED A VERDICT IT COULD NOT SUPPORT, AND NOTHING MEASURED IT.** Audited over all **22,753 claimed replacements** across 7,788 anchors, every one of them rendered to users as **"Obsoleted By"** in the card panel. **15.5%** counted a COST as an advantage — Prognostic Sphinx charges *discard a card* for hexproof and was offered over Soul of the Rapids, which has it unconditionally, with the difference reported as `Additional: discard`. **22.9%** of replacements added a restriction the original lacked (timing 7.1%, additional cost 8.9%, conditional 6.4%). Of the pairs where both cards have an activated ability, **29.9%** made that ability MORE expensive: Bartolomé del Presidio's sac ability is free, the "upgrade" charges `{2}`, and the index reported *Better Toughness*. **8.2%** were commander-illegal, acorn cards included. **30.8%** were played LESS than the card they outclassed. **36.5% failed a purely mechanical check**, and that is a floor — a regex cannot see semantics.
- **THE ROOT CAUSE IS ONE `.*`.** `MECHANICAL_TAGS["death_trigger"]` is `when .* dies`; `attack_trigger` and the damage triggers have the same shape. **The wildcard sits exactly where the subject noun lives**, so *"whenever a Goblin you control dies"* and *"whenever another creature dies"* produce byte-identical tag sets, `a.tags <= b.tags` is trivially true in both directions, and Boggart Mischief was offered over Bastion of Remembrance. **The gate was the substring the regex threw away.** It cannot be fixed in `MECHANICAL_TAGS`: that dict is model-facing and editing it invalidates `model_ability.pt`, so the subject is recovered separately in `power_creep.tribal_gates`, which captures the window between the trigger word and its verb and intersects it with the corpus's own creature types.
- **WHAT WAS ALREADY RIGHT MATTERS AS MUCH AS WHAT WAS WRONG.** Of the pairs where both cards are role-classified, **82.0% share a functional role other than `threat:body`** — the embedding is mostly finding cards that do the same job, so the RETRIEVAL half worked and only the JUDGEMENT half failed. That number is the reason a repair that bought precision by discarding recall would have fixed nothing, and it is asserted after the change too: **81.1%**.
- **A SCORE, NOT A GATE — and the first cut of the repair got the shape wrong.** Hard gates fixed every measured class (restrictions 22.9% → 0.1%, costs-as-gains → 0, ability-cost → 0) and were still wrong in KIND, for two reasons. A gate discards every near-miss, so a pilot never sees the pair that is 80% of an upgrade. And the restriction CLASSES are coarse enough to **cancel**: Frightshroud Courier and Arnim Zola both carry `conditional`, so the set difference is empty and a real narrowing passes silently. `strength` is 0.0–1.0 and **multiplicative**, so two problems compound rather than averaging, and the pilot sets their own line.
- **THE WEIGHTS ARE CALIBRATED AGAINST THE DOCUMENTED FAILURES, NOT CHOSEN.** Boggart Mischief → Bastion of Remembrance **0.12**, Bartolomé **0.20**, Prognostic Sphinx **0.28**, against Cancel → Counterspell at **0.61**. `tests/test_power_creep.py` anchors each one, so a weight cannot be tuned until the fixtures stop meaning anything. Fleet-wide the score **SEPARATES +0.359** — mean 0.271 where a problem is mechanically detectable against 0.630 where none is — which is the figure that says it is carrying weight rather than decorating a panel. `eval-obsolescence` prints the histogram and the separation on every run; **a change that does not move them did not do anything.**
- **TWO HARD GATES SURVIVE, AND ONLY TWO.** Legality, because an illegal card is not a candidate in any degree (8.2% → 0.0%); and *nothing left to compare on* — once tags are valenced, a pair whose only claimed advantage was a COST has no gains at all and is not a comparison. Everything else is priced. That is what took the index from 22,753 pairs to **20,827**: the drop is the two gates, not a threshold anybody tuned.
- **THE LIMIT IT DOES NOT FIX, STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN.** Leaden Myr (`{T}: Add {B}`, a mana rock) against H.E.R.B.I.E. (a flying combat creature) scores **0.405** — mid-range, no mechanical problem to detect, and the pair is nonsense. That is a RETRIEVAL failure at similarity 0.944, and no comparison score repairs the search. The strength measures how strong the comparison is *given* that the two cards do the same job; it does not check that they do.
- **`obsoleted_by` → `compare_with`, and the LABEL was the part making the claim.** "Obsoleted" is a statement about all contexts, almost never true, and useless when it is. The entry carries `gains`, `costs`, `narrows` and `strength` — **both sides** — and the viz badges them apart, because every badge used to be the same red and a card that CHARGED you something rendered identically to one that gave you something. Four consumers move together (`export/viz_index.py`'s `obs` block, `pool_facts.obsolete_in_pool`, `validate_considering`, the card panel) and each reads the old key as a fallback, since a reader that silently answers zero is worse than one that fails.
- **THE `newer` GATE IS NEAR A COIN FLIP AND IS KEPT ANYWAY.** It encodes "printed later ⇒ stronger" and predicts *actually played more* **67.7%** of the time (57.6% before the two gates; **43.6% for 2026 printings, below chance**). It stays because it is what makes the relation ANTISYMMETRIC — without it every pair appears twice, in both directions — and because printed later is evidence of a larger card pool rather than of a stronger card. Rank is reported beside it and never filtered on: Storm Crow is genuinely outclassed by a card nobody plays, which is structurally true and useless, and the pilot is the one who should see the number.
- **THE EVAL DUPLICATES THE RESTRICTION REGEXES ON PURPOSE.** `eval_obsolescence.GATES` re-implements the vocabulary `power_creep` uses rather than importing it. An eval that shares its subject's definition of a defect **cannot see a defect in that definition** — which is the failure mode that let the index ship for months. The residual 0.1% of restriction hits it still reports is exactly that duplication earning its keep, and it must be read card by card rather than driven to zero.

## Shared vocabulary

- **`analysis/` MAY NOT IMPORT `pilot/`, so anything both need moves DOWN.** The tribal gate needs the corpus's creature types, which `pilot/assess` had already derived — including the trap that `Artifact Creature — Treasure Dog` makes *Treasure* an honest creature type while "Treasures you control" means the token every time. Copying it would let the triage that WARNS a pilot and the index that RANKS cards for them drift apart on what a tribe is, so the scan lives in `analysis/common.creature_types(df)` and `assess` calls it. It returns an empty set when the frame has no `type_line`: a synthetic fixture legitimately has none, and raising would make every unit test of the caller carry a column it has no opinion about.
- **`RESTRICTION_PATTERNS` and `TAG_VALENCE` are SEPARATE dicts from `MECHANICAL_TAGS`**, for the reason `ROLE_PATTERNS` is: `MECHANICAL_TAGS` is model-facing and any edit invalidates `model_ability.pt` and forces a retrain of steps 3–5. A restriction class or a tag's sign changes often; the retrieval vocabulary must not. `DEFAULT_TAG_VALENCE = "context"` — an unvalenced tag is reported as a DIFFERENCE, never as an advantage, so adding a tag can never silently manufacture a gain.
- **The regexes run per PAIR, so they must be precomputed per CARD.** Step 11 is an O(n²) inner loop inside each supertype group; compiling the restriction, tribal and activation scans into the per-card record took it from over ten minutes back to **~5.5 min**. Anything new that reads oracle text belongs in the record-building pass, not the comparison.
