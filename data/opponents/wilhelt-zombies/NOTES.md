# Stuart — Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver (Zombie typal, sacrifice drain)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average Wilhelt deck, a stand-in for Stuart's real list.

## How it wins

- **Zombies that come back and drain on the way out.** The pilot's description
  (2026-11-12): Zombies get +1/+1 and come back from the graveyard, decayed tokens, and
  Blood Artist and Zulaport Cutthroat turning every sacrifice into a drain.
- **Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver** ({2}{U}{B}): whenever another Zombie he controls dies, if it
  didn't have decayed, he makes a 2/2 decayed Zombie; at his end step he may sacrifice a
  Zombie and draw a card. That is the end-step sacrifice that killed the pilot.
- sharknado 009: Sean was at 1 life with Wheel of Fortune in hand and a comeback planned;
  on Stuart's end step Stuart sacrificed a Zombie, the drain dealt 1, and Sean died before
  his turn. Stuart then outlasted Steven and took the game.

## How to answer it

- **Remove Blood Artist early** (the pilot's rule, 2026-11-12). Left on the table, every
  creature death drains the table and gains Stuart life, yours included.
- **Count his end step as damage.** At low life his end-step sacrifice is a reach of at
  least 1 per drain piece out; do not plan a comeback for your own turn if you cannot
  survive his end step.
- He is slow to start when he misses land drops (game 1 of 2026-11-12: stalled on three
  lands all night).
