# Tom — Purphoros, God of the Forge (mono-red ETB pings)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average Purphoros deck, a stand-in for Tom's real list. **KILL ON SIGHT**
(the pilot's call, 2026-11-10).

## How it wins

- **Purphoros, God of the Forge** ({3}{R}): indestructible; not a creature while Tom's
  devotion to red is under five. Whenever another creature enters under Tom's control, it
  deals 2 damage to each opponent. {2}{R}: his creatures get +1/+0 until end of turn.
  Every goblin token is 2 to each of us, so a token turn is a burn turn. The trigger hits
  PLAYERS only, never creatures: our small goblins die to his attacks and his other
  pingers (Goblin Sharpshooter, edgar-vampires 004), not to Purphoros.
- **Fast mana into a turn-two Purphoros** (goblin-storm 004: turn-one Sol Ring, four mana
  on turn two, Purphoros on turn two, the game effectively over by turn four).
- Its downside: it runs out of gas if it does not close (goblin-storm 004 late game). That
  only matters if the table lives through the first four turns.

## How to answer it

- **Upstream: deny the Sol Ring.** Early artifact removal on his opener is a legitimate
  tempo play (goblin-storm 005: Abrade on the Sol Ring delayed Purphoros by turns). Expect
  salt; do it anyway.
- **Once it lands, only the right removal works.** Indestructible (CR 702.12b) means
  "destroy" does nothing: Disenchant, Wrath-style wipes and a lethal damage pile all fail.
  It takes exile, bounce, tuck or sacrifice. While it is not a creature, creature removal
  (Swords to Plowshares, Path) cannot target it. As a commander it goes back to the
  command zone and comes back, so the answer buys turns, not the game.
- **No enchantment answer in hand: make Tom the removal target.** Pressure his life and
  his token makers, and race: every creature he adds is 2 off each of our lives.
