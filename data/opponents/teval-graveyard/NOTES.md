# Ryan — Teval, the Balanced Scale (B/G/U graveyard)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average Teval deck, a stand-in for Ryan's real list (a modified precon, per the
pilot). The pilot calls it Ryan's best deck. (The 2026-10-27 log spells it "T'Vol".)

## How it wins

- **Teval, the Balanced Scale** ({1}{B}{G}{U}, 4/4 flyer): whenever one or more cards leave
  his graveyard, he creates a 2/2 black Zombie Druid. Attacking mills three and returns a
  land from the graveyard, which is itself a trigger.
- **The loop: Teval + Gravecrawler + Phyrexian Altar** (goblin-storm 003). Sacrifice
  Gravecrawler to the Altar for one mana, then cast it from the graveyard for {B} (allowed
  while he controls a Zombie, and the Druids are Zombies). It leaves the graveyard, so Teval
  makes a Druid. Each loop is mana-neutral and adds a 2/2 Zombie, and the spare Druids
  sacrifice to the Altar for mana: an unbounded board and unbounded mana. In 003 it filled
  the board with enough Druids to swing lethal at both opponents.
- Otherwise "graveyard shenanigans" (edgar-vampires 006): value off cards moving out of the
  graveyard.

## How to answer it

- **The Altar is the weak point** (goblin-storm 003: Untimely Malfunction killed it and the
  engine stopped). Without a sacrifice outlet Gravecrawler cannot loop. Kill the outlet the
  moment the loop is visible; it is the cheapest piece to answer.
- Teval is the other required piece (no Teval, no Druids). Graveyard exile on Gravecrawler
  in response to a recast also breaks one loop, but the Altar or Teval breaks all of them.
- Politics: killing the Altar while Alex was also in the game drew a "kingmaker" complaint
  from Ryan; the pilot's answer was that without it the game was over. When the loop is
  live, answering it is not optional.
