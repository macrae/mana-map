# Oliver — Krenko, Mob Boss (mono-red tokens and pings)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average Krenko deck, a stand-in for Oliver's real list. Oliver's other deck at
this table is **Balin** (a token maker; no seat yet). **KILL KRENKO ON SIGHT** (the
pilot's call, 2026-11-10).

## How it wins

- **Krenko, Mob Boss** ({2}{R}{R}, 3/3): {T}: create X 1/1 Goblins, where X is the
  number of Goblins he controls. Doubling every activation, then pings and an alpha strike.
- sharknado 008: Krenko landed, stayed, and Oliver went off for 30-plus damage to Sean in
  one turn. goblin-storm 002: Oliver killed the table on turn five off Krenko tokens and
  pings.

## How to answer it

- **The window is the turn he lands.** Krenko taps to activate, so without haste the first
  activation comes on Oliver's next turn. Remove him before Oliver untaps. Every untap
  after that doubles what is left to answer.
- **Spot removal on the commander beats your own tempo play.** In sharknado 008 Sean held
  Swords to Plowshares and a second two-mana removal spell with the table asking for the
  kill, cast Shabraz instead, then passed another turn with mana open. The pilot's rule:
  when the whole table names the threat and only you can answer it, answer it.
- Once the board is wide, Krenko himself is no longer the problem; a sweeper or a blocker
  wall is.
