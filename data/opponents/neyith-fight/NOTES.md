# Alex — R/G fight (Neyith of the Dire Hunt)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average Neyith deck, a stand-in for Alex's real list. The pilot calls it his
R/G fight deck, bracket 3; the commander is the seat's reading (`source.json`), not yet
named in a log.

## How it plays

- **Fight is his removal.** He kills creatures one at a time by fighting them, and goes
  for the commander: in goblin-storm 003 he fought Zada.
- **Neyith of the Dire Hunt** ({2}{G}{G}, 3/3) draws him a card whenever his creatures fight
  or become blocked, so every fight is also card advantage. At his beginning of combat he
  can pay {2}{R/G} to double a creature's power and force it to be blocked.
- He hits hard once the board is clear: in goblin-storm 003 he killed Ryan and hit Sean for
  17 (40 to 23).

## How to answer it

- **A fight needs both creatures.** If either is gone, or is an illegal target, when the
  spell resolves, neither fights or deals damage (CR 701.14b). So in response to a fight:
  - redirect it or give your creature hexproof (Deflecting Swat moved the fight onto a 1/1
    goblin and saved Zada, goblin-storm 003); or
  - remove HIS fighter, and the whole spell does nothing.
- Hold protection for your commander rather than spending it early: his removal comes as
  targeted fights, not wipes.
