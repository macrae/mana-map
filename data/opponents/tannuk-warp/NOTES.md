# Alex — Tannuk, Steadfast Second (mono-red warp)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average Tannuk deck, a stand-in for Alex's real list; this page is what he
actually does with it.

## How it wins

- **Tannuk, Steadfast Second** ({2}{R}{R}, 3/5): his other creatures have haste, and every
  artifact and red creature card in his hand has warp {2}{R}. It is the whole deck: without
  it his big creatures cost full price and arrive without haste.
- He ramps to about 15 mana, warps a large number of big creatures in, and swings.
- **The Sundial line.** At his end step the warp exile triggers go on the stack. He gets
  priority first and taps **Sundial of the Infinite** ({1}, {T}: end the turn; only during
  its controller's turn). Ending the turn exiles every object on the stack (CR 724.1b), so
  the triggers are gone and the warped creatures stay for good.

## How to answer it

- **Tannuk is kill on sight.** Remove it at instant speed BEFORE his turn: the previous
  player's end step, or his upkeep. Exile or destroy beats a bounce or phase-out, which only
  buys a turn, and 5 toughness shrugs off small burn. Each death adds commander tax.
- **The Sundial goes in his main phase, after the warp mana is spent and before combat.**
  Answering the activation does nothing (CR 113.7a: the ability resolves without its
  source), and at his end step he holds priority before you can act. Killed in time, the
  warp triggers resolve normally and his board is exiled at end of turn.
- Counters that hit only noncreature spells do not stop a warped creature, and effects that
  lock out opponents only on YOUR turn (Grand Abolisher) do nothing against a Sundial that
  works only on his.
