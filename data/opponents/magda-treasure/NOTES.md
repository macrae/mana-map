# Steven — Magda (mono-red Dwarves, Treasure and Dragons)

The table's read of this seat, from the pilot's games. The decklist beside this file is
EDHREC's average **Magda, the Hoardmaster** deck, a stand-in for Steven's real list: the
pilot said "mono red", and Magda, the Hoardmaster is the mono-red Magda (Magda, Brazen
Outlaw is red-white). The average list does NOT run **Utvara Hellkite**, which Steven
"always fetches"; his real list does. **Magda sets the pace of this pod** (2026-11-12).

## How it wins

- **Cheap Dwarves make Treasure, then he tutors an artifact or Dragon onto the
  battlefield** and pops off fast (the pilot's description, 2026-11-12).
- **Utvara Hellkite** ({6}{R}{R}, flying): whenever a Dragon he controls attacks, he makes
  a 6/6 flying Dragon. Each attack multiplies the air force.
- edgar-vampires 008: treasures, a tutor, Utvara Hellkite, then a stack of 6/6 flyers;
  Steven was at 38 while Sean was at 20.

## How to answer it

- **Make him block, or make his blockers die.** edgar-vampires 008: Crossway
  Troublemakers gave the wide Vampire board deathtouch, so blocking with the big Dragons
  cost him the Dragons. sharknado 009: an 18-power flying, trampling Shabraz made him
  double-block with two 6/6 Dragons and cost him both.
- **The Dragons are the engine.** Removing the big evasive bodies is what slows him
  (the pilot's read after sharknado 009).
- He wins fast if left alone: do not spend the early turns ignoring him.
