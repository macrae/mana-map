## Summary

Teach Blue is one of five mono-colour teaching decks. Brand-new players play it against the other four. It has never been played and is still being tuned. Budget is about $100, rares allowed, no sideboard.

Blue's lesson is the stack. Players learn counterspells, flash, the untap step and upkeep. They also learn that card flow is a resource and that flyers win games. It is best taught last, after combat is understood. It leans on [Essence Scatter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Essence+Scatter) and [Quench](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quench) for the stack, [Pestermite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pestermite) and [Spectral Sailor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spectral+Sailor) for flash, and [Wind Drake](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wind+Drake) and [Air Elemental](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Air+Elemental) to win in the air.

## How it plays

You hold your mana and let the other player act first. Cheap flash creatures and counterspells make "what can I do right now?" a real question. The deck is built to teach that, and nothing here is measured yet.

The stack lesson is the heart of it. [Essence Scatter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Essence+Scatter) answers a creature and [Quench](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quench) answers anything, but the opponent can pay for Quench. Counterspells are capped on purpose so that new players do not sit through games where nothing resolves. Four counters in total is the cap, and whether that is the right number is open.

The flash lesson comes next. [Pestermite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pestermite) and [Spectral Sailor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spectral+Sailor) arrive on the opponent's end step. That teaches that you can cast things outside your own turn, and that the end step is the best moment to do it. Pestermite also taps or untaps a permanent, which shows the untap step from the other side.

The untap step is taught through [Claustrophobia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Claustrophobia) and [Frost Lynx](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Frost+Lynx). Both stop a creature from untapping. Nobody can act during the untap step itself, so the lesson is about why the creature stays tapped, not about responding.

Upkeep comes from [Geist of the Archives](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Geist+of+the+Archives), which scries at the start of each upkeep. Card flow comes from [Opt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Opt) and [Cloudkin Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cloudkin+Seer). They show that looking at cards and drawing are different things.

The win is in the air. [Wind Drake](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wind+Drake) and [Air Elemental](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Air+Elemental) fly over ground blockers, which also reinforces what combat taught earlier.
