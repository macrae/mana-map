## Summary

Heliod is a blue-white control deck that makes the whole table draw, then bills the table for it. Counterspells keep the stack quiet, the draw-punishers turn opponents' extra cards into our cards and treasure, and Heliod, the Warped Eclipse makes it all cheaper and gives us instant speed. It wins by casting [Approach of the Second Sun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun) twice, and the second cast is the whole plan.

Nobody is in a hurry here. The commander does not have to come down early, and the pilot's own lesson from both games is to live long enough to set up the win. The Numbers section has the honest picture: the engine shows up a lot, the win condition access is the thin part.

Its soft spot is the commander. Lose [Heliod, the Radiant Dawn // Heliod, the Warped Eclipse](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heliod%2C+the+Radiant+Dawn+%2F%2F+Heliod%2C+the+Warped+Eclipse) and the discount is gone. Protection came first in the v1.1 to v1.2 rebuilds for that reason.

## How it plays

**Set the table drawing.** Cheap draw-for-everyone permanents go down early: [Howling Mine](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Howling+Mine), [Kami of the Crescent Moon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kami+of+the+Crescent+Moon), [Font of Mythos](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Font+of+Mythos), [Dictate of Kruphix](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dictate+of+Kruphix), [Temple Bell](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Temple+Bell), [Kwain, Itinerant Meddler](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kwain%2C+Itinerant+Meddler). They are the fuel for everything else.

**Bill the table for it.** [Rhystic Study](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rhystic+Study), [Mystic Remora](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mystic+Remora), [Smothering Tithe](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Smothering+Tithe), [Consecrated Sphinx](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Consecrated+Sphinx) and [Faerie Mastermind](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Faerie+Mastermind) turn opponents' draws into cards and treasure for us. [Gleaming Splendor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gleaming+Splendor) makes treasure when an opponent draws a second card in a turn, so it only works once something else has the table drawing extra.

**Hold up the stack.** Eight-odd counterspells and free spells ([Force of Negation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Force+of+Negation), [Fierce Guardianship](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fierce+Guardianship), [Swan Song](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swan+Song) and friends) let us answer the table's threats and its answers to our plan. Cheap taxers ([Propaganda](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Propaganda), [Ghostly Prison](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghostly+Prison), [Archangel of Tithes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archangel+of+Tithes)) keep attackers off.

**Find and cast the win.** [Long-Term Plans](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Long-Term+Plans), [Mystical Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mystical+Tutor) and [Enlightened Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enlightened+Tutor) set up [Approach of the Second Sun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun). Stack 002 proves the finish: the second cast from hand wins as it resolves, and the only place anyone can stop it is on the stack. So the counter wall matters most on that turn. With Heliod flipped and treasure, the seven-mana cost is far less scary than it looks. In game 002 the pilot overthought that, and the window closed.

**Sequencing rules from the pilot.** Wipe first, then resolve Heliod. A deck with [Supreme Verdict](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Supreme+Verdict) and [Day of Judgment](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Day+of+Judgment) can kill its own commander. Tutor Approach to third from the top right away. Do not play [Smothering Tithe](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Smothering+Tithe) into an empty board, because it makes you the target. Do not use the channel-tutor spirit as a blocker when it should be tutoring.

**Playing it.** The commander can wait. Keep hands with two or three lands, a draw piece and a counter. The pilot admits momentum, tempo and table optics are the recurring gap, and that it is the pilot, not the deck.
