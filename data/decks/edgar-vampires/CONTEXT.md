## Summary

Edgar Markov is the pilot's bracket-4 Mardu vampire deck: white, black and a lean touch of red, built around life gain and life drain. The gentle plan is to flood the table with Vampires while [Edgar Markov](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Edgar+Markov) mints a 1/1 from the command zone off every Vampire cast. The plan the deck is actually built FOR is the drain infinite: [Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodthirsty+Conqueror) with [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vito%2C+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose) (proved in stack 004), or either of them with [Sanguine Bond](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanguine+Bond) or [Exquisite Blood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exquisite+Blood) (stacks 005, 006, 001). Infinite combos are welcome here.

In the pilot's own four words: blood drain, life gain, action at a distance, ferocious. He wants damage that is not combat damage, and a table that cannot wipe his way out of a win. He also wants more cards in hand when the game reaches turns seven and eight, because that is where every game so far has run dry.

It was sleeved on 2026-10-06 as v2.0.0. Several of the new cards were ordered and are played as proxies until they arrive.

## How it plays

**The soft start.** Cheap Vampires come down early and each one makes a token through Edgar's eminence. It works even if Edgar is never cast (stacks 002 and 010). The tokens chump, carry [Crossway Troublemakers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crossway+Troublemakers)'s deathtouch and lifelink, and feed the sacrifice outlets. [Anointed Procession](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Anointed+Procession) and [Elspeth, Storm Slayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elspeth%2C+Storm+Slayer) double that token flow.

**The kill, three ways.**
- The loop: a "when they lose life, I gain it" half ([Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodthirsty+Conqueror), [Exquisite Blood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exquisite+Blood)) next to a "when I gain life, they lose it" half ([Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vito%2C+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose), [Sanguine Bond](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanguine+Bond)). Each half feeds the other forever. [Marauding Blight-Priest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Marauding+Blight-Priest) is a smaller version of the second half. The Numbers section shows how often both halves are drawn together by turn six; it is a narrow window, which is why the tutors matter.
- Drain without combat: [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist), [Cruel Celebrant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cruel+Celebrant), [Sanctum Seeker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanctum+Seeker), [Wispdrinker Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wispdrinker+Vampire) and [Tithe Drinker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tithe+Drinker) chip away at the table every time something dies, attacks or arrives. [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodletter+of+Aclazotz) doubles all of it on the pilot's turn (stacks 003 and 007).
- The big swing: [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) turns the whole board into one huge lifelinker, and its counters can come off as -1/-1 on opposing creatures, which beats indestructible. Stack 009 shows three token payoffs stacking on one cheap Vampire.

**The tutors are the combo's insurance.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor), [Enlightened Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enlightened+Tutor) and [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent) go and get a missing half. The Forge AI plays this badly (it fetches Vish Kal and sits on Exquisite Blood), so Forge win rates understate the deck.

**Where it hurts.** It is creature-heavy and the table sweeps it. It rebuilds fast, but with small blockers rather than threats, and by turn eight or ten the hand is empty (games 001, 002, 003, 006). Cards that draw when creatures die ([Liliana, Dreadhorde General](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Liliana%2C+Dreadhorde+General), [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp), [Welcoming Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Welcoming+Vampire), [Bloodtracker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodtracker)) are the fix the pilot prefers. Sequencing matters: hold [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) and [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) until the wipe has passed, and do not swing a big lifelinker into a board of indestructible creatures (game 006).

**What the pilot does not want.** Flat +1/+1 anthems are too slow. A card earns its slot by multiplying or scaling, not by adding one.

## Pilot notes

<!-- ctx:gen record -->
- **6 game(s):** 0 won, 5 lost · 2026-08-22 to 2026-09-29
- **How games ended:** wipe 3, combo 1, raced 1, stalled 1
<!-- /ctx:gen record -->

Six games are logged (0 wins; the record block counts them). The same things keep happening.

- **The table wipes the board and the deck rebuilds small.** Multiple wipes in 001, 002, 003 and 006. It gets back up fast but into blockers, not threats.
- **It runs out of steam.** Hands empty by turn eight to ten (001, 002, 003, 006): "the consistent theme with Edgar is it runs out of steam." The v1.1.0 games (004, 005) ended ahead on board but lost to a clock it could not block.
- **Edgar late was piloting, not structure** (001): the pilot's own call, he waited for eight mana.
- **A mulligan keep of two lands was the mistake** in 002.
- **Sequencing in 006**: [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) came down too early and [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) too late; a 12/12 flying lifelinker was available but went at the player with indestructible creatures.
- **The pilot's read after 006**: it plays like a creature deck and he wants a life gain / life drain deck. v2.0.0 is the answer to that and has no games yet, so nothing here is yet a result of the new list.
