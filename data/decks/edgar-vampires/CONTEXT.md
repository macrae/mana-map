## Summary

Edgar Markov is the pilot's bracket-3 Mardu vampire deck: white, black and a lean touch of red, built around life gain and life drain. The plan is to flood the table with Vampires while [Edgar Markov](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Edgar+Markov) mints a 1/1 from the command zone off every Vampire cast, then drain the table out through deaths, life gain and tokens. It plays at bracket-3 tables, which is why it was brought down from bracket 4 on 2026-11-10 (v3.0.0).

The deck no longer has a two-card infinite. The old kill, a "they lose life, I gain it" half next to a "I gain life, they lose it" half, is gone. What stays is the gain-to-lose side, [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vito%2C+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose), [Sanguine Bond](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanguine+Bond), [Enduring Tenacity](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enduring+Tenacity) and [Marauding Blight-Priest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Marauding+Blight-Priest), which now just drain on every life gain. Those cards win slowly by themselves, so the kill is a pile of drain triggers rather than a loop.

In the pilot's own four words: blood drain, life gain, action at a distance, ferocious. He wants damage that is not combat damage, and a table that cannot wipe his way out of a win. He also wants more cards in hand when the game reaches turns seven and eight, because that is where every game so far has run dry. v3.0.0 leans on that: [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet), [Midnight Reaper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Midnight+Reaper) and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) turn deaths into cards, life and Vampires.

Sleeved on 2026-11-10 with three printed proxies ([Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex), [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet), [Midnight Reaper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Midnight+Reaper)). Change history has the whole story, including the reversal of the old bracket-4 plan.

## How it plays

**The soft start.** Cheap Vampires come down early and each one makes a token through Edgar's eminence. It works even if Edgar is never cast. [Cordial Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cordial+Vampire) grows every Vampire whenever any creature dies, so the swarm gets bigger when it is wiped at or traded off. [Charismatic Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Charismatic+Conqueror) makes a lifelink Vampire token whenever the table lets a creature or artifact arrive untapped. The tokens chump, carry [Crossway Troublemakers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crossway+Troublemakers)'s deathtouch and lifelink, and feed the sacrifice outlets. [Bloodghast](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodghast) is sacrifice fuel that keeps coming back. [Anointed Procession](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Anointed+Procession) and [Elspeth, Storm Slayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elspeth%2C+Storm+Slayer) double that token flow.

**Value from every death.** This is what the bracket-3 build adds. [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet) drains and gains for each creature of theirs that dies, and turns it into a Vampire token for the pilot. [Midnight Reaper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Midnight+Reaper) draws a card when one of the pilot's nontoken creatures dies, and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) does the same for the nontoken creatures that were not born here. [Morbid Opportunist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Morbid+Opportunist) and [Clavileño, First of the Blessed](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Clavile%C3%B1o%2C+First+of+the+Blessed) draw too, so a wipe or a sacrifice turn refills the hand instead of emptying it.

**The kill: a pile of drain, no loop.**
- Life gain becomes damage: [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vito%2C+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose), [Sanguine Bond](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanguine+Bond), [Enduring Tenacity](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enduring+Tenacity) and [Marauding Blight-Priest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Marauding+Blight-Priest) each drain when the pilot gains life. Lifelink tokens, Kalitas and the lifegain payoffs feed them.
- Drain without combat: [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist), [Cruel Celebrant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cruel+Celebrant), [Sanctum Seeker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanctum+Seeker), [Wispdrinker Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wispdrinker+Vampire) and [Tithe Drinker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tithe+Drinker) chip away at the table every time something dies, attacks or arrives. [Vraan, Executioner Thane](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vraan%2C+Executioner+Thane) and [Vein Ripper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vein+Ripper) add to it on every death. [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodletter+of+Aclazotz) doubles all of it on the pilot's turn.
- The big swing: [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) turns the whole board into one huge lifelinker, and its counters can come off as -1/-1 on opposing creatures, which beats indestructible.

**Deny them first.** [Anowon, the Ruin Sage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Anowon%2C+the+Ruin+Sage) makes each table member give up a non-Vampire, [Drana and Linvala](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Drana+and+Linvala) locks their activated abilities, and [Captivating Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Captivating+Vampire) grows the Vampires and can steal a creature. With [Charismatic Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Charismatic+Conqueror) out, four proactive Vampires make the table's life harder, and each is a Vampire cast for Edgar.

**Two tutors, kept.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) and [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor) are still here, with [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent), to fetch the engine piece the hand needs. With no loop to assemble, they find a payoff or an answer. The Forge AI plays tutors badly, so Forge understates the deck.

**Where it hurts.** It is creature-heavy and the table sweeps it. It rebuilds fast, but with small blockers rather than threats, and by turn eight or ten the hand was empty (games 001, 002, 003, 006). The death-draw cards ([Liliana, Dreadhorde General](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Liliana%2C+Dreadhorde+General), [Welcoming Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Welcoming+Vampire), [Morbid Opportunist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Morbid+Opportunist), [Clavileño, First of the Blessed](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Clavile%C3%B1o%2C+First+of+the+Blessed), [Midnight Reaper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Midnight+Reaper), [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex)) are the fix the pilot prefers. Sequencing matters: hold [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) and [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) until the wipe has passed, and do not swing a big lifelinker into a board of indestructible creatures (game 006).

**What the pilot does not want.** Flat +1/+1 anthems are too slow. A card earns its slot by multiplying or scaling, not by adding one. Standing keeps: [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) is never cut, and neither are [Elspeth, Storm Slayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elspeth%2C+Storm+Slayer), [Crossway Troublemakers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crossway+Troublemakers), [Tithe Drinker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tithe+Drinker) and [Master of Dark Rites](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Master+of+Dark+Rites).
