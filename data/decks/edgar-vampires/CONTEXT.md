## Summary

Edgar Markov is the pilot's bracket-4 Mardu vampire deck: white, black and a lean touch of red, built around life gain and life drain. The gentle plan is to flood the table with Vampires while [Edgar Markov](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Edgar+Markov) mints a 1/1 from the command zone off every Vampire cast. The plan the deck is actually built FOR is the drain infinite: [Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodthirsty+Conqueror) with [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vito%2C+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose) (proved in stack 004), or either of them with [Sanguine Bond](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanguine+Bond) or [Exquisite Blood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exquisite+Blood) (stacks 005, 006, 001). Infinite combos are welcome here.

In the pilot's own four words: blood drain, life gain, action at a distance, ferocious. He wants damage that is not combat damage, and a table that cannot wipe his way out of a win. He also wants more cards in hand when the game reaches turns seven and eight, because that is where every game so far has run dry.

It was sleeved on 2026-10-06 as v2.0.0. On 2026-10-20 the list was first corrected from a scan of the sleeves (v2.0.1), then moved to v2.1.0: fourteen swaps, printed proxies in the sleeves. Change history has both.

What v2.1.0 is for, in the pilot's words: a vampire deck that feels fast and threatening, with life gain and life drain as the core, ways to turn Vampire tokens into bigger threats, targeted removal, card draw and interaction, and as much protection as possible once the combo pieces are out. The package makes the deck cheaper to cast ([Urza's Incubator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urza%27s+Incubator), [Talisman of Conviction](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Talisman+of+Conviction), [Talisman of Hierarchy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Talisman+of+Hierarchy)), draws when things die ([Morbid Opportunist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Morbid+Opportunist), [Clavileño, First of the Blessed](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Clavile%C3%B1o%2C+First+of+the+Blessed)), drains when things die ([Vraan, Executioner Thane](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vraan%2C+Executioner+Thane), [Vein Ripper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vein+Ripper)), adds a third drain loop ([Enduring Tenacity](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enduring+Tenacity) with [Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodthirsty+Conqueror), proved in stack 012), and brings four Vampires that deny the table before the combo shows up ([Anowon, the Ruin Sage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Anowon%2C+the+Ruin+Sage), [Drana and Linvala](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Drana+and+Linvala), [Captivating Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Captivating+Vampire) and the already sleeved [Charismatic Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Charismatic+Conqueror)). [Bloodghast](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodghast) comes back as sacrifice fuel, [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) is one more outlet and [Exotic Orchard](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exotic+Orchard) fixes red.

## How it plays

**The soft start.** Cheap Vampires come down early and each one makes a token through Edgar's eminence. It works even if Edgar is never cast (stacks 002 and 010). [Cordial Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cordial+Vampire) grows every Vampire whenever any creature dies, so the swarm gets bigger when it is wiped at or traded off. [Charismatic Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Charismatic+Conqueror) makes a lifelink Vampire token whenever the table lets a creature or artifact arrive untapped. The tokens chump, carry [Crossway Troublemakers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crossway+Troublemakers)'s deathtouch and lifelink, and feed the sacrifice outlets. [Bloodghast](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodghast) is sacrifice fuel that keeps coming back, so the outlets never run out of bodies. [Anointed Procession](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Anointed+Procession) and [Elspeth, Storm Slayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elspeth%2C+Storm+Slayer) double that token flow.

**The kill, three ways.**
- The loop: a "when they lose life, I gain it" half ([Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodthirsty+Conqueror), [Exquisite Blood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exquisite+Blood)) next to a "when I gain life, they lose it" half ([Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vito%2C+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose), [Sanguine Bond](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanguine+Bond)). Each half feeds the other forever. [Marauding Blight-Priest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Marauding+Blight-Priest) is a smaller version of the second half. The Numbers section shows how often both halves are drawn together by turn six; it is a narrow window, which is why the tutors matter.
- A third loop: [Enduring Tenacity](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enduring+Tenacity) turns every life gain into a drain-back, and with [Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodthirsty+Conqueror) it goes round forever (stack 012, checker-passed). It needs no Vito and no Sanguine Bond, so it widens the ways to close.
- Drain without combat: [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist), [Cruel Celebrant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cruel+Celebrant), [Sanctum Seeker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sanctum+Seeker), [Wispdrinker Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wispdrinker+Vampire) and [Tithe Drinker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tithe+Drinker) chip away at the table every time something dies, attacks or arrives. [Vraan, Executioner Thane](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vraan%2C+Executioner+Thane) and [Vein Ripper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vein+Ripper) add to it on every death. [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodletter+of+Aclazotz) doubles all of it on the pilot's turn (stacks 003 and 007).
- The big swing: [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) turns the whole board into one huge lifelinker, and its counters can come off as -1/-1 on opposing creatures, which beats indestructible. Stack 009 shows three token payoffs stacking on one cheap Vampire.

**Deny them first.** [Anowon, the Ruin Sage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Anowon%2C+the+Ruin+Sage) makes each table member give up a non-Vampire, [Drana and Linvala](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Drana+and+Linvala) locks their activated abilities, and [Captivating Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Captivating+Vampire) grows the Vampires and can steal a creature. With [Charismatic Conqueror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Charismatic+Conqueror) already out, the pilot has four proactive Vampires that make the table's life harder before the combo is assembled, and each is a Vampire cast for Edgar.

**The tutors are the combo's insurance.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor), [Enlightened Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Enlightened+Tutor) and [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent) go and get a missing half. The Forge AI plays this badly (it fetches Vish Kal and sits on Exquisite Blood), so Forge win rates understate the deck.

**Where it hurts.** It is creature-heavy and the table sweeps it. It rebuilds fast, but with small blockers rather than threats, and by turn eight or ten the hand is empty (games 001, 002, 003, 006). Cards that draw when creatures die ([Liliana, Dreadhorde General](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Liliana%2C+Dreadhorde+General), [Welcoming Vampire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Welcoming+Vampire), [Morbid Opportunist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Morbid+Opportunist), [Clavileño, First of the Blessed](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Clavile%C3%B1o%2C+First+of+the+Blessed)) are the fix the pilot prefers. Sequencing matters: hold [Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vish+Kal%2C+Blood+Arbiter) and [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) until the wipe has passed, and do not swing a big lifelinker into a board of indestructible creatures (game 006).

**What the pilot does not want.** Flat +1/+1 anthems are too slow. A card earns its slot by multiplying or scaling, not by adding one.
