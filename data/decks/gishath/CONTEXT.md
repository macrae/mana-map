## Summary

Gishath is a Naya dinosaur deck built around one swing. [Gishath, Sun's Avatar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gishath%2C+Sun%27s+Avatar) has haste, vigilance and trample, and when he connects he reveals that many cards and drops every dinosaur in them onto the battlefield. The rest of the deck gets him there: ramp and cost reducers to cast him, haste and pumps so the 7/6 connects, and a pile of dinosaurs worth revealing.

Since v1.2.0 (6 Nov) it is a creature deck through and through. The pilot cut the noncreature red package (Purphoros and Molten Echoes are gone, see Change history) and put in more mana creatures, more dinosaurs, double strike, a real land base with green first, and recursion and wipe protection. Anthems are fine. It has no infinite combo, and the famous Marauding Raptor plus Polyraptor line is a draw machine, not an infinite (see Open questions).

The pilot's read after three games: the commander and his trigger are the real threat, and the mid-size dinosaurs ahead of him soak up the table's removal. It is a battlecruiser at bracket 3.

## How it plays

**The plan.** Ramp early with [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet), [Nature's Lore](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nature%27s+Lore), [Three Visits](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Three+Visits), [Skyshroud Claim](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skyshroud+Claim) and a crop of mana creatures: [Bloom Tender](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloom+Tender), [Faeburrow Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Faeburrow+Elder), [Drover of the Mighty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Drover+of+the+Mighty), [Atzocan Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Atzocan+Seer), [Ixalli's Lorekeeper](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ixalli%27s+Lorekeeper) and [Intrepid Paleontologist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Intrepid+Paleontologist). Cheapen the dinosaurs with [Knight of the Stampede](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Knight+of+the+Stampede), [Otepec Huntmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otepec+Huntmaster), [Urza's Incubator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urza%27s+Incubator) and [Hunting Velociraptor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hunting+Velociraptor). Land Gishath, swing, reveal, and the board fills itself. The Numbers section says how often the engine is online by turn five and how late the commander is usually cast. Those figures are drawn from the working list at the time and may not yet reflect v1.2.0.

**Why one connect ends it.** A big reveal puts several dinosaurs out at once, and [Pantlaza, Sun-Favored](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pantlaza%2C+Sun-Favored), [Quartzwood Crasher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quartzwood+Crasher), [Curious Altisaur](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Curious+Altisaur) and [Vaultborn Tyrant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vaultborn+Tyrant) turn each arrival into cards, tokens or damage. [Roaming Throne](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Roaming+Throne) named Dinosaur doubles the enter triggers, so [Earthshaker Dreadmaw](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Earthshaker+Dreadmaw), [Pantlaza, Sun-Favored](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pantlaza%2C+Sun-Favored) and [Tranquil Frillback](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tranquil+Frillback) fire twice. [Devil Dinosaur](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Devil+Dinosaur) makes every other dinosaur one size bigger and untargetable. [Berserkers' Onslaught](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Berserkers%27+Onslaught) gives attackers double strike, and with [Overwhelming Stampede](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Overwhelming+Stampede) or [Rydia's Return](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rydia%27s+Return) the swing gets much bigger. A bigger swing means a bigger reveal and more commander damage. Commander damage has ended both of the pilot's Gishath wins.

**Getting him to connect.** [Otepec Huntmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otepec+Huntmaster), [Regisaur Alpha](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Regisaur+Alpha) and [Palani's Hatcher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Palani%27s+Hatcher) give the dinosaurs haste, [Itzquinth, Firstborn of Gishath](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Itzquinth%2C+Firstborn+of+Gishath) has it himself, and [Kinjalli's Sunwing](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kinjalli%27s+Sunwing) flies and keeps the table's hasty plays from landing. Gishath's own reveal still comes after attackers are declared, so the arrivals do not attack that turn.

**Surviving the wraths.** Tables wipe a lot. The pilot's best games came from rebuilding faster than the table could answer. [Blasphemous Act](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasphemous+Act) is the one-sided wipe when your own board is empty. [Eerie Interlude](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eerie+Interlude), [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention), [Flawless Maneuver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Flawless+Maneuver), [Akroma's Will](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Akroma%27s+Will) and [Boros Charm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Boros+Charm) save the board when it is not. [Temple Altisaur](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Temple+Altisaur) shrugs off damage wipes. For getting back up afterward: [Atzocan Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Atzocan+Seer) returns a dinosaur from the graveyard, [Rydia's Return](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rydia%27s+Return) returns two permanents, and [Natural Order](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Natural+Order) turns a green creature into [Carnage Tyrant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Carnage+Tyrant) or another big threat.

**Weak spots.** Green is the pain. The pilot got stuck holding double-green dinosaurs, and v1.2.0 moved the land base to green first. The Numbers section shows colour on curve, but measured before the change. The enrage package ([Wrathful Raptors](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wrathful+Raptors), [Temple Altisaur](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Temple+Altisaur)) idles because the deck has few ways to ping its own dinosaurs. The goldfish has no blockers and no removal, so it flatters the board.

**Closing.** The pilot's own lesson from both losses: once the board is ahead, commit the attacks. Swing everything at everyone, and deal with the real threat at the table. Holding back cost games that were won.

**Accept losing dinosaurs.** The pilot's game plan point from game 003: this is a creature deck, and it wins through trample and big attacks. Trading dinosaurs to hurt their boards is fine. Being shy with them is how the deck loses.

**Patterns.** The commander plus the trigger is the threat, and the bodies ahead of him soak removal. Commander damage ends games. All three games turned on closing, not the list: the two losses (002, 003) were both a board that was ahead and attacks that were not committed. In 003 the pilot also says the doubling effects carried the deck back after removal, which is part of why wipe protection and recursion are the asks for v1.2.0.

**Molten Echoes.** The pilot found in play that it worked less well with Gishath than he expected, because Gishath's dinosaurs arrive after the attack. That, and a deck that should be creatures through and through, is why it left with Purphoros.

**The pilot's follow-ups.** Close lethal when it is on the board, and protect planeswalkers. Commit attacks when ahead, and pick the real threat at the table to hit.
