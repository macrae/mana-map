## Summary

The Ur-Dragon is a five-colour Dragon pile that wants to cheat the nine-mana commander out early. Cheap Dragon discounts and mana dorks get it down around turn five, then the Dragons come in bunches and every one of them pings, copies or burns something. It is bracket 3 on paper. The pilot says played right it is a strong three, and game 007 showed the ceiling: a win on turn six or seven. The usual way it loses is not the cards. The table sees the board getting big and kills the pilot first. Mana (not enough, or no green) is the other way it loses.

The list is sleeved at v1.3.0. The pilot's standing line after the best game yet is "leave this deck alone".

## How it plays

**The plan.** Get [The Ur-Dragon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Ur-Dragon) out early. Its eminence makes other Dragon spells cheaper and its attack trigger draws a card and drops a permanent from hand. [Dragonspeaker Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dragonspeaker+Shaman), [Dragonlord's Servant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dragonlord%27s+Servant), [Urza's Incubator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urza%27s+Incubator) and [Herald's Horn](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Herald%27s+Horn) cut Dragon costs. Dorks and rocks like [Bloom Tender](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloom+Tender), [Faeburrow Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Faeburrow+Elder), [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) and [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet) pay the rest. Five colours online means the Tender and the Elder tap for a lot.

**What wins.** Dragons that trigger on other Dragons. [Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Miirym%2C+Sentinel+Wyrm) copies each Dragon that follows it. [Terror of the Peaks](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Terror+of+the+Peaks) and [Scourge of Valkas](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scourge+of+Valkas) turn every Dragon entering into damage to the face or to a blocker. [Firespitter Whelp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Firespitter+Whelp) pings every Dragon cast, and [Panharmonicon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Panharmonicon) doubles the entry triggers. Game 007 was exactly this: Miirym, then Whelp with Panharmonicon, and the table was dead in a couple of turns. The Numbers section shows how often the pieces actually come together by turn six and eight.

**The second plan.** Treasure. [Goldspan Dragon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goldspan+Dragon), [Ganax, Astral Hunter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ganax%2C+Astral+Hunter), [Old Gnawbone](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Old+Gnawbone) and [Gadrak, the Crown-Scourge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gadrak%2C+the+Crown-Scourge) make Treasures, and [Smaug the Magnificent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Smaug+the+Magnificent) turns them into damage. The Numbers section says the goldfish can spend only two of the six Treasure sources, so it undersells this plan.

**Combat.** [Thrakkus the Butcher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Thrakkus+the+Butcher) doubles Dragon power, [Atarka, World Render](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Atarka%2C+World+Render) gives double strike, [Twinflame Tyrant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twinflame+Tyrant) and [Solphim, Mayhem Dominus](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Solphim%2C+Mayhem+Dominus) double damage. [Sneak Attack](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sneak+Attack) and [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) and [Temur Ascendancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Temur+Ascendancy) fix the "no haste" problem.

**What goes wrong.**
- The table reads the board. Games 003 and 004 were lost to this: a fast ramp, a pile of Treasures, and three players kill the pilot in one round.
- The deck does not recover from board wipes, and it is not good at winning from far behind. It wins by being quiet, or by being too fast to answer.
- Colour trouble. Game 005 had no green source all game, and Farseek and the green Dragons sat in hand.
- Blood Moon is accepted as a risk. The pilot is not building around it.

Protection used to be thin. v1.3.0 added [Asceticism](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Asceticism), [Mithril Coat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mithril+Coat) and [Stubborn Denial](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stubborn+Denial) to answer that.

The pilot's own words are the source here. The record above has the tally; games 001 to 006 were on older lists and 007 is the only one on the current one.

**Three patterns keep coming back.**
- **Telegraphing.** Games 003, 004 and 005. The pilot ramps fast, tips the table off, and gets killed in one round. The cure the pilot names: hold the big piece (Twinflame Tyrant in 005, Gadrak in 003) until the Dragons are down, and watch the quiet player instead (Steven in 004).
- **Colour trouble.** Games 001 (Blood Moon), 002 (lands behind the curve) and 005 (no green). The pilot's mulligan idea from 005: with no green source, think about shipping the hand.
- **Passive on the stack.** Game 006: the pilot let removal resolve on the commander with Bloom Tender untapped. Pause before the stack closes and look for dorks and instant-speed plays.

**Game 007** (the one on v1.3.0, a win). Faeburrow Elder and Urza's Incubator got the commander down on turn five. Miirym doubled things, then Firespitter Whelp with Panharmonicon killed Alex. The pilot also talked Ryan out of removing the commander. The pilot thinks it is a three played well, and wants the deck left alone.

**Mulligans.** Game 006: the pilot says he would not stand by the keep of three lands, Arcane Signet, Smaug and Thrakkus. It was a hand to ship.

**Tutor use.** Game 005: Enlightened Tutor fetched Sol Ring when a green source was what the hand needed. Tutor for the fixing first when colours are short.

**Table.** The pilot's pods run little to no white. Token decks are common.

**Pilot wishes from the old feedback.** Bracket 4 was approved in July. The pilot keeps coming back to Wrathful Red Dragon and Hellkite Tyrant (neither is in the 99 now).

## Open questions

1. **Is the pod's table-reading the real limit?** Claim: holding the loudest pieces (Gadrak, Twinflame Tyrant) until the Dragons are down should win more games than changing cards. Effect to look for: fewer losses coded politics in the record. This needs more games, not the goldfish.
2. **Does a no-green hand deserve a mulligan?** Claim: shipping a hand with no green source and green cards in it beats keeping. The Numbers section has no figure for it; it would need a paired goldfish test.
3. **Can the goldfish see the Treasure plan?** The Numbers section says it spends two of the six Treasure sources. Until that is modelled, Smaug, Old Gnawbone and Goldspan look weaker than they play.
4. **Forge has not seen the current list.** The last pod run is on V4, an older list.
