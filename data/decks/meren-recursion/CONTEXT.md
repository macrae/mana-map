## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator beatdown, with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. The point of the deck is big bodies early for what they cost. Tutor or entomb the fattest creature into the yard, reanimate it on turn three or four, and let Meren return it again later. Dorks, [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) and [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) get her down early. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands back a creature card, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

Meren's counters are the slow part, so the deck now makes its own deaths. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) and [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) give her a body every upkeep or end step to sacrifice, and nothing has to be drawn to keep the clock ticking.

The plan is to bin the fattest creatures on purpose and bring them back fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) fill the yard. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Living Death](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Living+Death), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead) and Meren empty it onto the table. The last pass leaned into this: more loaders and more reanimation, so the big bodies land early and Meren is the backup, not the only way back. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm), [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk), [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) and [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Junji%2C+the+Midnight+Sky).

The pilot asked for the absolute best, most banger Meren deck: up to three Game Changers, no two-card infinites. The Game Changers are [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) and [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor). The bracket floor is 3. The drain package is gone. The deck wins with big creatures, not with death triggers.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise), [Elvish Mystic](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Mystic) and [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) are the dorks. [Nature's Lore](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nature%27s+Lore) and [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetch lands, and the two plain Swamps are there so the Elder always has a basic to find. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) and [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet) are the rocks. [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) turns a board of small bodies into a burst of mana. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Fodder into free outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Carrion Feeder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Carrion+Feeder), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) and [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) are the steady supply: a Faerie each upkeep, a decayed Zombie each end step, a deathtouch Snake whenever you have none. [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier), [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) the Goat from Woe Strider and those three token makers are the bodies they eat. Jadar's Zombie can't block and dies after it attacks, so it feeds Meren either way. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) costs a life a turn, which is the price of the clock. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) draw cards off those deaths, so the engine refuels itself.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber), [Dina's Guidance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dina%27s+Guidance) and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) put the big creature in the yard. [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) are the newest loaders: Unmarked Grave bins any nonlegendary card, and Final Parting finds two cards, one for the hand and one for the yard, so it is also a tutor. [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) turns a spare body into the right one. [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia) mills the yard full on the side.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it for free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Dread Return](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dread+Return) and [Living Death](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Living+Death) do the job on demand. Necromancy can come in at instant speed, and Persist brings back a nonlegendary creature a size smaller. Both are what let a fatty land by turn four or so without waiting on Meren. [Living Death](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Living+Death) is the big swing: it puts every yard creature on the table at once, so it wants a yard you filled. [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness) returns a card to hand when the battlefield is the wrong place.

**The edict loop.** Creatures that clear the way on entering pay twice, because Meren returns one every end step. [Accursed Marauder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Accursed+Marauder), [Plaguecrafter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Plaguecrafter), [Ravenous Chupacabra](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ravenous+Chupacabra), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) and [Sheoldred // The True Scriptures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred+%2F%2F+The+True+Scriptures) each take out something or force a sacrifice, over and over. [Spore Frog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spore+Frog) fogs every turn, so the beatdown is safe while it builds.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) drains and clears a permanent whenever it enters or attacks. [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis) can't be cast with mana at all: the token makers tap to convoke it and a full yard delves it, so it often lands free, and it can be cast again from the graveyard after a wipe. It is legendary, so [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist) can't take it, which is why it leans on casting itself. [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Junji%2C+the+Midnight+Sky) comes back when it dies. [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk) turns its own death into a handful of small creatures, and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) trades a body up the curve. [Glissa Sunslayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glissa+Sunslayer) is a cheap threat that wins combat.

**Finding pieces.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent), [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling), [Eldritch Evolution](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eldritch+Evolution) and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) find the creature you need. [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) both finds it and bins it.

No games are logged for V6. Everything above comes from the pilot's brief and the card text.

What the pilot has said: no two-card infinites, three-card lines are fine, up to three Game Changers. He wants the absolute best, most banger Meren deck, fast and consistent, with nasty creatures on the table early. The bash is the plan.

For V4 he named the point of the deck: get big bodies in early relative to their mana cost. Tutor or entomb them into the yard, reanimate early, and let Meren's experience return them later. The token makers are there to feed that clock.

**V5 (2026-11-07).** The pilot wanted the big bodies on the table early, not left waiting on Meren's counters. In: [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave), two loaders and two reanimation spells. The goldfish read before the swap pointed the right way on three things: more games with a six-drop or bigger out by turn six, an earlier first one, and more turns saved on big bodies. The figures are in the saved read, not repeated here. It is a goldfish read with no removal and no blockers, so it says the plan is faster, not that it is safer. Not yet played.

**V4 (2026-11-07).** The pilot wanted Meren's experience to climb without waiting on a drawn outlet or a drawn fodder piece. In: [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia), [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer), each a body every turn. Out: Golgari Grave-Troll, Icetill Explorer, Tireless Provisioner. Grave-Troll was a slow filler, Explorer and Provisioner were ramp that left a small body at best. Nothing has been measured against V3 yet, so whether the clock is faster is open.

**V3 (2026-11-06, 0c3e0fadc0d4).** The pilot asked for the absolute best Meren. DeckCheck's power tune, steered by his prompt, turned the monster bash into an optimized reanimator beatdown. Bracket floor 3, no two-card infinites. The Game Changers moved to Survival of the Fittest, Gaea's Cradle and Demonic Tutor. It added the free outlets and draw engine (Altar of Dementia, High Market, Yawgmoth, Grim Haruspex, Skullclamp), the edict creatures, and Archon, Kokusho, Massacre Wurm, Protean Hulk, Reanimate and Living Death. It cut the drain pieces, because they were backup in V2 and the plan was already the bash. Kalonian Hydra and Sedgemoor Witch left because Archon, Kokusho and Massacre Wurm do the same job harder.

**V2 (2026-11-06, ea931d31adf2).** The deck was rebuilt from a sacrifice-and-drain engine into a Golgari monster bash in 18 swaps, because the drain trio was rare and the multiplier never came together. Still true from V1: the builder left out Chain of Smog, Exquisite Blood, Gravecrawler, Ivy Lane Denizen, Mikaeus, the Unhallowed and Springheart Nantuko, because each pairs with something in the pool into a two-card infinite.

- Does the new yard plan work? The claim to test: with a free outlet out by turn four, Meren reaches enough counters that fat creatures come back free by the midgame. The Numbers need Meren's counters by turn, and that is not in the block.
- Is the deck too slow to fill the yard? If a logged game shows a full hand and an empty yard, the claim is that one more cheap binner (a second Vile Entomber-style card) beats another top-end threat.
- [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk) and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) sit near infinite lines. Re-run the bracket check after every change, to keep the rule of no two-card infinites. The token makers are new since the last check.
- Do the token makers speed the clock? The claim: with one of [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) or [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) out by turn three, Meren holds more counters by turn eight than in V3, and fat creatures return free sooner. The Numbers do not carry Meren's counters by turn yet, so this needs that figure.
- Does Hogaak fight Meren for the yard? The claim: delving Hogaak eats the creatures the reanimation spells and Meren want, so in games where it is cast early, a later Living Death or Reanimate has fewer targets. The Numbers have no row for it yet. Also unknown: whether the goldfish reads convoke and delve (recent commit says it now does) and whether Forge's AI casts it at all, since no cast check has been run.
- Does Forge's AI play the tutor-and-reanimate plan? No cast check has been run on the newer cards (Living Death, Reanimate, Protean Hulk, Birthing Pod, and the three token makers).
- Do the loaders and reanimation land the fatty early? The claim: with Unmarked Grave, Final Parting, Necromancy or Persist in the opening hand and a fatty findable, a six-drop or bigger is on the table by turn four or five in most games. The Numbers carry no row for that yet.
- Did the four cuts cost the mana? Deathrite Shaman and Elves of Deep Shadow were early mana. The claim: the commander is still cast by turn six at about the same rate, which the Numbers' commander-cast row shows. Compare it with V4.
- Does Forge's AI play Necromancy, Persist and Final Parting? No cast check has been run on them.
- No games yet. The first few should say whether the slow spots are mana, finding the right fatty, or surviving removal.
