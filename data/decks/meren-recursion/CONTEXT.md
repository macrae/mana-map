## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. The point is big bodies early for what they cost. Bin the fattest creature, bring it back on turn three or four, and let Meren return it again later. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands one creature card back to your hand, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

The plan is to fill the yard on purpose and empty it fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) load it. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Victimize](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Victimize) and Meren empty it. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis), [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) and the new drain-and-removal fatties: [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) and [Shriekmaw](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shriekmaw).

V18 is built around one line the pilot named himself. Attack with a big creature, sacrifice it in the post-combat main to do something, then bring it back in his end step untapped. [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) is the payoff. He grows with every creature card in the yard and can sacrifice another creature to drain each opponent, so a returned fatty becomes damage to the whole table. Sacrifice the fatty when its enter trigger is removal or drain. Sacrifice fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns only ONE card per end step, so choose what comes back. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn.

[Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia), [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) give Meren a body to sacrifice every turn, so her counters do not wait on a lucky draw. Experience counters are the player's, in one pool. That is why [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) (each Meren trigger gives an extra counter) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) (counters off landfall) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

The deck keeps its answers and its safety: [Toxic Deluge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toxic+Deluge), [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) and [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) face the table. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention), [Veil of Summer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Veil+of+Summer) and [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) protect the fatty. [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) is the finisher for a wide board.

The Game Changers are [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) and [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), among others the bracket check names. The bracket floor is 3. There is still no two-card infinite, and three-card lines are fine. The Summary block above has the combo count.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise), [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) and [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elves+of+Deep+Shadow) are the dorks. [Nature's Lore](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nature%27s+Lore) and [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetch lands, and the plain Swamp and Forest are there for them. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) is the rock. [Dark Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Ritual) is the explosive start: three black mana for a turn-one [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb) or a turn-two [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate). [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) makes a lot of black mana off the Swamp count, and [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urborg%2C+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth) makes every land a Swamp. [Sylvan Library](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sylvan+Library) smooths the draws. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Fodder into free outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market), [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) makes a Faerie each upkeep, [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) a decayed Zombie each end step, [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) a deathtouch Snake whenever you have none, and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) an Insect on every land drop. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) and the Goat from [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider) are the other bodies they eat. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) costs a life a turn, which is the price of the clock. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) draw cards off those deaths.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber) and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) put the big creature in the yard. [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) bins any nonlegendary card. [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) finds two cards, one for the hand and one for the yard. [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) turns a spare body into the right one. [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze) eats the other yards and grows, so it answers a graveyard plan and still feeds on ours.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Victimize](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Victimize) and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) do the job on demand. Necromancy can come in at instant speed. Persist brings back a nonlegendary creature a size smaller. Victimize returns two creatures for one sacrifice. Agadeem's Awakening is the big swing: it returns several creatures at once, each with a different mana value, and it is also a land. [Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Malakir+Rebirth+%2F%2F+Malakir+Mire) saves a creature from dying, or is simply a land.

**The sac-the-fatty loop.** This is the V18 heart. Swing with the big creature, sacrifice it after combat, and Meren returns it at the end step untapped. Each time it comes back its enter trigger fires again. [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) drains the table. [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) sacrifices another creature to destroy one of theirs. [Shriekmaw](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shriekmaw) kills a creature. [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One) and [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) clear something every time they land. [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the sacrifice into a drain, and the returned body's power is the size of it. [Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tergrid%2C+God+of+Fright+%2F%2F+Tergrid%27s+Lantern) steals what opponents lose to those edicts. [Spore Frog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spore+Frog) fogs, so the build-up is safe.

**The combo lines.** The pilot said to lean into the combos. [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis) with [Infested Thrinax](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Infested+Thrinax) or [Korozda Guildmage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Korozda+Guildmage) and an altar or free outlet makes a three-card infinite. [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) with both altars is another. The Summary block has the count. Still no two-card infinite. [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) turns a wide board of tokens into the kill.

**Sweep and survive.** [Toxic Deluge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toxic+Deluge) and [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) wipe the table, and the deck likes it: its own dead feed Meren, and she brings the best of them back. [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) also drains when creatures die. [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) kills a creature for almost nothing. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention) makes the board hexproof and indestructible. [Veil of Summer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Veil+of+Summer) stops a counterspell and draws if they went blue or black. [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) gives a reanimated fatty haste and shroud. [Bojuka Bog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bojuka+Bog) exiles an opponent's graveyard, and would eat ours if pointed the wrong way.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis) can't be cast with mana at all: the token makers tap to convoke it and a full yard delves it. It is legendary, so [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist) can't take it. [Glissa Sunslayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glissa+Sunslayer) is a cheap threat that wins combat. [Infested Thrinax](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Infested+Thrinax) makes tokens off nontoken deaths for a turn.

**Finding pieces and answers.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) finds the card you need, and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) both finds a creature and bins one. [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy), [Beast Within](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Within), [Deadly Rollick](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deadly+Rollick), [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) and [Boseiju, Who Endures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Boseiju%2C+Who+Endures) handle what the creatures do not. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every one of your deaths cost the table a creature.

**How much of this we can see.** The goldfish has no opponents. It cannot measure the sweepers, the protection, the removal or the graveyard hate. The goldfish is not the arbiter here: its figures feed the strategist's judgment, and the V18 loop is the pilot's call.

No games are logged yet. Everything here comes from the pilot's words and the card text.

What the pilot has said, from the start: no two-card infinites, three-card lines are fine. He wants the absolute best, most banger Meren deck, fast and consistent, with nasty creatures on the table early. The point is big bodies early relative to their mana cost: tutor or entomb them into the yard, reanimate early, and let Meren's experience return them later.

V13 to V15. [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) and [Victimize](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Victimize) came in. After a diagnosis (the deck leans on its graveyard and is exposed to counterspells) came [Veil of Summer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Veil+of+Summer), [Sylvan Library](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sylvan+Library) and [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze). The pilot then restored [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm): "I have a scute swarm dude!!! bodies baby".

V16. "Lean into the combos." [Korozda Guildmage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Korozda+Guildmage), [Infested Thrinax](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Infested+Thrinax) and [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) add three-card infinite lines: [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis) with Thrinax or Korozda and an altar or outlet, and Ozox with both altars. There is still no two-card infinite, and the bracket stays 3.

V17. Meren's ruling: experience counters belong to the player, in one pool. So [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) (each Meren trigger gives an extra counter) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) (a counter per landfall, earthbend on attack) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

V18. His core line, in his words: "attacking with a big creature... sacking it in post-combat main to do something... and then bring it back in my end step untapped." [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the returned body's power into a drain on each opponent. [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) and [Shriekmaw](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shriekmaw) are enter triggers Meren re-buys. Sacrifice the fatty when its trigger is removal or drain, fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns one card per end step. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn (CR 302.6). This was the strategist's call with the pilot's go-ahead, not the goldfish's.

Earlier calls still standing: the pilot turned down Ancient Tomb (another Game Changer) and Gravecrawler (a two-card infinite with Phyrexian Altar).
