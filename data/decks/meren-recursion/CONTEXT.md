## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. The point is big bodies early for what they cost. Bin the fattest creature, bring it back on turn three or four, and let Meren return it again later. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands one creature card back to your hand, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

The plan is to fill the yard on purpose and empty it fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) load it. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Victimize](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Victimize) and Meren empty it. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk) and the fatties that do something both coming in and dying: [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm), [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night), [Shriekmaw](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shriekmaw) and [Skyfisher Spider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skyfisher+Spider).

The deck is built around one line the pilot named himself: Meren "wants to bring cards back IN THE END STEP". Swing with a big creature, sacrifice it for value, bring it back at the end step, and it comes back untapped with its enter trigger fired again. Sacrifice the fatty when its enter trigger is removal or drain, and when its death trigger pays too. Sacrifice fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns only ONE card per end step, so choose what comes back. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn.

The pilot's wish list shapes the rest: big bodies that get something on entering and on dying, a way to sacrifice them and turn their power or mana value into tokens or removal, and "stacked/nested tutoring is money". [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) is critical: sacrifice a fatty and its power becomes Zombies. [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) is the nested tutor: sacrifice a creature, pay X, and put a creature of that mana value or less onto the battlefield.

[Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) is the other payoff. He grows with the creature cards in the yard and can sacrifice another creature to drain each opponent. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia), [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) give Meren a body to sacrifice every turn, so her counters do not wait on a lucky draw. Experience counters are the player's, in one pool. That is why [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) (each Meren trigger gives an extra counter) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) (counters off landfall) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

The sacrifice package from V21 stays. [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm) flips into a planeswalker whenever a creature enters from the yard or is cast from it. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) is endless fodder that never spends Meren's end-step return. [Savra, Queen of the Golgari](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Savra%2C+Queen+of+the+Golgari) and [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) make a black sacrifice an edict for every opponent.

The deck keeps its answers and its safety: [Toxic Deluge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toxic+Deluge), [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) and [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) face the table, and [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) is a one-sided wipe of small creatures every time it lands. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention), [Veil of Summer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Veil+of+Summer) and [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) protect the fatty.

The Game Changers are [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) and [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), among others the bracket check names. The bracket floor is 3. There is still no two-card infinite. The Summary block above has the combo count.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise) and [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) are the dorks. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetches lands, and the plain Swamp and Forest are there for it. The pilot cut the extra ramp in V21: mana is not what limits this deck. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) is the rock. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb) is the explosive start, and [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate) follows it. There is no ritual any more: the thirty-fifth land took that slot. [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) makes a lot of black mana off the Swamp count, and [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urborg%2C+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth) makes every land a Swamp. [Sylvan Library](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sylvan+Library) smooths the draws. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Fodder into outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market), [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) are outlets that cost mana and give something back. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) makes a Faerie each upkeep, [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) a decayed Zombie each end step, [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) a deathtouch Snake whenever you have none, and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) an Insect on every land drop. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton), [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm) and the Goat from [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider) are the other bodies they eat. The Skeleton walks back from the yard for {1}{B}, so it feeds an outlet every turn for free as far as Meren is concerned. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) costs a life a turn, which is the price of the clock. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) draw cards off those deaths.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber) and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) put the big creature in the yard. [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) bins any nonlegendary card. [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) finds two cards, one for the hand and one for the yard. [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) discards a fatty on entering and tutors a creature to hand. [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze) eats the other yards and grows, so it answers a graveyard plan and still feeds on ours.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Victimize](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Victimize) and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) do the job on demand. Necromancy can come in at instant speed. Victimize returns two creatures for one sacrifice. Agadeem's Awakening returns several creatures at once, each with a different mana value, and it is also a land. [Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Malakir+Rebirth+%2F%2F+Malakir+Mire) saves a creature from dying, or is simply a land.

**The end-step loop.** This is the heart of the deck. Swing, sacrifice after combat, and Meren returns the body at the end step untapped. Each time it comes back its enter trigger fires again, and each time it dies its death trigger fires. What each fatty does:
- [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) drains each opponent when it dies, and Meren brings it back to do it again.
- [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) shrinks their small creatures every return, one-sided, and taxes their deaths.
- [Skyfisher Spider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skyfisher+Spider) turns a sacrifice into the removal of any nonland permanent on entering.
- [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) drains the table.
- [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) sacrifices another creature to destroy one of theirs, and gets something again when it dies.
- [Shriekmaw](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shriekmaw) kills a creature.
- [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One) and [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) clear something every time they land.
- [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the sacrifice into a drain, and the returned body's power is the size of it.
- [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) turns the sacrificed body's power into Zombies, so the bigger the fatty, the bigger the swarm.
- [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) sacrifices a body to put a creature onto the battlefield from the library, so one sacrifice is a tutor and a reanimation at once. It grows with the yard.
- [Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tergrid%2C+God+of+Fright+%2F%2F+Tergrid%27s+Lantern) steals what opponents lose to those edicts. [Spore Frog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spore+Frog) fogs, so the build-up is safe.

**The sacrifice package.** Each reanimation or Meren return of a creature can also flip [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm), which then makes an Insect and mills two, or later copies the creatures in the yard. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) is the cheap body to sacrifice: it returns itself, so Meren's single end-step slot stays on the fatty. With [Savra, Queen of the Golgari](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Savra%2C+Queen+of+the+Golgari) out, sacrifice a black creature and pay two life: each opponent sacrifices a creature. Sacrifice a green one and you gain two. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) on top makes a black sacrifice two edicts per opponent. [Savra, Queen of the Golgari](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Savra%2C+Queen+of+the+Golgari) is also a free outlet in her own right.

[Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) is the same loop on a three-mana body. It costs three, so Meren returns it from three counters. Sacrifice it and its enter trigger fires again: discard a fatty, tutor a creature ([Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord), [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) or a combo piece). It is Survival of the Fittest on a body. Its {1},{T} untap hits [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) or [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) for more mana.

**The combo line.** The pilot said to lean into the combos, and no two-card infinite. [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) with both altars ([Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar) and [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar)) is the three-card line that is left. The Summary block has the count. The main-phase Hogaak lines are gone: they did not use Meren's end step.

**Sweep and survive.** [Toxic Deluge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toxic+Deluge) and [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) wipe the table, and the deck likes it: its own dead feed Meren, and she brings the best of them back. [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) also drains when creatures die. [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) kills a creature for almost nothing. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention) makes the board hexproof and indestructible. [Veil of Summer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Veil+of+Summer) stops a counterspell and draws if they went blue or black. [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) gives a reanimated fatty haste and shroud. [Bojuka Bog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bojuka+Bog) exiles an opponent's graveyard, and would eat ours if pointed the wrong way.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Glissa Sunslayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glissa+Sunslayer) is a cheap threat that wins combat. [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) flies, and its death is a drain, so even a removed Kokusho is a win for us.

**Finding pieces and answers.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) finds the card you need, and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) both finds a creature and bins one. [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) and [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) are tutors on bodies. [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy), [Beast Within](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Within), [Deadly Rollick](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deadly+Rollick), [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) and [Boseiju, Who Endures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Boseiju%2C+Who+Endures) handle what the creatures do not. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every one of your deaths cost the table a creature.

**How much of this we can see.** The goldfish has no opponents. It cannot measure the sweepers, the protection, the removal or the graveyard hate. The goldfish is not the arbiter here: its figures feed the strategist's judgment, and the end-step loop is the pilot's call.

No games are logged yet. Everything here comes from the pilot's words and the card text.

What the pilot has said, from the start: no two-card infinites, three-card lines are fine. He wants the absolute best, most banger Meren deck, fast and consistent, with nasty creatures on the table early. The point is big bodies early relative to their mana cost: tutor or entomb them into the yard, reanimate early, and let Meren's experience return them later.

V13 to V15. [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) came back after the pilot said: "I have a scute swarm dude!!! bodies baby". Veil of Summer, Sylvan Library and Scavenging Ooze came in after a diagnosis (the deck leans on its graveyard and is exposed to counterspells).

V17. Meren's ruling: experience counters belong to the player, in one pool. So [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

V18. His core line, in his words: "attacking with a big creature... sacking it in post-combat main to do something... and then bring it back in my end step untapped." [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the returned body's power into a drain. Sacrifice the fatty when its trigger is removal or drain, fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns one card per end step. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn (CR 302.6). This was the strategist's call with the pilot's go-ahead, not the goldfish's.

V19 (2026-11-09). [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) in for Fauna Shaman. His words: "3 CMC discard and tutor a creature to hand! Plus ability to untap target permanent!!!!" That is Survival of the Fittest on a body, re-fired at each Meren return.

V20 (2026-11-09). [Karn's Bastion](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Karn%27s+Bastion) in for one Forest: proliferate from a land slot at no spell cost. - **Q049** promoted — Under the stated one_fodder_per_turn policy, in games where a free outlet is out by T4 Meren still reaches fewer than 3 experience counters by T8 in most games, so the to-hand mode is the normal midgame and the fat creatures arrive by cost, not by counters.

- Does the end-step loop work at a real table? The claim: with Meren out and a fatty with a good enter and death trigger in the yard, sacrificing it after combat and getting it back at the end step clears or drains every turn cycle. It needs logged games, because the goldfish has no opponents.
- Does Meren returning just one card per end step bottleneck the loop? The claim: with two good fatties in the yard, the second waits a full turn, so a second reanimation spell in the same turn is worth more than a second Meren trigger.
- Do Kokusho and Massacre Wurm pay together? The claim: with Massacre Wurm out, each opponent creature that dies to our edicts or Skyfisher Spider costs them life on top, and Kokusho's death drain closes games a turn earlier than the drain-less fatties did.
- Is Ghoulcaller Gisa the sacrifice sink? The claim: sacrificing the fatty to Gisa makes a Zombie swarm of its power in a single activation, and a returned Gisa is always worth the end-step slot. Gisa and Fiend Artisan have not had a Forge cast check.
- Is there enough fodder for the paying outlets now that Mazirek is gone? The claim: Skeleton, the token makers and Grist keep both Gisa and Fiend Artisan fed without spending the fatties.
- Does Fiend Artisan's X land the right fatty? The claim: with a few mana to spare, one sacrifice is a tutor and a reanimation that fixes the missing piece better than the Survival line does.
- With the Hogaak lines and Dark Ritual gone, does the early game still land Meren on time? The claim: Birds, Llanowar Elves, Sakura-Tribe Elder and Sol Ring, with thirty-five lands, keep Meren's first cast where the Numbers block has it. Check the commander-cast lines after the next games.
- Do the sweepers still fit? The claim: when Toxic Deluge or The Meathook Massacre resolves, our dead give Meren enough counters that the board is rebuilt faster than the table's.
- Does the bracket check still read 3? Re-run it, and check that no two-card infinite crept in with Gisa, Fiend Artisan and the altars.
- No games yet. The first few should say whether the slow spots are mana, finding the right fatty, or surviving removal.
