## Summary

Meren is a Golgari attrition engine. Every creature that dies feeds her experience counters, and at each end step she brings one back, free when it is cheap enough for her count and to hand when it is not. The deck turns those deaths into damage with drains, and it is built to never run out of bodies.

The plan is to have the board threatening by the middle of the game, and to win on a count of deaths, not on a loop. The pilot does not play two-card infinites, so none of them are in the 99 (the exclusions are in Change history). The longer lines that remain are welcome but never the plan.

It is a bracket 4 list built from a pool, still on the bench with no games played. Everything the Numbers section says is a goldfish reading with no blockers and no removal, so read it as an upper bound on speed.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana out and a cheap body down. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Chrome Mox](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chrome+Mox), [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet) and [Deathrite Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deathrite+Shaman) speed Meren up, and the Numbers section has her cast by turn six in nearly every game. A sacrifice outlet like [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer) or [Carrion Feeder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Carrion+Feeder) on turn one or two is the best start, because it makes every later body count twice.

**The ratchet.** Meren's counters only go up. Each creature that dies adds one, so the things she returns get bigger over the game and the trigger never whiffs. The counters stay even if she is removed.

**Fodder and the outlet.** [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) and [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) are bodies that come back or leave more bodies behind. A free sacrifice outlet eats them. [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar) and [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar) turn each sacrifice into mana, and that mana buys the next piece.

**The drain.** The win is a pile of deaths with a drain on the table. [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat), [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) and [Grave Venerations](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Venerations) hit every opponent for each death. [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) and [Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sephiroth%2C+Fabled+SOLDIER+%2F%2F+Sephiroth%2C+One-Winged+Angel) only hit one target, so they are weaker at killing a table. [Starscape Cleric](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Starscape+Cleric) is the multiplier: it turns each lifegain drain trigger into a second table-wide loss, and it makes Blood Artist hit everyone. The Numbers section shows how often each piece is drawn, and how rarely the whole trio shows up together.

**Grave Pact.** [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every one of your deaths cost the table a creature, so sacrificing into it clears blockers while you keep the grind going.

**Finding pieces.** The deck tutors for a missing engine piece rather than for a combo. [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) and [Eldritch Evolution](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eldritch+Evolution) turn spare bodies into the one that matters. [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor) and [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent) find anything.

**Graveyard.** [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Dawnhand Eulogist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dawnhand+Eulogist), [Trystan, Callous Cultivator // Trystan, Penitent Culler](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Trystan%2C+Callous+Cultivator+%2F%2F+Trystan%2C+Penitent+Culler) and [Deadbridge Chant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deadbridge+Chant) fill the yard so Meren and the reanimation spells have targets. [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness) and [Phyrexian Reclamation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Reclamation) bring a card back to hand when the battlefield is the wrong place.
