## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. The point is big bodies early for what they cost, and a lot of cheap bodies that make Meren's engine start on its own. Bin the fattest creature, bring it back early, and let Meren return it again later. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands one creature card back to your hand, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

The plan is to fill the yard on purpose and empty it fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive) and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) load it by choice, and [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder), [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six), [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver), [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) and the surveil on [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) mill or sort it by volume. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) and Meren empty it. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk) and the fatties that do something both coming in and dying: [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm), [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night), [Hapatra, the Desert Fang](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hapatra%2C+the+Desert+Fang) (one-sided counters sized by the yard). [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) sits beside Meren and copies whatever comes back from the yard, once a turn. [Unshakable Tail](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unshakable+Tail) turns all that filling into cards: each time a creature card is milled or surveilled into the yard, it investigates.

The plan has three parts. Meren's window is turns six to eight, returning two-, three- and four-drops for free, and the pilot's point is that this is where much of her value lives: "there is a lot to Meren in just reanimating 2, 3, 4, or even 5 cmc creatures... the ETB and death triggers are huge!" The big fatties are few and chosen to win the game. [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) is the one seven-plus target, and it is a Reanimate and Animate Dead target, never a Meren target. [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan), [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk) and [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) are the ones Meren brings back. The early fatty comes from [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb) or [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive) into [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate) or [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), not from experience. The pilot's focus this round: "we need experience counter ramp to land a big fatty by turn 6, 5 at the earliest."

The deck is built around one line the pilot named himself: Meren "wants to bring cards back IN THE END STEP". Swing with a big creature, sacrifice it for value, bring it back at the end step, and it comes back untapped with its enter trigger fired again. Sacrifice the fatty when its enter trigger is removal or drain, and when its death trigger pays too. Sacrifice fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns only ONE card per end step, so choose what comes back. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn.

The pilot's wish list shapes the rest: big bodies that get something on entering and on dying, a way to sacrifice them and turn their power or mana value into tokens or removal, and "stacked/nested tutoring is money". [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) is critical: sacrifice a fatty and its power becomes Zombies. [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) is the nested tutor: sacrifice a creature, pay X, and put a creature of that mana value or less onto the battlefield.

[Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) is the other payoff. He grows with the creature cards in the yard and can sacrifice another creature to drain each opponent. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Bitterbloom Bearer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterbloom+Bearer), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia), [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) give Meren a body to sacrifice every turn, so her counters do not wait on a lucky draw. Experience counters are the player's, in one pool. That is why [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) (each Meren trigger gives an extra counter) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) (counters off landfall) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

The sacrifice package from V21 stays. [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm) flips into a planeswalker whenever a creature enters from the yard or is cast from it. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) is endless fodder that never spends Meren's end-step return. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every death of ours an edict for every opponent.

V23 balanced the sacrifice engine, in the pilot's words: "we need sac outlets to kill creatures, it's part of the engine." [Blasting Station](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasting+Station) is the outlet that kills. [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat) and [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) pay on every death of ours, tokens included. [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) makes fodder in every upkeep. [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) and [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) give a cheap body something to do on dying and on entering. The aim is enough tokens, sacrificed, to build experience counters and get Meren's recursion online as early as possible.

The deck keeps its answers and its safety. [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) faces the table, and [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) is a one-sided wipe of small creatures every time it lands. Both are sweepers that suit us, because our own dead feed Meren. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention), [Tamiyo's Safekeeping](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tamiyo%27s+Safekeeping) and [Golgari Charm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Golgari+Charm) look after the engine. The review that added them found the deck's biggest exposure was graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void, Bojuka Bog), with little but [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy) able to answer an artifact or enchantment, which [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) now backs up, and Meren herself almost unprotected.

The deck carries two Game Changers, [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) and [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor). The bracket floor is 3. There is still no two-card infinite. [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) now completes three-card lines beside the altars and [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat). The Summary block above has the combo count.

V26 is about the yard. The pilot felt the deck was light on milling: "the plan is to play cards from the graveyard primarily... we need to fill the graveyard faster than casting creatures and them dying." So three volume-mill bodies went in. [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder) mills about three when it enters and hands you a land, and it fires again on every Meren return. [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six) mills three each time it attacks, and it lets any nonland permanent card in your yard be cast on your turn by discarding a land, a second play-from-the-graveyard engine beside Meren. [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver) mills two every upkeep and can exile itself to return any card from the yard to hand. [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze) stays as the one repeatable answer to someone else's graveyard.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) is the last mana dork, and the pilot kept it. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetches lands, and the basic Swamps are there for it. The pilot cut the extra ramp in V21, and the ramp that matters now is experience, not mana. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) is the rock. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb) is the explosive start, and [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate) follows it. There is no ritual any more: the thirty-fifth land took that slot. [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) makes a lot of black mana off the Swamp count, and [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urborg%2C+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth) makes every land a Swamp. [Sylvan Library](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sylvan+Library) smooths the draws. [Fabled Passage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fabled+Passage) and [Exotic Orchard](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exotic+Orchard) fix black and green without the price of the old dual. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Bodies that feed Meren on their own.** The early engine needs a death and an outlet together, and the pilot's fix is bodies with a sacrifice built in: "bodies with built in sac outlets solve for the early engine assembly problem." Three of them carry it.
- [Hangarback Walker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hangarback+Walker) cast for nothing dies on entering, with no outlet needed. Its mana value is zero, so Meren returns it at any experience and it dies again. With [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor), cast for one, it leaves two Thopters.
- [Fell Stinger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fell+Stinger) exploits a spare body, or itself, for a death and two cards at two life. Meren returns it from three counters.
- [Wriggling Grub](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wriggling+Grub) dies into two Worms, so with an outlet one card is three deaths. Meren returns it from two counters.
Each is a cheap return for her and a death she did not have to pay for. That is the early counters, and the deck does not wait on an outlet to start.

**Fodder into outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Blasting Station](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasting+Station), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market), [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) are outlets that cost mana and give something back. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) makes a Faerie each upkeep, and [Bitterbloom Bearer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterbloom+Bearer) does the same from a two-mana flash flyer that Meren returns at two experience, [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) a decayed Zombie each end step, [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) a deathtouch Snake whenever you have none, and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) an Insect on every land drop. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton), [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm) and the Goat from [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider) are the other bodies they eat. The Skeleton walks back from the yard for {1}{B}, so it feeds an outlet every turn for free as far as Meren is concerned. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) and [Bitterbloom Bearer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterbloom+Bearer) each cost a life a turn, which is the price of the clock. [Blasting Station](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasting+Station) is a free outlet that pings any target and untaps whenever a creature enters, so a token maker feeds it all turn. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) draw cards off those deaths, tokens included. [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat) drains each opponent on every death of ours, and pays back the life [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) costs. [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) leaves a Spawn, which is mana, when a nontoken creature of ours dies.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive) and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) put the big creature in the yard on purpose. Fauna Shaman does it every turn: discard a creature card, fetch another. It is a creature itself, so a wipe or a removal spell ends the engine, and Meren can return it. [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) discards a fatty on entering and tutors a creature to hand. [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze) eats the other yards and grows, so it answers a graveyard plan and still feeds on ours.

**Fill the yard.** The loaders above aim; the millers fill it. [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder) is a two-mana body that mills about three and gives a land, and Meren returns it for cheap, so every return mills again. [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six) mills three whenever it attacks and turns the yard into a second hand: nonland permanents there can be cast on your turn by discarding a land, and the lands you mill and take are the lands to discard. [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver) mills two in each upkeep, and when the yard has the card you need it exiles itself to take it to hand. [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) mills three coming and going. More cards in the yard feed [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan), which grow with it. The risk is the same as the plan: it wants the yard to be safe, so watch for their graveyard hate.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) do the job on demand. Every one of these is sorcery speed now, so the deck has no instant-speed return. Cauldron of Essence is a second return every turn, at sorcery speed: sacrifice a creature in your main phase and the best one in the yard comes straight back, any mana value. The sacrifice is a death for Meren's counters and a drain on the table. Agadeem's Awakening returns several creatures at once, each with a different mana value, and it is also a land. [Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Malakir+Rebirth+%2F%2F+Malakir+Mire) saves a creature from dying, or is simply a land.

**The end-step loop.** This is the heart of the deck. Swing, sacrifice after combat, and Meren returns the body at the end step untapped. Each time it comes back its enter trigger fires again, and each time it dies its death trigger fires. What each fatty does:
- [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) drains each opponent when it dies, and Meren brings it back to do it again.
- [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) shrinks their small creatures every return, one-sided, and taxes their deaths.
- [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) copies a creature that enters from or is cast from the yard, once each turn. See the next paragraph.
- [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) sacrifices another creature to destroy one of theirs, and gets something again when it dies.
- [Hapatra, the Desert Fang](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hapatra%2C+the+Desert+Fang) puts -1/-1 counters on one of each opponent's creatures when she enters, as many as the biggest mana value in our yard. It never touches our board, counters beat indestructible, and the fatties we bin set the size. Meren re-buys her for another shot.
- [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk) and [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) clear something every time they land.
- [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the sacrifice into a drain, and the returned body's power is the size of it.
- [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) turns the sacrificed body's power into Zombies, so the bigger the fatty, the bigger the swarm.
- [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) sacrifices a body to put a creature onto the battlefield from the library, so one sacrifice is a tutor and a reanimation at once. It grows with the yard.
- [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) mills three on entering and on dying, so it is an early return for Meren that fills the yard each time.

**Turn the filling into cards.** [Unshakable Tail](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unshakable+Tail) is a three-mana Zombie that surveils 1 when it enters and every upkeep, and investigates whenever creature cards go from the library to the yard. That is [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder), [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six), [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver), [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia) and its own surveil. The Clues are card draw, which is the deck's short axis, and they are also bodies-in-waiting for a sacrifice outlet. For {2} and a Clue it returns itself from the yard to hand, and Meren can re-buy it for three. [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) can copy it coming back. Its cost is the instant-speed return: the deck has none left.

**Do it twice.** [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) is a three-mana 3/3 that surveils 2 on entering. Whenever another creature of ours enters from the graveyard or is cast from it, it makes a token copy of that creature, once each turn. That covers Meren's end-step return, [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), creatures cast off [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six)'s retrace, and [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton). The copy fires its enter trigger too, so a returned [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) or [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) pays twice. A copy of a legend still fires its enter trigger before the legend rule takes one away, and a copied Kokusho's death still drains. The pilot: "I approve." The Diviner has to be on the table before the return, so cast her first.

**The sacrifice package.** Each reanimation or Meren return of a creature can also flip [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm), which then makes an Insect and mills two, or later copies the creatures in the yard. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) is the cheap body to sacrifice: it returns itself, so Meren's single end-step slot stays on the fatty. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes each death of ours an edict: every opponent sacrifices a creature.

[Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) is the same loop on a three-mana body. It costs three, so Meren returns it from three counters. Sacrifice it and its enter trigger fires again: discard a fatty, tutor a creature ([Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord), [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) or a combo piece). It is a one-shot tutor on a body that fires again at each Meren return. Its {1},{T} untap hits [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) or [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) for more mana.

**The combo line.** The pilot said to lean into the combos, and no two-card infinite. [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) with both altars ([Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar) and [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar)) is the three-card line from before. V23 adds three more through [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog): [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) with [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat); [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) with [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar); and [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) with [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar). None is a two-card line. The Summary block has the count. The main-phase Hogaak lines are gone: they did not use Meren's end step.

**Sweep and survive.** [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) wipes the table, and the deck likes it: its own dead feed Meren, and she brings the best of them back. It also drains when creatures die. The old wrath was cut in V24 because it killed our own tokens and engine. [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) is the other sweeper and leaves our side alone. Three things keep the engine alive. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention) makes the board hexproof and indestructible. [Tamiyo's Safekeeping](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tamiyo%27s+Safekeeping) does the same for one permanent, Meren included, for a turn, at a single green mana. [Golgari Charm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Golgari+Charm) is the flexible one at instant speed: it regenerates the whole board through a destroy wipe, destroys an enchantment, or gives everything -1/-1 to clear tokens. [Bojuka Bog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bojuka+Bog) exiles an opponent's graveyard, and would eat ours if pointed the wrong way.

**Count your life and your lanes.** [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) costs a life for every creature that dies, so count your life before a big sacrifice turn unless [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat) is out to pay it back. Meren returns one card per end step, so a second lane matters. [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) is the second lane every turn, and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) is the burst that puts several creatures back at once. [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) copies once a turn, so a main-phase Cauldron return and Meren's end-step return share that one copy.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) flies, and its death is a drain, so even a removed Kokusho is a win for us.

**The tutor on the end step.** Cast [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor) ({B}, instant, any card to the top for two life) at the end step of the player before you. Then our upkeep does the rest: [Unshakable Tail](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unshakable+Tail)'s surveil or [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver)'s mill bins the card, so the tutor is also an Entomb, or we simply draw it. It finds whatever the board lacks. A fatty is in the yard and nothing is returning it: fetch [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate) or [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence). No free outlet: fetch one. A table to punish: [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact). The price is two life, so skip it on a turn that already pays a lot, like [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate) on a big fatty, [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy), [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) or [Fell Stinger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fell+Stinger).

**Finding pieces and answers.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) finds the card you need, and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) finds a creature and bins one every turn while she lives. [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) and [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) are tutors on bodies. [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy), [Deadly Rollick](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deadly+Rollick) and [Boseiju, Who Endures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Boseiju%2C+Who+Endures) handle what the creatures do not. [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) is the cheap answer Meren can re-buy: for {1} and a sacrifice it destroys an artifact or an enchantment at instant speed, and the sacrifice is a death, so it is an experience counter. With nothing to answer, the same activation proliferates, which grows Meren's experience and [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor)'s counters. Meren returns it from two counters. The pilot: "cankerbloom is amazing in this deck!!!!" [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every one of your deaths cost the table a creature.

**How much of this we can see.** The goldfish has no opponents. It cannot measure the sweepers, the protection, the removal or the graveyard hate. The goldfish is not the arbiter here: its figures feed the strategist's judgment, and the end-step loop is the pilot's call.

What the pilot has said, from the start: no two-card infinites, three-card lines are fine. He wants the absolute best, most banger Meren deck, fast and consistent, with nasty creatures on the table early. The point is big bodies early relative to their mana cost: tutor or entomb them into the yard, reanimate early, and let Meren's experience return them later.

V18. His core line, in his words: "attacking with a big creature... sacking it in post-combat main to do something... and then bring it back in my end step untapped." [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the returned body's power into a drain. Sacrifice the fatty when its trigger is removal or drain, fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns one card per end step. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn (CR 302.6). This was the strategist's call with the pilot's go-ahead, not the goldfish's.

V19 (2026-11-09). [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) in for Fauna Shaman. His words: "3 CMC discard and tutor a creature to hand! Plus ability to untap target permanent!!!!" That is Survival of the Fittest on a body, re-fired at each Meren return.

V22 (2026-11-13). The pilot's direction, in his words: Meren "wants to bring cards back IN THE END STEP". The plan is "the bodies we recur, ... being able to swing with a creature, sack it for value, bring it back in end step and trigger ETB, and it's untapped". He wants "big bodies that get something on ETB and dying", "a way to sacrifice them and convert their power or mana value into something like tokens or targeted removal", and "stacked/nested tutoring is money". "Noxious Gearhulk is dope! Archon of Cruelty is money!" "Cavalier of Night is great! because we get something on coming in and dying." "Gisa is critical." So the list moved toward bodies that pay on entering and dying, and away from lines that do not use the end step.

V23 (2026-11-13). He called the deck a "banger" and asked for balance across the whole axis: "we need sac outlets to kill creatures, it's part of the engine, we need to sac creatures and get stuff." And: "consider the token generators that feed the sac outlets as we are ramping experience counters.. and ways to fill the graveyard ... and card draw." And: "let's surgically optimize so we have a balance across the full axis ... can generate enough tokens, sac them, gain experience counters ... and get Meren recursion online ASAP." Still no games logged, so none of this has met a table.
