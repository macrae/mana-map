## Summary

This is a Golgari monster bash with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. Green dorks and land ramp land her around turn three. After that she tutors from the graveyard: every creature that dies gives her an experience counter, and at each end step she hands back a creature card, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once her counters have caught up.

The plan is to bin the fattest creatures on purpose, then bring them back fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber) and [Dina's Guidance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dina%27s+Guidance) fill the yard. Meren and the reanimation spells empty it onto the table. Removal on a body (the Chupacabra, the Gearhulk, [Sheoldred // The True Scriptures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred+%2F%2F+The+True+Scriptures)) comes back every end step and clears the way.

The pilot's words for V2: lean hard on Meren's tutoring, get the experience pumping, make it fast, and win with a regular monster creature bash. It is bracket 3. There are no two-card infinites, by the pilot's rule. Three-card lines are fine. The drain pieces that are left ([Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat), [Pitiless Plunderer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pitiless+Plunderer), [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre), [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact)) are backup, not the plan.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise), [Elvish Mystic](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Mystic), [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves), [Skull Prophet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skull+Prophet) and [Deathrite Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deathrite+Shaman) are the dorks. [Nature's Lore](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nature%27s+Lore), [Kodama's Reach](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kodama%27s+Reach) and [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) are the land ramp. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet) and [Chrome Mox](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chrome+Mox) are the rocks. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early. The goal is a turn-three Meren.

**Fodder into free outlets.** Every death pumps her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Carrion Feeder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Carrion+Feeder), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar) and [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar) let you sacrifice for free. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton), [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog), [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) and the Goat from Woe Strider are the bodies they eat. [Skull Prophet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skull+Prophet) mills two a turn, and that feeds the yard too.

**Bin the fat.** Put the biggest creatures in the graveyard on purpose. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber), [Dina's Guidance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dina%27s+Guidance) and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) all do it, and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) turn spare bodies into the right one. [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan), [Kalonian Hydra](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kalonian+Hydra), [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Junji%2C+the+Midnight+Sky), [Sheoldred // The True Scriptures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred+%2F%2F+The+True+Scriptures) and [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk) are the targets worth binning.

**Meren's choice.** While her experience is low, she hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it to the battlefield for free, so the early game is about pumping counters and the late game is about the biggest thing in the yard. [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead) and [Dread Return](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dread+Return) do the same job on demand. [Graveshifter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Graveshifter), [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness) and [Trystan's Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Trystan%27s+Command) return a card to hand when the battlefield is the wrong place.

**The bash.** [Kalonian Hydra](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kalonian+Hydra) doubles each attack. [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings two Zombies every time it enters or attacks. [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Junji%2C+the+Midnight+Sky) comes back when it dies and takes a creature with it. [Glissa Sunslayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glissa+Sunslayer) and [Sedgemoor Witch](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sedgemoor+Witch) are cheap threats that gain cards when they connect. The creatures with enter triggers ([Ravenous Chupacabra](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ravenous+Chupacabra), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Sheoldred // The True Scriptures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred+%2F%2F+The+True+Scriptures), [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness)) pay twice, because Meren returns one every end step.

**Backup drain.** If the board stalls, [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat), [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) and [Pitiless Plunderer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pitiless+Plunderer) turn the deaths into damage and mana. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes your sacrifices cost the table a creature each. They are insurance. The deck is trying to win on creature damage.

**Finding pieces.** [Vampiric Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampiric+Tutor) and [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent) find anything. [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling) and [Eldritch Evolution](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eldritch+Evolution) go get the creature you need. That leaves a lot of ways to find a bomb or the right outlet.
