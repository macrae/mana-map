## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. The point is big bodies early for what they cost. Bin the fattest creature, bring it back on turn three or four, and let Meren return it again later. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands one creature card back to your hand, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

The plan is to fill the yard on purpose and empty it fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive) and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) load it by choice, and [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder), [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six), [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver), [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) and the surveil on [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) mill or sort it by volume. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) and Meren empty it. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk) and the fatties that do something both coming in and dying: [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm), [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night), [Hapatra, the Desert Fang](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hapatra%2C+the+Desert+Fang) (one-sided counters sized by the yard). [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) sits beside Meren and copies whatever comes back from the yard, once a turn. [Unshakable Tail](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unshakable+Tail) turns all that filling into cards: each time a creature card is milled or surveilled into the yard, it investigates.

The deck is built around one line the pilot named himself: Meren "wants to bring cards back IN THE END STEP". Swing with a big creature, sacrifice it for value, bring it back at the end step, and it comes back untapped with its enter trigger fired again. Sacrifice the fatty when its enter trigger is removal or drain, and when its death trigger pays too. Sacrifice fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns only ONE card per end step, so choose what comes back. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn.

The pilot's wish list shapes the rest: big bodies that get something on entering and on dying, a way to sacrifice them and turn their power or mana value into tokens or removal, and "stacked/nested tutoring is money". [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) is critical: sacrifice a fatty and its power becomes Zombies. [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) is the nested tutor: sacrifice a creature, pay X, and put a creature of that mana value or less onto the battlefield.

[Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) is the other payoff. He grows with the creature cards in the yard and can sacrifice another creature to drain each opponent. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia), [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) give Meren a body to sacrifice every turn, so her counters do not wait on a lucky draw. Experience counters are the player's, in one pool. That is why [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) (each Meren trigger gives an extra counter) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) (counters off landfall) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

The sacrifice package from V21 stays. [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm) flips into a planeswalker whenever a creature enters from the yard or is cast from it. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) is endless fodder that never spends Meren's end-step return. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every death of ours an edict for every opponent.

V23 balanced the sacrifice engine, in the pilot's words: "we need sac outlets to kill creatures, it's part of the engine." [Blasting Station](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasting+Station) is the outlet that kills. [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat) and [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) pay on every death of ours, tokens included. [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) makes fodder in every upkeep. [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) and [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) give a cheap body something to do on dying and on entering. The aim is enough tokens, sacrificed, to build experience counters and get Meren's recursion online as early as possible.

The deck keeps its answers and its safety. [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) faces the table, and [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) is a one-sided wipe of small creatures every time it lands. Both are sweepers that suit us, because our own dead feed Meren. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention), [Tamiyo's Safekeeping](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tamiyo%27s+Safekeeping) and [Golgari Charm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Golgari+Charm) look after the engine. The review that added them found the deck's biggest exposure was graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void, Bojuka Bog), with little but [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy) able to answer an artifact or enchantment, which [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) now backs up, and Meren herself almost unprotected.

The deck carries two Game Changers, [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) and [Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tergrid%2C+God+of+Fright+%2F%2F+Tergrid%27s+Lantern). The bracket floor is 3. There is still no two-card infinite. [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) now completes three-card lines beside the altars and [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat). The Summary block above has the combo count.

V26 is about the yard. The pilot felt the deck was light on milling: "the plan is to play cards from the graveyard primarily... we need to fill the graveyard faster than casting creatures and them dying." So three volume-mill bodies went in. [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder) mills about three when it enters and hands you a land, and it fires again on every Meren return. [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six) mills three each time it attacks, and it lets any nonland permanent card in your yard be cast on your turn by discarding a land, a second play-from-the-graveyard engine beside Meren. [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver) mills two every upkeep and can exile itself to return any card from the yard to hand. [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze) stays as the one repeatable answer to someone else's graveyard.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise) and [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) are the dorks. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetches lands, and the basic Swamps are there for it. The pilot cut the extra ramp in V21: mana is not what limits this deck. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) is the rock. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb) is the explosive start, and [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate) follows it. There is no ritual any more: the thirty-fifth land took that slot. [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) makes a lot of black mana off the Swamp count, and [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urborg%2C+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth) makes every land a Swamp. [Sylvan Library](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sylvan+Library) smooths the draws. [Fabled Passage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fabled+Passage) and [Exotic Orchard](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Exotic+Orchard) fix black and green without the price of the old dual. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Fodder into outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Blasting Station](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasting+Station), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market), [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) are outlets that cost mana and give something back. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) makes a Faerie each upkeep, [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) a decayed Zombie each end step, [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) a deathtouch Snake whenever you have none, and [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) an Insect on every land drop. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton), [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm) and the Goat from [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider) are the other bodies they eat. The Skeleton walks back from the yard for {1}{B}, so it feeds an outlet every turn for free as far as Meren is concerned. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) costs a life a turn, which is the price of the clock. [Blasting Station](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasting+Station) is a free outlet that pings any target and untaps whenever a creature enters, so a token maker feeds it all turn. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) draw cards off those deaths, tokens included. [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat) drains each opponent on every death of ours, and pays back the life [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) costs. [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog) leaves a Spawn, which is mana, when a nontoken creature of ours dies.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber) and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) put the big creature in the yard on purpose. Fauna Shaman does it every turn: discard a creature card, fetch another. It is a creature itself, so a wipe or a removal spell ends the engine, and Meren can return it. [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) discards a fatty on entering and tutors a creature to hand. [Scavenging Ooze](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scavenging+Ooze) eats the other yards and grows, so it answers a graveyard plan and still feeds on ours.

**Fill the yard.** The loaders above aim; the millers fill it. [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder) is a two-mana body that mills about three and gives a land, and Meren returns it for cheap, so every return mills again. [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six) mills three whenever it attacks and turns the yard into a second hand: nonland permanents there can be cast on your turn by discarding a land, and the lands you mill and take are the lands to discard. [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver) mills two in each upkeep, and when the yard has the card you need it exiles itself to take it to hand. [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) mills three coming and going. More cards in the yard feed [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan), which grow with it. The risk is the same as the plan: it wants the yard to be safe, so watch for their graveyard hate.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) do the job on demand. Every one of these is sorcery speed now, so the deck has no instant-speed return. Cauldron of Essence is a second return every turn, at sorcery speed: sacrifice a creature in your main phase and the best one in the yard comes straight back, any mana value. The sacrifice is a death for Meren's counters and a drain on the table. Agadeem's Awakening returns several creatures at once, each with a different mana value, and it is also a land. [Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Malakir+Rebirth+%2F%2F+Malakir+Mire) saves a creature from dying, or is simply a land.

**The end-step loop.** This is the heart of the deck. Swing, sacrifice after combat, and Meren returns the body at the end step untapped. Each time it comes back its enter trigger fires again, and each time it dies its death trigger fires. What each fatty does:
- [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) drains each opponent when it dies, and Meren brings it back to do it again.
- [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) shrinks their small creatures every return, one-sided, and taxes their deaths.
- [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) copies a creature that enters from or is cast from the yard, once each turn. See the next paragraph.
- [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) drains the table.
- [Cavalier of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cavalier+of+Night) sacrifices another creature to destroy one of theirs, and gets something again when it dies.
- [Hapatra, the Desert Fang](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hapatra%2C+the+Desert+Fang) puts -1/-1 counters on one of each opponent's creatures when she enters, as many as the biggest mana value in our yard. It never touches our board, counters beat indestructible, and the fatties we bin set the size. Meren re-buys her for another shot.
- [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One) and [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) clear something every time they land.
- [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the sacrifice into a drain, and the returned body's power is the size of it.
- [Ghoulcaller Gisa](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ghoulcaller+Gisa) turns the sacrificed body's power into Zombies, so the bigger the fatty, the bigger the swarm.
- [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) sacrifices a body to put a creature onto the battlefield from the library, so one sacrifice is a tutor and a reanimation at once. It grows with the yard.
- [Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tergrid%2C+God+of+Fright+%2F%2F+Tergrid%27s+Lantern) steals what opponents lose to those edicts. [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier) mills three on entering and on dying, so it is an early return for Meren that fills the yard each time.

**Turn the filling into cards.** [Unshakable Tail](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unshakable+Tail) is a three-mana Zombie that surveils 1 when it enters and every upkeep, and investigates whenever creature cards go from the library to the yard. That is [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber), [Satyr Wayfinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Satyr+Wayfinder), [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six), [Nyx Weaver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx+Weaver), [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia) and its own surveil. The Clues are card draw, which is the deck's short axis, and they are also bodies-in-waiting for a sacrifice outlet. For {2} and a Clue it returns itself from the yard to hand, and Meren can re-buy it for three. [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) can copy it coming back. Its cost is the instant-speed return: the deck has none left.

**Do it twice.** [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) is a three-mana 3/3 that surveils 2 on entering. Whenever another creature of ours enters from the graveyard or is cast from it, it makes a token copy of that creature, once each turn. That covers Meren's end-step return, [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), creatures cast off [Six](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Six)'s retrace, and [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton). The copy fires its enter trigger too, so a returned [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) or [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) pays twice. A copy of a legend still fires its enter trigger before the legend rule takes one away, and a copied Kokusho's death still drains. The pilot: "I approve." The Diviner has to be on the table before the return, so cast her first.

**The sacrifice package.** Each reanimation or Meren return of a creature can also flip [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm), which then makes an Insect and mills two, or later copies the creatures in the yard. [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) is the cheap body to sacrifice: it returns itself, so Meren's single end-step slot stays on the fatty. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes each death of ours an edict: every opponent sacrifices a creature.

[Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) is the same loop on a three-mana body. It costs three, so Meren returns it from three counters. Sacrifice it and its enter trigger fires again: discard a fatty, tutor a creature ([Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord), [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) or a combo piece). It is a one-shot tutor on a body that fires again at each Meren return. Its {1},{T} untap hits [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) or [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) for more mana.

**The combo line.** The pilot said to lean into the combos, and no two-card infinite. [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) with both altars ([Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar) and [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar)) is the three-card line from before. V23 adds three more through [Pawn of Ulamog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pawn+of+Ulamog): [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) with [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat); [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) with [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar); and [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozox%2C+the+Clattering+King) with [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar). None is a two-card line. The Summary block has the count. The main-phase Hogaak lines are gone: they did not use Meren's end step.

**Sweep and survive.** [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) wipes the table, and the deck likes it: its own dead feed Meren, and she brings the best of them back. It also drains when creatures die. The old wrath was cut in V24 because it killed our own tokens and engine. [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) is the other sweeper and leaves our side alone. Three things keep the engine alive. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention) makes the board hexproof and indestructible. [Tamiyo's Safekeeping](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tamiyo%27s+Safekeeping) does the same for one permanent, Meren included, for a turn, at a single green mana. [Golgari Charm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Golgari+Charm) is the flexible one at instant speed: it regenerates the whole board through a destroy wipe, destroys an enchantment, or gives everything -1/-1 to clear tokens. [Bojuka Bog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bojuka+Bog) exiles an opponent's graveyard, and would eat ours if pointed the wrong way.

**Count your life and your lanes.** [Dark Prophecy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Prophecy) costs a life for every creature that dies, so count your life before a big sacrifice turn unless [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zulaport+Cutthroat) is out to pay it back. Meren returns one card per end step, so a second lane matters. [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One) returns a creature at each of our upkeeps, and [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) is the second lane every turn, and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) is the burst that puts several creatures back at once. [Twilight Diviner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twilight+Diviner) copies once a turn, so a main-phase Cauldron return and Meren's end-step return share that one copy.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) flies, and its death is a drain, so even a removed Kokusho is a win for us.

**Finding pieces and answers.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor) finds the card you need, and [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) finds a creature and bins one every turn while she lives. [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) and [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) are tutors on bodies. [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy), [Deadly Rollick](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deadly+Rollick) and [Boseiju, Who Endures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Boseiju%2C+Who+Endures) handle what the creatures do not. [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) is the cheap answer Meren can re-buy: for {1} and a sacrifice it destroys an artifact or an enchantment at instant speed, and the sacrifice is a death, so it is an experience counter. With nothing to answer, the same activation proliferates, which grows Meren's experience and [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor)'s counters. Meren returns it from two counters. The pilot: "cankerbloom is amazing in this deck!!!!" [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every one of your deaths cost the table a creature.

**How much of this we can see.** The goldfish has no opponents. It cannot measure the sweepers, the protection, the removal or the graveyard hate. The goldfish is not the arbiter here: its figures feed the strategist's judgment, and the end-step loop is the pilot's call.

No games are logged yet. Everything here comes from the pilot's words and the card text.

What the pilot has said, from the start: no two-card infinites, three-card lines are fine. He wants the absolute best, most banger Meren deck, fast and consistent, with nasty creatures on the table early. The point is big bodies early relative to their mana cost: tutor or entomb them into the yard, reanimate early, and let Meren's experience return them later.

V13 to V15. [Scute Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scute+Swarm) came back after the pilot said: "I have a scute swarm dude!!! bodies baby". Veil of Summer, Sylvan Library and Scavenging Ooze came in after a diagnosis (the deck leans on its graveyard and is exposed to counterspells).

V17. Meren's ruling: experience counters belong to the player, in one pool. So [Winding Constrictor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Winding+Constrictor) and [Toph, Earthbending Master](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toph%2C+Earthbending+Master) raise her ceiling, and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician)'s proliferate does too.

V18. His core line, in his words: "attacking with a big creature... sacking it in post-combat main to do something... and then bring it back in my end step untapped." [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) turns the returned body's power into a drain. Sacrifice the fatty when its trigger is removal or drain, fodder when the sacrifice itself pays. Meren returns one card per end step. A creature she returns at the end step can attack next turn (CR 302.6). This was the strategist's call with the pilot's go-ahead, not the goldfish's.

V19 (2026-11-09). [Formidable Speaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Formidable+Speaker) in for Fauna Shaman. His words: "3 CMC discard and tutor a creature to hand! Plus ability to untap target permanent!!!!" That is Survival of the Fittest on a body, re-fired at each Meren return.

V20 (2026-11-09). [Karn's Bastion](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Karn%27s+Bastion) in for one Forest: proliferate from a land slot at no spell cost. The strategist read more proliferate as mostly win-more, so this is the one clean addition. Sakura-Tribe Elder finds basic Swamps, and the fetches still find the typed duals.

V21 (2026-11-10). The sacrifice package: [Grist, Voracious Larva // Grist, the Plague Swarm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+Voracious+Larva+%2F%2F+Grist%2C+the+Plague+Swarm), [Reassembling Skeleton](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reassembling+Skeleton) and [Savra, Queen of the Golgari](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Savra%2C+Queen+of+the+Golgari) in, with Mazirek. Persist went because the deck is heavy in legendary creatures it can't return. Nature's Lore and Elves of Deep Shadow went because ramp is not the deck's limit.

V22 (2026-11-13). The pilot's direction, in his words: Meren "wants to bring cards back IN THE END STEP". The plan is "the bodies we recur, ... being able to swing with a creature, sack it for value, bring it back in end step and trigger ETB, and it's untapped". He wants "big bodies that get something on ETB and dying", "a way to sacrifice them and convert their power or mana value into something like tokens or targeted removal", and "stacked/nested tutoring is money". "Noxious Gearhulk is dope! Archon of Cruelty is money!" "Cavalier of Night is great! because we get something on coming in and dying." "Gisa is critical." So the list moved toward bodies that pay on entering and dying, and away from lines that do not use the end step.

V23 (2026-11-13). He called the deck a "banger" and asked for balance across the whole axis: "we need sac outlets to kill creatures, it's part of the engine, we need to sac creatures and get stuff." And: "consider the token generators that feed the sac outlets as we are ramping experience counters.. and ways to fill the graveyard ... and card draw." And: "let's surgically optimize so we have a balance across the full axis ... can generate enough tokens, sac them, gain experience counters ... and get Meren recursion online ASAP." Still no games logged, so none of this has met a table.

V24 (2026-11-13). A weakness review, not a game. Graveyard hate and a lack of Meren protection were the exposures, so Golgari Charm and Tamiyo's Safekeeping came in for Toxic Deluge and Final Parting, and a Forest became a Swamp. His words: "this is such a dope deck". How-to-play from the review: count your life before a big Dark Prophecy turn, and use Sheoldred and the burst reanimation as the second lane beside Meren's one return a turn.

2026-11-14 (budget-v2). Bayou and Survival of the Fittest were too expensive, so the budget versions came in. Fauna Shaman is back after V19 took it out for Formidable Speaker: the Speaker is a tutor on entering, the Shaman is one every turn. No games are logged on it yet.

V26 (2026-11-22), study 001. The pilot: the deck felt light on milling, and "the plan is to play cards from the graveyard primarily... we need to fill the graveyard faster than casting creatures and them dying." In: Satyr Wayfinder, Six, Nyx Weaver (the volume millers), Exotic Orchard and Fabled Passage and Fauna Shaman (already in since budget-v2). Out: Tendershoot Dryad, Bone Splinters, Savra, Queen of the Golgari, and the budget-v2 outs Bayou, Darkbore Pathway and Survival of the Fittest. He said "approve, make the swap" and kept Scavenging Ooze, the only repeatable graveyard hate. One correction: this file called Savra a free sacrifice outlet and said her edict cost two life. She was neither (she charges mana, and pays only when you sacrifice something), and she is gone. The Context now says the deck runs two Game Changers, Demonic Tutor and Tergrid. No games are logged on V26.

V27 (2026-11-22), study 005. [Hapatra, the Desert Fang](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hapatra%2C+the+Desert+Fang) in for Shriekmaw. The pilot: "shriekmaw is not bad... but compare that to toxic deluge at 5, 6 or 8 and, with tutoring cards to the graveyard there's a clear line here ... it feels like Hapatra is the stronger, meaner, card." She is one-sided, so our board stays. The counters beat indestructible. The size is the biggest mana value in our yard, and the binned fatties and the V26 millers set it. Meren re-buys her, to hand early and onto the battlefield once the experience counters catch up. No games yet, so none of this has met a table.

V28 (2026-11-22), studies 006 and 007. Twilight Diviner and Cankerbloom in; Skyfisher Spider and Unmarked Grave out. On the Diviner he said "I approve", and asked for a search to put artifact and enchantment removal back "in an optimal way". It copies any creature that comes back from the yard, once a turn: Meren's end-step return, the reanimation spells, retrace, the Skeleton. On Cankerbloom: "cankerbloom is amazing in this deck!!!!" It is instant-speed artifact or enchantment removal that Meren can re-buy, its sacrifice is a death, and it proliferates when nothing needs answering. It does the answering job Skyfisher Spider did, on a cheaper body. Unmarked Grave went with the Spider: the millers, Entomb and Buried Alive load the yard already.

V29 (2026-11-22), study 008. [Cauldron of Essence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cauldron+of+Essence) in for Victimize. The pilot: "yeah, man, I feel like Cauldron of Essence is an upgrade!??!" It is a second return every turn beside Meren's end step, to the battlefield at any mana value. The sacrifice is a death (experience for Meren, a drain on the table), and a creature that dies to it can come back at the end step. He accepted that it is main-phase only. What we lose: Victimize's two-fatty turn after Buried Alive. [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) still bursts. Twilight Diviner copies once a turn, so the Cauldron return and Meren's return share that copy. No games yet on it.

V30 (2026-11-22), study 009. [Unshakable Tail](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unshakable+Tail) in for Necromancy. The pilot: "approve, make the swap and save." The reason: the deck fills its yard by volume now, and Tail turns that fill into Clues, card draw being the deck's short axis. It surveils on entering and every upkeep, and returns itself from the yard. The price is Necromancy: the only instant-speed return, the only one that could take an opponent's creature, and the one way to get a Diviner copy on an opponent's turn. No games are logged on it yet.

Earlier calls still standing: the pilot turned down Ancient Tomb (another Game Changer) and Gravecrawler (a two-card infinite with Phyrexian Altar).

**V30 (2026-11-22), study 009.** In: Unshakable Tail. Out: Necromancy. Lands unchanged at thirty-five.
- Unshakable Tail: it investigates whenever creature cards go from library to yard, so Entomb, Buried Alive, the millers and the surveils become Clues. It returns itself for {2} and a Clue, Meren re-buys it for three, and Diviner can copy it.
- Necromancy out: the deck loses its only instant-speed reanimation, its only way to take an opponent's creature, and the Diviner copy on an opponent's turn. Reanimate, Animate Dead, Cauldron of Essence and Meren all work at sorcery speed.
- Open: whether the Clues come fast enough to replace what Necromancy did.

**V29 (2026-11-22), study 008.** In: Cauldron of Essence. Out: Victimize. Lands unchanged at thirty-five.
- Cauldron of Essence: a second return every turn, in the main phase, straight to the battlefield. The sacrifice is a death for Meren and a drain on the table, and every other death drains as well. With Yawgmoth and Pawn of Ulamog it is a three-card line, bracket 3 by the pilot's rule. Pilot: "I feel like Cauldron of Essence is an upgrade."
- Victimize out: it was one burst of two fatties, a one-shot. The Buried Alive two-fatty turn is gone. Agadeem's Awakening still bursts.
- Open: main-phase only, so it will not make an instant-speed play, and the Diviner copy is shared with Meren's end step.

**V28 (2026-11-22), studies 006 and 007.** In: Twilight Diviner, Cankerbloom. Out: Skyfisher Spider, Unmarked Grave. Lands unchanged at thirty-five.
- Twilight Diviner: a body that copies a creature entering from the yard, once each turn, so Meren's end-step return and every reanimation spell fires its enter trigger twice. Surveil 2 helps the yard too. Pilot: "I approve."
- Cankerbloom for Skyfisher Spider: the artifact or enchantment answer is back, at instant speed, on a body Meren re-buys for two. Its sacrifice is a death, and it proliferates when nothing needs answering. Pilot: "cankerbloom is amazing in this deck!!!!" (It left at V21 and is back.)
