## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator beatdown, with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. Dorks, [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) and [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) get her down early. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands back a creature card, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

The plan is to bin the fattest creatures on purpose and bring them back fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) and the rest fill the yard. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Living Death](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Living+Death), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead) and Meren empty it onto the table. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm), [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk), [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) and [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Junji%2C+the+Midnight+Sky).

The pilot asked for the absolute best, most banger Meren deck: up to three Game Changers, no two-card infinites. The Game Changers are [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) and [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor). The bracket floor is 3. The drain package is gone. The deck wins with big creatures, not with death triggers.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise), [Elvish Mystic](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Mystic), [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves), [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elves+of+Deep+Shadow) and [Deathrite Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deathrite+Shaman) are the dorks. [Nature's Lore](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nature%27s+Lore) and [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetch lands, and the two plain Swamps are there so the Elder always has a basic to find. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) and [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet) are the rocks. [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) turns a board of small bodies into a burst of mana. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Fodder into free outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Carrion Feeder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Carrion+Feeder), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Stitcher's Supplier](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stitcher%27s+Supplier), [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom) and the Goat from Woe Strider are the bodies they eat. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) draw cards off those deaths, so the engine refuels itself.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber), [Dina's Guidance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dina%27s+Guidance) and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) put the big creature in the yard. [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) and [Fiend Artisan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiend+Artisan) turn spare bodies into the right one. [Skull Prophet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skull+Prophet), [Golgari Grave-Troll](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Golgari+Grave-Troll) and [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia) mill or fill the yard on the side.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it for free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Dread Return](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dread+Return) and [Living Death](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Living+Death) do the job on demand. [Living Death](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Living+Death) is the big swing: it puts every yard creature on the table at once, so it wants a yard you filled. [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness) returns a card to hand when the battlefield is the wrong place.

**The edict loop.** Creatures that clear the way on entering pay twice, because Meren returns one every end step. [Accursed Marauder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Accursed+Marauder), [Plaguecrafter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Plaguecrafter), [Ravenous Chupacabra](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ravenous+Chupacabra), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) and [Sheoldred // The True Scriptures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred+%2F%2F+The+True+Scriptures) each take out something or force a sacrifice, over and over. [Spore Frog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spore+Frog) fogs every turn, so the beatdown is safe while it builds.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) drains and clears a permanent whenever it enters or attacks. [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kokusho%2C+the+Evening+Star) pays you back when it dies. [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Junji%2C+the+Midnight+Sky) comes back when it dies. [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk) turns its own death into a handful of small creatures, and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) trades a body up the curve. [Glissa Sunslayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glissa+Sunslayer) is a cheap threat that wins combat.

**Finding pieces.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent), [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling), [Eldritch Evolution](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eldritch+Evolution) and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) find the creature you need. [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) both finds it and bins it.
