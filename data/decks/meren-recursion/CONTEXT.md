## Summary

This is an optimized bracket 3 Golgari reanimator, with [Meren of Clan Nel Toth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Meren+of+Clan+Nel+Toth) as the engine. The point is big bodies early for what they cost. Bin the fattest creature, bring it back on turn three or four, and let Meren return it again later. Every creature that dies gives her an experience counter. At each end step she hands a creature card back to your hand, or puts it straight onto the battlefield once the counters have caught up.

The plan is to fill the yard on purpose and empty it fast. [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) load it. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead) and Meren empty it. The targets are [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm), [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk) and [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan).

[Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) and [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) give Meren a body to sacrifice every turn, so her counters do not wait on a lucky draw.

V7 is the pilot's call for the most competitive list in bracket 3, and it adds what the first six versions lacked: a way to deal with the table and a way to keep the engine alive. [Toxic Deluge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toxic+Deluge), [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) and [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) answer opponents. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention) and [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) protect the fatty. [Dark Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Ritual) makes turn-one Entomb into turn-two Reanimate possible. [Bojuka Bog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bojuka+Bog) shuts off an opponent's graveyard plan. [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) with [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urborg%2C+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth) turns the lands into a lot of black mana.

The Game Changers are [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest), [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) and [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor). The bracket floor is 3, with no two-card infinites. The deck wins with big creatures, not with death triggers.

## How it plays

**Early.** Get mana and a body down. [Birds of Paradise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birds+of+Paradise), [Elvish Mystic](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Mystic) and [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) are the dorks. [Nature's Lore](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nature%27s+Lore) and [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) fetch lands, and the plain Swamp is there so the Elder has a basic to find. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) and [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet) are the rocks. [Dark Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Ritual) is the explosive start: three black mana for a turn-one [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb) or a turn-two [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate). [Gaea's Cradle](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gaea%27s+Cradle) turns a board of small bodies into a burst of mana, and [Cabal Coffers](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cabal+Coffers) does the same for Swamps, which [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Urborg%2C+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth) makes of every land. The Numbers section has how often Meren is cast and how early.

**Fodder into free outlets.** Deaths pump her counters. [Viscera Seer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viscera+Seer), [Carrion Feeder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Carrion+Feeder), [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider), [Ashnod's Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ashnod%27s+Altar), [Phyrexian Altar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Altar), [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia), [High Market](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=High+Market), [Phyrexian Tower](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Tower) and [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Yawgmoth%2C+Thran+Physician) let you sacrifice for free or close to it. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom), [Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jadar%2C+Ghoulcaller+of+Nephalia) and [Ophiomancer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ophiomancer) and [Grist, the Hunger Tide](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grist%2C+the+Hunger+Tide) are the steady supply: an Insect from Grist, a Faerie each upkeep, a decayed Zombie each end step, a deathtouch Snake whenever you have none. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), [Cankerbloom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Cankerbloom), [Haywire Mite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Haywire+Mite) and the Goat from [Woe Strider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Woe+Strider) are the other bodies they eat. [Bitterblossom](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bitterblossom) costs a life a turn, which is the price of the clock. [Skullclamp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skullclamp) and [Grim Haruspex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grim+Haruspex) draw cards off those deaths, so the engine refuels itself.

**Bin the fat.** [Entomb](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Entomb), [Buried Alive](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Buried+Alive), [Vile Entomber](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vile+Entomber), [Dina's Guidance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dina%27s+Guidance) and [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) put the big creature in the yard. [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) bins any nonlegendary card. [Final Parting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Final+Parting) finds two cards, one for the hand and one for the yard. [Fauna Shaman](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fauna+Shaman) turns a spare body into the right one. [Altar of Dementia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Altar+of+Dementia) mills the yard full on the side.

**Bring it back.** While her counters are low, Meren hands the creature to your hand and you cast it. Once the counters catch up with its cost she returns it free. [Reanimate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reanimate), [Animate Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Animate+Dead), [Necromancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Necromancy), [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist), [Dread Return](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dread+Return) and [Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Agadeem%27s+Awakening+%2F%2F+Agadeem%2C+the+Undercrypt) do the job on demand. Necromancy can come in at instant speed. Persist brings back a nonlegendary creature a size smaller. Agadeem's Awakening is the big swing: it returns several creatures at once, each with a different mana value, and it is also a land when the hand wants one. [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness) returns a card to hand when the battlefield is the wrong place. [Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Malakir+Rebirth+%2F%2F+Malakir+Mire) saves a creature from dying, or is simply a land.

**The edict loop.** Creatures that clear the way on entering pay twice, because Meren returns one every end step. [Accursed Marauder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Accursed+Marauder), [Plaguecrafter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Plaguecrafter), [Ravenous Chupacabra](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ravenous+Chupacabra), [Sheoldred, Whispering One](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+Whispering+One), [Noxious Gearhulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Noxious+Gearhulk), [Massacre Wurm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Massacre+Wurm) and [Sheoldred // The True Scriptures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred+%2F%2F+The+True+Scriptures) each take out something or force a sacrifice, over and over. [Spore Frog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spore+Frog) fogs every turn, so the beatdown is safe while it builds.

**Sweep and survive.** This is the V7 addition. [Toxic Deluge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Toxic+Deluge) and [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) wipe the table, and the deck is built to like it: its own dead feed Meren, and she brings the best of them back. [The Meathook Massacre](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=The+Meathook+Massacre) also drains when creatures die, which pays for the fodder. [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) kills a creature for almost nothing. [Heroic Intervention](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heroic+Intervention) makes the board hexproof and indestructible, which beats a wipe or a removal spell. [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) gives a reanimated fatty haste and shroud, so it attacks the turn it lands and cannot be targeted. [Bojuka Bog](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bojuka+Bog) exiles an opponent's graveyard, so play it when it hits theirs. It would also eat ours if pointed the wrong way.

**The bash.** [Grave Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Titan) brings Zombies on entering and attacking. [Archon of Cruelty](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Archon+of+Cruelty) drains and clears a permanent whenever it enters or attacks. [Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hogaak%2C+Arisen+Necropolis) can't be cast with mana at all: the token makers tap to convoke it and a full yard delves it, so it often lands free, and it can be cast again from the graveyard after a wipe. It is legendary, so [Unmarked Grave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Unmarked+Grave) and [Persist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Persist) can't take it, which is why it leans on casting itself. [Protean Hulk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Protean+Hulk) turns its own death into a handful of small creatures, and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) trades a body up the curve. [Glissa Sunslayer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glissa+Sunslayer) is a cheap threat that wins combat.

**Finding pieces.** [Demonic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Demonic+Tutor), [Diabolic Intent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Diabolic+Intent), [Beseech the Mirror](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beseech+the+Mirror) and [Birthing Pod](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Birthing+Pod) find the card you need. [Survival of the Fittest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Survival+of+the+Fittest) both finds a creature and bins one.

**Removal and answers.** [Assassin's Trophy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Assassin%27s+Trophy), [Beast Within](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Within), [Deadly Rollick](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deadly+Rollick), [Dismember](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dismember) and [Boseiju, Who Endures](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Boseiju%2C+Who+Endures) handle what the creatures do not. [Grave Pact](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grave+Pact) makes every one of your deaths cost the table a creature.

**How much of this we can see.** The goldfish has no opponents. It cannot measure the sweepers, the protection, the removal or the graveyard hate. The V7 additions are the pilot's judgment about playing against real tables, not a measured gain.

## Pilot notes

<!-- ctx:gen record -->
- No games logged yet (`manamap pilot deck-notes meren-recursion add …`).
<!-- /ctx:gen record -->

No games are logged for V7. Everything above comes from the pilot's brief and the card text.

What the pilot has said: no two-card infinites, three-card lines are fine, up to three Game Changers. He wants the absolute best, most banger Meren deck, fast and consistent, with nasty creatures on the table early. The bash is the plan.

For V4 he named the point of the deck: get big bodies in early relative to their mana cost. Tutor or entomb them into the yard, reanimate early, and let Meren's experience return them later. The token makers are there to feed that clock.

For V5 he asked for loaders and reanimation so the big bodies land early. Small dorks that never became the fatty were the price.

For V6 he picked a threat that survives a wipe and can come down free. His call over the other candidates (Mazirek, the Gitrog Monster, Yargle) was resilience.

For V7 he asked for the most competitive bracket 3 list. He wants interaction, protection, speed, graveyard hate, more mana and lands that double as spells. He turned down Ancient Tomb, because it would be the fourth Game Changer, and Gravecrawler, because it makes a two-card infinite with Phyrexian Altar.
