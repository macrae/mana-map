## Summary

This is the Foundations Commander blue precon, played as printed. It is the deck Sean brings when the table wants less power. [Sai, Master Thopterist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sai%2C+Master+Thopterist) makes a flying Thopter every time an artifact spell is cast, and almost everything in the deck is an artifact. So you cast cheap things, the board fills with bodies, and a few cards turn that crowd into damage or cards.

It is a friendly, proactive deck, not a combo deck. The bracket floor in the block above is the lowest, and no known line is complete in the 99. It plays blue only, with a handful of colourless lands.

## How it plays

**Early.** Sai costs three and is the first thing you want. The fuel is cheap artifacts: [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet), [Mind Stone](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mind+Stone), the Myr, and the two cost reducers [Etherium Sculptor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Etherium+Sculptor) and [Foundry Inspector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Foundry+Inspector). Each one is also a Thopter, because it is an artifact spell with Sai out. The Numbers section says how often that fuel shows up by turn six.

**Middle.** The board is wide and small. [Master of Etherium](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Master+of+Etherium), [Chief of the Foundry](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chief+of+the+Foundry), [Steel Overseer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Steel+Overseer) and [Nettlecyst](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nettlecyst) make the crowd bigger. [Vedalken Archmage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vedalken+Archmage) and [Thopter Spy Network](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Thopter+Spy+Network) turn it into cards. Sai's own sacrifice ability turns two artifacts into a card whenever the hand runs dry.

**Finish.** Two ways to win. Go wide and pump, or land one huge artifact: [Myr Battlesphere](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Myr+Battlesphere), [Darksteel Juggernaut](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Darksteel+Juggernaut), [Kappa Cannoneer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kappa+Cannoneer), [Broodstar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Broodstar), [Graaz, Unstoppable Juggernaut](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Graaz%2C+Unstoppable+Juggernaut). [Whirler Rogue](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Whirler+Rogue) makes one attacker unblockable. The Numbers section has the kill line and the median kill turn. The goldfish is slow to kill here, which suits a deck that is meant to be gentle.

**Protection.** [Propaganda](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Propaganda) and [Aetherize](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Aetherize) slow the table down while the board builds. [Counterspell](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Counterspell), [Negate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Negate) and [Launch Mishap](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Launch+Mishap) answer the one thing that matters. [Padeem, Consul of Innovation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Padeem%2C+Consul+of+Innovation) makes the artifacts hexproof.

**The goldfish undercounts this deck.** It does not model Sai's Thopters or his sacrifice-draw. Every board and damage figure in Numbers is a floor, and the Thopters it never makes are the deck's main source of bodies.
