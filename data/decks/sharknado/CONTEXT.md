## Summary

This is the Sharknado: [Shabraz, the Skyshark](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shabraz%2C+the+Skyshark) and [Brallin, Skyshark Rider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brallin%2C+Skyshark+Rider) as partner commanders, in white, blue and red. It is a modal voltron deck. Either commander wins by 21 commander damage, and the board picks which one.

[Shabraz, the Skyshark](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shabraz%2C+the+Skyshark) is the primary. He flies, and every card drawn is a counter on him and a life for you, so draw doublers turn him into a huge flyer fast. [Brallin, Skyshark Rider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brallin%2C+Skyshark+Rider) is the second road. He grows and pings the table on every discard, and [Torbran, Thane of Red Fell](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Torbran%2C+Thane+of+Red+Fell), [Gratuitous Violence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gratuitous+Violence), double strike and trample turn that size into a hit that ends the game.

Since v2.1.0 (2026-10-26) the deck also carries real interaction and standing draw: counters, removal, a free silence on the table, and three draw engines that keep cards coming between wheels. The wheels are the finishers, fired with a commander already out, and [Wheel of Fortune](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wheel+of+Fortune) under [Consecrated Sphinx](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Consecrated+Sphinx) is a proved kill line. The deck sits at three Game Changers ([Consecrated Sphinx](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Consecrated+Sphinx), [Rhystic Study](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rhystic+Study), [Smothering Tithe](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Smothering+Tithe)) to stay in Bracket 3.

Since v2.2.0 (2026-10-27) it also has a way to punish the table's draws: [Razorkin Needlehead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Razorkin+Needlehead) pings the table on every card they draw, and [Shark Typhoon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shark+Typhoon) is a cycler that pays both commanders and leaves a shark behind.

Sean plays this list on printed proxies.

## How it plays

**The plan.** Land a commander, grow it, protect it, swing. Which commander depends on the hand and the table. Shabraz is the default: he flies, and the draw engine feeds him. Brallin is the pick when Torbran or Gratuitous Violence is out, or when a trampling, double-striking rider can get through. Torbran's bonus and doubled damage both count as Brallin's commander damage, so those cards are real commander-damage cards, not just burn. Shabraz is white and blue, not a red source, so Torbran's bonus does not count toward his commander damage. Gratuitous Violence and Fiery Emancipation still do.

**Shabraz's road: draw, and draw twice.** [Teferi's Ageless Insight](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Teferi%27s+Ageless+Insight) and [Bard, King of Dale](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bard%2C+King+of+Dale) each double every extra draw. Each draw is a counter on Shabraz. [Lae'zel, Vlaakith's Champion](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lae%27zel%2C+Vlaakith%27s+Champion) adds a counter on top of every counter, so a seven-card wheel puts a very large number of counters on him. [Knowledge Is Power](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Knowledge+Is+Power) adds the cards drawn this turn to every creature's size, so a draw turn is also a pump turn. [Rhystic Study](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rhystic+Study) and [Mystic Remora](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mystic+Remora) draw off the table's spells, and [Consecrated Sphinx](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Consecrated+Sphinx) draws two every time an opponent draws, which a wheel turns into a flood. [Faerie Mastermind](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Faerie+Mastermind) is a smaller engine that draws off what the table does. [Razorkin Needlehead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Razorkin+Needlehead) turns the same table draws into damage (see Brallin's road).

**Brallin's road: discard, then hit.** Each discard is a counter on him and a ping to each opponent. [Torbran, Thane of Red Fell](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Torbran%2C+Thane+of+Red+Fell) makes every red hit bigger, his pings included. [Gratuitous Violence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gratuitous+Violence) doubles any damage a creature of yours deals, and [Fiery Emancipation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Emancipation) triples damage from any source you control, as a backup for either commander. [Glint-Horn Buccaneer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Glint-Horn+Buccaneer) and [Magmakin Artillerist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Magmakin+Artillerist) each ping per discard, so with the Artillerist out a wheel hits twice, and Torbran and the doublers multiply both. [Marauding Mako](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Marauding+Mako) grows off the same discards. [Razorkin Needlehead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Razorkin+Needlehead) pings each opponent once for every card they draw, so a wheel hits them on the draw as well as the discard. With Torbran out each ping is bigger, and a [Wheel of Fortune](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wheel+of+Fortune) or [Reforge the Soul](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reforge+the+Soul) is a big hit to each opponent from the Needlehead alone, on top of Brallin and the Artillerist. This is rules-checked (the rules-question agent, 2026-10-27), not a proved stack. It is a two-mana creature that dies to anything, so it wants a quiet turn. The Numbers section has the second-damage-source line.

**Make the swing count.** Double strike is everywhere on purpose, because it doubles the commander's damage twice over with Torbran and Gratuitous Violence. [Blade Historian](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blade+Historian) gives every attacker double strike, so both commanders at once. [Shu Yun, the Silent Tempest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shu+Yun%2C+the+Silent+Tempest) hands double strike to one creature off any noncreature spell. [Embercleave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Embercleave) adds trample and +1/+1. [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jeskai+Ascendancy) pumps and untaps the team on every noncreature spell and loots. [Akroma's Will](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Akroma%27s+Will) gives the team flying, vigilance and double strike, and with a commander out it also protects them. [Inti, Seneschal of the Sun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Inti%2C+Seneschal+of+the+Sun) turns a discard into a counter and trample on an attacker.

**Get there.** [Brotherhood Regalia](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brotherhood+Regalia) makes a commander unblockable and gives it ward. [Robe of Stars](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Robe+of+Stars) adds toughness to the carrier and can phase it out in response to removal. [Chaos Warp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chaos+Warp), [Get Lost](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Get+Lost) and [Swords to Plowshares](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swords+to+Plowshares) clear a blocker.

**Find the pieces.** [Idyllic Tutor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Idyllic+Tutor) fetches an enchantment: [Gratuitous Violence](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gratuitous+Violence), [Fiery Emancipation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Emancipation), [Knowledge Is Power](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Knowledge+Is+Power), [Teferi's Ageless Insight](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Teferi%27s+Ageless+Insight), [Rhystic Study](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rhystic+Study), [Mystic Remora](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mystic+Remora), [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jeskai+Ascendancy). [Stoneforge Mystic](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stoneforge+Mystic) fetches an equipment and can put it onto the battlefield for two mana, which makes [Embercleave](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Embercleave) or [Robe of Stars](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Robe+of+Stars) arrive cheap.

**Wheels are finishers.** The one-shot wheels are [Wheel of Fortune](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wheel+of+Fortune), [Windfall](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Windfall), [Tolarian Winds](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Tolarian+Winds) and [Reforge the Soul](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reforge+the+Soul), and they all discard, so they all pay Brallin. [Jace's Archivist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jace%27s+Archivist) is a wheel on a body. [Collective Defiance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Collective+Defiance), [Incendiary Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Incendiary+Command) and [Fateful Showdown](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fateful+Showdown) wheel and do something else, and [Prismari Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Prismari+Command) is a loot-and-burn version. They are meant to land with a commander out. This came straight from Sean's games: too many wheels, a hand of wheels with nothing to cash them on, and a table that does not enjoy having its hand taken.

**Shark Typhoon.** Cycling it at instant speed for X and one blue is a discard and a draw in one. The discard pays Brallin (a counter and a ping) and [Marauding Mako](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Marauding+Mako) (a counter). The draw pays Shabraz (a counter and a life). It also leaves an X/X flying Shark, and Brallin's red ability can give that Shark trample. Left on the table as an enchantment, it makes a Shark whenever you cast a noncreature spell. So it is a smaller wheel you can fire in response to anything.

**Loot instead of wheeling.** The smaller discards keep both commanders growing without emptying the hand: [Faithless Looting](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Faithless+Looting), [Frantic Search](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Frantic+Search), [Sorcerer Class](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sorcerer+Class), [Artist's Talent](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Artist%27s+Talent), [Kitsa, Otterball Elite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kitsa%2C+Otterball+Elite), [Mary Read and Anne Bonny](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mary+Read+and+Anne+Bonny), [Ledger Shredder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ledger+Shredder), [Teferi, Master of Time](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Teferi%2C+Master+of+Time). Mary Read loots every turn from the turn she lands (she has haste) and a discarded Island makes a Treasure. Teferi can loot on anyone's turn. [Rielle, the Everwise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Rielle%2C+the+Everwise) draws off the first discard each turn.

**Keep them alive.** The engine is two creatures that die to anything. [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) gives shroud and haste. [Flawless Maneuver](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Flawless+Maneuver) is free with a commander out and makes your team indestructible. [Deflecting Swat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deflecting+Swat) is also free with a commander out and redirects a spell or ability. [Loran's Escape](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Loran%27s+Escape) gives hexproof and indestructible. [March of Swirling Mist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=March+of+Swirling+Mist) phases creatures out. [Grand Abolisher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grand+Abolisher) stops the table casting spells or using abilities on your turn, so nothing answers the swing. [Command Beacon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Command+Beacon) puts a dead commander back in hand so the tax is paid once. [Otawara, Soaring City](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otawara%2C+Soaring+City) can bounce a threat to a commander when it is also a land.

**Answers.** [Swords to Plowshares](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swords+to+Plowshares), [Get Lost](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Get+Lost) and [Chaos Warp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chaos+Warp) handle a creature, enchantment or any permanent. [Negate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Negate), [Stubborn Denial](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Stubborn+Denial) and [Izzet Charm](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Izzet+Charm) counter noncreature spells, and Denial gets harder to pay for once a creature is out.

**Mana leans red and white, and red is still the thinnest.** The v2.2.0 land pass swapped two lands that mostly made no real red for [Thundering Falls](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Thundering+Falls) (blue and red, enters tapped, surveils) and [Sundown Pass](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sundown+Pass) (red and white, untapped when you control two other lands). [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Arcane Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arcane+Signet), [Azorius Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Azorius+Signet), [Izzet Signet](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Izzet+Signet) and [Talisman of Creativity](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Talisman+of+Creativity) pay for the second spell, and [Smothering Tithe](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Smothering+Tithe) makes Treasure off the table's draws. The Numbers section's on-curve colour line says whether any colour is thin.

**What the goldfish can say.** It has no blockers and no removal, and it does not model the modal plan. Forge's AI played the adds in the proof run except for two it held. Read the Numbers section as a ceiling for how often things line up, never as a verdict on the table.

**Proved lines.** Stack 009: [Wheel of Fortune](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wheel+of+Fortune) under [Consecrated Sphinx](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Consecrated+Sphinx) draws a huge pile, and the cleanup discard fires Brallin until all three opponents lose together. Stack 001 (Windfall with both commanders out) and stack 004 (Artist's Talent adding to every ping) were proved on earlier lists; read them as shape, not as proof on this one.

**Keep the land-and-ramp hand; cast the first commander on curve.** Sean's play rule since 2026-10-27, after game 004, where both commanders were held for a wheel that never came. Q012 settled the keep: in the goldfish, a keepable seven with lots of lands and ramp and no payoff gets a commander out sooner than one holding a payoff, and the commander and the loots dig to the payoffs. Q013 settled the hold: holding does not take the commander out of danger, because it must be on the table before the wheel resolves anyway, and in a game with no early wheel the hold line never casts it. Protection is usually in hand by then ([Deflecting Swat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deflecting+Swat) was in 004). The exception is a wipe you can see coming the turn you would cast.

**The fast table: Alex's warp deck.** The seat itself (Tannuk, the Sundial line, when to answer each) lives in `data/opponents/tannuk-warp/NOTES.md`. What this deck brings to it: Tannuk dies to [Swords to Plowshares](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swords+to+Plowshares), [Get Lost](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Get+Lost) or [Chaos Warp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chaos+Warp); [Otawara, Soaring City](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otawara%2C+Soaring+City) and [Teferi, Master of Time](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Teferi%2C+Master+of+Time)'s −3 only buy a turn. The Sundial dies to [Chaos Warp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chaos+Warp), [Prismari Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Prismari+Command) or [Otawara, Soaring City](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otawara%2C+Soaring+City). Nothing in the 99 counters a creature spell, and [Grand Abolisher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Grand+Abolisher) does not help. Hold [Chaos Warp](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chaos+Warp) at that table: it answers either piece. (Q014, 2026-10-27.)

**What the table misreads.** They see a draw deck and two creatures and rank you low. A flying shark with a pile of counters and double strike is the correction.

**001 (2026-10-06, three-player pod at home, first game with the deck).** Opponents were Stuart (Rock Cleaver zombie tokens) and Steven (a card-draw deck with a lot of counterspells). Sean called the game good. No result is recorded.

- **Fast start from [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring).** It was in the opening hand. Sean says the speed came from that.
- **Short on wheels early,** and light on ramp. He never drew [Jeska's Will](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Jeska%27s+Will) or [Mana Geyser](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mana+Geyser). He wants more of that kind of card, or mana creatures.
- **Two commanders, twice the management.** [Brallin, Skyshark Rider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brallin%2C+Skyshark+Rider) was removed a couple of times and the tax came on. [Lightning Greaves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Greaves) and [Swiftfoot Boots](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swiftfoot+Boots) protected them and were "really good".
- **The payoff.** Once the discards and draws started, both commanders grew fast. Sean gave one flying, or trample, to get through. [Echo of Eons](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Echo+of+Eons) made everyone discard and draw seven, made Shabraz huge, and nearly killed Stuart on commander damage.
- **No removal.** At times he needed creature or enchantment removal and had none, so he could not prune the boards. Steven's counterspells made the end grindy.
- **Discarding useful cards** to fire a wheel hurt him.
- **Idea he likes:** wheels that also do something else, like counter a spell and wheel, or clear creatures and wheel. He wants more wheels that chain into wheels.

His words: "it's fun, it's doing what it's supposed to do, but it definitely needs some finesse." He is not itching to change anything. Many cards have not been played yet, and he wants a couple more games before locking anything. He asked for hypotheses to test, not swaps.

**002 (2026-10-20, four-person pod at Oliver's, the second game and the first on v1.3.0).** Same pod as before: Oliver, Tom and Ryan. A loss per the log. Sean said the wheels with the commanders out "went pretty well", and that the deck is actually good, but it needs calibrating.

- **The kept hand.** He mulliganed, and still isn't sure he should have kept what he had: one playable creature and a pile of wheels. A hand of wheels with nothing to cash them in on is a hand that does nothing.
- **Too many wheels.** His read: "there might be too many wheels in the wheel deck." This is the first time he has said the count itself is wrong, not just the mix.
- **Two commander taxes.** Two commanders mean two taxes on the board. [Brallin, Skyshark Rider](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brallin%2C+Skyshark+Rider) is the red four-drop, so he arrives late and costs more each time he is removed.
- **The table does not enjoy it.** Making people discard their hand is not fun for them. Oliver was already discarding creatures off the board and more besides. Sean's point: you are trying to set up, you hold cards, and someone keeps taking them. If the whole deck is built on that, it is annoying politically, and that matters in a pod you play again.
- **His thesis: wheels are finishers.** "The wheels need to be few and far between; they need to come in and hit when Brallin or Shabraz are on the table." The picture he painted: both commanders already out and big, a wheel lands, the table discards and Brallin pings everyone, everyone draws and Shabraz grows and he gains life, then a second wheel, then red mana for trample, and a flying trampling commander shark swings. Wheels as the turn you win on, not as early tempo.
- **A note on the goldfish.** He said this is a reminder to downweight those metrics. The goldfish has no removal and no opposing interaction, so it pushes the deck the wrong way. The Numbers section is a ceiling for how often things line up, never a verdict on the table.

Read together with 001: the shape that works is one or both commanders on the table first, then the wheels. Three games' worth of notes now say the same thing in different words (hold the second commander, save the big wheel, wheels as finishers). It is a pattern the pilot is converging on, not a rule yet, and no list change has been agreed.

Of the things Sean said he missed after 001, the v1.3.0 branch added removal and answers, wheels that do something else, and a way to buy a commander back. Ramp is the one it did not add to. Game 002 is the first look at that list, and its main finding is about how many wheels there are, not which.

**After game 002, the plan.** On 2026-10-22 Sean named the deck's main win condition: 21 commander damage from either commander, chosen by the board. That is the base of v2.0.0. The "too many wheels" and "wheels are finishers" notes above became the wheel cut. Game 003 was the first on v2.0.0.

**003 (2026-10-25, the first game on v2.0.0).** No result is recorded, and no opponents. Sean's words: "goddamn, just played shabraz... it's slooooowwwww..... maybe it's a sequencing thing, maybe it's the cards I saw... but no removal, slow to get cards out, got mana starved one round... not the best showing tonight." He had no result to give, so none is inferred here.

- **Slow.** He was playing Shabraz.
- **Slow to get cards out.** Cards came out of hand slowly.
- **Mana-starved for one round.** One round stalled for lack of mana.
- **No removal seen.** The same gap he named after 001.
- **He does not know why.** He names two suspects himself, his sequencing and the cards he happened to see, and does not pick between them. A single game cannot split them.

The v2.1.0 list (2026-10-26) answers the "no removal" gap that games 001 and 003 both named: it adds removal, counters and a free table-wide silence.

**004 (2026-10-27, Ryan's house, a loss, the first game logged on v2.2.0).** The deck sha on the log entry matches the list on disk, so this is the sleeved v2.2.0. Three players: Sean on Sharknado, Alex on his warp deck, Ryan on Slivers (nothing much popped off for Ryan). The cause is logged as raced. Sean's words: the engine ran well and the sequencing felt right, but Alex's deck was a faster class of deck, and a land-heavy opener without payoffs never found the wheels.

- **The engine ran.** Draw and discard loops moved Sean through the deck all game: the Ledger connive creature ([Ledger Shredder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ledger+Shredder), as far as the log says) and [Mary Read and Anne Bonny](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mary+Read+and+Anne+Bonny) (haste, draw a card, discard a card). He cast a lot of spells, and Alex said things were happening.
- **[Smothering Tithe](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Smothering+Tithe) did the ramping.** It made steady Treasure, which was the mana the hand needed. Sean thinks it may have been under-used.
- **No wheel.** None was drawn. Sean held the commanders back to avoid a wipe and wait for one, and it never showed. [Deflecting Swat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deflecting+Swat) was in hand again.
- **The kept hand.** A lot of lands, ramp, one artifact mana creature and one wheel, with few creatures or payoffs. [Gemstone Caverns](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gemstone+Caverns) was in it, but Sean won the roll and went first, so it never made any colour. He now thinks the keep was not ideal. This is the second game where the hand is the doubt: game 002 was a pile of wheels and one creature, game 004 a pile of lands and one wheel.
- **Both commanders, sooner.** He wants both out early and has no clean way to cheat them in or ramp to them faster. The discard-a-card parts of the deck also slow it. A discard-to-top-of-library option came up and costs tempo.
- **Alex's warp deck.** It ramps very high and warps out a large number of creatures. An artifact lets him end the turn before his end step, so the warped creatures stay. Big threats arrive cheaply and keep coming. Sean's read: without Alex's warp turns, Sharknado does much more.

Read with the earlier games: 002 said wheels are finishers and need the commanders out first, 003 said slow and no removal, and 004 says the commanders-first plan can stall on a hand with no wheel and no creature to carry it. The engine pieces worked in this game. The problem was the hand and the opponent's speed, and one game cannot split those. It is the first game on v2.2.0 and neither [Razorkin Needlehead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Razorkin+Needlehead) nor [Shark Typhoon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shark+Typhoon) is mentioned in the log.
