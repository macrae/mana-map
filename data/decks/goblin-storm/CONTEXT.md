## Summary

Zada is a red goblin deck that wins by making a wide board and then pointing one cheap spell at Zada, [Zada, Hedron Grinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zada%2C+Hedron+Grinder). She copies a spell that targets only her once for each other creature you control. A one-mana cantrip across a big goblin board is a pile of draws, treasure and a hasted team.

The bodies come first and the spells second. Goblin presence is the whole deck: a hand of cantrips and storm cards with no small goblins does nothing, and both games we have lost came from keeping that hand.

The list moved on 2026-10-18 to v1.1.0 (branch meaner-v1). The idea was meaner and faster, true bracket 3: more bodies for Zada to copy onto, and red mana that scales with the board. The extra bodies are [Beetleback Chief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beetleback+Chief), [Goblin Instigator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Instigator), [Legion Warboss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Legion+Warboss) and [Fable of the Mirror-Breaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fable+of+the+Mirror-Breaker). [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge) turns every new body into damage. A set of cheap Zada targets replaced the clunkier cards. The Summary block above carries the bracket and the DeckCheck reading. The new cards arrive over the next days, so the sleeved deck is a mix until they land. No game has been played with the new list.

## How it plays

**Early turns.** Make small goblins. [Dragon Fodder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dragon+Fodder), [Krenko's Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%27s+Command), [Mogg War Marshal](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mogg+War+Marshal), [Searslicer Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Searslicer+Goblin), [Goblin Lackey](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Lackey), [Goblin Instigator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Instigator), [Beetleback Chief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beetleback+Chief) are the hands we want. [Legion Warboss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Legion+Warboss) makes a goblin every combat. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Skirk Prospector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skirk+Prospector), [Ruby Medallion](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ruby+Medallion) speed it up. The Numbers section says how often the commander lands and how often an early token maker shows up. Those figures were measured on the working list, so read them with that in mind.

**Zada lands.** She costs four and comes down on turn four or so. Once she is out, each cheap targeted spell aimed at her is copied for every other creature. [Crimson Wisps](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crimson+Wisps), [Expedite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Expedite), [Fists of Flame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fists+of+Flame), [Wild Ride](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wild+Ride), [Impolite Entrance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Impolite+Entrance), [Might of the Meek](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Might+of+the+Meek) draw or give haste and trample. [Reckless Ransacking](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reckless+Ransacking), [Sudden Breakthrough](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sudden+Breakthrough) each leave a treasure, so one cast becomes a treasure for every body. [Infuriate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Infuriate), [Ticked Off](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ticked+Off) make the hit bigger. The proved line is stack 001: Crimson Wisps on Zada with a goblin board gives a hasted team and a stack of draws.

**The storm turn.** Spells cast in a row feed the storm cards and the magecraft engine. [Empty the Warrens](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Empty+the+Warrens) cast as a late spell makes a swarm of goblins, and with [Impact Tremors](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Impact+Tremors) out every body pings the table (stack 002). [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge) does the same job harder: each body that enters hits every opponent. [Fists of Flame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fists+of+Flame) turns a long draw count into one huge trampler (stack 003). [Twinflame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twinflame) and [Electroduplicate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Electroduplicate) copy the best creatures for a turn, and each copy is another body entering.

**Copy the engine.** [Fable of the Mirror-Breaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fable+of+the+Mirror-Breaker) makes a goblin, loots, and then flips into a Kiki-Jiki that copies a nonlegendary creature each turn. Copying [Siege-Gang Commander](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Siege-Gang+Commander) or [Beetleback Chief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beetleback+Chief) is more bodies and more pings.

**No loop now.** The two-card loop with [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Storm-Kiln+Artist) (stack 005) left with Haze of Rage, so the deck has no proved infinite today. That is on purpose: it is what takes the bracket down. Storm-Kiln Artist is still a fine engine that grows with every spell and artifact.

**Krenko is not a combo here.** [Krenko, Mob Boss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%2C+Mob+Boss) with [Skirk Prospector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skirk+Prospector) and [Goblin Warchief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Warchief) looked infinite in the combo graph. Stack 004 refuted it for this deck. Krenko is a very strong engine that doubles the board each turn, and nothing more.

**Mana.** The ramp is the swarm. [Battle Hymn](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Battle+Hymn) and [Brightstone Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brightstone+Ritual) count creatures, and Skirk Prospector turns a spare token into red. [Seething Song](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Seething+Song) is the plain burst. On the big turn count goblins, not lands.

**Mulligan.** Keep goblin bodies plus lands plus a cheap cantrip. Ship hands that are all payoff. The pilot's rule from both losses: goblin presence gates the mulligan, not cantrip density.

Two games, two losses, both on v1.0.0, both with the same cause underneath: I kept a hand with no small goblins. No games yet on v1.1.0.

- **Game 001 (Alex's, three players):** a clean mulligan hand of land, one-mana spells, [Crimson Wisps](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crimson+Wisps) and [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Storm-Kiln+Artist), and zero goblins. The rule I keep from both: goblin presence gates the mulligan, not cantrip density.
