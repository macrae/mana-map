## Summary

Zada is a red goblin deck that wins by making a wide board and then pointing one cheap spell at Zada, [Zada, Hedron Grinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zada%2C+Hedron+Grinder). She copies a spell that targets only her once for each other creature you control. A one-mana cantrip across a big goblin board is a pile of draws, treasure and a hasted team.

The bodies come first and the spells second. Goblin presence is the whole deck: a hand of cantrips and storm cards with no small goblins does nothing, and both games we have lost came from keeping that hand. Zada herself is not required for every line. Some of the proved turns run off [Impact Tremors](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Impact+Tremors) or [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Storm-Kiln+Artist) with her still in the command zone.

The deck is the precon-as-printed list, sleeved and untouched since the first build. It has not won a game yet, and the two losses were keeps, not list problems. See Pilot notes.

## How it plays

**Early turns.** Make small goblins. [Dragon Fodder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dragon+Fodder), [Krenko's Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%27s+Command), [Mogg War Marshal](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mogg+War+Marshal), [Searslicer Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Searslicer+Goblin) and [Goblin Lackey](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Lackey) are the hands we want. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring), [Skirk Prospector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skirk+Prospector) and [Ruby Medallion](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ruby+Medallion) speed it up. The Numbers section says how often the commander lands and how often an early token maker shows up.

**Zada lands.** She costs four and comes down on turn four or so. Once she is out, each cheap targeted spell aimed at her is copied for every other creature. [Crimson Wisps](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crimson+Wisps), [Expedite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Expedite), [Fists of Flame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fists+of+Flame) and [Wild Ride](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wild+Ride) all do real work this way. The proved line is stack 001: Crimson Wisps on Zada with a goblin board gives a hasted team and a stack of draws.

**The storm turn.** Spells cast in a row feed the storm cards and the magecraft engine. [Empty the Warrens](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Empty+the+Warrens) cast as a late spell makes a swarm of goblins, and with [Impact Tremors](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Impact+Tremors) out every body pings the table (stack 002). [Fists of Flame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fists+of+Flame) turns a long draw count into one huge trampler (stack 003). [Haze of Rage](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Haze+of+Rage) with buyback and [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Storm-Kiln+Artist) is a true infinite loop (stack 005). That loop is the only proved infinite we have.

**Krenko is not a combo here.** [Krenko, Mob Boss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%2C+Mob+Boss) with [Skirk Prospector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skirk+Prospector) and [Goblin Warchief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Warchief) looked infinite in the combo graph. Stack 004 refuted it for this deck. Krenko is a very strong engine that doubles the board each turn, and nothing more.

**Mana.** The ramp is the swarm. [Battle Hymn](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Battle+Hymn) and [Brightstone Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brightstone+Ritual) count creatures, and Skirk Prospector turns a spare token into red. On the big turn count goblins, not lands.

**Mulligan.** Keep goblin bodies plus lands plus a cheap cantrip. Ship hands that are all payoff. The pilot's rule from both losses: goblin presence gates the mulligan, not cantrip density.

## Pilot notes

<!-- ctx:gen record -->

- **How games ended:** raced 1, stalled 1

Two games, two losses, both with the list as sleeved and the same cause underneath: I kept a hand with no small goblins.

- **Game 001 (Alex's, three players):** a clean mulligan hand of land, one-mana spells, [Crimson Wisps](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crimson+Wisps) and [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Storm-Kiln+Artist), and zero goblins. The copy half of the engine had nothing to multiply. I called it a bad keep, not a deck problem.
- **Game 002 (Oliver's, five players):** kept lands and [Krenko, Mob Boss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%2C+Mob+Boss), with expensive spells and no small goblins. The table was three red decks, and Oliver won on turn five off Krenko tokens and pings.

The rule I keep from both: goblin presence gates the mulligan, not cantrip density. No change to the deck.
