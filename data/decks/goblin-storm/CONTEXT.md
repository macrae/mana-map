## Summary

Zada is a red goblin deck that wins by making a wide board and then pointing one cheap spell at [Zada, Hedron Grinder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Zada%2C+Hedron+Grinder). She copies a spell that targets only her once for each other creature you control. A one-mana cantrip across a big goblin board is a pile of draws, treasure and a hasted team.

The bodies come first and the spells second. Goblin presence is the whole deck: a hand of cantrips and storm cards with no small goblins does nothing, and both games on record were lost by keeping that hand.

The list moved on 2026-10-27 to v1.2.0 (branch storm-tune-v1), printed proxies in the sleeves. The aim is a push on DeckCheck's Threat Index, read through Zada's text, with interaction and protection as the two key axes. The deck now has red answers again ([Abrade](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Abrade), [Untimely Malfunction](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Untimely+Malfunction), [Harsh Mentor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Harsh+Mentor)), real protection for Zada that never gives her shroud ([Hexing Squelcher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hexing+Squelcher), [Deflecting Swat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deflecting+Swat), [Plaza of Heroes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Plaza+of+Heroes), next to [Swiftfoot Boots](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swiftfoot+Boots)), and more spells that become a team-wide effect when Zada copies them. The Summary block above still shows the DeckCheck reading from the older list, so the push is not measured yet.

Kept on purpose: [Brightstone Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brightstone+Ritual) (the pilot's favourite), [Blasphemous Act](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blasphemous+Act), [Past in Flames](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Past+in+Flames) and [Swiftfoot Boots](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Swiftfoot+Boots). The pilot rejects mass land denial in bracket 3, so there is no Blood Moon.

## How it plays

**Early turns.** Make small goblins. [Dragon Fodder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dragon+Fodder), [Krenko's Command](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%27s+Command), [Mogg War Marshal](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mogg+War+Marshal), [Searslicer Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Searslicer+Goblin), [Goblin Lackey](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Lackey), [Goblin Instigator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Instigator) and [Hexing Squelcher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hexing+Squelcher) are the hands we want. [Legion Warboss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Legion+Warboss) makes a goblin every combat. [Sol Ring](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sol+Ring) and [Skirk Prospector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skirk+Prospector) speed it up. The Numbers section says how often the commander lands and how often an early token maker shows up. Those figures were measured on an older list, so read them with that in mind.

**Zada lands.** She costs four and comes down on turn four or so. Once she is out, each cheap targeted spell aimed at her is copied for every other creature. [Crimson Wisps](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Crimson+Wisps), [Expedite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Expedite), [Fists of Flame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fists+of+Flame), [Wild Ride](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wild+Ride), [Impolite Entrance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Impolite+Entrance) and [Might of the Meek](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Might+of+the+Meek) draw or give haste and trample. [Accelerate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Accelerate) is the best of the new ones: a hasted team and a card per creature, at instant speed. [Ancestral Anger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ancestral+Anger) is a card per creature plus trample for one mana. [Reckless Ransacking](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reckless+Ransacking) and [Sudden Breakthrough](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sudden+Breakthrough) each leave a treasure, so one cast becomes a treasure for every body. [Infuriate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Infuriate) and [Ticked Off](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ticked+Off) make the hit bigger. The proved line is stack 001: Crimson Wisps on Zada with a goblin board gives a hasted team and a stack of draws.

**Copies that do not need a target.** [Heat Shimmer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heat+Shimmer) and [Molten Duplication](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Molten+Duplication) make every creature copy itself with haste. Zada's own copy becomes a second legend and goes to the legend rule, so keep the original. [Twinflame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Twinflame) and [Electroduplicate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Electroduplicate) do the same job for the best bodies for a turn, and each copy is another body entering.

**The storm turn.** Spells cast in a row feed the storm cards and the magecraft engine. [Empty the Warrens](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Empty+the+Warrens) cast as a late spell makes a swarm of goblins, and with [Impact Tremors](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Impact+Tremors) out every body pings the table (stack 002). [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge) does the same job harder: each body that enters hits every opponent. [Fists of Flame](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fists+of+Flame) turns a long draw count into one huge trampler (stack 003). [Battle Hymn](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Battle+Hymn) is the ritual that grows with the board. It has no target, so Zada does not copy it, but it pays for the big turn.

**Copy the engine.** [Fable of the Mirror-Breaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fable+of+the+Mirror-Breaker) makes a goblin, loots, and then flips into a Kiki-Jiki that copies a nonlegendary creature each turn. Copying [Siege-Gang Commander](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Siege-Gang+Commander) is more bodies and more pings.

**Answers and protection.** The deck can answer things again. [Abrade](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Abrade) hits a creature or breaks an artifact. [Untimely Malfunction](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Untimely+Malfunction) redirects a single-target spell or ability, breaks an artifact, or stops two creatures blocking. [Harsh Mentor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Harsh+Mentor) pings an opponent who activates an ability of an artifact, creature or land. For Zada herself, [Hexing Squelcher](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hexing+Squelcher) gives the other creatures ward and keeps our spells from being countered, [Deflecting Swat](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Deflecting+Swat) is free with a commander out, and [Plaza of Heroes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Plaza+of+Heroes) can exile itself to give a legend hexproof and indestructible. Never shroud: Zada's own spells must target her.

**No loop now.** The two-card loop with [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Storm-Kiln+Artist) (stack 005) left with Haze of Rage, so the deck has no proved infinite today. That is on purpose: it is what keeps the bracket at three. Storm-Kiln Artist is still a fine engine that grows with every spell and artifact.

**Krenko is not a combo here.** [Krenko, Mob Boss](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Krenko%2C+Mob+Boss) with [Skirk Prospector](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skirk+Prospector) and [Goblin Warchief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Warchief) looked infinite in the combo graph. Stack 004 refuted it for this deck. Krenko is a very strong engine that doubles the board each turn, and nothing more.

**Finding the key goblin.** [Goblin Matron](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Matron) is a goblin body that fetches another goblin to hand. The pilot keeps it for Krenko or Siege-Gang on a goblin body.

**Mana.** The ramp is the swarm. [Brightstone Ritual](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Brightstone+Ritual) and [Battle Hymn](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Battle+Hymn) count creatures, and Skirk Prospector turns a spare token into red. On the big turn count goblins, not lands. The new lands ([Three Tree City](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Three+Tree+City), [Sokenzan, Crucible of Defiance](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sokenzan%2C+Crucible+of+Defiance), [Plaza of Heroes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Plaza+of+Heroes)) all come in untapped, replacing tapped cyclers.

**Mulligan.** Keep goblin bodies plus lands plus a cheap cantrip. Ship hands that are all payoff. The pilot's rule from both losses: goblin presence gates the mulligan, not cantrip density.
