## Summary

Teach Red is a sixty-card Modern deck built to show brand-new players what red is. It is mono-red, has no sideboard, and aims at about $100 with rares allowed. It is unplayed and calibrated by argument only.

The deck is cheap creatures that hit fast, plus burn. [Raging Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raging+Goblin) and [Skizzik](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skizzik) show haste. [Shock](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shock) and [Lightning Strike](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Strike) show burn in response. [Borderland Marauder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Borderland+Marauder) shows that a creature can change when it attacks. [Fiery Hellhound](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Hellhound) gives spare mana a use, and [Goblin Heelcutter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Heelcutter) gets past big blockers. It plays against the four other mono-colour teach decks. The suggested first game is against teach-white, to learn combat.

## How it plays

Red wants to be the aggressor. Creatures come down early, and the burn clears a blocker or finishes the player. Most of the lessons are about timing inside a turn.

Haste and summoning sickness: [Raging Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raging+Goblin) attacks the turn it lands, which shows what a new creature normally cannot do. [Skizzik](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skizzik) repeats the lesson at a higher cost.

Burn in response: [Shock](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shock) and [Lightning Strike](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Strike) are instants. Cast one while the other player's spell or attack is on the stack and watch it resolve first.

Attacking is different from blocking: [Borderland Marauder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Borderland+Marauder) is a small 1/2 that gets +2/+0 when it attacks, until end of turn. It teaches an attack trigger and what "until end of turn" means, and why the same creature is weak on defence and strong on offence.

The end step: [Goblin Heelcutter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Heelcutter) can dash in, and dashing is visibly cheaper than casting it, so it is the one place the deck teaches dash. It returns to hand at end of turn. [Skizzik](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skizzik) is sacrificed at end of turn unless it was kicked.

Spare mana: [Fiery Hellhound](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Hellhound) turns extra red mana into power, so a late land is never dead.

Getting past blockers: [Goblin Heelcutter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Heelcutter) stops a creature from blocking, which shows that attacking is more than raw size.

Casting after combat: [Goblin Boarders](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Boarders) gets its raid bonus if you attacked first. [Skewer the Critics](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skewer+the+Critics) is cheaper if you dealt damage first (spectacle). Both teach that the second main phase matters.

Start of combat: [Valor Singer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Valor+Singer) does its work at that step.

Must-attack and counters: [Bloodcrazed Neonate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodcrazed+Neonate) has to attack each combat and gets a counter when it deals damage. [Ravenous Giant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ravenous+Giant) has an upkeep cost, so it teaches that upkeep happens before you draw.

Against teach-white, combat is the thing to watch. The Numbers section says the goldfish does not model this deck.

## Change history

<!-- ctx:gen history -->
- **V2** (2026-11-03) — in: Borderland Marauder; out: Mardu Scout
- **v1.0.0 · V1** (2026-11-02) — first list, 60 cards. _first teaching list: calibrated by argument, unplayed_
<!-- /ctx:gen history -->

v1.0.0, saved and tagged, unplayed. The strategist read red as the weakest of the five teach decks. Out: the three end-of-turn-sacrifice haste creatures, one Bloodcrazed Neonate, one Raging Goblin. In: three Fiery Hellhound, two Goblin Heelcutter. Fiery Hellhound gives spare mana a use. Goblin Heelcutter gets past big blockers.

V2 (commit 0892a555): out four Mardu Scout, in four Borderland Marauder. In mono-red, Mardu Scout's dash cost and cast cost are the same mana, which confuses a newcomer. Borderland Marauder teaches one clear idea instead: an attack trigger that lasts until end of turn. The dash lesson now lives on Goblin Heelcutter alone, where dashing is cheaper than casting. Raging Goblin moved to a paper printing; how it plays did not change. Nothing here has been tested in play.
