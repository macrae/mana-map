## Summary

Teach Red is a sixty-card Modern deck built to show brand-new players what red is. It is mono-red, has no sideboard, and aims at about $100 with rares allowed. It is unplayed and calibrated by argument only.

The deck is cheap creatures that hit fast, plus burn. [Raging Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raging+Goblin) and [Skizzik](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skizzik) show haste. [Shock](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shock) and [Lightning Strike](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Strike) show burn in response. [Fiery Hellhound](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Hellhound) gives spare mana a use, and [Goblin Heelcutter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Heelcutter) gets past big blockers. It plays against the four other mono-colour teach decks. The suggested first game is against teach-white, to learn combat.

## How it plays

Red wants to be the aggressor. Creatures come down early, and the burn clears a blocker or finishes the player. Most of the lessons are about timing inside a turn.

Haste and summoning sickness: [Raging Goblin](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raging+Goblin) attacks the turn it lands, which shows what a new creature normally cannot do. [Skizzik](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skizzik) repeats the lesson at a higher cost.

Burn in response: [Shock](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Shock) and [Lightning Strike](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lightning+Strike) are instants. Cast one while the other player's spell or attack is on the stack and watch it resolve first.

The end step: [Mardu Scout](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mardu+Scout) and [Goblin Heelcutter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Heelcutter) can dash in and return to hand at end of turn. [Skizzik](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skizzik) is sacrificed unless it was kicked.

Spare mana: [Fiery Hellhound](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Hellhound) turns extra red mana into power, so a late land is never dead.

Getting past blockers: [Goblin Heelcutter](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Heelcutter) stops a creature from blocking, which shows that attacking is more than raw size.

Casting after combat: [Goblin Boarders](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Goblin+Boarders) gets its raid bonus if you attacked first. [Skewer the Critics](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Skewer+the+Critics) is cheaper if you dealt damage first (spectacle). Both teach that the second main phase matters.

Start of combat: [Valor Singer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Valor+Singer) does its work at that step.

Must-attack and counters: [Bloodcrazed Neonate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Bloodcrazed+Neonate) has to attack each combat and gets a counter when it deals damage. [Ravenous Giant](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ravenous+Giant) has an upkeep cost, so it teaches that upkeep happens before you draw.

Against teach-white, combat is the thing to watch. The Numbers section says the goldfish does not model this deck.
