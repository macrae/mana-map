## Summary

Valgavoth is a Rakdos hand-size burn deck at bracket three, and it has never been played. The plan is to hurt opponents on THEIR turn for drawing, holding and discarding cards, so [Valgavoth, Harrower of Souls](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Valgavoth%2C+Harrower+of+Souls) draws and grows every round. Sean calls him "a powerful card advantage engine and damage threat that slots perfectly into the Hand Size burn strategy".

Getting him down early is the point. He has flying and ward, and his counters matter, so the deck wants him cast by turn four and keeps ways to protect him and to move his counters. The Numbers section has how often the goldfish casts him and when.

The deck grew out of a shelved search for a Rakdos storm deck. Kuja lost to Valgavoth because Kuja makes go-wide Wizard tokens and pays off on our turn. This deck does neither.

The goldfish cannot read about half these cards, so nothing in Numbers is evidence of strength yet.

## How it plays

**The engine.** Valgavoth triggers on an opponent's first life loss during each of their own turns. He puts a counter on himself and draws us a card. A ping on our own turn does nothing for him. So the deck is built to make opponents lose life while it is their turn.

**Three ways to cause that loss.**
- Draw punishers: [Underworld Dreams](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Underworld+Dreams), [Fate Unraveler](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fate+Unraveler), [Kederekt Parasite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kederekt+Parasite), [Razorkin Needlehead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Razorkin+Needlehead), [Ob Nixilis, the Hate-Twisted](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ob+Nixilis%2C+the+Hate-Twisted), [Orcish Bowmasters](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Orcish+Bowmasters), [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sheoldred%2C+the+Apocalypse) and [Scrawling Crawler](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Scrawling+Crawler). [Spiteful Visions](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Spiteful+Visions) feeds them an extra draw each draw step.
- Upkeep and hand-size damage: [Black Vise](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Black+Vise), [Viseling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Viseling), [Lavaborn Muse](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Lavaborn+Muse), [Roiling Vortex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Roiling+Vortex) and [Sulfuric Vortex](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sulfuric+Vortex) all hit at the start of their turn.
- Spell and discard punishers: [Painful Quandary](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Painful+Quandary) and [Kaervek the Merciless](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Kaervek+the+Merciless) punish their casting. [Megrim](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Megrim), [Liliana's Caress](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Liliana%27s+Caress), [Quest for the Nihil Stone](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quest+for+the+Nihil+Stone) and [Waste Not](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Waste+Not) punish discarding.

**Wheels.** [Magus of the Wheel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Magus+of+the+Wheel), [Reforge the Soul](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Reforge+the+Soul) and [Dark Deal](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dark+Deal) refill every hand. They go out only with a punisher already on the battlefield, so the wheel is the payoff, never the setup.

**Multipliers.** [Furnace of Rath](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Furnace+of+Rath) doubles every point of damage and [Fiery Emancipation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Emancipation) triples ours. Together with the punishers they are the reach.

**Keeping Valgavoth alive.** He gets a protection suite and a counter-saver. The Ozolith and Nesting Grounds can carry his counters to another creature if he dies.

**Mana.** Plenty of rocks and a wide spread of two-colour lands, aimed at casting him by turn four. The triple-colour cards are the weak spot, see Open questions.

**What it avoids.** No go-wide tokens, no flat anthems, no two-card infinites.

## Pilot notes

<!-- ctx:gen record -->
- No games logged yet (`manamap pilot deck-notes valgavoth add …`).
<!-- /ctx:gen record -->

Never played, so nothing here comes from a game. What follows is what Sean decided while building it.

- He wants Valgavoth on the battlefield as soon as possible: "getting Valgavoth on the battlefield asap is critical... it has flying and protection, and those counters are important".
- Budget matters. He rejected Mox Diamond as too expensive. Badlands, Gemstone Caverns and Wheel of Fortune were cut for price, and Magus of the Wheel took the Wheel of Fortune slot.
- Standing dislikes: go-wide tokens, flat anthems, two-card infinites. Other infinites are fine.
- The goldfish has no blockers or removal and does not read about half the list. Treat its figures as a floor on the plan, not a verdict.

## Open questions

<!-- ctx:gen queue -->
- Nothing queued for this deck yet. After a game, `/incubate` turns what you noticed into testable claims.

- Do Black Vise and Viseling, which want full hands, clash with the wheels that empty them? Test: play games with the wheels and the vises together and watch whether the vises ever go quiet.
- Is the wheel count right? Too few and the punishers wait. Too many and the vises idle.
- Does Valgavoth reach commander-damage lethal in a realistic game, or is he only an engine? Play and see.
- The colour sources are a few short of the triple-pip target: [Underworld Dreams](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Underworld+Dreams) wants three black, [Furnace of Rath](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Furnace+of+Rath) and [Fiery Emancipation](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fiery+Emancipation) want three red. Another source or a swap is the likely fix.
- [Fable of the Mirror-Breaker](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Fable+of+the+Mirror-Breaker) and [Generous Plunderer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Generous+Plunderer) are template leftovers. Neither fits the plan cleanly and both are candidates to swap out.
