## Summary

Emiel is a Selesnya blink deck, built by the deterministic builder and still on the bench. The motor is printed on the commander: for {3} [Emiel the Blessed](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Emiel+the+Blessed) blinks any other creature, so every "when this enters" in the 99 becomes something you can do again. Her second ability turns each arrival into a +1/+1 counter for one mana, so the deck is also a counters deck, and a lot of the 99 puts counters on things.

The plan is to stack entry triggers (draw on a body, ramp on a body, removal on a body), double them, and grow the board until the table is under threat by turn six. It is built for bracket 4. It has no two-card infinite combos on purpose: Sean will not play them, and the builder's exclusion list removes the cards that made them. Power comes from stacked triggers and a mana sink that scales.

The weak point is that the engine is the commander. She will get removed, so the deck needs protection and a way to keep going without her. Nothing has been played yet. See Pilot notes and Open questions.

## How it plays

You cast Emiel around turn four. The Numbers section has how often she is out by turn six. The mana creatures and treasure makers in the list are there to get her out and pay for her ability.

Once she is down, every creature you play is a trigger you can reset. [Wall of Omens](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wall+of+Omens) draws again, [Eternal Witness](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Eternal+Witness) gets back a spent card, [Hanged Executioner](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hanged+Executioner) and [Knight of Autumn](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Knight+of+Autumn) answer a problem on a body, and [Sun Titan](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Sun+Titan) rebuys cheap permanents. [Restoration Angel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Restoration+Angel) does the same job at instant speed, with flash, so it also saves a creature from removal. [Panharmonicon](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Panharmonicon) makes every one of those creature triggers fire twice, which is the biggest single step up in the deck. [Seedborn Muse](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Seedborn+Muse) untaps your permanents on every other player's turn, so Emiel can blink on their turn without costing you your own.

The second half of the deck is counters. [Hardened Scales](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Hardened+Scales), [Branching Evolution](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Branching+Evolution), [Ozolith, the Shattered Spire](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ozolith%2C+the+Shattered+Spire), [Caradora, Heart of Alacria](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Caradora%2C+Heart+of+Alacria), [Michelangelo, Weirdness to 11](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Michelangelo%2C+Weirdness+to+11) and [Pir, Imaginative Rascal](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Pir%2C+Imaginative+Rascal) all make counters bigger, and [Doubling Season](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Doubling+Season) doubles counters and tokens both. Many small creatures hand out counters when they arrive. Blinking one of those with Emiel is a few counters for {3}. This is how the board gets big, and [Avenger of Zendikar](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Avenger+of+Zendikar) and [Securitron Squadron](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Securitron+Squadron) are the payoffs that turn a pile of mana into a wide board. [Walking Ballista](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Walking+Ballista) turns spare mana into reach.

Exactly how well the counters plan works is unmeasured. The goldfish has no blockers and no removal, so it tells us about mana and timing, not about surviving a board wipe or a removal spell on Emiel.
