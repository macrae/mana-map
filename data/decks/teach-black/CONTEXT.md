## Summary

A mono-black teaching deck for brand-new players, still unplayed and calibrated by argument only. It shows what black is: cheap removal, creatures that come back from the graveyard, and paying life for things. The strategist read black as the strongest of the five teach decks, so the list on disk trims one copy each of its cleanest removal, card flow and best flyer. The suggested opponent is teach-green.

The lessons it carries: an upkeep with a cost ([Phyrexian Arena](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Arena), [Vampire Lacerator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampire+Lacerator), [Priest of the Blood Rite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Priest+of+the+Blood+Rite)), instant-speed removal and what a target can be ([Disfigure](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Disfigure), [Doom Blade](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Doom+Blade), [Murder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Murder)), the graveyard as a zone ([Gravedigger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gravedigger), [Raise Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raise+Dead)), dies triggers ([Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist)), life and creatures as resources ([Village Rites](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Village+Rites)), deathtouch and lifelink in combat ([Typhoid Rats](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Typhoid+Rats), [Vampire Nighthawk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampire+Nighthawk), [Child of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Child+of+Night)), and the end step ([Dogged Pursuit](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dogged+Pursuit), [Savage Gorger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Savage+Gorger)).

## How it plays

It is a patient deck. Early on, a blocker like [Typhoid Rats](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Typhoid+Rats) keeps a bigger creature from attacking, because deathtouch trades up. [Child of Night](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Child+of+Night) is the simple two-drop: it attacks, blocks and gains life, with nothing to explain. Removal waits in hand and is cast on the other player's turn, which shows a new player that instants do not need your own turn.

Midgame, [Gravedigger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gravedigger) and [Raise Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raise+Dead) teach that the graveyard is a zone you can use, and [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) shows a trigger firing every time something dies. [Phyrexian Arena](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Arena) shows paying life each upkeep for extra cards, and [Vampire Nighthawk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampire+Nighthawk) is the clean finish: it flies, kills what it blocks, and gains life. [Priest of the Blood Rite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Priest+of+the+Blood+Rite) is the big body and likely the strongest card here.

This list is all Swamps, so the ramp lesson comes from the other decks. Against teach-green, expect the other side to be bigger and faster early, and the removal and deathtouch to do the equalising. The Numbers section says the goldfish does not model Modern, so none of this is measured. Nothing has been played yet.
