## Summary

A mono-black teaching deck for brand-new players, still unplayed and still being tuned. It is built to show what black is: cheap removal, creatures that come back from the graveyard, and paying life for cards. Each card is there to teach one thing about the turn. The suggested opponent is teach-green, played against the other four mono-colour teach decks.

The lessons it carries: an upkeep with a cost ([Phyrexian Arena](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Phyrexian+Arena), [Vampire Lacerator](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampire+Lacerator), [Priest of the Blood Rite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Priest+of+the+Blood+Rite)), instant-speed removal and what a target can be ([Disfigure](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Disfigure), [Doom Blade](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Doom+Blade), [Murder](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Murder)), the graveyard as a zone ([Gravedigger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gravedigger), [Raise Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raise+Dead)), dies triggers ([Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist)), life and creatures as resources ([Village Rites](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Village+Rites)), deathtouch and lifelink in combat ([Typhoid Rats](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Typhoid+Rats), [Vampire Nighthawk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampire+Nighthawk)), and the end step ([Dogged Pursuit](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Dogged+Pursuit), [Savage Gorger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Savage+Gorger)).

## How it plays

It is a patient deck. Early on, a blocker like [Typhoid Rats](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Typhoid+Rats) keeps a bigger creature from attacking, because deathtouch trades up. Removal waits in hand and is cast on the other player's turn, which shows a new player that instants do not need your own turn.

Midgame, [Gravedigger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Gravedigger) and [Raise Dead](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Raise+Dead) teach that the graveyard is a zone you can use, and [Blood Artist](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blood+Artist) shows a trigger firing every time something dies. [Vampire Nighthawk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vampire+Nighthawk) is the clean finish: it flies, kills what it blocks, and gains life.

Mana ramp is not really what black teaches. This list is all Swamps, so the ramp lesson comes from the other decks. Against teach-green, expect the other side to be bigger and faster early, and the removal and deathtouch to do the equalising. The Numbers section says the goldfish does not model Modern, so none of this is measured. Nothing has been played yet.
