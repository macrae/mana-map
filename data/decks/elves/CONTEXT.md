## Summary

This is Sean's all-in Modern Elves list: mono-green, 60 cards, about $100, built for best-of-one against very strong opponents. There is no sideboard on purpose. Every dollar is in the main deck.

The plan is to go wide fast and end the game with one alpha strike. One-drop elves and [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl) make the mana. [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) and [Elvish Archdruid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Archdruid) turn a pile of elves into a lot more. [Collected Company](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Collected+Company) drops two more bodies at instant speed. Then one of four finishers ends it: [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth), either [Overrun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Overrun), or [Triumph of the Hordes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Triumph+of+the+Hordes), which gives infect so ten poison wins whatever the life total.

The goldfish and Forge do not model Modern, so none of this is measured. The Numbers section has nothing for kill turn, and nobody should read the plan above as a clock.

## How it plays

**Turn one and two.** Play a one-drop. [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) and [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) are the mana. [Arbor Elf](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arbor+Elf) untaps a Forest, so it needs one. [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl) on a Forest does the same job without a body, so it dodges sweepers and does not feed Heritage Druid. The Numbers section reads green on curve as very likely once creatures count as sources.

**The untap engine.** [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) taps three untapped elves for GGG. [Nettle Sentinel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nettle+Sentinel) untaps whenever you cast a green spell, which is nearly every spell here. [Quirion Ranger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quirion+Ranger) and [Wirewood Symbiote](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wirewood+Symbiote) each untap a creature once a turn by bouncing something: Ranger returns a Forest, Symbiote returns an Elf. Tap a body, untap it, tap it again. Bouncing [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster) to replay it is a real line, because it makes a token when an elf enters.

**Mana that scales.** [Elvish Archdruid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Archdruid) adds G for each Elf you control, so the more elves the better it gets. This is what replaced the flat anthem the first list had. A flat bonus does little early. Archdruid pays more the wider you are.

**Going wide.** [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster) makes a 1/1 elf each turn another elf shows up. [Collected Company](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Collected+Company) puts two cheap elves on the board at instant speed, ideally at the end of the opponent's turn, so the board arrives out of reach of sorcery-speed sweepers.

**Digging for the finishers.** [Overrun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Overrun) and [Triumph of the Hordes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Triumph+of+the+Hordes) are sorceries, so [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling) and [Collected Company](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Collected+Company) cannot find them. The deck digs for them with cards: [Beast Whisperer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Whisperer) draws on every creature spell, [Leaf-Crowned Visionary](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Leaf-Crowned+Visionary) draws on every Elf spell and [Elvish Visionary](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Visionary) replaces itself. [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling) finds [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth), which is a creature.

**Ending it.** One alpha strike. [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) gives every creature trample and +X/+X. [Overrun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Overrun) gives +3/+3 and trample. [Triumph of the Hordes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Triumph+of+the+Hordes) gives +1/+1, trample and infect, so ten poison wins and the opponent's life total stops mattering. Triumph wants roughly four or five bodies. Having four ways to end it means one dead card does not strand the hand.

**Protection and interaction.** There is almost none. [Vines of Vastwood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vines+of+Vastwood) stops a removal spell on the key creature and doubles as a pump. [Haywire Mite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Haywire+Mite) is the only answer to a noncreature artifact or enchantment. Sean accepts this. The deck aims to win before a strong opponent can answer it, and a sweeper or fast removal on [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) is where it is weakest.

**Not measured.** There is no goldfish clock, no mulligan data and no Forge result for this deck. See Open questions.

## Cards by role

### get mana out early

[Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves), [Arbor Elf](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arbor+Elf), [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl), [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid), [Elvish Archdruid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Archdruid)

The one-drops that make the first turns work. Arbor Elf and Utopia Sprawl need a Forest. Heritage Druid and Archdruid make real mana only once there are other elves.

### untap the elves and run the engine

[Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid), [Nettle Sentinel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nettle+Sentinel), [Quirion Ranger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quirion+Ranger), [Wirewood Symbiote](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wirewood+Symbiote), [Arbor Elf](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arbor+Elf)

Heritage Druid taps three elves for GGG. Nettle Sentinel untaps when you cast a green spell. Ranger and Symbiote each untap a creature once a turn by bouncing a Forest or an Elf. Arbor Elf untaps a Forest. Symbiote is an Insect, so it does not count as an elf for Heritage Druid or Archdruid.

### draw and dig for the finisher

[Beast Whisperer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Whisperer), [Leaf-Crowned Visionary](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Leaf-Crowned+Visionary), [Elvish Visionary](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Visionary)

Whisperer draws on every creature spell. Leaf-Crowned draws on every Elf spell. Elvish Visionary replaces itself. These are how the deck finds the sorcery finishers, which Chord and Company cannot.

### go wide

[Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster), [Collected Company](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Collected+Company), [Elvish Archdruid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Archdruid)

Warmaster makes elf tokens whenever another elf enters. Company adds two bodies at instant speed. Archdruid grows with every elf.

### find the one creature

[Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling)

An instant that fetches any creature up to the mana spent, with convoke so tapped elves pay. Its main job is to find [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth).

### turn the board into one alpha strike

[Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth), [Overrun](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Overrun), [Triumph of the Hordes](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Triumph+of+the+Hordes)

Four finishers in the deck. Craterhoof is a creature and can be fetched. Overrun and Triumph are sorceries and have to be drawn or dug for. Triumph is the poison route: ten infect damage wins regardless of life total.

### protect the board and answer artifacts or enchantments

[Vines of Vastwood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vines+of+Vastwood), [Haywire Mite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Haywire+Mite)

Vines stops opposing targeting on a creature for a turn and pumps it when kicked. Haywire Mite is a one-mana body that can sacrifice to exile a noncreature artifact or enchantment.

### lands

[Forest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Forest)

Sixteen basic Forests. None enter tapped. Ranger and Sprawl both care about having Forests, and the deck has no other land type.

- **V1** (2026-10-09) — first list, 60 cards

**V1 to V2 (2026-10-11).** V1 was a budget list at about $115 with one finisher: Craterhoof, found by three Chords. Sean rebuilt it as an all-in 60 for about $100 for best-of-one, with no sideboard on purpose. The sideboard was added during the rebuild and then removed. Craterhoof went out and came back.

Out: Beast Whisperer x2, Chord of Calling x3, Elvish Clancaller x4, Elvish Warmaster x2, Haywire Mite x1. In: Collected Company x4, Elvish Archdruid x2, Elvish Visionary x1, Leaf-Crowned Visionary x2, Overrun x2, Triumph of the Hordes x1.

Why: one finisher is easy to strand, so there are now four. The scaling mana of Archdruid and the draw of Leaf-Crowned replaced Clancaller's flat anthem, which does little early. Company gives instant-speed width. None of this was measured. It is Sean's judgement of what wins fast against very strong opponents.

## Open questions

<!-- ctx:gen queue -->
- Nothing queued for this deck yet. After a game, `/incubate` turns what you noticed into testable claims.
<!-- /ctx:gen queue -->

- **Is three sorcery finishers plus one Craterhoof enough density?** Overrun and Triumph cannot be fetched by Chord or Company, so they depend on the draw and dig cards. Testable in paper games by counting how often a game stalls with the board ready and no finisher in hand.
- **Triumph or a second Overrun?** Triumph wins at ten poison whatever the life total, but needs about four or five bodies and an opponent who cannot gain life or remove infect. A second Overrun needs lethal damage the usual way. Expected effect: Triumph wins through a high life total or lifegain, while Overrun is steadier if the board is small.
- **How does it do against the current top decks?** Eldrazi, Izzet Prowess, Goryo's, Esper Blink and Affinity are the field Sean named. Nobody has tested it against any of them. The worry is the fast ones kill first and the interactive ones remove [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) or sweep the board. This needs paper games.
- **How fast does it kill?** There is no kill-turn figure and no tool to produce one for Modern. A hand-counted line or logged games are the only source.
- **Is 16 lands right?** The audit puts the mana base under the 60-card band, but the deck leans on creatures and [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl) for mana. Testable by paper games with a land swapped for a one-drop.
- **Is the thin interaction acceptable in best-of-one?** Two Vines and one Haywire Mite. Sean accepts it. The first games should show whether that costs more than it gains.
