## Summary

This is Sean's budget Modern Elves list: a mono-green, 60-card, no-sideboard pile of one-mana elves that go wide fast and end the game with a single big turn. The 16 [Forest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Forest) are the only lands. The rest of the mana comes from creatures and [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl), so the deck wants to be doing things on turn one.

The plan is simple. Drop mana elves, untap them, draw cards off the creature spells, then turn the board into damage. [Elvish Clancaller](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Clancaller) makes every other elf bigger. [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster) keeps making new elf tokens. [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) is the one-of finisher, and [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling) is how the deck finds it.

It is the bench's first 60-card deck. The goldfish, Forge, the bracket and the benchmark are Commander-only, so none of them has a word to say about it. The Numbers section is thin on purpose.

## How it plays

**Turn one and two.** Play a one-drop. [Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves) and [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) are the mana. [Arbor Elf](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arbor+Elf) untaps a Forest, so it is a Llanowar that needs a Forest to be useful. [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl) on a Forest does the same job without a body, so it dodges sweepers and does not feed Heritage Druid. The Numbers section reads green on curve as very likely once the creatures count as sources.

**The untap engine.** This is the part that makes the deck go. [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) taps three untapped elves for GGG. [Nettle Sentinel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nettle+Sentinel) does not untap on its own, but untaps whenever you cast a green spell, which is nearly every spell here. [Quirion Ranger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quirion+Ranger) and [Wirewood Symbiote](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wirewood+Symbiote) each untap a creature once a turn by bouncing something. Ranger returns a Forest, which you replay if you have not played a land. Symbiote returns an Elf. Tap a body for mana, untap it, tap it again. Bouncing a [Beast Whisperer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Whisperer) or [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster) to replay it is also a real line, because the Warmaster makes a token when an elf enters.

**Drawing cards.** [Beast Whisperer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Whisperer) draws a card for every creature spell, and nearly the whole deck is creatures. Once it lands, a turn of dropping one-drops refills your hand.

**Going wide.** [Elvish Clancaller](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Clancaller) gives the other elves +1/+1. [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster) makes a 1/1 elf each turn that another elf shows up. Both turn a pile of mana dorks into an army that no longer looks like mana dorks. The Clancaller can also fetch a second Clancaller late. The Warmaster has a late-game pump if the game stalls.

**Ending it.** [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) gives every creature trample and +X/+X, with X the number of creatures. [Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling) fetches it, or whichever creature the board needs, and convoke lets the elves pay for it by tapping. That is why the deck wants its creatures out and ready and not attacking early.

**Protection and interaction.** There is almost none. [Vines of Vastwood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vines+of+Vastwood) stops a removal spell on the key creature and doubles as a pump. [Haywire Mite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Haywire+Mite) is the only answer to a noncreature artifact or enchantment. A single sweeper or a fast removal spell on [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) is where this deck is weakest. The audit flags interaction as under the 60-card band.

**Not measured.** There is no goldfish clock, no mulligan data and no Forge result for this deck. Where a pilot would want a "kills on turn N" figure, there is none. See Open questions.

## Cards by role

### get mana out early

[Llanowar Elves](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Llanowar+Elves), [Arbor Elf](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arbor+Elf), [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl), [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid)

The one-drops that make the first turns work. Arbor Elf and Utopia Sprawl need a Forest to work. Heritage Druid makes mana only by tapping three elves.

### untap the elves and run the engine

[Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid), [Nettle Sentinel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nettle+Sentinel), [Quirion Ranger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quirion+Ranger), [Wirewood Symbiote](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wirewood+Symbiote), [Arbor Elf](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Arbor+Elf)

Heritage Druid taps three elves for GGG. Nettle Sentinel untaps when you cast a green spell. Quirion Ranger and Wirewood Symbiote each untap a creature once a turn by bouncing a Forest or an Elf. Arbor Elf untaps a Forest. Symbiote is an Insect, so it does not count as an elf for Heritage Druid.

### draw cards off the creatures

[Beast Whisperer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Beast+Whisperer)

Draws on every creature spell, which is almost every spell in the deck.

### make the elves bigger and more numerous

[Elvish Clancaller](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Clancaller), [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster)

Clancaller gives other elves +1/+1 and can fetch another Clancaller. Warmaster makes elf tokens, once a turn, whenever another elf enters, and pumps the team late.

### find the right creature (toolbox)

[Chord of Calling](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Chord+of+Calling)

An instant that fetches any creature up to the mana spent, with convoke so tapped elves pay. It finds [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth) or the one body the board needs.

### turn the board into damage

[Craterhoof Behemoth](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Craterhoof+Behemoth), [Elvish Warmaster](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Warmaster), [Elvish Clancaller](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Elvish+Clancaller)

Craterhoof gives trample and +X/+X, where X is the creatures you control. The lords make that X count for more.

### protect the board and answer artifacts or enchantments

[Vines of Vastwood](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Vines+of+Vastwood), [Haywire Mite](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Haywire+Mite)

Vines stops opposing targeting on a creature for a turn and pumps it when kicked. Haywire Mite is a one-mana body that can sacrifice to exile a noncreature artifact or enchantment, and gains 2 life if it dies.

### lands

[Forest](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Forest)

Sixteen basic Forests. None enter tapped. Quirion Ranger and Utopia Sprawl both care about having Forests, and the deck has no other land type.

- **How fast does it kill?** There is no kill-turn figure, because the goldfish does not model 60-card decks. Until a game or a hand-counted line gives one, we do not know what turn this deck wins on.
- **Is 16 lands too few?** The audit puts the mana base under the 60-card band for land count. This deck leans on creatures and [Utopia Sprawl](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Utopia+Sprawl) for mana, so the band may not apply. Testable by paper games with and without a land or two swapped for something that costs one.

- **Combos.** None are in the deck as built. Three are one card short per the Numbers: [Quirion Ranger](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Quirion+Ranger) with Ashaya, [Wirewood Symbiote](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Wirewood+Symbiote) with Maskwood Nexus, and [Heritage Druid](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Heritage+Druid) with [Nettle Sentinel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nettle+Sentinel) and Cloudstone Curio. Whether any is worth playing in Modern is Sean's call.
