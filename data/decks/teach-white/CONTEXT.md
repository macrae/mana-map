## Summary

This is the white deck of five mono-colour teaching decks. It is a 60-card Modern list with no sideboard, built to a budget of about $100 with rares allowed. It is written for brand-new players, and it plays against the other four teach-* decks.

White's job is to teach combat. Small fliers, lifelinkers and tokens attack, the opponent decides how to block, and a few instants change the result. The deck also teaches casting after combat ([Mardu Hordechief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mardu+Hordechief)), +1/+1 counters ([Ajani's Pridemate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ajani%27s+Pridemate), [Blade Instructor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blade+Instructor)), the upkeep ([Nyx-Fleece Ram](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx-Fleece+Ram)), the end step ([Otherworldly Journey](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otherworldly+Journey)) and responding at instant speed ([Karametra's Blessing](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Karametra%27s+Blessing), [Village Bell-Ringer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Village+Bell-Ringer)).

It has not been played yet and is still being tuned. Suggested first game: against teach-red, because red's damage makes the combat lessons bite.

## How it plays

The deck is a cheap creature curve that attacks. [Healer's Hawk](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Healer%27s+Hawk) is the one-drop: it flies and gains life. At two mana, [Ajani's Pridemate](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Ajani%27s+Pridemate) grows whenever you gain life, and [Nyx-Fleece Ram](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Nyx-Fleece+Ram) gains a life each upkeep, so the two feed each other and show that counters stay on a creature.

At three, [Blade Instructor](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Blade+Instructor) puts a counter on an attacker each time it attacks, so the team grows as the game goes on. Play [Mardu Hordechief](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Mardu+Hordechief) after combat so the raid bonus makes a Warrior token. That teaches why the order of phases matters.

At four and five, the deck turns up the pressure. [Falconer Adept](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Falconer+Adept) and [Leonin Warleader](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Leonin+Warleader) make tokens every time they attack, and the Warleader's cats have lifelink. [Serra Angel](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Serra+Angel) attacks without tapping, so she is also ready to block.

The tricks teach the stack. Hold [Karametra's Blessing](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Karametra%27s+Blessing) for a block so a trade flips. [Village Bell-Ringer](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Village+Bell-Ringer) has flash, so you can cast it mid-combat as a surprise blocker. [Otherworldly Journey](https://manamap.seanmacrae.com/viz/index.html?cards=Otherworldly+Journey) saves a creature from removal and brings it back at the end step with a counter.

White has no ramp, so the deck cannot show mana ramp itself. The other four decks cover that and the differences between colours. Flying, vigilance and lifelink are what white contributes.
